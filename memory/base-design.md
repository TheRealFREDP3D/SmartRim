# Base Design

## Site Selection (before building)

- Check terrain fertility near the base location (wiki: Growing). Rich soil > normal soil > stoney soil.
- Place the main growing zone on rich soil if available within ~20 tiles of the shelter. Normal soil is acceptable for secondary crops.
- Stony soil only for plants with low fertility requirements.
- Also consider: water source (river), mountain cover (defense), geysers (power), flat ground.
- Terrain fertility affects growth speed, not yield. Planting on normal soil is roughly as work-efficient, just slower.

## Shelter (Day 1)

- Default starter shelter: 7x7 room with walls + door. Temporary only.
- Early game: combo barracks + dining + rec room into one large room to save work (community tip). Separate as colony grows.
- Horseshoe pin: place chairs in throwing area; pawns sit and play for extra comfort (community tip)
- Do NOT pack everything into one room long-term. Separate room roles per the wiki (Room_roles):
  - Bedroom: bed only (mood from impressiveness). One per colonist.
  - Dining room: table + chairs (mood from impressiveness).
  - Kitchen: stove (cleanliness affects food poisoning and work speed).
  - Workshop: workbenches (no mood, but cleanliness affects work speed).
  - Hospital: medical beds (cleanliness affects surgery success).
  - Laboratory: research bench (cleanliness affects research speed).
  - Rec room: horseshoes, chess, TV (mood from impressiveness).
- A room can serve multiple roles (e.g., bedroom + dining room) but mixing kitchen/workshop with living spaces causes problems.
- Smaller (4x4-5x5) only for emergency starter shacks; larger needs more materials/time
- Use existing ruins/mountains as walls to save resources
- Pathing: wood or steel floor gives 100% movement speed; bare ground slows pawns. Metal floors clean at 60% speed vs carpet 200% (community tip)
- Rooms without corners save materials (walls block explosions) (community tip)
- 11x11 rooms are a good sweet spot for most purposes (community tip)
- Keep power supplies and storage off the beaten path (raiders target them)
- Batteries separate from generators (explosion cascade risk)
- Main stockpile as freezer: prevents perishable spoilage (community tip)
- Turn off unused workstations to save power (community tip)
- Keep doors open inside base for hauling efficiency; close during raids/infestations (community tip)
- Roofs held up in 6-tile radius by a single wall/column (community tip)
- Keep home area compact (cleaning/fire management)

## Freezer

- Room below 0°C (32°F) using coolers
- Double-walled for insulation
- Two coolers for redundancy
- Hot side of cooler faces outdoors
- Set below -5°C to -10°C

## Kitchen

- Separate room (stops "food poisoning from dirty cooking area")
- Enclosed, floored
- Light inside
- Nearby ingredient stockpile (raw food only)
- Limit foot traffic through kitchen

## Bedrooms

- One per colonist. A shared barracks (multiple colonists in one room) is a mood penalty: Dull barracks -5 and Awful barracks -7 versus a private bedroom (wiki: Thoughts, Bedroom)
- At least 3x3
- Bed + light minimum
- Spacious+Impressive = +10 mood

## Killbox

- Single entrance to colony (no door)

- Walled corridor leading in
- Sandbags inside for cover
- Turrets covering the corridor
- Funnel all raids through chokepoint

## Room Roles

- Bedroom, dining room, workshop, hospital
- Clean rooms = better mood + efficiency
- Reduce traffic through production areas

## Power

- Wind turbine: place in clear area, no obstructions
- Connect with power conduits
- Replace torches with standing lamps once powered

## Mountain Bases

- Safe from drop pod raids and siege mortars
- Vulnerable to insect infestations
- Build under Thin Roof for mountain protection without infestation risk
