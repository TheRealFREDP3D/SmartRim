**Peg legs** are a [artificial body part](/wiki/Artificial_body_part "Artificial body part") that replaces a pawn's leg. It is the worst kind of leg available, but is better than having a missing leg.

## Acquisition[[edit](/index.php?title=Peg_leg&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Acquisition")]

Unlike most other prostheses, peg legs don't have a separate item form. Instead, when ordering a [surgical operation](/wiki/Health#Operations "Health") on a pawn, [![Wood](/images/thumb/3/33/Woodlog_c.png/16px-Woodlog_c.png)](/wiki/Wood "Wood") 1 [Wood](/wiki/Wood "Wood"), [![Herbal medicine](/images/thumb/a/af/Herbal_medicine.png/16px-Herbal_medicine.png)](/wiki/Herbal_medicine "Herbal medicine") 2 [Herbal medicines](/wiki/Herbal_medicine "Herbal medicine") is consumed and the peg leg is added to the pawn. Converting a log to a peg leg does not take any work. Notably, when removed the peg leg turns back into a wooden log. 

## Summary[[edit](/index.php?title=Peg_leg&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Summary")]

Peg legs replace the user's [entire organic leg](/wiki/Table_of_Human_Body_Parts "Table of Human Body Parts"). The peg leg has a part efficiency of 60%. This partially replaces the functionality of a normal leg - when coupled with each leg body part having a [Moving](/wiki/Moving "Moving") importance of 50%, it results in restoring 30% [moving](/wiki/Moving "Moving") per missing leg replaced, or a loss of 20% when replacing a healthy leg. 

Moving, in turn, affects the following [stats](/wiki/Stats "Stats"): [Hunting Stealth](/wiki/Hunting_Stealth "Hunting Stealth"), [Melee Dodge Chance](/wiki/Melee_Dodge_Chance "Melee Dodge Chance"), [Move Speed](/wiki/Move_Speed "Move Speed"). This results in a directly proportional change to a pawn's [move speed](/wiki/Move_speed "Move speed"), and a change in [melee dodge chance](/wiki/Melee_dodge_chance "Melee dodge chance") equivalent to losing 3.6 levels of [Melee](/wiki/Melee "Melee") skill per leg replaced. Due to how the stat is fit to a post-process curve, this can result in the melee dodge chance dropping to 0% for pawns with skill under 9, when replacing one leg, or under 12 for both legs. 

Installing a leg replacement can affect a pawn's character quality by affecting their Moving capacity. A replacement part with lower than 100% part efficiency will reduce the pawn's value if replacing a healthy part, but installing even a simple peg leg when the leg was entirely missing will increase their value. 

As an artificial part, peg legs will give a mood buff to anyone with the [Body modder](/wiki/Body_modder "Body modder") trait, and a mood penalty to anyone with the [Body purist](/wiki/Body_purist "Body purist") trait. 

### Installation[[edit](/index.php?title=Peg_leg&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Installation")]

Installing the part requires 1,500 [ticks](/wiki/Time "Time") (25 secs) of work, [![Wood](/images/thumb/3/33/Woodlog_c.png/16px-Woodlog_c.png)](/wiki/Wood "Wood") 1 [wood](/wiki/Wood "Wood"), 2x medicine of [herbal](/wiki/Herbal_medicine "Herbal medicine") quality or better, and a [Medical skill](/wiki/Skills#Medical "Skills") of 3. 

Removing the part requires 2,000 [ticks](/wiki/Time "Time") (33.33 secs) of work, 1x medicine of [herbal](/wiki/Herbal_medicine "Herbal medicine") quality or better, and has no [Medical skill](/wiki/Skills#Medical "Skills") requirements. 

If the operation fails, the wood will be destroyed. 

## Analysis[[edit](/index.php?title=Peg_leg&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Analysis")]

The peg leg is certainly better than a missing or otherwise inoperative leg, but you should strongly consider replacing it with a [modern prosthesis](/wiki/Prosthetic_leg "Prosthetic leg") as soon as possible. Movement is important for combat, many types of work, and general efficiency (getting up from bed to work...), making it a very valuable stat. For pawns that don't move as much, like researchers, this upgrade is less important. 

In cases where only the foot is missing, a [wooden foot](/wiki/Wooden_foot "Wooden foot") only has half the [Moving](/wiki/Moving "Moving") penalty, which means it restores more movement, and it has the exact same material and research requirements. 

### Prisoners[[edit](/index.php?title=Peg_leg&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Prisoners")]

Alternatively, peg legs can be installed on your [prisoners](/wiki/Prisoner "Prisoner"), making them easier to stop when starting a prison escape or going [berserk](/wiki/Berserk "Berserk"). As peg legs actively make the pawn worse, it isn't recommended to install peg legs on those who you would like to recruit, at least until you can make [bionic leg](/wiki/Bionic_leg "Bionic leg") replacements. 

Unlike healthy organic legs, peg legs can be removed; removing them will result in no leg, and removing both will permanently incapacitate a pawn. This is great if you want to keep prisoners long term without them attempting to a break out, such as for selling them, harvesting [hemogen packs](/wiki/Hemogen_pack "Hemogen pack"),[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC") or [extract genes](/wiki/Gene_extractor "Gene extractor")[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC") from. However, if the [Dead calm](/wiki/Dead_calm "Dead calm") gene[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC") can be applied to them instead, it is superior as they won't require a colonist or [slave](/wiki/Slave "Slave")[![Content added by the Ideology DLC](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/16px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Content added by the Ideology DLC") to feed them and legs won't have to be reinstalled if they are to be sold.
