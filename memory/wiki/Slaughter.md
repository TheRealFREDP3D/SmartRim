**Orders** are used to designate actions upon objects, which are carried out by [colonists](/wiki/Colonist "Colonist"). 

## Manual ordering[[edit](/index.php?title=Orders&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Manual ordering")]

While orders will be executed per the colonist's usual [Work](/wiki/Work "Work") priorities and [Schedule](/wiki/Schedule "Schedule"), a manual order will be followed immediately, usually cancelling whatever current task they are occupied with. 

To give a colonist a manual order, select the colonist (via left-click or from one of several in-game menus), and right-click the desired target item. A menu will pop up listing all possible orders in that context. Note that a pawn must be permitted to perform that task in the [Work](/wiki/Work#Manual_priorities "Work") tab, and must be [capable](/wiki/Work#Incapable_of_work_types "Work") of that particular type of work. 

If a desired or expected order is not listed, then there is something preventing the pawn from performing that task at that time. 

For multiple, successive orders, hold [`⇧ Shift`](/wiki/Controls "Controls") while right-clicking and selecting an order.[![Content exclusively from the PC Edition](/images/thumb/8/89/PC.png/16px-PC.png)](/wiki/PC_edition "Content exclusively from the PC Edition")[Console? Steam Deck?]

Queued orders will be carried out in the sequence they were input. This is handy to add tasks _after_ their _current_ activity, including sleeping, eating or recreating; the new task will be begun once that (one) current activity is fulfilled. 

Note that Colonists will **not** follow their normal [Schedule](/wiki/Schedule "Schedule") while following manual orders. Eating, Recreation, even Sleeping will be ignored until all listed orders are completed, or they fall asleep from exhaustion, or have a [mental break](/wiki/Mental_break "Mental break") from a low [mood](/wiki/Mood "Mood") due to being deprived of their basic needs. Keep this in mind when giving orders, especially when issuing long lists and/or when near a pawn's usual rest/relaxation time. 

## Deconstruct[[edit](/index.php?title=Orders&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Deconstruct")]

Main article: [Deconstruct](/wiki/Deconstruct "Deconstruct")

[![Deconstruct.png](/images/e/e1/Deconstruct.png)](/wiki/File:Deconstruct.png)

**Deconstruct** orders your colonists to take apart the highlighted object. Only objects with the [building](/wiki/Building "Building") tag can be deconstructed; this includes [structures](/wiki/Structure "Structure"), [floors](/wiki/Floor "Floor"), [furniture](/wiki/Furniture "Furniture"), [temperature](/wiki/Temperature "Temperature") items, decorative items, and minified buildings. You cannot deconstruct most other items such as [apparel](/wiki/Apparel "Apparel") or [weapons](/wiki/Weapons "Weapons"), but you can destroy them at a [smelter](/wiki/Electric_smelter "Electric smelter"). 

The [construction](/wiki/Construction "Construction") skill has a significant effect on the speed of deconstructing an object. How long it takes to deconstruct an object is determined by its [work to build](/wiki/Work_To_Build "Work To Build"), while the speed at which it is deconstructed is determined by [construction speed](/wiki/Construction_speed "Construction speed") of the colonist. Deconstruction will provide a small amount of [experience](/wiki/Experience "Experience") to the Construction skill. 

In most cases, deconstructing a building will give a refund of 50% of the build cost of the item; for odd-numbered values, there is a 50/50 chance to round up or down. (e.g., a [wall](/wiki/Wall "Wall") section, which costs 5 [wood](/wiki/Wood "Wood") or stone [blocks](/wiki/Block "Block") to build, will return either 2 or 3 of that building material.) All resulting material is left on the square where the object had been. 

Some buildings refund 100% of their build cost. The buildings are: [Shard beacon](/wiki/Shard_beacon "Shard beacon")[![Content added by the Anomaly DLC](/images/thumb/3/32/AnomalyIcon.png/16px-AnomalyIcon.png)](/wiki/Anomaly_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Anomaly DLC"), [Shard inhibitor](/wiki/Shard_inhibitor "Shard inhibitor")[![Content added by the Anomaly DLC](/images/thumb/3/32/AnomalyIcon.png/16px-AnomalyIcon.png)](/wiki/Anomaly_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Anomaly DLC"), [Wastepack atomizer](/wiki/Wastepack_atomizer "Wastepack atomizer")[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC"), [Grav field extender](/wiki/Grav_field_extender "Grav field extender")[![Content added by the Odyssey DLC](/images/thumb/7/7b/OdysseyIcon.png/16px-OdysseyIcon.png)](/wiki/Odyssey_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Odyssey DLC"), [Gravcore power cell](/wiki/Gravcore_power_cell "Gravcore power cell")[![Content added by the Odyssey DLC](/images/thumb/7/7b/OdysseyIcon.png/16px-OdysseyIcon.png)](/wiki/Odyssey_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Odyssey DLC"), [Gravship substructure](/wiki/Gravship_substructure "Gravship substructure")[![Content added by the Odyssey DLC](/images/thumb/7/7b/OdysseyIcon.png/16px-OdysseyIcon.png)](/wiki/Odyssey_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Odyssey DLC"), [Pilot console](/wiki/Pilot_console "Pilot console")[![Content added by the Odyssey DLC](/images/thumb/7/7b/OdysseyIcon.png/16px-OdysseyIcon.png)](/wiki/Odyssey_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Odyssey DLC"), [Signal jammer](/wiki/Signal_jammer "Signal jammer")[![Content added by the Odyssey DLC](/images/thumb/7/7b/OdysseyIcon.png/16px-OdysseyIcon.png)](/wiki/Odyssey_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Odyssey DLC"). There are also buildings that cannot be built that refund 100%, which are: [mech high-shield](/wiki/Mech_high-shield "Mech high-shield"),[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC") [mech low-shield](/wiki/Mech_low-shield "Mech low-shield"),[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC") [mech node](/wiki/Mech_node "Mech node"),[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC") and [mechanoid ship chunk](/wiki/Mechanoid_ship_chunk "Mechanoid ship chunk").[![Content added by the Odyssey DLC](/images/thumb/7/7b/OdysseyIcon.png/16px-OdysseyIcon.png)](/wiki/Odyssey_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Odyssey DLC")

Buildings that refund nothing are: [Campfire](/wiki/Campfire "Campfire"), [Flagstone](/wiki/Flagstone "Flagstone"), [Hidden conduit](/wiki/Hidden_conduit "Hidden conduit"), [Power conduit](/wiki/Power_conduit "Power conduit"), [Torch lamp](/wiki/Torch_lamp "Torch lamp"), [Wall torch lamp](/wiki/Wall_torch_lamp "Wall torch lamp"), [Waterproof conduit](/wiki/Waterproof_conduit "Waterproof conduit"), [Blood torch](/wiki/Blood_torch "Blood torch")[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC"), [Darktorch](/wiki/Darktorch "Darktorch")[![Content added by the Ideology DLC](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/16px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Content added by the Ideology DLC"), [Fungus darktorch](/wiki/Fungus_darktorch "Fungus darktorch")[![Content added by the Ideology DLC](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/16px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Content added by the Ideology DLC"). This is also true for terrain like [broken asphalt](/wiki/Broken_asphalt "Broken asphalt"), [packed dirt](/wiki/Packed_dirt "Packed dirt") and [insect sludge](/wiki/Environment "Environment") [![Content added by the Odyssey DLC](/images/thumb/7/7b/OdysseyIcon.png/16px-OdysseyIcon.png)](/wiki/Odyssey_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Odyssey DLC"). 

Additionally, a [biosculpter pod](/wiki/Biosculpter_pod "Biosculpter pod")[![Content added by the Ideology DLC](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/16px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Content added by the Ideology DLC") yields 0.25 of the build cost when deconstructed. 

Deconstruction can be useful when resources are desperately needed or when structures are no longer required, or when it's simply time to remodel. Existing ruins found on maps can be deconstructed for free building materials. 

Constructing an item that can be minified (i.e., uninstalled, moved and then placed again), loading it onto a [caravan](/wiki/Caravan "Caravan"), and then later deconstructing it again can sometimes be a good way to reduce the "shipping weight" of the final total resources. Compare the weight of the item to the final weight of the items returned. ([Urns](/wiki/Urn "Urn") are a good example of this.) 

While it is under the [construction work type](/wiki/Work#Construct "Work"), deconstruction is given a lower priority within the [work](/wiki/Work "Work") task, and therefore usually takes place after all Construction has been completed. Give a pawn specific orders (by right-clicking the target item) for urgent deconstructions. 

## Mine[[edit](/index.php?title=Orders&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Mine")]

Main article: [Mine](/wiki/Mine "Mine")

[![Mine.png](/images/a/a0/Mine.png)](/wiki/File:Mine.png)

The **mine** order designates areas of [stone](/wiki/Stone "Stone"), [ore](/wiki/Ore "Ore"), or [compacted machinery](/wiki/Compacted_machinery "Compacted machinery") to be mined out. [Colonists](/wiki/Colonists "Colonists") or [slaves](/wiki/Slave "Slave")[![Content added by the Ideology DLC](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/16px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Content added by the Ideology DLC") assigned to [Mining](/wiki/Work#Mine "Work") will mine then mine the designated area, or can manually instructed to mine specific tiles. Mining these resources will yield an amount of resources or [stone chunk](/wiki/Stone_chunk "Stone chunk") at a rate dependent on what is mined and the [Mining Yield](/wiki/Mining_Yield "Mining Yield") stat of the colonist. The [Mining Speed](/wiki/Mining_Speed "Mining Speed") of the colonist and HP of what is being mined determined the speed at which the tile is cleared. 

Note that only those things listed above, or areas that are covered by fog-of-war can "hold" a mining designation. In the event that an open area is uncovered when mining or an [ancient shrine](/wiki/Ancient_shrine "Ancient shrine") in encountered, the designation placed on the affected area will be lost. If planning a base via the Mine order, overlaying with [plans](/wiki/Plan "Plan") may prevent the design being lost if an open area is discovered. 

Colonists can only mine tiles directly adjacent to their current tile and in four cardinal directions. They cannot mine diagonally. 

## Smooth[[edit](/index.php?title=Orders&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Smooth")]

[![SmoothSurface.png](/images/d/d2/SmoothSurface.png)](/wiki/File:SmoothSurface.png)

The **Smooth** order designates natural stone surfaces to be smoothed out, be they [natural stone walls](/wiki/Structure#Natural_walls "Structure"), [rough stone](/wiki/Rough_stone "Rough stone"), or [rough-hewn stone](/wiki/Rough-hewn_stone "Rough-hewn stone"). Smoothing is done by pawns assigned to [Construction](/wiki/Construction "Construction"). 

Unsmoothed natural stone walls and floors have a negative beauty value, which can bring the quality of a room down. In contrast, smoothed stone walls and floors have a positive beauty value. 

## Haul things[[edit](/index.php?title=Orders&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Haul things")]

Main article: [Haul things](/wiki/Haul_things "Haul things")

[![Haul.png](/images/7/76/Haul.png)](/wiki/File:Haul.png)

The **"haul things"** order marks [chunks](/wiki/Chunk "Chunk") and other items to be [hauled](/wiki/Hauling "Hauling") to [stockpile zone](/wiki/Stockpile_zone "Stockpile zone"). Most other items are automatically "haulable" so long as they are not forbidden. For any item, you may have to allow the item or set a valid [stockpile zone](/wiki/Stockpile_zone "Stockpile zone") for it to be hauled. 

This order is carried out by colonists assigned to [haul](/wiki/Work#Haul "Work") in the work menu.

## Cut plants[[edit](/index.php?title=Orders&action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: Cut plants")]

Main article: [Cut plants](/wiki/Cut_plants "Cut plants")

[![CutPlants.png](/images/3/38/CutPlants.png)](/wiki/File:CutPlants.png)

Orders your colonists to take cut and remove [plants](/wiki/Plants "Plants"). This will harvest any resources possible from the plant before destroying it. It is possible to click and drag this tool to order your colonists to remove plants from a large area. This tool can also be used to remove trees that are not fully grown and therefore are not able to be selected with the "chop wood" order. Trees that are not fully mature and are cut down using this tool will not yield the full amount of logs. 

Only colonists assigned to [plant cutting](/wiki/Work#Plants "Work") will cut plants. The speed of cutting plants depends on the colonist's [plants skill](/wiki/Plants_\(skill\) "Plants \(skill\)"). 

## Harvest[[edit](/index.php?title=Orders&action=edit&section=7 "Edit section: Harvest")]

Main article: [Harvest](/wiki/Harvest "Harvest")

[![HarvestPlants.png](/images/3/38/HarvestPlants.png)](/wiki/File:HarvestPlants.png)

**Harvest** marks [plants](/wiki/Plants "Plants") to be harvested by colonists assigned to [Plant cut](/wiki/Work#Plant_cut "Work"). Characters assigned to [Grow](/wiki/Work#Grow "Work") also harvest but with the intention of clearing the area to plant new crops. Harvesting of plants provides edibles such as [agave](/wiki/Agave "Agave"), [berries](/wiki/Berry "Berry"), [rice](/wiki/Rice "Rice"), [potatoes](/wiki/Potatoes "Potatoes"), [corn](/wiki/Corn "Corn"), [haygrass](/wiki/Haygrass "Haygrass"), and processable herbs like [smokeleaf](/wiki/Smokeleaf "Smokeleaf"), [psychoid leaves](/wiki/Psychoid_leaves "Psychoid leaves"), [hops](/wiki/Hops "Hops"), [healroot](/wiki/Healroot "Healroot") and [devilstrand](/wiki/Devilstrand "Devilstrand"). Plants that haven't borne produce will be destroyed in the process. 

This should not be confused with the term "[Harvest](/wiki/Human_resources "Human resources")" that is also a term used in medical operations, meaning to remove a body organ from a donor, usually with intent to transplant the organ to another patient or sell for profit. Harvestable organs in-game include: [heart](/wiki/Heart "Heart"), [lungs](/wiki/Lung "Lung"), [kidney](/wiki/Kidney "Kidney") and [livers](/wiki/Liver "Liver"). 

Unskilled or injured characters can fail at harvesting, destroying the harvest. 

## Chop wood[[edit](/index.php?title=Orders&action=edit&section=8 "Edit section: Chop wood")]

Main article: [Chop wood](/wiki/Chop_wood "Chop wood")

[![ChopWood.png](/images/c/ca/ChopWood.png)](/wiki/File:ChopWood.png)

**Chop wood** is used to mark [trees](/wiki/Trees "Trees") or [Saguaro cacti](/wiki/Saguaro_cactus "Saguaro cactus") to be cut down, dropping [wood](/wiki/Wood "Wood") in their place. It will only mark trees that have achieved at least 40% growth.  


There are two variations: 

  * Click Chop Wood then click one or more trees individually.
  * Click Chop Wood then click and drag to select multiple trees.



Trees marked in this way are marked with yellow shears. 

Trees, even if they have not achieved 40% growth, can be harvested by using Cut Plant instead of Chop Wood. Trees marked in this way are marked with a yellow machete. 

Trees bear an amount of wood based on their growth. 

Only colonists assigned the [plant cutting](/wiki/Work#Work "Work") job will chop down trees. A colonist may be directly ordered to chop down a marked tree. Select a colonist assigned to plant cutting, right-click a marked tree, click Prioritize cutting. The time it takes a colonist to chop down a tree it determined by their [Plant Work Speed](/wiki/Plant_Work_Speed "Plant Work Speed"). 

## Hunt[[edit](/index.php?title=Orders&action=edit&section=9 "Edit section: Hunt")]

Main article: [Hunt](/wiki/Hunt "Hunt")

[![Hunt.png](/images/e/e1/Hunt.png)](/wiki/File:Hunt.png)

**Hunt** is an [order](/wiki/Order "Order") used to mark wild [animals](/wiki/Animals "Animals") to be automatically hunted and killed by [hunters](/wiki/Work#Hunt "Work"). Once a hunter's target is [downed](/wiki/Downed "Downed"), they will finish it off at point-blank range by cutting its throat. Hunters will only hunt animals inside their allowed areas. If a properly configured [stockpile zone](/wiki/Stockpile_zone "Stockpile zone") is present and has room, the hunter will then haul the [corpse](/wiki/Corpse "Corpse") back before resuming hunting. These corpses can then be butchered by [cooks](/wiki/Work#Cook "Work") to yield [meat](/wiki/Meat "Meat") and [leather](/wiki/Leather "Leather"), with type and quantity of both depending on the animal hunted. 

Animals can be marked for hunting in the [Wildlife](/wiki/Work#Wildlife "Work") menu. Multiple animals can be selected clicking and dragging the mouse down the "Mark animals for Hunting" column. This tab also shows the revenge chance for each animal and has a special icon for [predators](/wiki/Animals#Predation "Animals"). All wild animals on the current map are shown there. If an animal is injured or [downed](/wiki/Downed "Downed"), it will be highlighted as such. Such animals may be 'finished off' for easy meat. 

Hunters only hunt when equipped with a [ranged weapon](/wiki/Weapons#Ranged_Weapons "Weapons"), when their Hunting task is active in the [Work](/wiki/Work "Work") menu, and when all other jobs of a higher priority are finished. 

Some animals have a chance to "revenge" when harmed, that is turn [manhunter](/wiki/Manhunter "Manhunter") and attack any nearby [humans](/wiki/Human "Human"). Each time an animal is injured, a check whether the animal will turn manhunter is performed. When an animal turns manhunter there is a chance that any animal of the same species within a radius of 25 tiles with valid path-finding to the attacker will also turn manhunter. When marking an animal for hunting with a chance to turn manhunter, a warning will be displayed. 

Hunters are coded to shoot marked animals from a maximum distance to minimize the risk of injury should the target turn [manhunter](/wiki/Manhunter "Manhunter"). However, animals will flee from nearby gunshots and may inadvertently move closer to the hunter. Weapons with a long range and high damage per shot are the safest for hunting weapons, such as the [greatbow](/wiki/Greatbow "Greatbow") and [bolt-action rifle](/wiki/Bolt-action_rifle "Bolt-action rifle"), while short-range weapons, low damage weapons and weapons that predominantly get their DPS from low-damage but high rates of fire, such as the [machine pistol](/wiki/Machine_pistol "Machine pistol") are poor choices. [Fact check] The [hunting stealth](/wiki/Hunting_stealth "Hunting stealth") of the hunter directly scales the chance of manhunting. 

## Slaughter[[edit](/index.php?title=Orders&action=edit&section=10 "Edit section: Slaughter")]

Main article: [Animal husbandry#Slaughtering](/wiki/Animal_husbandry#Slaughtering "Animal husbandry")

[![Slaughter.png](/images/4/4a/Slaughter.png)](/wiki/File:Slaughter.png)

Marks a tamed animal to be slaughtered; done by [handlers](/wiki/Work#Handle "Work") in the field where the animal stands. Properly slaughtered animal corpses will give 50% more meat and leather compared to hunting or killing them with direct attacks.

[![Tame.png](/images/9/92/Tame.png)](/wiki/File:Tame.png)

Marks a wild animal for an animal handler to try [taming](/wiki/Animal_husbandry#Taming "Animal husbandry") it. 

## Uninstall[[edit](/index.php?title=Orders&action=edit&section=12 "Edit section: Uninstall")]

[![Uninstall.png](/images/e/ef/Uninstall.png)](/wiki/File:Uninstall.png)

Marks a movable object to uninstalled and boxed, usually so that it can be reinstalled in a new location. Boxed objects can be sold to traders. 

## Claim[[edit](/index.php?title=Orders&action=edit&section=13 "Edit section: Claim")]

Main article: [Claim](/wiki/Claim "Claim")

[![Claim.png](/images/e/ef/Claim.png)](/wiki/File:Claim.png)

Used to claim neutral [structures](/wiki/Structure "Structure") for the [colony](/wiki/Colony "Colony"), including [ancient shrines](/wiki/Ancient_shrine "Ancient shrine"), existing [ruins](/wiki/Ruins "Ruins"), abandoned [siege](/wiki/Raider "Raider") camps or destroyed [faction bases](/wiki/Faction_base "Faction base"). Claiming structures other than ones built by the player will automatically add them to your [home area](/wiki/Home_area "Home area"). Colonists will repair, maintain and extinguish fires in a claimed area or structure. This can be removed from the [architect](/wiki/Architect "Architect") menu.

## Strip[[edit](/index.php?title=Orders&action=edit&section=14 "Edit section: Strip")]

[![Strip.png](/images/9/91/Strip.png)](/wiki/File:Strip.png)

Strip the [apparel](/wiki/Apparel "Apparel"), including [utility](/wiki/Utility "Utility") items, from [corpses](/wiki/Corpse "Corpse") and [downed](/wiki/Downed "Downed") pawns. Click Strip from the Orders menu and click any number of pawns to designate stripping. Strip orders are executed by [haulers](/wiki/Work#Haul "Work"). A pawn must have a [manipulation](/wiki/Manipulation "Manipulation") greater than 0% to strip someone. 

Stripping can be done by reverse designation. Select one or more pawns and click the strip icon to mark them for stripping. 

Stripping can also be done by prioritizing a colonist. Select a colonist, right-click the target pawn, and click Strip.[Haul Work Enabled?]

Note that apparel stripped from corpses will be [tainted](/wiki/Tainted "Tainted"). See that page for details. 

## Open[[edit](/index.php?title=Orders&action=edit&section=15 "Edit section: Open")]

[![Open.png](/images/7/7f/Open.png)](/wiki/File:Open.png)

Marks a non-empty container to be opened by a [hauler](/wiki/Work#Haul "Work"). Works on [graves](/wiki/Grave "Grave"), [sarcophagi](/wiki/Sarcophagus "Sarcophagus"), and [cryptosleep caskets](/wiki/Cryptosleep_casket "Cryptosleep casket"). 

## Plan[[edit](/index.php?title=Orders&action=edit&section=16 "Edit section: Plan")]

Main article: [Plan](/wiki/Plan "Plan")

[![PlanOn.png](/images/3/33/PlanOn.png)](/wiki/File:PlanOn.png)

Place planning designations. These don't do anything except help you plan future expansions. 

Plans can only be placed inside the buildable area of the map, shown by a white border. An error (_Too close to map edge._) will appear while attempting to place plans outside. 

Plans can't be selected like [walls](/wiki/Wall "Wall") and [doors](/wiki/Door "Door"). 

A planning designation will automatically be removed once something (like a wall) is built on top of it. 

## Remove plans

Main article: [Plan](/wiki/Plan "Plan")

[![PlanOff.png](/images/7/74/PlanOff.png)](/wiki/File:PlanOff.png)

Does the opposite of the plan **order** , in that it removes plans you've laid out.
