A **bionic arm** is an [artificial body part](/wiki/Artificial_body_part "Artificial body part") that acts as a replacement for a pawn's natural arm, increasing [Manipulation](/wiki/Manipulation "Manipulation") while also acting as a modest [body part weapon](/wiki/Body_part_weapon "Body part weapon").

## Acquisition[[edit](/index.php?title=Bionic_arm&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Acquisition")]

Bionic arms can be crafted at a [fabrication bench](/wiki/Fabrication_bench "Fabrication bench") once the [Bionic replacements](/wiki/Research#Bionic_replacements "Research") research project has been completed. Each requires [![Plasteel](/images/thumb/8/8c/Plasteel_c.png/16px-Plasteel_c.png)](/wiki/Plasteel "Plasteel") 15 [Plasteel](/wiki/Plasteel "Plasteel"), [![Advanced component](/images/thumb/d/d6/Advanced_component.png/16px-Advanced_component.png)](/wiki/Advanced_component "Advanced component") 4 [Advanced components](/wiki/Advanced_component "Advanced component"), 26,000 [ticks](/wiki/Time "Time") (7.22 mins) of work modified by the [general labor speed](/wiki/General_Labor_Speed "General Labor Speed") of the crafter, and a [crafting](/wiki/Crafting "Crafting") skill of 8.   
Bionic arms can be bought from the following traders: [Orbital exotic goods trader](/wiki/Trader/Orbital_Exotic_Goods_Trader "Trader/Orbital Exotic Goods Trader"), [Outlander base trader](/wiki/Trader/Base_Outlander_Standard "Trader/Base Outlander Standard"), [Outlander exotic goods trader](/wiki/Trader/Caravan_Outlander_Exotic "Trader/Caravan Outlander Exotic"), [Slave ship](/wiki/Trader/Orbital_Pirate_Merchant "Trader/Orbital Pirate Merchant"). 

They can also be found in [ancient shrines](/wiki/Ancient_shrine "Ancient shrine"), offered as a [quest](/wiki/Quest "Quest") reward, or [bought](/wiki/Trade "Trade") from exotic traders, [Empire](/wiki/Empire "Empire") traders, and at [Outlander](/wiki/Outlander "Outlander") [faction bases](/wiki/Faction_base "Faction base"). They may also be found occasionally inside of [flesh sacks](/wiki/Flesh_sack "Flesh sack") ,[![Content added by the Anomaly DLC](/images/thumb/3/32/AnomalyIcon.png/16px-AnomalyIcon.png)](/wiki/Anomaly_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Anomaly DLC") with a ~ 1.95% chance to drop from them. 

## Summary[[edit](/index.php?title=Bionic_arm&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Summary")]

### Body part[[edit](/index.php?title=Bionic_arm&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Body part")]

Bionic arms replace the user's organic arm up to, and including, the shoulder. They are universal and can be installed on either the left or right. The bionic arm has a part efficiency of 125%. This not only fully replaces the functionality of a normal arm - when coupled with the arm body part having a [Manipulation](/wiki/Manipulation "Manipulation") importance of 50%, it results in an increase in manipulation of +12.5% over natural for each bionic arm installed. Manipulation, in turn, affects a great many [stats](/wiki/Stats "Stats"). For a full list of affected stats, see [Manipulation](/wiki/Manipulation#Affected_stats "Manipulation"). 

### Combat[[edit](/index.php?title=Bionic_arm&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Combat")]

A bionic arm replaces the natural "Fist" attack of the replaced arm with an almost 50% more powerful, but otherwise equivalent, punch.

### Installation[[edit](/index.php?title=Bionic_arm&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Installation")]

Installing the part requires 2,500 [ticks](/wiki/Time "Time") (41.67 secs) of work, 2x medicine of [herbal](/wiki/Herbal_medicine "Herbal medicine") quality or better, and a [Medical skill](/wiki/Medical "Medical") of 5. 

Removing the part requires 2,000 [ticks](/wiki/Time "Time") (33.33 secs) of work, 1x medicine of [herbal](/wiki/Herbal_medicine "Herbal medicine") quality or better, and has no [Medical skill](/wiki/Medical "Medical") requirements. 

If the operation fails, the part will be destroyed. 

## Analysis[[edit](/index.php?title=Bionic_arm&action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: Analysis")]

First, any reasonably important pawn with a destroyed or [prosthetic arm](/wiki/Prosthetic_arm "Prosthetic arm") should get a bionic replacement. Then, the increase to manipulation is best used for the [Doctoring](/wiki/Doctoring "Doctoring"), [Crafting](/wiki/Crafting "Crafting"), or [Construction](/wiki/Skills "Skills"); doctors tend faster and with higher quality, while crafters and constructors make objects faster (quality is unaffected). Manipulation is one of the few ways to increase [general labor speed](/wiki/General_labor_speed "General labor speed"), so it is great for skill-less crafting tasks like [stonecutting](/wiki/Stonecutter%27s_table "Stonecutter's table") and [smelting](/wiki/Electric_smelter "Electric smelter"). 

Manipulation will also increase the [Artistic](/wiki/Skills "Skills"), [Mining](/wiki/Skills "Skills"), [Plants](/wiki/Skills "Skills"), and [Research](/wiki/Skills "Skills") (up to 110% Manipulation) skills, but these skills are generally less important to do _fast_ in a mid- to late- game colony that can make bionic arms. The [drill arm](/wiki/Drill_arm "Drill arm")[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC") and [field hand](/wiki/Field_hand "Field hand")[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC") provide a much greater boost to Mining or Plants, though lower [Moving](/wiki/Moving "Moving") in the process. For combat, bionic arms will increase [Melee Hit Chance](/wiki/Melee_Hit_Chance "Melee Hit Chance"), but they only increase [Shooting Accuracy](/wiki/Shooting_Accuracy "Shooting Accuracy") if Manipulation is below 100%. While manipulation often goes below 100% due to the effects of [pain](/wiki/Pain "Pain") and [injury](/wiki/Injury "Injury"), [bionic eyes](/wiki/Bionic_eye "Bionic eye") are superior if combat, ranged or melee, is your main priority. 

Unlike organic parts, bionic arms don't contain subparts - all damage is taken by the main, 30 HP arm, instead of spread throughout the bones, fingers, and shoulder. While a bionic arm won't lose stray fingers to a gun shot, this makes it easier for it to be completely destroyed. In addition, the artificial part never feels [pain](/wiki/Pain "Pain") and never bleeds.
