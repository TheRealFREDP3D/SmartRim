A **bed** is used by [colonists](/wiki/Colonists "Colonists") for sleeping to recover their [rest](/wiki/Rest "Rest"). 

There is a version for two colonists called the [double bed](/wiki/Double_bed "Double bed"), and a deluxe version known as the [royal bed](/wiki/Royal_bed "Royal bed").

## Acquisition[[edit](/index.php?title=Bed&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Acquisition")]

Beds can be constructed once the [Complex furniture](/wiki/Research#Complex_furniture "Research") research project has been completed. Each requires [![Stuff](/images/thumb/7/7e/Buildingmats.png/16px-Buildingmats.png)](/wiki/Stuff "Stuff") 45 [Stuff](/wiki/Stuff "Stuff") ([Metallic](/wiki/Metallic "Metallic")/[Woody](/wiki/Woody "Woody")/[Stony](/wiki/Stony "Stony"), 450 for [SVMs](/wiki/Small_volume_material "Small volume material")) and 800 [ticks](/wiki/Time "Time") (13.33 secs) of work modified by the [construction speed](/wiki/Construction_speed "Construction speed") of the builder and the [work to build factor](/wiki/Property:Work_To_Build_Factor "Property:Work To Build Factor") and [offset](/wiki/Property:Work_To_Build_Offset "Property:Work To Build Offset") of the material.   
Beds can be bought from the following traders: [Orbital bulk goods trader](/wiki/Trader/Orbital_Bulk_Goods "Trader/Orbital Bulk Goods"), [Outlander base trader](/wiki/Trader/Base_Outlander_Standard "Trader/Base Outlander Standard"), [Outlander bulk goods trader](/wiki/Trader/Caravan_Outlander_Bulk_Goods "Trader/Caravan Outlander Bulk Goods"). 

Beds may also be offered for sale by [bulk goods traders](/wiki/Bulk_goods_trader "Bulk goods trader") or found in [faction bases](/wiki/Faction_base "Faction base"). 

## Summary[[edit](/index.php?title=Bed&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Summary")]

For a description of mechanics shared by all sleep furniture, see [Sleep furniture mechanics](/wiki/Sleep_furniture#Mechanics "Sleep furniture").

Beds are a form of [sleep furniture](/wiki/Sleep_furniture "Sleep furniture"). Only one pawn can sleep on one at a time.

### Comfort[[edit](/index.php?title=Bed&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Comfort")]

[Comfort](/wiki/Comfort "Comfort") is a measure of how high a pawn can refill their comfort meter while using the piece of furniture. A pawn sleeping in a bed with a higher comfort value can achieve a higher level. It does not affect the rate at which comfort increases, only the maximum level. Comfort levels over 100% have no additional effect. Comfort varies by [quality](/wiki/Quality "Quality") but not material. Beds of the same quality level will all have the same comfort, regardless of what they're constructed from. An adjacent [end table](/wiki/End_table "End table") and a nearby [dresser](/wiki/Dresser "Dresser") will each increase the base comfort by a further +0.05. This increase is applied before the quality multiplier.

### Surgery Success Chance Factor[[edit](/index.php?title=Bed&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Surgery Success Chance Factor")]

The [Surgery Success Chance Factor](/wiki/Surgery_Success_Chance_Factor "Surgery Success Chance Factor") is exactly what it sounds like - a multiplier on the chance for a successful surgery assuming it is performed in this bed. The base value depends on the bed type with most beds and bedrolls having a base value of 100%, then modified by the bed's quality level. Construction material does not matter. 

* [Quality](/wiki/Quality "Quality") | Awful  | Poor  | Normal  | Good  | Excellent  | Masterwork  | Legendary   
---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---  
[Surgery Success Chance Factor](/wiki/Surgery_Success_Chance_Factor "Surgery Success Chance Factor") | 90%  | 95%  | 100%  | 105%  | 110%  | 115%  | 130%   
  
This table assumes the bed is inside a clean, properly lit room. For the full effects of light, cleanliness and placing the bed outside see [Surgery Success Chance Factor](/wiki/Surgery_Success_Chance_Factor "Surgery Success Chance Factor").

### Healing[[edit](/index.php?title=Bed&action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: Healing")]

A pawn resting in a bed of any quality will have their [Immunity Gain Speed](/wiki/Immunity_Gain_Speed "Immunity Gain Speed") multiplied by ×1.07 and heal an additional +8 HP from [injuries](/wiki/Injury "Injury") per day. 

## Analysis[[edit](/index.php?title=Bed&action=edit&section=7 "Edit section: Analysis")]

[![](/images/thumb/c/c5/Bed_endtables_2.png/200px-Bed_endtables_2.png)](/wiki/File:Bed_endtables_2.png)

[](/wiki/File:Bed_endtables_2.png "Enlarge")

Space efficient placement of beds, [end table](/wiki/End_table "End table"), and [dresser](/wiki/Dresser "Dresser").

Beds are a clear upgrade from [sleeping spots](/wiki/Sleeping_spot "Sleeping spot") and [bedrolls](/wiki/Bedroll "Bedroll"), allowing colonists to sleep less and recover faster whilst avoiding the penalty for sleeping on the ground. In most [biomes](/wiki/Biome "Biome"), [wood](/wiki/Wood "Wood") is a common resource, so beds are easy to create. This means that [Crashlanded](/wiki/Scenario_system#Crashlanded "Scenario system") pawns should build beds almost immediately, while [New Tribes](/wiki/New_Tribe "New Tribe") should build beds as soon as they research [Complex furniture](/wiki/Complex_furniture "Complex furniture"). 

### Comparison[[edit](/index.php?title=Bed&action=edit&section=8 "Edit section: Comparison")]

Compared to the other beds: 

  * A [sleeping spot](/wiki/Sleeping_spot "Sleeping spot") has worse rest quality than all but low quality [stone block](/wiki/Stone_block "Stone block") and [bioferrite](/wiki/Bioferrite "Bioferrite")[![Content added by the Anomaly DLC](/images/thumb/3/32/AnomalyIcon.png/16px-AnomalyIcon.png)](/wiki/Anomaly_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Anomaly DLC") beds, inferior comfort to all beds, and unlike all other sleeping options, inflicts a −4 _Slept on the ground_ [moodlet](/wiki/Mood "Mood")[Thought template] for sleeping on the ground. Just the extra hour of work a day from a Normal quality, non-stone bed makes the upgrade from spots well worth it.
  * A [bedroll](/wiki/Bedroll "Bedroll") is about equal to a standard bed that is one [quality](/wiki/Quality "Quality") level lower, e.g., a normal bed ~= a good bedroll. Therefore, standard beds should be built if available. Bedrolls do not require Complex Furniture researched, although they do require [textiles](/wiki/Textile "Textile") which are much less accessible in the early game in most biomes. Bedrolls have a unique benefit; they can used by [caravans](/wiki/Caravan "Caravan") to rest, so some bedrolls may be worth keeping even after Complex Furniture. In material scarce biomes like [sea ice](/wiki/Sea_ice "Sea ice"), textiles from animals and raiders may be more available than materials for beds.
  * [Double beds](/wiki/Double_bed "Double bed") cost 5 materials less than 2 beds and allow lovers to do [lovin'](/wiki/Lovin%27 "Lovin'"). However, pawns dislike sleeping with someone who is not a spouse or lover.
  * [Hospital beds](/wiki/Hospital_bed "Hospital bed") are much better for [injury](/wiki/Injury "Injury"), [disease](/wiki/Disease "Disease") recovery, or [surgery](/wiki/Surgery "Surgery"), though require a fair bit of [research](/wiki/Research "Research") first. Outside of medical use, hospital beds are more expensive for little/no gain.
  * [Royal beds](/wiki/Royal_bed "Royal bed") are an expensive upgrade, but offer a better Rest Effectiveness, as well as other stats. 
    * A bed of +1 [quality](/wiki/Quality "Quality") level is better in terms of Rest. While the royal bed would be better for Comfort, an excellent quality bed reaches the maximum possible comfort (with [end table](/wiki/End_table "End table") and [dresser](/wiki/Dresser "Dresser")).
    * Typically, it is more efficient to "roll" for the higher quality bed than it is to build a royal bed. See [royal bed](/wiki/Royal_bed#Comparison_to_ordinary_Beds "Royal bed") for details.
  * [Slab beds](/wiki/Slab_bed "Slab bed")[![Content added by the Ideology DLC](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/16px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Content added by the Ideology DLC") are cheaper and please Pain is Virtue pawns, but provide no [comfort](/wiki/Comfort "Comfort"). They are a clear choice for Pain is Virtue and poor otherwise.
