**Penoxycyline** is a [medical](/wiki/Medical_drugs "Medical drugs") [drug](/wiki/Drug "Drug") for preventing [disease](/wiki/Disease "Disease"). It protects against catching [malaria](/wiki/Malaria "Malaria"), [sleeping sickness](/wiki/Sleeping_sickness "Sleeping sickness"), and the [plague](/wiki/Plague "Plague").

## Acquisition[[edit](/index.php?title=Penoxycyline&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Acquisition")]

Penoxycylines can be crafted at a [drug lab](/wiki/Drug_lab "Drug lab") once the [Penoxycyline production](/wiki/Research#Penoxycyline_production "Research") research project has been completed. Each requires [![Neutroamine](/images/thumb/3/3c/Neutroamine_c.png/16px-Neutroamine_c.png)](/wiki/Neutroamine "Neutroamine") 2 [Neutroamine](/wiki/Neutroamine "Neutroamine") and 600 [ticks](/wiki/Time "Time") (10 secs) of work modified by the [drug synthesis speed](/wiki/Drug_Synthesis_Speed "Drug Synthesis Speed") of the crafter.   
Penoxycylines can be bought from the following traders: [Orbital bulk goods trader](/wiki/Trader/Orbital_Bulk_Goods "Trader/Orbital Bulk Goods"), [Orbital combat supplier](/wiki/Trader/Orbital_Combat_Supplier "Trader/Orbital Combat Supplier"), [Orbital exotic goods trader](/wiki/Trader/Orbital_Exotic_Goods_Trader "Trader/Orbital Exotic Goods Trader"), [Outlander base trader](/wiki/Trader/Base_Outlander_Standard "Trader/Base Outlander Standard"), [Outlander bulk goods trader](/wiki/Trader/Caravan_Outlander_Bulk_Goods "Trader/Caravan Outlander Bulk Goods"), [Outlander slaver](/wiki/Trader/Caravan_Outlander_Pirate_Merchant "Trader/Caravan Outlander Pirate Merchant"), [Outlander visitor](/wiki/Trader/Visitor_Outlander_Standard "Trader/Visitor Outlander Standard"), [Slave ship](/wiki/Trader/Orbital_Pirate_Merchant "Trader/Orbital Pirate Merchant"), [Empire base trader](/wiki/Trader/Base_Empire_Standard "Trader/Base Empire Standard")[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC"), [Imperial trader caravan](/wiki/Trader/Empire_Caravan_Trader_General "Trader/Empire Caravan Trader General")[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC"), [Orbital imperial trader](/wiki/Trader/Orbital_Empire "Trader/Orbital Empire")[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC"). Note that [neutroamine](/wiki/Neutroamine "Neutroamine") can only be obtained from traders or found in cargo pods. 

## Summary[[edit](/index.php?title=Penoxycyline&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Summary")]

Penoxycyline completely prevents users from catching any of the following [diseases](/wiki/Disease "Disease") while its "high" is active: 

  * [Malaria](/wiki/Malaria "Malaria")
  * [Plague](/wiki/Plague "Plague")
  * [Sleeping sickness](/wiki/Sleeping_sickness "Sleeping sickness")



Despite the wording of the description, it does nothing to prevent or treat [infections](/wiki/Infection "Infection"), or any other diseases or ailments besides the three above. 

When the [Disease event](/wiki/Events/Incidental#Disease "Events/Incidental") occurs, pawns that were protected from getting the disease by penoxycyline will be specifically noted as such. Penoxycyline does nothing to treat pre-existing diseases. 

Penoxycyline is not addictive and has no direct side effects. Penoxycyline _can_ contribute to [overdoses](/wiki/Overdose "Overdose"), increasing severity by 0.08-0.14 per dose consumed. This severity is not enough to cause any health effect on its own. However, it can cause minor overdoses when taken along with [hard drugs](/wiki/Hard_drugs "Hard drugs"). 

## Analysis[[edit](/index.php?title=Penoxycyline&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Analysis")]

Diseases usually aren't a fatal problem by the time you can get a good supply of [neutroamine](/wiki/Neutroamine "Neutroamine") and have the [Penoxycyline production](/wiki/Research#Penoxycyline_production "Research") research. For an otherwise healthy colonist: a decent doctor, in a clean hospital, using [herbal medicine](/wiki/Herbal_medicine "Herbal medicine") or [medicine](/wiki/Medicine "Medicine") is usually enough to treat the disease (so long as the colonist rests for the duration). However, this drug can still be helpful. 

Penoxycyline can reduce the time spend resting in bed, especially in tropical biomes where disease is prevalent and sleeping sickness is possible. If you don't want to use penoxycyline on every colonist, use it on [sickly](/wiki/Sickly "Sickly") pawns first, then doctors. This prevents your doctors from getting sick when a disease outbreak occurs, so they are able to treat the rest of the colony. [Super immune](/wiki/Super_immune "Super immune") pawns should be last priority due to their faster recovery rate. It can also be used by pawns that are about to go caravaning since they are somewhat more vulnerable to diseases. If you can afford the neutroamine cost (and won't be using it for [medicine](/wiki/Medicine "Medicine"), [wake-up](/wiki/Wake-up "Wake-up"), or [go-juice](/wiki/Go-juice "Go-juice")), then this drug is a net positive. 

While the in-game description says to _"Take every five days"_ , the effect actually lasts just over half a day longer. This gives pawns some leeway, in case colonists don't consume the pills promptly. Set a [drug policy](/wiki/Drug_policy "Drug policy") to tell the pawns to take one every 5 days and keep one in the inventory to take as soon as possible, or be cheap, set it to "6 days" and live dangerously.

## Version history[[edit](/index.php?title=Penoxycyline&action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: Version history")]

  * [0.15.1279](/wiki/Version/0.15.1279 "Version/0.15.1279") \- Added as **Malari-block** , and only prevents [Malaria](/wiki/Malaria "Malaria")
  * [0.16.1393](/wiki/Version/0.16.1393 "Version/0.16.1393") \- Reworked. Renamed from **Malari-block** to **Penoxycyline** and now prevents a wide variety of infections. Cost decreased from 5 [neutroamine](/wiki/Neutroamine "Neutroamine") to 3.
  * [Alpha 17](/wiki/Alpha_17 "Alpha 17") \- Cost is further reduced from 3 to 2 neutroamine. It also received an overhaul, such that instead of instantly boosting disease immunity to 61%, it now provides true immunity and cannot reactively increase immunity against a disease that is already in progress.
