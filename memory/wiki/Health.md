[Characters](/wiki/Characters "Characters"), like [colonists](/wiki/Colonist "Colonist"), [raiders](/wiki/Raider "Raider"), etc., can become [injured](/wiki/Injury "Injury") (via the environment or fighting). 

Each injury affects a different part of the colonists' well being, and can be found under a character's **Health** tab. 

Under the health tab there are two sub-tabs, one for a health "Overview" which includes medical treatment preference and food restriction (1.0 onwards), and a second tab for scheduling any "Operations" that a colonist requires.

#### Sight[[edit](/index.php?title=Health&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Sight")]

Main article: [Sight](/wiki/Sight "Sight")

Sight is a capacity that contributes to stats including [shooting accuracy](/wiki/Shooting_Accuracy "Shooting Accuracy"), [melee hit chance](/wiki/Melee_Hit_Chance "Melee Hit Chance"), and for colonists, nearly every work stat. Humans and animals have eyes, while [scythers](/wiki/Scyther "Scyther") and [centipedes](/wiki/Centipede "Centipede") have functionally equivalent sight sensors. Losing an eye or sensor reduces sight by 50%. Humans aged 50 or older may develop cataracts which drastically reduces sight by 50%. An eye can be upgraded with a [bionic eye](/wiki/Bionic_eye "Bionic eye"). 

#### Hearing[[edit](/index.php?title=Health&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Hearing")]

Main article: [Hearing](/wiki/Hearing "Hearing")

Hearing is a capacity that contributes to [trade price improvement](/wiki/Trade_Price_Improvement "Trade Price Improvement"). Hearing is reduced by damaged, missing ears, or hearing loss. Hearing can be improved past 100% with a [bionic ear](/wiki/Bionic_ear "Bionic ear"), however no derived stat benefits from doing so. 

#### Moving[[edit](/index.php?title=Health&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Moving")]

Main article: [Moving](/wiki/Moving "Moving")

Moving is a pawn's capacity to use their legs to move about. It's a major factor in a pawn's overall [movement speed](/wiki/Move_Speed "Move Speed"). Moving is reduced when a leg, femur, tibia, foot, toe or their spine is injured. 

Consciousness applies a 1 for 1 multiplier on moving, while breathing and blood pumping does the same but only up to -10% moving, so anything that affects any of these stats will also increase or reduce moving by the same amount. Moving is also directly reduced by certain ailments including [bad back](/wiki/Bad_back "Bad back") and [frail](/wiki/Frail "Frail"). 

Any time a pawn's moving is reduced to below 15%, they become incapacitated and can no longer do anything. They can be rescued or captured at this point. An injured pawn may eventually recover on their own as the pain levels drop, although blood loss may prevent this or cause them to collapse again. 

#### Manipulation[[edit](/index.php?title=Health&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Manipulation")]

Main article: [Manipulation](/wiki/Manipulation "Manipulation")

Manipulation is a pawn's capacity to perform any task with their [hands](/wiki/Body_Parts#Hand "Body Parts"). It is an important factor in almost every task a colonist can perform. 

Anatomically, bipeds use their left and right arms for manipulation, and each side contributes its efficiency level to 50% of the manipulation stat. Total loss of a shoulder, arm, radius, humerus or hand will render that side totally useless and reduce the pawn's manipulation by 50%. Loss of a finger will result in about an 8% manipulation penalty per finger. For quadrupeds, manipulation is reduced by injury to their [jaw](/wiki/Body_Parts#Jaw "Body Parts") instead. For both, injury to the spine will also reduce manipulation. 

Consciousness applies a 1 for 1 multiplier on manipulation, so anything that affects consciousness (pain, brain damage, diseases, drugs, joywires, etc.) will also increase or reduce manipulation by the same amount. Manipulation is also reduced by ailments including [bad back and frailty](/wiki/Injury#old_age "Injury"). Manipulation is modified by prosthetics including power claws, prosthetic arms, and bionic arms. 

#### Talking[[edit](/index.php?title=Health&action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: Talking")]

Main article: [Talking](/wiki/Talking "Talking")

A character's capacity of speech. Directly affected by consciousness. 

#### Eating[[edit](/index.php?title=Health&action=edit&section=7 "Edit section: Eating")]

Main article: [Eating](/wiki/Eating "Eating")

Eating is the capacity of biological pawns to consume food and is a major factor of [Eating Speed](/wiki/Eating_Speed "Eating Speed"). It is reduced by a damaged jaw. Even when missing a jaw a pawn can still eat, though at a reduced rate. A damaged or missing jaw can be replaced with a denture or bionic jaw 

#### Breathing[[edit](/index.php?title=Health&action=edit&section=8 "Edit section: Breathing")]

Main article: [Breathing](/wiki/Breathing "Breathing")

The ability of a character to breathe. Affects consciousness and movement. 

#### Blood Filtration[[edit](/index.php?title=Health&action=edit&section=9 "Edit section: Blood Filtration")]

Main article: [Blood Filtration](/wiki/Blood_Filtration "Blood Filtration")

A character's ability to fight off diseases. Affects consciousness and immunity gain speed. 

#### Blood Pumping[[edit](/index.php?title=Health&action=edit&section=10 "Edit section: Blood Pumping")]

Main article: [Blood Pumping](/wiki/Blood_Pumping "Blood Pumping")

Blood Pumping is an essential function provided by the heart. It directly influences the moving stat. A pawn whose blood pumping reaches 0% will die instantly. 

#### Digestion[[edit](/index.php?title=Health&action=edit&section=11 "Edit section: Digestion")]

Main article: [Digestion](/wiki/Digestion "Digestion")

Digestion is a pawn's ability to digest food and convert it to energy efficiently. The better a pawn's Digestion value, the less rest they will need. A pawn whose Digestion reaches 0% will die instantly. 

#### Consciousness[[edit](/index.php?title=Health&action=edit&section=12 "Edit section: Consciousness")]

Main article: [Consciousness](/wiki/Consciousness "Consciousness")

Consciousness is a creature's ability to remain active, and in the case of your colonists, controllable. Blood loss, starvation and pain can have a serious effect on a creature's consciousness if they reach higher levels. Pawns below 30% consciousness will not wake up. When a medical operation is performed, anesthesia is induced, which renders the person unconscious until the effect of the anesthetic medication has worn off. This makes them incapable of moving, along with being incapable of leaving the bed. 

Consciousness is directly influenced by Efficiency of consciousness source, Pain, Blood pumping, Breathing and Blood filtration. The formula is: 
    
    
       **E(C) = E(CS) * (1 - Clamp((Pain - 0.1) * 4 / 9, 0, 0.4)) * (1 - 0.2 * △E(BP)) * (1 - 0.2 * △E(Br)) * (1 - 0.1 * △E(BF))**
    

where 
    
    
     E(C) is the Efficiency of Consciousness
     E(CS) is the Efficiency of Consciousness source (e.g., the brain)
     Pain is the total pain
     △E(BP) = 1 - E(BP), E(BP) is the Efficiency of Blood pumping
     △E(Br) = 1 - E(Br), E(Br) is the Efficiency of Breathing
     △E(BF) = 1 - E(BF), E(BF) is the Efficiency of Blood filtration
    

From the fomula, we know that when total pain is below 10%, it makes no influence on consciousness. And when 10% <= pain <= 100%, the fomula can be simplified as: 
    
    
       **E(C) = E(CS) * [(Pain - 0.1) * 4 / 9] * (1 - 0.2 * △E(BP)) * (1 - 0.2 * △E(Br)) * (1 - 0.1 * △E(BF))**
    

## Pain[[edit](/index.php?title=Health&action=edit&section=13 "Edit section: Pain")]

    _Main Article:[Pain](/wiki/Pain "Pain")_

Most biological creatures including [colonists](/wiki/Colonist "Colonist"), [raiders](/wiki/Raider "Raider"), and [animals](/wiki/Animals "Animals") feel pain caused by [injuries](/wiki/Injury "Injury"). A pawn's pain level ranges from 0% to 100% and is displayed on their Health tab's Overview. Pain levels are None, Little, Moderate, Severe, and Extreme.[Detail Needed] Even a little pain causes unhappy [thoughts](/wiki/Thoughts_list "Thoughts list"). Once pain passes 10%, it'll start to have an effect on [consciousness](/wiki/Consciousness "Consciousness"). If a pawn's pain level meets or exceeds their [Pain Shock Threshold](/wiki/Pain_Shock_Threshold "Pain Shock Threshold") they'll go into [shock](/wiki/Injury#Shock "Injury"), unable to move. Pawns with the [Wimp](/wiki/Wimp "Wimp") trait will be incapacitated with only minor pain of 30%. 

Most pain is temporary, but [scars](/wiki/Injury#Scarring "Injury") and certain [diseases](/wiki/Disease "Disease") can cause long-term pain. A [painstopper](/wiki/Painstopper "Painstopper") implant or the [painblock](/wiki/Painblock "Painblock") psycast [![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC") will get rid of all a colonist's pain and negate any pain-related mood penalties. Note that colonists with the [Masochist](/wiki/Masochist "Masochist") trait or an ideoligion with the [Pain: Idealized](/wiki/Pain_\(precept\) "Pain \(precept\)") precept[![Content added by the Ideology DLC](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/16px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Content added by the Ideology DLC") get a mood _bonus_ from pain instead of a penalty, so it's usually better to leave them alone. 

Injuries to the [artificial parts](/wiki/Artificial_body_parts "Artificial body parts") do not cause any pain, so colonists with a lot of bionics will generally incur less pain during combat. 

### Formula[[edit](/index.php?title=Pain&action=edit&section=T-1 "Edit section: ")]

Pain for a given pawn is the sum of the pain from each individual injury, with two extra steps at the end: 

  * `pain = pain / healthFactor` for pawns with a healthFactor other than 1.0 (like [grizzly bears](/wiki/Grizzly_bear "Grizzly bear") and other large animals). Humans have a healthFactor of 1.0, so this doesn't impact humans
  * `pain = pain * painFactor` from health conditions which alter pain. This includes pain-reducing drugs like [flake](/wiki/Flake "Flake") and [yayo](/wiki/Yayo "Yayo") as well as anesthetic used during operations



Pain from an individual injury is the product of the severity of the injury (the number of HP missing for things like cuts and burns) and the pain per severity for the injury type. For example: 

  * 30 burn damage causes 30 damage × 0.01875 pain per severity = 56.25% pain
  * 30 cut damage causes 30 damage × 0.0125 pain per severity = 37.5% pain



For the complete table of pain vs damage, see [Health Difficulties](/wiki/Health_Difficulties "Health Difficulties")

An exceptional kind of injury is scarification. They take the form of 0.5 HP scars, but give either 11% or 4% pain, for a pain factor of 0.08 or 0.22. 

### Thresholds[[edit](/index.php?title=Pain&action=edit&section=T-2 "Edit section: ")]

Serious  | 15% | −10 | +10 | +5  
Intense  | 40% | −15 | +15 | +7  
Mind-shattering  | 80% | −20 | +20 | +9  
  
A standard pawn will go down upon reaching mind-shattering pain.  
Pain inflicts a [consciousness](/wiki/Consciousness "Consciousness") penalty that scales with the pain percentage after exceeding 10%. It conforms to the following equation: `IF Pain[%] <= 10% THEN -0% ELSE -{(Pain-0.1)/2.25}[%]` For example, 15% pain is 0.15 - 0.1 = 0.05 / 2.25 = 0.02 or 2% consciousness loss.

## Health tab[[edit](/index.php?title=Health&action=edit&section=14 "Edit section: Health tab")]

All creatures have a health tab, located above the pawn's inspect pane. 

    Overview tab 

    The first section lists the creature's type, gender and age. Hovering over this section will display the creature's exact age and date born.
    Self-tend (turned off by default) allows a doctor to tend their own wounds, although with reduced efficiency.
    The creature's bodily systems are listed here with their current effectiveness. Not all systems are found among all types of creatures (i.e. animals don't have Talking).

    Operations tab 

    The Operations tab is only available for colonists, colony animals, and prisoners. Bills can be added here to request surgical operations.

    Injuries 

    Listed here are the creature's health conditions and injuries. Implants, bionic parts, and prosthetics are also listed here. Hovering over an entry provides detailed information.
    A red blood droplet indicates a bleeding injury. The size of the droplet indicates severity of the bleeding. Overall bleeding rate is listed at the bottom.
    An open circle indicates a condition that needs tending, usually requiring periodic treatments before fully cured. A solid circle indicates the condition has been tended to.
    A bandage icon indicates a treated injury. A white bandage indicates high quality treatment. Darker shades indicate lower quality treatment, more likely to result in scarring or infection. _See[Doctoring](/wiki/Doctoring "Doctoring")_

## Cold and Heat Injuries[[edit](/index.php?title=Health&action=edit&section=15 "Edit section: Cold and Heat Injuries")]

Hypothermia: Causes a negative thought, and pawns will eventually lose consciousness.  
Frostbite: Can cause limbs and fingers/toes to fall off.   
Heatstroke: Causes a negative thought, and can affect consciousness and even set a pawn on [fire](/wiki/Fire "Fire").  
Burns: Causes low-medium pain and creates a risk of infection. 

## Treatment[[edit](/index.php?title=Health&action=edit&section=16 "Edit section: Treatment")]

For most injuries, you can treat [colonists](/wiki/Colonist "Colonist") with or without [medicine](/wiki/Medicine "Medicine") (with typically better results if a medicinal substance is provided), and also have them lay in [hospital beds](/wiki/Hospital_bed "Hospital bed") or any other bed or sleeping spot assigned as a medical spot, though Hospital Beds further improve the chance of them being treated properly. Humans heal wounds at a base rate of [8 damage per day](/wiki/Injury#Healing_Rate "Injury") before bonuses. 

### Improper Treatment[[edit](/index.php?title=Health&action=edit&section=17 "Edit section: Improper Treatment")]

Treating an injury poorly _may_ result in [scarring](/wiki/Injury#Scarring "Injury"), reduced [healing rate](/wiki/Stat#Healing_Rate "Stat") or infections. 

### Operations[[edit](/index.php?title=Health&action=edit&section=18 "Edit section: Operations")]

See also: [Surgery](/wiki/Surgery "Surgery")

Under the Health tab, you can assign an operation to a [colonist](/wiki/Colonist "Colonist") by clicking on the bill sub-tab. Operations cannot be performed without a proper [Medkit](/wiki/Medicine "Medicine") to apply an anesthetic. Operation success chance depends highly on the doctor's skill level. Keep in mind that keeping the room as clean as possible (also optionally with the use of [sterile tiles](/wiki/Sterile_tile "Sterile tile")) will help preventing possible infection. As of Alpha 16, there are three categories of surgery failure – minor, catastrophic, and ridiculous. Ridiculous hits all body parts, minor and catastrophic hit parts near the surgery site. 

##### Transplants[[edit](/index.php?title=Health&action=edit&section=19 "Edit section: Transplants")]

For missing [internal organs](/wiki/Table_of_Human_Body_Parts "Table of Human Body Parts"), either a donor organ or an [artificial equivalent](/wiki/Artificial_body_parts#Body_-_Organs "Artificial body parts") can be used. Some diseases can be cured through a transplant. [Artery blockages](/wiki/Artery_blockage "Artery blockage") can be cured with a heart transplant and [asthma](/wiki/Ailments#Asthma "Ailments") can be cured with lung transplants. Be wary that your colonists may suffer [mood penalties](/wiki/Mood#Someone_organ-harvested "Mood") when you harvest an organ, with the "donor" will suffer a -30 mood penalty for over a week. See [Human Resources](/wiki/Human_Resources "Human Resources") for details and mitigation strategies. 

##### Limb Replacement[[edit](/index.php?title=Health&action=edit&section=20 "Edit section: Limb Replacement")]

From [peg legs](/wiki/Peg_leg "Peg leg") to [bionic arms](/wiki/Bionic_arm "Bionic arm"), you could either replace a colonists' missing limbs, augment them, or cruelly punish your [enemies](/wiki/Raider "Raider") with peg legs out of spite. Bionic parts have a bonus to specific actions or movement. For example, a [bionic leg](/wiki/Bionic_leg "Bionic leg") will increase your colonist's [moving speed](/wiki/Move_Speed "Move Speed") by 12.5%. Having a second bionic leg will increase the speed further by 12.5%, so having both right and left bionic legs will increase the speed by 25%. 

##### Implants[[edit](/index.php?title=Health&action=edit&section=21 "Edit section: Implants")]

You can install implants onto a natural part to improve its functions. 

Implants are not mutually exclusive so you can have more than 1 per part. 

##### Amputations[[edit](/index.php?title=Health&action=edit&section=22 "Edit section: Amputations")]

Limbs can also be removed due to health reasons. If an arm/leg becomes infected it can be amputated to save the person.
