Go-juice

[![Go-juice](/images/1/17/Gojuice_b.png)](/wiki/File:Gojuice_b.png "Go-juice")

A synthetic performance-enhancing drug developed for space marines during the early days of interplanetary warfare. Go-juice blocks pain, increases movement speed, and improves the user's melee and shooting abilities. The military chemists who created it were never able to remove its addictiveness. Some saw this as a downside; others saw it as a benefit.

**Go-juice** is a potent [hard](/wiki/Hard_drugs "Hard drugs") combat [drug](/wiki/Drug "Drug") that almost completely blocks [pain](/wiki/Pain "Pain") and significantly improves a pawn's capabilities, at the risk of addiction and overdose.

## Acquisition[[edit](/index.php?title=Go-juice&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Acquisition")]

Go-juice can be crafted at a [drug lab](/wiki/Drug_lab "Drug lab") once the [Go-juice production](/wiki/Research#Go-juice_production "Research") research project has been completed. Each requires [![Neutroamine](/images/thumb/3/3c/Neutroamine_c.png/16px-Neutroamine_c.png)](/wiki/Neutroamine "Neutroamine") 2 [Neutroamine](/wiki/Neutroamine "Neutroamine"), [![Yayo](/images/thumb/0/0d/Yayo_c.png/16px-Yayo_c.png)](/wiki/Yayo "Yayo") 1 [Yayo](/wiki/Yayo "Yayo") and 600 [ticks](/wiki/Time "Time") (10 secs) of work modified by the [drug synthesis speed](/wiki/Drug_Synthesis_Speed "Drug Synthesis Speed") of the crafter.   
Go-juice can be bought from the following traders: [Orbital bulk goods trader](/wiki/Trader/Orbital_Bulk_Goods "Trader/Orbital Bulk Goods"), [Orbital combat supplier](/wiki/Trader/Orbital_Combat_Supplier "Trader/Orbital Combat Supplier"), [Orbital exotic goods trader](/wiki/Trader/Orbital_Exotic_Goods_Trader "Trader/Orbital Exotic Goods Trader"), [Outlander base trader](/wiki/Trader/Base_Outlander_Standard "Trader/Base Outlander Standard"), [Outlander bulk goods trader](/wiki/Trader/Caravan_Outlander_Bulk_Goods "Trader/Caravan Outlander Bulk Goods"), [Outlander slaver](/wiki/Trader/Caravan_Outlander_Pirate_Merchant "Trader/Caravan Outlander Pirate Merchant"), [Outlander visitor](/wiki/Trader/Visitor_Outlander_Standard "Trader/Visitor Outlander Standard"), [Slave ship](/wiki/Trader/Orbital_Pirate_Merchant "Trader/Orbital Pirate Merchant"), [Empire base trader](/wiki/Trader/Base_Empire_Standard "Trader/Base Empire Standard")[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC"), [Imperial trader caravan](/wiki/Trader/Empire_Caravan_Trader_General "Trader/Empire Caravan Trader General")[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC"), [Orbital imperial trader](/wiki/Trader/Orbital_Empire "Trader/Orbital Empire")[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC"). 

It can also be found on [raiders](/wiki/Raider "Raider") and [purchased](/wiki/Trade "Trade"). 

## Summary[[edit](/index.php?title=Go-juice&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Summary")]

Go-juice is a potent combat stimulant that has a variety of effects. These can be broken into effects that occur: 

  * Instantaneously and one time upon using the drug.
  * While high on the drug.
  * While addicted to the drug.
  * While withdrawing from the drug.



### Upon injection[[edit](/index.php?title=Go-juice&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Upon injection")]

A pawn taking go-juice will do so right where they stand without looking for a place to sit first. Go-juice is taken by injection, taking 80 [ticks](/wiki/Time "Time") (1.33 secs). It has the following one time effects: 

  * +40% Chemical [recreation](/wiki/Recreation "Recreation")
  * If the pawn has the [chemical interest](/wiki/Chemical_interest "Chemical interest") or [chemical fascination](/wiki/Chemical_fascination "Chemical fascination") trait: 
    * +30% [Chemical need](/wiki/Chemical_need "Chemical need")
  * +40% [Rest](/wiki/Rest "Rest")
  * +15% [Psyfocus](/wiki/Psyfocus "Psyfocus")[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC")
  * 0.5% Chance for a [major overdose](/wiki/Overdose#Major "Overdose")
  * +18% to +35%  [Overdose](/wiki/Overdose "Overdose") severity
  * **+50%** Go-juice high severity _(see "Go-juice high" below)_
  * 2.6% [Addiction](/wiki/Addiction "Addiction") chance
  * If the pawn has an addiction already: 
    * +90% Go-juice need _(see "Addiction" below)_
    * −20%  Addiction progress _(see "Withdrawal" below)_



### Go-juice high[[edit](/index.php?title=Go-juice&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Go-juice high")]

> "Go-juice in the bloodstream. It supercharges combat-related abilities, and instantly increases psyfocus when first injected."

While each go-juice injection increases the severity of the go-juice high by 50% _(see "Upon injection" above)_ , it decreases by 75% per day again, meaning that the high of a single dose lasts **16 hours**. The maximum severity of a go-juice high is 100% which is reached with 2 go-juice. Regardless of the go-juice high severity, the effects are always the same. Only their duration changes accordingly: 

  * +5 [Mood](/wiki/Mood "Mood") (_"I feel pumped but steady. I am the bullet in flight, ready to cut through you."_)
  * ×10% [Pain](/wiki/Pain "Pain") (meaning all pain will be divided by ten)
  * +20% [Consciousness](/wiki/Consciousness "Consciousness")
  * +35% [Sight](/wiki/Sight "Sight")
  * +50% [Moving](/wiki/Moving "Moving")



### Tolerance[[edit](/index.php?title=Go-juice&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Tolerance")]

As of version [1.2.3005](/wiki/Version/1.2.3005 "Version/1.2.3005"), go-juice no longer gives a pawn any tolerance at all when taken, despite the effects of tolerance still existing in-game. This has been confirmed as intended behaviour.[1]

### Addiction[[edit](/index.php?title=Go-juice&action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: Addiction")]

> "A chemical addiction to go-juice. Long-term presence of go-juice has caused neurological adaptations at the cellular level, so the brain can no longer function properly without the drug. Without regular doses of go-juice, withdrawal symptoms will begin. However, extended abstinence will force the brain to adapt back to its normal state, resolving the addiction."
> 
> -- Addiction description

Each dose of go-juice carries a 2.6% chance of addiction. There is therefore no safe dose interval. 

The addiction progresses by 4.5% per day from the initial 50%. Once it reaches 100% the addiction is healed, meaning it takes about 11.1 days to overcome the addiction. Consuming go-juice during an addiction will reduce the addiction progress by 20% again _(see "Upon injection" above)_. 

Developing an addiction means a pawn has a go-juice [need](/wiki/Needs#Drug_addictions "Needs"). The need for go-juice falls by 33.3% per day, consuming go-juice satisfies the need by 90% _(see "Upon injection" above)_ , meaning the pawn will need to consume go-juice at least every 2.7 days to prevent withdrawal symptoms. 

### Withdrawal[[edit](/index.php?title=Go-juice&action=edit&section=7 "Edit section: Withdrawal")]

> "Because of a go-juice addiction, this person needs to regularly consume the drug to avoid withdrawal symptoms."
> 
> -- Need description

As soon as the go-juice need reaches 0%, the pawn suffers from the following symptoms: 

  * −22 [Mood](/wiki/Mood "Mood") (_"I'm all fuzzy and can't think straight. My limbs feel heavy, I'm tired and hungry, everything hurts. And why won't my eyes focus properly?"_)
  * ×300% [Pain](/wiki/Pain "Pain") (meaning all pain will be tripled)
  * −20% [Consciousness](/wiki/Consciousness "Consciousness")
  * −50% [Moving](/wiki/Moving "Moving")
  * −20% [Sight](/wiki/Sight "Sight")
  * −20% [Blood pumping](/wiki/Blood_pumping "Blood pumping")
  * −10% [Manipulation](/wiki/Manipulation "Manipulation")
  * +30% [Sleep Fall Rate](/wiki/Sleep_Fall_Rate "Sleep Fall Rate") (meaning the pawn needs to sleep about a 30% sooner)
  * +50% [Hunger rate factor](/wiki/Hunger "Hunger") (meaning the pawn needs to eat about 50% sooner)
  * Mental break chances: 
    * [Hard drug binge](/wiki/Hard_drug_binge "Hard drug binge"): on average every 40 days. This averages to 0.3 hard drug binges during each withdrawal.
    * [Psychotic wandering](/wiki/Psychotic_wandering "Psychotic wandering"): on average every 10 days. This averages to 1.1 wanderings during each withdrawal.



## Analysis[[edit](/index.php?title=Go-juice&action=edit&section=8 "Edit section: Analysis")]

Go-juice is a stimulant and pain-killer, with a significant impact on combat performance. [Wake-up](/wiki/Wake-up "Wake-up") and [luciferium](/wiki/Luciferium "Luciferium") are the next best drugs for combat, but wake-up largely offers work-related boosts, while luciferium is a permanent addiction (and works with go-juice). 

### Combat[[edit](/index.php?title=Go-juice&action=edit&section=9 "Edit section: Combat")]

Increases to Sight and Consciousness boosts accuracy, both ranged and melee. Increased Moving helps with all forms of [kiting](/wiki/Kiting "Kiting") tactics and makes it easier to retreat. Increased medical tend speed can save a pawn that's bleeding out quickly. Like the painstopper, the large reduction in pain allows a pawn to stand up far longer and win more fights. But they will continue fighting until very close to death - or when physically made unable. 

Finally, go-juice provides slight boosts to mood, recreation, and rest. While weaker in this regard than most other drugs, they can still help prevent [mental breaks](/wiki/Mental_break "Mental break"). 

The risk of addiction and overdose is always present, but addiction is better than death. For example, a miner digging out far away from your base will appreciate carrying go-juice, for when a [raid](/wiki/Raid "Raid") or [manhunter pack](/wiki/Manhunter "Manhunter") spawns near them. Withdrawal symptoms are harsh, but its 11.1 day withdrawal (assuming no future doses are taken) can be managed by [arresting](/wiki/Arrest "Arrest") the addict in question. Go-juice has the second shortest withdrawal, behind [ambrosia](/wiki/Ambrosia "Ambrosia"). 

### Misc work[[edit](/index.php?title=Go-juice&action=edit&section=10 "Edit section: Misc work")]

Besides combat, the increase in Moving can make pawns much faster at hauling and somewhat quicker at other tasks. Usually this is not worth the risk of addiction unless you _need_ to repair your defenses. But if you have a pawn you don't care about, like an unwanted [wanderer](/wiki/Events#Wanderer_joins "Events") or some desperate refugees,[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC") then you can use go-juice to expedite your work. 

Go-juice's consciousness boost also improves the odds of successful surgery, but this rarely matters, because the surgery success chance can usually be capped at 98% with a [sterile room](/wiki/Sterile_tile "Sterile tile"), [hospital bed](/wiki/Hospital_bed "Hospital bed"), and a good doctor. 

    **Tip:** Unless you need an immediate and significant boost in chemical [recreation](/wiki/Recreation "Recreation"), [rest](/wiki/Rest "Rest") or [psyfocus](/wiki/Psyfocus "Psyfocus")[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC") it is recommended to wait at least 4.8 hours between the first and the second dose of go-juice. Otherwise the overdose severity might build up resulting in a [minor overdose](/wiki/Overdose#Minor "Overdose") (this is unrelated to the 0.5% chance for a [major overdose](/wiki/Overdose#Major "Overdose") that comes with each injection). Be particularly careful when using go-juice in combination with other drugs! The third dose and any dose after that shouldn't be taken earlier than 16 hours after the second to last dose. Otherwise some hours of the go-juice high will be wasted.

### Stats[[edit](/index.php?title=Go-juice&action=edit&section=11 "Edit section: Stats")]

Assuming a healthy, but otherwise unaugmented pawn, the bonuses from consuming go-juice translates to: 

  * A +50% increase to [Move Speed](/wiki/Move_Speed "Move Speed"), equivalent to an increase of 2.3 c/s on a healthy, unencumbered pawn.
  * A +20% increase in [General Labor Speed](/wiki/General_Labor_Speed "General Labor Speed").
  * A +30% increase in [Medical Tend Quality](/wiki/Medical_Tend_Quality "Medical Tend Quality") at [Medical](/wiki/Medical "Medical") skill 10. At Medical 20, this is a 27% increase, at Medical 5, a 32% increase
  * A 1.2× multiplier to [Medical Surgery Success Chance](/wiki/Medical_Surgery_Success_Chance "Medical Surgery Success Chance").
  * An increase in [Shooting Accuracy](/wiki/Shooting_Accuracy "Shooting Accuracy") equivalent to +4.2 extra levels of [Shooting](/wiki/Shooting "Shooting").
  * An increase in [Melee Hit Chance](/wiki/Melee_Hit_Chance "Melee Hit Chance") equivalent to +6.6 extra levels of [Melee](/wiki/Melee "Melee").
  * An increase in [Melee Dodge Chance](/wiki/Melee_Dodge_Chance "Melee Dodge Chance") equivalent to +11.8 extra levels of Melee.



### Drug dependency[[edit](/index.php?title=Go-juice&action=edit&section=12 "Edit section: Drug dependency")]

The [Go-juice dependency](/wiki/Go-juice_dependency "Go-juice dependency") gene,[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC") found in [hussars](/wiki/Hussar "Hussar"), completely removes the chance of normal addiction and overdose. But at 5 days without the drug, the pawn will incur severe penalties. At the least, dependency is less severe than actual addiction or withdrawal. 

Each dose of go-juice requires [![Neutroamine](/images/thumb/3/3c/Neutroamine_c.png/16px-Neutroamine_c.png)](/wiki/Neutroamine "Neutroamine") 2 [Neutroamine](/wiki/Neutroamine "Neutroamine"), [![Yayo](/images/thumb/0/0d/Yayo_c.png/16px-Yayo_c.png)](/wiki/Yayo "Yayo") 1 [Yayo](/wiki/Yayo "Yayo") to create. A stack of 50 neutroamine, worth [![Silver](/images/thumb/5/57/Silver_c.png/16px-Silver_c.png)](/wiki/Silver "Silver") 300 before [Trade Price Improvement](/wiki/Trade_Price_Improvement "Trade Price Improvement") and [difficulty](/wiki/Difficulty "Difficulty"), can last a single pawn for 125 days, or just over 2 years. With 0 Social skill in [Losing is Fun](/wiki/Difficulty "Difficulty"), this price increases to [![Silver](/images/thumb/5/57/Silver_c.png/16px-Silver_c.png)](/wiki/Silver "Silver") 504\. In practice, this supply will end up being somewhat shorter if you want to use go-juice for combat or work purposes. Meanwhile, psychoid/yayo can be grown inside your colony. A drug-dependent pawn will survive for up to 60 days without the drug, usually more than enough time to form a [caravan](/wiki/Caravan "Caravan") or find an [orbital trader](/wiki/Comms_console "Comms console"). 

Overall, go-juice dependency is very affordable; you'll just need [Go-juice production](/wiki/Research#Go-juice_production "Research") and [Psychite refining](/wiki/Research#Psychite_refining "Research") researched, and some form of [trade](/wiki/Trade "Trade"). In exchange, a dependent pawn can get all the benefit of go-juice at any time, with 0% chance of any health problems whatsoever.
