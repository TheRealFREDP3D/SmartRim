A **solar generator** is [building](/wiki/Building "Building") that generates [power](/wiki/Power "Power") from sunlight.

## Acquisition[[edit](/index.php?title=Solar_generator&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Acquisition")]

Solar generators can be constructed once the [Solar panel](/wiki/Research#Solar_panel "Research") research project has been completed. Each requires [![Steel](/images/thumb/9/90/Steel_c.png/16px-Steel_c.png)](/wiki/Steel "Steel") 100 [Steel](/wiki/Steel "Steel"), [![Component](/images/thumb/4/40/Component.png/16px-Component.png)](/wiki/Component "Component") 3 [Components](/wiki/Component "Component"), 2,500 [ticks](/wiki/Time "Time") (41.67 secs) of work modified by the [construction speed](/wiki/Construction_speed "Construction speed") of the builder, and a [construction](/wiki/Construction "Construction") skill of 6. 

Once constructed, they cannot be re-installed; they can only be deconstructed.  
Solar generators can not be bought from traders. 

## Summary[[edit](/index.php?title=Solar_generator&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Summary")]

[![StumpChoppedA \(cropped\).png](/images/thumb/6/6a/StumpChoppedA_%28cropped%29.png/40px-StumpChoppedA_%28cropped%29.png)](/wiki/Category:Stubs "Category:Stubs") | **This section is a[stub](/wiki/Category:Stubs "Category:Stubs").** You can help RimWorld Wiki by **[expanding it](https://rimworldwiki.com/index.php?title=Solar_generator&action=edit). Solar generators produce up to 1700 W of [power](/wiki/Power "Power") at 100% natural [light](/wiki/Light "Light"). Power is a direct product of light level; for example, 50% daylight gives 850 W. An [eclipse](/wiki/Eclipse "Eclipse") blocks the sun, but [weather](/wiki/Weather "Weather") conditions like rain, fog, or snow will _not_ reduce any power. A [roof](/wiki/Roof "Roof") reduces power output, proportional to the tiles covered. However, there's no "overshadow" from mountains, despite any graphics of long shadows seen in the game. 

Solar generators only operate by the world light level; light from ordinary sources such as a [standing lamp](/wiki/Standing_lamp "Standing lamp") or [sun lamp](/wiki/Sun_lamp "Sun lamp") will not cause them to generate power. The latitude of your starting position will affect solar cycles, with equator zones giving the strong consistent output and the north pole struggling to reach peak output. 

Lightning striking causes the generator to create a small amount of power. 

Generators are temporarily disabled by sources of [EMP](/wiki/EMP "EMP"), with downtime scaling to the EMP damage dealt.[Verify] During this time, it will not generate any power. Unlike mechanoids, generators do not adapt to EMP and thus can be continuously restunned. 

## Analysis[[edit](/index.php?title=Solar_generator&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Analysis")]

Solar generators are "free" energy; once built, they do not need fueling, they simply give and keep giving... while they have sunshine. They do not give their maximum power from start to finish; as daylight grows, the power they provide slowly grows to their full capacity, and then tapers off again back to zero as dusk and night approach. 

The exact amount of sun depends on your latitude and season. See [Sunlight](/wiki/Sunlight "Sunlight") for details. In a single day near the equator, there are roughly: 

  * 9 hours of full sunlight - from 8h to 17h
  * 8 hours of no sunlight - from 20h to 4h.
  * 7 hours of reduced sunlight - from 4h to 8h, and 17h to 20h.



[Batteries](/wiki/Batteries "Batteries") are the easiest way to make power consistent, allowing power use at night, so long as an [eclipse](/wiki/Eclipse "Eclipse") or [short circuit](/wiki/Short_circuit "Short circuit") doesn't happen. 

Alternatively, you can accept that not all items need to be powered 24/7. Most production benches are only used when colonists are awake, and many buildings like [smelters](/wiki/Electric_smelter "Electric smelter") and [crematoriums](/wiki/Electric_crematorium "Electric crematorium") don't need to be used daily. The most pertinent case is with [sun lamps](/wiki/Sun_lamp "Sun lamp"), which shuts down as night begins. Two solar generators will roughly power a sun lamp. However, the solar generator's full output doesn't always align with the sun lamp's on period, so batteries remain helpful. 

### Comparison[[edit](/index.php?title=Solar_generator&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Comparison")]

Compared to [wind turbines](/wiki/Wind_turbine "Wind turbine"), solar generators take up less space (so are easier to protect) and provide fairly consistent power. While wind works day and night, even with a [battery](/wiki/Battery "Battery"), power may turn off randomly at a crucial moment. However, using solar requires research, and they consistently shut off at night, or during an [eclipse](/wiki/Eclipse "Eclipse"). 

It is worth noting that solar generators are low-lying - you can place them in front of a wind turbine, without blocking the wind.
