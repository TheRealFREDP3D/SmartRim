**Sandbags** are a [security](/wiki/Security "Security") building that can be placed to provide pawns [cover](/wiki/Cover "Cover"), sheltering them from ranged attacks.

## Acquisition[[edit](/index.php?title=Sandbags&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Acquisition")]

Sandbags can be constructed, each requiring [![Stuff](/images/thumb/7/7e/Buildingmats.png/16px-Buildingmats.png)](/wiki/Stuff "Stuff") 5 [Stuff](/wiki/Stuff "Stuff") ([Fabric](/wiki/Fabric "Fabric")/[Leathery](/wiki/Leathery "Leathery")) and 180 [ticks](/wiki/Time "Time") (3 secs) of work modified by the [construction speed](/wiki/Construction_speed "Construction speed") of the builder and the [work to build factor](/wiki/Property:Work_To_Build_Factor "Property:Work To Build Factor") and [offset](/wiki/Property:Work_To_Build_Offset "Property:Work To Build Offset") of the material. 

Once constructed, they cannot be re-installed; they can only be deconstructed.  
Sandbags can not be bought from traders. 

## Summary[[edit](/index.php?title=Sandbags&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Summary")]

See also: [Barricade](/wiki/Barricade "Barricade")

Sandbags provide 55% [cover](/wiki/Cover "Cover") effectiveness, meaning that bullets aimed at targets behind them have a 55% chance to hit the barricades, leaving only a 45% chance to hit anything behind them. Cover won't shelter pawns from melee attacks, thrown projectiles, or explosions. Both colonists and [enemies](/wiki/Raider "Raider") benefit from cover, as do [turrets](/wiki/Turrets "Turrets"). They provide identical cover to [barricades](/wiki/Barricades "Barricades"). 

Sandbags are placed on the ground. They slow down pawns walking over them, and do not allow a pawn to "stop" to fire or take cover if they are standing on one. They take damage from bullets and explosions. 

Sandbags are largely interchangeable with [barricades](/wiki/Barricades "Barricades"), as they both have identical [cover](/wiki/Cover "Cover"), base HP, and similar material costs. However, sandbags are made of [textiles](/wiki/Textile "Textile") and are always nonflammable, while barricades are made of [metal](/wiki/Metal "Metal"), [wood](/wiki/Wood "Wood"), and [stone](/wiki/Stone "Stone") materials and their flammability depends on the material chosen. Sandbags also require much less work to make, but barricades are somewhat more [beautiful](/wiki/Beauty "Beauty"). Unlike barricades, sandbags cannot enclose a [pen](/wiki/Pen "Pen") like the [fence](/wiki/Fence "Fence"). 

## Analysis[[edit](/index.php?title=Sandbags&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Analysis")]

Sandbags and barricades are, in most respects, identical. The material affects the building's health, but not its defensive properties. Sandbags are generally faster to create, making them better in an offensive operation. Barricades are slightly more beautiful, but require more work to make, so are marginally better at permanent defenses. Just remember that [raiders](/wiki/Raiders "Raiders") will use cover to the same effect, so deconstruct unneeded buildings when you can. 

Either building can be combined with [walls](/wiki/Wall "Wall") for more cover. Walls provide superior cover, but a full wall will block line of fire. Having a wall with multiple 1-2 tile gaps of sandbags will give more cover than simply using sandbags, while still allowing pawns to fire through. 

Sandbags can also be used to slow down raiders, as the process of entering (but not walking through) a sandbag slows pawns down. In a hallway, sandbags can be placed with a 1-tile gap between them. [Fences](/wiki/Fence "Fence") are cheaper for this purpose, but sandbags do let [pen animals](/wiki/Pen_animal "Pen animal") in. This can be occasionally relevant against [yttakin pirates](/wiki/Yttakin_pirates "Yttakin pirates"),[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC") who bring [wild boars](/wiki/Wild_boar "Wild boar") as raiders. 

Note that sandbags and barricades can no longer be used to prevent enemies from standing in a specific position; as of 1.6, enemies can now stop and fire while standing on any object. They can still be somewhat effective in causing enemies to path around a position of cover, but if the enemy does path to that cell with the barricade, sandbag or any other object, they can always fire.

## Version history[[edit](/index.php?title=Sandbags&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Version history")]

  * Alpha 16/17? - In earlier versions it used to have 70% cover rating.
  * Beta 19 - Received a new, extended description and a retexture, and its cost was reduced from 6 [steel](/wiki/Steel "Steel") to 5.
  * 1.1 - Crafting recipe was changed to use [textiles](/wiki/Textiles "Textiles") rather than steel, and its health was nerfed (450 -> 300), along with its cover value (65 -> 57%).
  * [1.3.3066](/wiki/Version/1.3.3066 "Version/1.3.3066") \- Fill percent (i.e. cover) reduced from 57% to 55%.
  * 1.6 - Enemies can now stand on a sandbag when damaged; standing on a sandbag no longer prevents enemies from taking cover.
