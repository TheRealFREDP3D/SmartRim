## Acquisition[[edit](/index.php?title=Nutrient_paste_meal&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Acquisition")]

Unlike other meals, nutrient paste meals are not produced via a [bill](/wiki/Bill "Bill"). Instead, [Human](/wiki/Human "Human") pawns seeking a meal can approach a [powered](/wiki/Power "Power") [nutrient paste dispenser](/wiki/Nutrient_paste_dispenser "Nutrient paste dispenser"), stand in the interaction spot, and instantly create a nutrient paste meal, consuming 0.3 [nutrition](/wiki/Nutrition "Nutrition") of any [food](/wiki/Food "Food") inside the [hoppers](/wiki/Hopper "Hopper") linked to the dispenser. For a detailed description of how the dispenser consumes the required nutrition, see the [nutrient paste dispenser](/wiki/Nutrient_paste_dispenser#Summary "Nutrient paste dispenser") page. 

The meal is created in the hands of the pawn using the dispenser. Once the meal is produced, the pawn will act the same as if they had retrieved any cooked meal from storage with [Hungry](/wiki/Saturation "Saturation") pawns taking the meal to be consumed, while [Wardens](/wiki/Work#Warden "Work") and [Doctors](/wiki/Work#Doctor "Work") will take the meal to their charges. Note that [animals](/wiki/Animal "Animal") cannot use the nutrient paste dispenser, but [prisoners](/wiki/Prisoner "Prisoner") can use dispensers with interaction spots in their prison. 

These meals can't normally be stockpiled. Should the pawn dispensing the meal be interrupted between dispensing and eating the meal, they will drop it. This is done by limiting a pawn's area restriction to just a nutrient paste dispenser, or by simply drafting them. This behavior can be exploited to stockpile meals, but their short shelf life of 0.75 days means they should be immediately frozen. See [below](/wiki/Nutrient_paste_dispenser#Manually_producing_meals "Nutrient paste dispenser") for a more in-depth analysis of this strategy. 

## Summary[[edit](/index.php?title=Nutrient_paste_meal&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Summary")]

A nutrient paste meal can be eaten for 0.9 [nutrition](/wiki/Nutrition "Nutrition"). As paste takes 0.3 nutrition to make, it has a nutrition efficiency of 300%. Nutrient paste cannot cause [food poisoning](/wiki/Food_poisoning "Food poisoning"). 

When consumed, nutrient paste gives a −4 _Ate nutrient paste meal_ [thought](/wiki/Thought "Thought") for 1 [day](/wiki/Time "Time"). This mood penalty is not applied to [Ascetic](/wiki/Ascetic "Ascetic") pawns and those with the [Eating Nutrient Paste: Don't Mind](/wiki/Eating_nutrient_paste "Eating nutrient paste")[![Content added by the Ideology DLC](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/16px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Content added by the Ideology DLC") ideoligious precept. If the meal was made out of [insect meat](/wiki/Insect_meat "Insect meat"), [human meat](/wiki/Human_meat "Human meat"), or [twisted meat](/wiki/Twisted_meat "Twisted meat")[![Content added by the Anomaly DLC](/images/thumb/3/32/AnomalyIcon.png/16px-AnomalyIcon.png)](/wiki/Anomaly_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Anomaly DLC") their respective moodlets will be applied. If [Ideology DLC](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Ideology DLC") is active and the meal was made out of [raw fungus](/wiki/Raw_fungus "Raw fungus"), its normal moodlet will also be applied. 

## Analysis[[edit](/index.php?title=Nutrient_paste_meal&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Analysis")]

Nutrient paste is the most efficient way to prepare a meal, noticeably better than [simple](/wiki/Simple_meal "Simple meal") and [fine meals](/wiki/Fine_meal "Fine meal") (180% efficiency). This makes nutrient paste useful in a famine, even if you don't plan to use it indefinitely. In addition, no cooking is necessary. This avoids [food poisoning](/wiki/Food_poisoning "Food poisoning") entirely and can save a lot of time off a cook's hands. Nutrient paste is a poor choice for [caravans](/wiki/Caravan "Caravan"), given how quickly nutrient paste spoils and the tedious micro required to make stacks of them. Using only nutrient paste meals can also be a useful tool for containing the spread of [metalhorror](/wiki/Metalhorror "Metalhorror")[![Content added by the Anomaly DLC](/images/thumb/3/32/AnomalyIcon.png/16px-AnomalyIcon.png)](/wiki/Anomaly_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Anomaly DLC") infections, as nutrient paste meals are not prepared by a cook. 

However, nutrient paste meals give a -4 [mood](/wiki/Mood "Mood") debuff, which ends up being -9 net mood compared to [fine meals](/wiki/Fine_meal "Fine meal"). It is advised to not give nutrient paste to pawns with a [trait](/wiki/Trait "Trait") that gives a negative modifier to mental break threshold, because they can push a pawn over the edge. Because pawns prefer normal meals over nutrient paste, you can set restrictions to prevent normal pawns from consuming all of the good meals, leaving them to the volatile ones. 

Nutrient paste is also the most efficient [animal](/wiki/Animal "Animal") food, who don't care about [mood](/wiki/Mood "Mood"), assuming it can eat the full 0.9 nutrition without [overeating](/wiki/Overeating "Overeating"). But using it as such requires a pawn to manually produce nutrient paste meals, since animals can't use the dispenser. 

The mood penalty is negated if the colonist eating it has a precept "Eating Nutrient paste" as "Don't mind" for their [Ideoligion](/wiki/Ideoligion "Ideoligion"),[![Content added by the Ideology DLC](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/16px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Content added by the Ideology DLC") making it a powerful choice for day-to-day work flow.

## HEALTH ADVISORY WARNING[[edit](/index.php?title=Nutrient_paste_meal&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: HEALTH ADVISORY WARNING")]

[![CowboyHat.png](/images/thumb/4/40/CowboyHat.png/40px-CowboyHat.png)](/wiki/File:CowboyHat.png)| **May contain witty humour, not-so-witty humour, injokes, and references to Ludeon Forums and cowboy hats.**  
---|---  
  
Food is deeply tied to the [mental well-being of your colonists](/wiki/Mood "Mood") and there are few things for your pawns to look forward to in life on [the rim](/wiki/Lore#Rimworld "Lore") besides throwing horseshoes or [sleep](/wiki/Death "Death"). 

Long term effects of replacing their [meals](/wiki/Simple_meal "Simple meal") with bland, textureless options can cause: 

• [Mood disturbances](/wiki/Mental_break "Mental break")

• [Irritability](/wiki/Mental_break#Insulting_spree "Mental break") and disinterest in food 

• [Social withdrawal](/wiki/Mental_break#Hide_in_room "Mental break") (since eating is social for many people) 

• In severe, long-term cases: [depersonalization](/wiki/Mental_break#Psychotic_wandering "Mental break"), [anhedonia](/wiki/Mental_break#Catatonic_breakdown "Mental break"), and in rare cases, [psychotic symptoms in vulnerable individuals.](/wiki/Mental_break#Berserk "Mental break")
