**Psychic droner ship parts** drop as part of a map [Event](/wiki/Event "Event"). A crashed ship part, accompanied by small [fires](/wiki/Fire "Fire") and [mechanoids](/wiki/Mechanoids "Mechanoids"), lands at a random [unroofed](/wiki/Roof "Roof") location when the event triggers. This ship part carries a psychic device that influences organic beings' minds.

## Summary[[edit](/index.php?title=Ship_part_\(psychic_droner\)&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Summary")]

This ship part contains a hostile psychic device that emits a psychic drone that negatively affects the [mood](/wiki/Mood "Mood") of all humans on the map of a gender chosen at random. This drone begins at a "low" −12, but grows progressively stronger the longer the ship part remains, therefore it is advisable to destroy the part as quickly as possible. A pawn's individual [psychic sensitivity](/wiki/Psychic_sensitivity "Psychic sensitivity") will modify the effect of the drone. If multiple psychic droner parts are present, the target gender of the active drone will be the one from the most recent droner or the one that last increased its intensity. 

The psychic device is also capable of emitting concentrated bursts of psychic energy, driving all animals in the immediate vicinity insane.[Detail needed]

### Psychic drone stages[[edit](/index.php?title=Ship_part_\(psychic_droner\)&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Psychic drone stages")]

## Analysis[[edit](/index.php?title=Ship_part_\(psychic_droner\)&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Analysis")]

For general advice on dealing with the guarding mechanoids, see [Crashed ship parts](/wiki/Crashed_ship_parts "Crashed ship parts").

The urgency of destroying the psychic droner will depend on the effect it has over colonists, which depends on their individual [traits](/wiki/Traits "Traits") and [genes](/wiki/Genes "Genes"),[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC") but specially on their psychic sensitivity. A colony with high sensitivity pawns ([highmates](/wiki/Highmates "Highmates"), [sanguophages](/wiki/Sanguophages "Sanguophages"), good [psycasters](/wiki/Psycast "Psycast")[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC")...) will want to get rid of it as soon as possible, while a [hussar](/wiki/Hussar "Hussar")-only colony can ignore it indefinitely other than the occasional animal insanity wave. Generally speaking, unmodified baseliners will want to remove it sooner rather than later, as the starting low drone is already significant and it will only get worse. 

In order to control mood while planning and dealing with the threat, lavish meals and mood boosting drugs can prove useful. For highly sensitive pawns, or just about anyone how may be at risk of mental breaks, having psychic foil helmets may be a good treatment while the droner is active. [Mind-numb serum](/wiki/Mind-numb_serum "Mind-numb serum")[![Content added by the Anomaly DLC](/images/thumb/3/32/AnomalyIcon.png/16px-AnomalyIcon.png)](/wiki/Anomaly_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Anomaly DLC") can make the mood threat much more manageable without reducing the psychic sensitivity of psycasters. 

The dropped advanced component is a notable reward early on. Since crashed ship part incidents are likely to have happened by the point the colony is researching advanced industrial projects, the looted components are helpful and may save a trade trip or at least some money when aiming to build things like [ground-penetrating scanners](/wiki/Ground-penetrating_scanner "Ground-penetrating scanner") before [Advanced fabrication](/wiki/Research#Advanced_fabrication "Research") is researched.

## Trivia[[edit](/index.php?title=Ship_part_\(psychic_droner\)&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Trivia")]

Crashed ship parts not only have a [Flammability](/wiki/Flammability "Flammability") of 0%, but also take no damage from attacks with the [Flame](/wiki/Damage_Types "Damage Types") damage type. This is in contrast to, for example, a [stone](/wiki/Stone "Stone") [wall](/wiki/Wall "Wall") which will not be ignited by [molotov cocktails](/wiki/Molotov_cocktails "Molotov cocktails") but will take the 10 flame damage from the initial attack.
