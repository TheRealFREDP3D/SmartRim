**Cloth** is a basic plant-based [fabric](/wiki/Fabric "Fabric") that can be grown by planting the [cotton plant](/wiki/Cotton_plant "Cotton plant"). It can be used to create [clothing](/wiki/Clothing "Clothing") and a few specialty items like [carpets](/wiki/Carpet "Carpet"). It is 20% more flammable than most other textiles.

## Acquisition[[edit](/index.php?title=Cloth&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Acquisition")]

Cloth is primarily obtained by growing the [cotton plants](/wiki/Cotton_plant "Cotton plant") in a [growing zone](/wiki/Growing_zone "Growing zone"). Each mature plant yields ![Cloth](/images/thumb/8/80/Cloth_b.png/16px-Cloth_b.png) 10 light gray cloth, depending on the harvester's [Plant Harvest Yield](/wiki/Plant_Harvest_Yield "Plant Harvest Yield") stat and the storyteller's [Harvest Yield](/wiki/AI_Storytellers#Harvest_Yield "AI Storytellers") setting, which varies by difficulty. 

Alternatively, it can also be acquired through [trading](/wiki/Trading "Trading") or the [cargo pods](/wiki/Cargo_pods "Cargo pods") event. Note that colored cloth can only be bought, not grown, crafted, or [dyed](/wiki/Dye "Dye").

### Color[[edit](/index.php?title=Cloth&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Color")]

While players can only grow cotton to harvest light grey cloth, bulk goods traders or faction bases offer coloured cloth for sale. Using different colors of cloth changes the color of some[Which?] item(s) crafted with it. E.g. [flak vests](/wiki/Flak_vest "Flak vest") will be the same color as the cloth used to make it. 

## Analysis[[edit](/index.php?title=Cloth&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Analysis")]

Any analysis for cloth needs to be broken down into two main sections; using it as a material for stuffable items and using it for the items it is required for. 

### As stuff[[edit](/index.php?title=Cloth&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: As stuff")]

Cloth is the worst protective textile in the game, with the weakest sharp and heat armor of all textiles, and _zero_ blunt protection (a trait shared with [wools](/wiki/Wool "Wool")). Cloth is alright against temperature, being 9th place against heat, and 17th against cold. 

Cloth's main redeeming quality is that it comes from a [plant](/wiki/Cotton_plant "Cotton plant"), so it doesn't rely on animals or traders. In a temperate or tropical biome, colonies can easily farm cotton on a large scale, useful for the early and mid-game. Any clothing at all may be used to prevent negative [moodlets](/wiki/Mood "Mood") for [tattered](/wiki/Tattered "Tattered") or [nonexistent](/wiki/Mood#Naked "Mood") clothes, and cloth at least gives decent temperature regulation. As [furniture](/wiki/Furniture "Furniture"), cloth provides no [beauty](/wiki/Beauty "Beauty") value, but its ease of production make it useful for bulk projects. Just watch out for fire, as cloth is a very flammable substance. 

### Unique recipes[[edit](/index.php?title=Cloth&action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: Unique recipes")]

Cloth also has another purpose: it is required to create items like [medicine](/wiki/Medicine "Medicine"), [carpets](/wiki/Carpets "Carpets"), [flak vests](/wiki/Flak_vest "Flak vest"), and [poker tables](/wiki/Poker_table "Poker table"). No other textile may be used to create such items. 

This means that despite its poor utility as stuff, very few colonies will forgo using cloth entirely. Even in colonies where grow space is limited, a small [growing zone](/wiki/Growing_zone "Growing zone") or a few [hydroponics basins](/wiki/Hydroponics_basin "Hydroponics basin") dedicated to its production allows a colony to transition to key mid and late game technologies. For colonies engaging significantly with trade, purchasing is also feasible if less reliable.
