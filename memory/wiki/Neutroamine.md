**Neutroamine** is an item that cannot be crafted and must be sourced externally. It can be combined with [herbal medicine](/wiki/Herbal_medicine "Herbal medicine") and [cloth](/wiki/Cloth "Cloth") to create standard [medicine](/wiki/Medicine "Medicine"), or synthesize various [drugs](/wiki/Drugs "Drugs") with it at a [drug lab](/wiki/Drug_lab "Drug lab"), such as [Penoxycyline](/wiki/Penoxycyline "Penoxycyline") and [wake-up](/wiki/Wake-up "Wake-up").

## Acquisition[[edit](/index.php?title=Neutroamine&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Acquisition")]

Neutroamine cannot be crafted, instead the primary source is [trade](/wiki/Trade "Trade") with [orbital traders](/wiki/Orbital_trade_beacon "Orbital trade beacon") and [Outlander](/wiki/Outlander "Outlander") settlements and caravans. 

Alternatively, it can also be acquired, albeit unreliably, through: 

  * Looting [raiders](/wiki/Raiders "Raiders") that attack your colony.
  * Raiding other [faction bases](/wiki/Faction_base "Faction base").
  * Cargo pod [event](/wiki/Event "Event").
  * In [ancient dangers](/wiki/Ancient_danger "Ancient danger").

These are the key insights: 

  * **The[medicine](/wiki/Medicine "Medicine") recipe loses money.** Even if cloth and herbal medicine are grown rather than purchased, a proper comparison is the cost of buying [![Medicine](/images/thumb/7/79/Medicine_industrial_a.png/16px-Medicine_industrial_a.png)](/wiki/Medicine "Medicine") 1 medicine and selling [![Cloth](/images/thumb/8/80/Cloth_b.png/16px-Cloth_b.png)](/wiki/Cloth "Cloth") 3 cloth and [![Herbal medicine](/images/thumb/a/af/Herbal_medicine.png/16px-Herbal_medicine.png)](/wiki/Herbal_medicine "Herbal medicine") 1 herbal medicine vs buying ![Neutroamine](/images/thumb/3/3c/Neutroamine_c.png/16px-Neutroamine_c.png) 2 neutroamine. Assuming no [trade price improvement](/wiki/Trade_price_improvement "Trade price improvement"), this works out to [![Silver](/images/thumb/5/57/Silver_c.png/16px-Silver_c.png)](/wiki/Silver "Silver") -16.5 silver for selling cloth and herbal medicine and buying medicine vs [![Silver](/images/thumb/5/57/Silver_c.png/16px-Silver_c.png)](/wiki/Silver "Silver") -16.8 silver for buying the neutroamine. This doesn't really change with trade price improvements since then you'll be better of crafting the cloth into valuable items for sale. It might, however, be useful to have a small stockpile of neutroamine if proper medicine is needed urgently.
  * **For all other recipes it's generally better to buy the ingredients instead of the product.** Especially when some ingredients are already at hand.
  * **The best way to make profit out of neutroamine is[wake up](/wiki/Wake_up "Wake up").** It takes no additional ingredients and even with no trade price improvement it nets [![Silver](/images/thumb/5/57/Silver_c.png/16px-Silver_c.png)](/wiki/Silver "Silver") 4.2 silver for every wake up crafted out of bought neutroamine.
  * **[![Content added by the Anomaly DLC](/images/thumb/3/32/AnomalyIcon.png/16px-AnomalyIcon.png)](/wiki/Anomaly_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Anomaly DLC")With extra bioferrite at hand, crafting[voidsight serum](/wiki/Voidsight_serum "Voidsight serum") is the most profitable.** Since you still need to buy the neutroamine, crafting the bioferrite into something else is preferable, however.

## Trivia[[edit](/index.php?title=Neutroamine&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Trivia")]

The use of neutroamine as an intermediary element to synthesize a different drug is inspired by the use of methylamine to create methamphetamine in the show "[Breaking Bad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breaking_Bad)". [1]
