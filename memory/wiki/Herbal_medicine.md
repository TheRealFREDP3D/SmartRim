**Herbal medicine** is a natural [medicine](/wiki/Medicine_\(disambiguation\) "Medicine \(disambiguation\)") used in [doctoring](/wiki/Doctoring "Doctoring") to improve the results of medical treatment over not using medicine at all, but is [significantly less effective](/wiki/Medical_Potency "Medical Potency") than [industrial-tech](/wiki/Medicine "Medicine") and [glitterworld medicine](/wiki/Glitterworld_medicine "Glitterworld medicine").

## Acquisition[[edit](/index.php?title=Herbal_medicine&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Acquisition")]

Herbal medicine can be harvested from [healroot](/wiki/Healroot "Healroot") and [wild healroot](/wiki/Wild_healroot "Wild healroot"), purchased from neolithic [traders](/wiki/Trade "Trade"), or found on [Tribal](/wiki/Tribal "Tribal") pawns. 

A [gauranlen tree](/wiki/Gauranlen_tree "Gauranlen tree")[![Content added by the Ideology DLC](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/16px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Content added by the Ideology DLC") [dryad](/wiki/Dryad "Dryad") that has specialized into the [Medicinemaker dryad](/wiki/Medicinemaker_dryad "Medicinemaker dryad") caste will create four units of herbal medicine every two days. 

## Summary[[edit](/index.php?title=Herbal_medicine&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Summary")]

[![StumpChoppedA \(cropped\).png](/images/thumb/6/6a/StumpChoppedA_%28cropped%29.png/40px-StumpChoppedA_%28cropped%29.png)](/wiki/Category:Stubs "Category:Stubs") | **This article is a[stub](/wiki/Category:Stubs "Category:Stubs").** You can help RimWorld Wiki by **[expanding it](https://rimworldwiki.com/index.php?title=Herbal_medicine&action=edit). Any form of medicine can be used whenever a [doctor](/wiki/Doctoring "Doctoring") tends to an [injury](/wiki/Injury "Injury") or [disease](/wiki/Disease "Disease"). In most cases, medicine does not directly cause healing. Instead, using medicine and better types of medicine will increase [tend quality](/wiki/Tend_quality "Tend quality"), increasing the [rate of healing](/wiki/Injury#Healing_rate "Injury") for injuries, or slowing down progression of a disease. For [gut worms](/wiki/Gut_worms "Gut worms") and [muscle parasites](/wiki/Muscle_parasites "Muscle parasites"), tend quality is directly used to cure the disease. 

With a Medical Potency of 60%, herbal medicine has a 60% multiplier on both [tend quality](/wiki/Tend_quality "Tend quality") and [surgery success](/wiki/Doctoring#Operation_Success_Rate "Doctoring"). Herbal medicine has a maximum tend quality of 70%. 

In addition, herbal medicine will spoil in 150 days, or 2.5 years, if not refrigerated. See [Temperature](/wiki/Temperature#Temperature_thresholds "Temperature") for details. It will also [deteriorate](/wiki/Deterioration "Deterioration") when left outdoors, under the usual conditions. 

### Experience[[edit](/index.php?title=Herbal_medicine&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Experience")]

The amount of XP gained from tending depends on the type of medicine used, and what it is used on (human or animal). The equation for this is: 

XP = Patient XP Factor × Medicine XP Factor × Doctor's Learning Rate   
---  
  
Where: 

  * Patient XP Factor is a multiplier from the patient type. ×175 for animal patients, and ×500 for human patients.
  * Medicine XP Factor is the medicine's potency multiplied by 0.7, and clamped in the range of 0.5 and 1.
  * Doctor's Learning Rate is the doctor's own multiplier on XP gained for the medicine skill. See "[Improving Skills](/wiki/Skills#Improving_skills "Skills")" for more details.

## Analysis[[edit](/index.php?title=Herbal_medicine&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Analysis")]

Herbal medicine is a cost-efficient choice for treating injuries and illnesses. With a good doctor, herbal medicine will be sufficient to treat almost all [diseases](/wiki/Disease "Disease"), where its maximum tend quality of 70% will suffice. Even if you don't have a good doctor, if a patient is sufficiently far ahead of their disease, then herbal can be used to save industrial medicine. 

It often isn't practical to use herbal medicine for surgery, as a 60% medical potency means surgery has a greater chance to fail. But in the right setup and significant investment, it is possible to reach the 98% [surgery success chance cap](/wiki/Surgery "Surgery"), even with herbal medicine. For example, the following conditions give 98% success with herbal medicine: 

  * Surgeon with 19 [Medical](/wiki/Medical "Medical") skill, and with (at least) 125% [Manipulation](/wiki/Manipulation "Manipulation") and 100% [Sight](/wiki/Sight "Sight")
  * [Sterile room](/wiki/Sterile_tile "Sterile tile") (0.6 Cleanliness) that is well-[lit](/wiki/Light "Light")
  * [Hospital bed](/wiki/Hospital_bed "Hospital bed") of _excellent[quality](/wiki/Quality "Quality") or higher_



For reference, [medicine](/wiki/Medicine "Medicine") would allow a skill 8, manipulation 100% surgeon to perform with the same failure chance. 

Note that some surgeries, like [carcinoma](/wiki/Carcinoma "Carcinoma") removal, are more difficult than usual. Others, like [ovum](/wiki/Ovum "Ovum") extraction,[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC") are much easier and require less stringent conditions. It is also possible to use herbal medicine for [prisoners](/wiki/Prisoner "Prisoner") and other pawns you don't care about, even if you don't reach the surgery success cap.
