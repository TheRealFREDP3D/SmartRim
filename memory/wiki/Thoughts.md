## Room stats[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=11 "Edit section: Room stats")]

These thoughts occur when a colonist personally uses a room (and not just passes through it). [Mods](/wiki/Mods "Mods") can create additional moods that are triggered by other room types, but in vanilla the only rooms are: 

  * Bedroom/Barracks
  * Rec(reation) room
  * Dining room
  * Prison cell
  * Hospital
  * Throne room[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC") ([Royalty DLC](/wiki/Royalty_\(DLC\) "Royalty \(DLC\)") only)



Note that most of these can be combined with others, although bedroom/barracks are mutually exclusive. 

Also, a general "[space](/wiki/Space "Space")" thought can be triggered where a colonist spends much of their time, based on the size of that room. 

#### Intensities[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=12 "Edit section: Intensities")]

Each of these thoughts have a range of intensities, listed here from worst to best. This is dependent on the room's [Impressiveness](/wiki/Impressiveness "Impressiveness") stat. Most rooms don't have the full range listed. 

  1. Awful
  2. Dull
  3. Mediocre
  4. Decent
  5. Slightly impressive
  6. Somewhat Impressive (/ Impressive*)
  7. Very impressive
  8. Extremely impressive
  9. Unbelievably impressive
  10. Wondrously impressive



    

    _(* As listed in Mood effects for "bedrooms", "cells" and "barracks", below, this is simply "Impressive".)_

These thoughts are given by (only) these rooms. If a room fills more than one definition, it can give multiple thoughts: 

  * **Bedrooms** : When a colonist is designated to sleep alone (or with a lover*) in a room. 
    * Full range except Dull.
    * Somewhat Impressive is listed as simply "Impressive"
  * **Barracks** : When multiple* colonists are designated to sleep in the same room. 
    * Full range.
    * Somewhat Impressive is listed as simply "Impressive"
    * As a rule of thumb, a poor quality barracks gives a -5 to mood vs. the equivalent private bedroom, higher quality barracks give -4.
  * **Dining room** : When a colonist eats in a room. This can simply be any room with a table and chair. 
    * Starts from Decent.
  * **Rec room** : When a colonist uses Recreation facilities in a room. 
    * Starts from Decent.
  * **Prison cell** : When a colonist sleeps alone in a room with a bed/spot designated as a prison bed. 
    * Full range except Dull and Mediocre.
    * Somewhat Impressive is listed as simply "Impressive"
  * **Prison barracks** : When multiple colonists sleeps together in a room with beds/spots designated as prison beds. 
    * Full range except Mediocre and Decent.
    * Somewhat Impressive is listed as simply "Impressive"
  * **Hospital** : When a colonist stays at a hospital due to injury, illness or surgery. 
    * Full range except Mediocre and Decent.



    

    _(* A pair of colonists who are[lovers](/wiki/Romance "Romance") and sleep in the same bed do not count against each other when defining a barracks.)_

## Thought details[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=26 "Edit section: Thought details")]

#### Justified execution[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=27 "Edit section: Justified execution")]

This thought occurs in every colonist when a "guilty" [prisoner](/wiki/Prisoner "Prisoner") is killed by a colonist. A prisoner is guilty for 24 hours after attacking the colony, killing/downing a colonist or attempting escape. 

#### Someone was organ-harvested[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=28 "Edit section: Someone was organ-harvested")]

This thought occurs in every colonist when an organ is harvested from a prisoner or guest. This does not occur when the organ is harvested by the installation of a replacement, such as a [bionic heart](/wiki/Bionic_heart "Bionic heart"), nor does it happen when removing an artificial body part. 

#### Justified execution of colonist[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=29 "Edit section: Justified execution of colonist")]

This thought occurs in every colonist when a "guilty" colonist that is held prisoner is subsequently killed by another colonist. A prisoner is guilty for 24 hours after attacking the colony, killing/downing a colonist or attempting escape. 

#### Colonist organ-harvested[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=30 "Edit section: Colonist organ-harvested")]

This thought occurs in every colonist when an organ is harvested from a colonist. This does not occur when the organ is harvested by the installation of a replacement, such as a [bionic heart](/wiki/Bionic_heart "Bionic heart"), nor does it happen when removing an artificial body part. 

#### A prisoner was sold[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=31 "Edit section: A prisoner was sold")]

This thought occurs in every colonist when a prisoner is sold into slavery. 

#### Ate fine meal[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=32 "Edit section: Ate fine meal")]

This thought occurs when a humanoid eats a [fine meal](/wiki/Meals "Meals"). 

#### Ate lavish meal[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=33 "Edit section: Ate lavish meal")]

This thought occurs when a humanoid eats a [lavish meal](/wiki/Meals "Meals"). 

#### Ate nutrient paste meal[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=34 "Edit section: Ate nutrient paste meal")]

This thought occurs when a humanoid eats a [nutrient paste meal](/wiki/Nutrient_paste_meal "Nutrient paste meal"). 

#### Ate without table[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=35 "Edit section: Ate without table")]

This thought occurs when a humanoid eats without a table. 

It occurs frequently even if you have tables, for many times colonists aren't bothered to go long distances to a table to eat and will simply eat off the floor instead. This can be avoided by watching pawns who are far from base, and when they pull out food and start to eat, draft and immediately undraft them, then have them haul the dropped food back to storage. 

#### Ate raw food[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=36 "Edit section: Ate raw food")]

This thought occurs when a humanoid eats [raw food](/wiki/Raw_food "Raw food"). 

#### [Beauty](/wiki/Beauty "Beauty")[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=37 "Edit section: Beauty")]

These thoughts occur when a human's environment opinion rises above or falls below certain values. 

  * Hideous environment: When their opinion dips below 15.
  * Very ugly environment: When their opinion dips below 30.
  * Ugly environment: When their opinion dips below 42.
  * Pleasant environment: When their opinion rises above 58.
  * Very pleasant environment: when their opinion rises above 70.
  * Gorgeous environment: When their opinion rises above 85.



#### Bionics[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=38 "Edit section: Bionics")]

These thoughts occur when a pawn with specific traits has bionic body parts installed on them. 

For Body modder pawns: 

  * Body modder frustrated: no parts.
  * Body modder pleased: 1 part.
  * Body modder quite pleased: 2 parts.
  * Body modder delighted: 3 parts.
  * Body modder enchanted: 4 parts.
  * Body modder overjoyed: 5 parts.
  * Machine body is complete: 6 or more parts.



For Body purist pawns: 

  * Body purist squeamish: 1 part.
  * Body purist disgusted: 2 parts.
  * Body purist violated: 3 parts.
  * Body purist horrified: 4 parts.
  * Body purist nightmare: 5 parts.
  * I have become what I hate: 6 or more parts.



#### Cabin fever (outdated information)[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=39 "Edit section: Cabin fever \(outdated information\)")]

This thought occurs when a humanoid has spent a great amount of time inside. It has 2 stages: 

  * Mild: more than 140000 ticks (7 game days) inside.
  * Serious: more than 280000 ticks (14 game days) inside.



The humanoid does not take on this thought when incapacitated (whether indoors or not) or imprisoned, but the timer for ticks inside is still active. 

#### Cannibalism[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=40 "Edit section: Cannibalism")]

This thought occurs when a humanoid eats [human meat](/wiki/Raw_food "Raw food") in various forms. 

  * Raw cannibalism: When the humanoid eats raw human meat, butchered or not. This happens concurrently with the 'Ate raw food' debuff.
  * Cooked cannibalism: When the humanoid eats human meat cooked into meals.



#### Catharsis[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=41 "Edit section: Catharsis")]

This thought occurs after a colonist has a [mental break](/wiki/Mood#Mental_breakdown "Mood") due to poor [mood](/wiki/Mood "Mood"). 

For this thought to occur the mental break must be allowed to run its course. Interrupting the break, by downing or arresting the affected colonist, will cause this thought to not occur. Berserk breaks are an exception, being able to grant the Catharsis buff even if interrupted. 

#### Cold[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=42 "Edit section: Cold")]

This thought occurs when the outside temperature drops below a pawn's [minimum comfortable temperature](/wiki/Minimum_comfortable_temperature "Minimum comfortable temperature"). There are 3 variations depending on how low the temperature is relative to this value 

  * Cold: 1 °C – 10 °C (1.8 °F – 18 °F) below comfort.
  * Very cold: 11 °C – 20 °C (19.8 °F – 36 °F) below comfort.
  * Shivering: 21 °C (37.8 °F) or lower below comfort.



#### My <relation> <name> died[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=43 "Edit section: My <relation> <name> died")]

This thought occurs when a colonist's family member dies. The death need not happen in the player's colony to cause this mood. Severity is dependent on the closeness of the relation - with children and spouses being highest, and relations outside of their immediate family being lower. 

#### Colonist banished[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=44 "Edit section: Colonist banished")]

This thought occurs when a colonist is banished from the colony or a caravan. 

#### Colonist left to die[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=45 "Edit section: Colonist left to die")]

This thought occurs when a colonist is abandoned from a caravan in conditions where he will most likely not survive. It usually occurs if the outside temperature is outside of the colonist's comfortable range, or the colonist is downed. 

If this colonist is a relative, friend or rival of another colonist, they will gain the thought relating to their deaths (eg "My son died" if a son is abandoned) on top of this thought. 

#### Colonist left unburied[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=46 "Edit section: Colonist left unburied")]

This thought occurs when a colonist corpse is in the open for more than 80000 ticks (4 game days). 

#### Comfort[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=47 "Edit section: Comfort")]

This thought occurs when a colonist's [comfort](/wiki/Comfort "Comfort") rises above or falls below a certain value. It has various stages: 

  * Uncomfortable: When comfort falls below 10%.
  * Comfortable: When comfort reaches 60% and above.
  * Very comfortable: When comfort reaches 70% and above.
  * Extremely comfortable: When comfort reaches 80% and above.
  * Luxuriantly comfortable: When comfort reaches 90% and above.



#### Tainted apparel[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=48 "Edit section: Tainted apparel")]

This though occurs when a pawn is wearing apparel stripped from a corpse. Mood effect ranges from -3 to -8 based on how many pieces the pawn's wearing.   
Bloodlust pawns don't gain the debuff. 

#### Defeated hostile leader[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=49 "Edit section: Defeated hostile leader")]

This occurs when the colony kills an enemy faction leader. 

#### Disturbed sleep[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=50 "Edit section: Disturbed sleep")]

This thought occurs when a humanoid or animal walks within seven squares (without walls in between) of a sleeping humanoid, or the humanoid is called to do work while sleeping. 

#### Exhaustion[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=51 "Edit section: Exhaustion")]

These thoughts occur when a humanoid's rest level dips below certain values. See [Rest](/wiki/Rest "Rest"). 

  * Drowsy: When rest level dips below 28%.
  * Tired: When rest level dips below 14%.
  * Exhausted: When rest level dips below 1%. Afterwards the pawn may collapse and start sleeping on the ground.



#### Forced to take drugs[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=52 "Edit section: Forced to take drugs")]

This thought occurs when you administer non-medical drugs to a [teetotaler](/wiki/Teetotaler "Teetotaler") using a medical operation. 

Medical drugs such as [penoxycyline](/wiki/Penoxycyline "Penoxycyline") do not trigger this thought. 

#### Hot[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=53 "Edit section: Hot")]

This thought occurs when the outside temperature is higher than the pawn's [maximum comfortable temperature](/wiki/Maximum_comfortable_temperature "Maximum comfortable temperature"). There are 4 variations depending on high the temperature is relative to this value. 

  * Hot: 1 °C – 10 °C (1.8 °F – 18 °F) above comfortable temperature.
  * Very hot: 11 °C – 20 °C (19.8 °F – 36 °F) above comfortable temperature.
  * Extremely hot: 21 °C – 30 °C (37.8 °F – 54 °F) above comfortable temperature.
  * Burning up: 31 °C (55.8 °F) or higher above comfortable temperature.



#### Human leather apparel[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=54 "Edit section: Human leather apparel")]

This though occurs when a pawn is wearing apparel made using human leather. Mood effect ranges from -3 to -8 based on how many pieces the pawn's wearing.   
Bloodlust or Cannibal pawns instead gain a mood buff from +3 to +8. 

#### Hungover (pounding)[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=55 "Edit section: Hungover \(pounding\)")]

This thought is the first of three hangover stages that occurs after a humanoid has become drunk. It has a mood effect of -10. Consciousness is reduced by 18% and is accompanied by mild vomiting. 

#### Hungover (strong)[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=56 "Edit section: Hungover \(strong\)")]

This thought is the second hangover stage that occurs after a humanoid has become drunk. It has a mood effect of -5. Consciousness is reduced by 8%. 

#### Hungover (slight)[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=57 "Edit section: Hungover \(slight\)")]

This thought is the third and final hangover stage that occurs after a humanoid has become drunk. It has a mood effect of -2. Consciousness is reduced by 3%. 

#### Hunger[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=58 "Edit section: Hunger")]

These thoughts occurs when a humanoid is hungry and needs food. There are several severity levels depending on how long the pawn hasn't had access to food. See [Saturation](/wiki/Saturation "Saturation"). 

  * Hungry: When saturation level dips below 0.3.
  * Urgently hungry: When saturation level dips below 0.2.
  * Starving: When saturation level reaches 0 and [malnutrition](/wiki/Malnutrition "Malnutrition") kicks in, which will eventually kill the colonist. 
    * Trivial: 0 - 19% severity of malnutrition.
    * Minor: 20 - 39% severity of malnutrition.
    * Moderate: 40 - 59% severity of malnutrition.
    * Severe: 60 - 79% severity of malnutrition.
    * Extreme: 80 - 100% severity of malnutrition. After this, the unfortunate pawn dies.



#### I Butchered humanlike[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=59 "Edit section: I Butchered humanlike")]

This thought occurs when a human processes a human corpse at a butcher table. 

#### Imprisoned[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=60 "Edit section: Imprisoned")]

This thought occurs when a humanoid is kept as a [Prisoner](/wiki/Prisoner "Prisoner"). 

#### Insulted[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=61 "Edit section: Insulted")]

This thought occurs when a humanoid is insulted by another humanoid. 

#### Darkness[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=62 "Edit section: Darkness")]

This thought occurs when a humanoid has spent more than 240 [ticks](/wiki/Time "Time") (4 secs) away from any light source. 

The humanoid does not take on this thought when sleeping (whether in darkness or not), but the timer for ticks away from a light source is still active while sleeping. 

#### Inebriated (warm)[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=63 "Edit section: Inebriated \(warm\)")]

This thought occurs after a humanoid consumes [beer](/wiki/Beer "Beer") and their alcohol level reaches 10%. Pain level is reduced by 10%, while manipulation is reduced by 2%. 

#### Inebriated (tipsy)[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=64 "Edit section: Inebriated \(tipsy\)")]

This thought occurs after a humanoid consumes [beer](/wiki/Beer "Beer") and their alcohol level reaches 25%. Pain level is reduced by 20%, while consciousness is reduced by 10%. 

#### Inebriated (drunk)[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=65 "Edit section: Inebriated \(drunk\)")]

This thought occurs after a humanoid consumes [beer](/wiki/Beer "Beer") and their alcohol level reaches 40%. Pain level is reduced by 50%, while consciousness is reduced by 35%, accompanied by mild vomiting. At this level it triggers a tale that may show up in an art description; and the humanoid will soon start having a hangover. 

#### Inebriated (hammered)[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=66 "Edit section: Inebriated \(hammered\)")]

This thought occurs after a humanoid consumes [beer](/wiki/Beer "Beer") and their alcohol level reaches 70%. Pain level is reduced by 70%, while consciousness is reduced by 60%, accompanied by vomiting. Consuming more beer at this stage may trigger a blackout. 

#### Joywire[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=67 "Edit section: Joywire")]

A pawn with a [joywire implant](/wiki/Joywire "Joywire") has a permanent +30 mood buff. However, the implant also permanently reduces consciousness.

#### My organ harvested[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=69 "Edit section: My organ harvested")]

This thought occurs when a pawn has an organ harvested. This stacks for every organ taken. 

#### Naked[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=70 "Edit section: Naked")]

This thought occurs when a non-nudist humanoid is not wearing any [clothing](/wiki/Clothing "Clothing"). 

#### New colony hope[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=71 "Edit section: New colony hope")]

Occurs once "New colony optimism" expires. This thought is the second stage of the start-of-game "boost" to help the newly landed colonists keep their mood up while they set up beds, furniture, decorations, and other structures. 

#### New colony optimism[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=72 "Edit section: New colony optimism")]

A starting "boost", this thought occurs at the start of the game to help the newly landed colonists keep their mood up while they set up beds, furniture, decorations, and other structures. 

#### Observed corpse[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=73 "Edit section: Observed corpse")]

This thought occurs when a corpse is visible to a colonist - not buried or incinerated.  
Corpses can be seen within a radius of 4 tiles, but not if they are being carried by someone else.  
If a pawn goes to the corpse with the intent to bury it and notices it first because of this, they will not have this thought, nor while carrying the corpse to its final resting place.  
Blind pawns (Sight 0%) can't see corpses and won't get this moodlet. 

#### Pain[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=74 "Edit section: Pain")]

These thoughts occur when a humanoid is under [pain](/wiki/Pain "Pain"). It has 4 stages depending on pain severity. 

  * Pain (little): When pain is between 1 - 14%.
  * Pain (moderate): When pain reaches 15%.
  * Pain (acute)： When pain reaches 40%.
  * Pain (shock)：When pain reaches 80%. Colonists in this state are incapacitated.



#### Passion (work)[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=75 "Edit section: Passion \(work\)")]

These thoughts occur when a pawn is doing work they have a passion for (see the bio). 

  * Minor passion: When a pawn does work they have a passion level of interested for.
  * Burning passion: When a pawn does work they have a passion level of burning for.



These work passions, especially burning, can help a colonist who does that work full time become much more likely to gain inspirations and much less likely to have mental breaks due to a much better mood most of the time. Combined with the exp boost to skills a pawn is passionate about, it makes passion in a desired field invaluable - a colonist with a moderate skill level but a burning passion is in many cases superior to a mid-high skill pawn due to the ability to become incredibly skilled in a comparatively short period of time. 

As dumb labor like hauling and cleaning are not tied to a skill, no one will ever gain happiness from them. Even thousands of years in the future, we still all have boring chores to do. 

#### Psychic effects[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=76 "Edit section: Psychic effects")]

Psychic drones occur during the [psychic ship](/wiki/Events#Psychic_ship "Events") or [psychic drone](/wiki/Events#Psychic_drone "Events") events. 

  * Low: Psychic drone event, or 0 - 2.5 days after the crash.
  * Medium: Psychic drone event, or 2.5 - 5 days after the crash.
  * High: Psychic drone event, or 5 - 7.5 days after the crash.
  * Extreme: 7.5 days or later after the crash.



Psychic soothes occur during the [psychic soothe event](/wiki/Events#Psychic_soothe "Events"). 

#### Reading[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=77 "Edit section: Reading")]

A colonist with a passion for intellectual will gain a thought while reading any [book](/wiki/Books "Books"). Similarly, any colonist with a passion for a given skill will gain a different thought while reading a related [textbook](/wiki/Textbook "Textbook"). Those 2 thoughts can be active at the same time and they are stronger for colonists with a burning passion. 

#### Recreation[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=78 "Edit section: Recreation")]

Main article: [Recreation](/wiki/Recreation "Recreation")

These thoughts occur when the recreation meter of a humanoid rises or falls below certain values. 

  * Recreation-starved: When the recreation meter is at 0%.
  * Recreation-deprived: When the recreation meter is between 1% and 14%.
  * Recreation unfulfilled: When the recreation meter is between 15% and 29%.
  * Recreation satisfied: When the recreation meter is between 70% and 84%.
  * Recreation fully satisfied: When the recreation meter is at least 85%.



#### Sick[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=79 "Edit section: Sick")]

This thought occurs when a colonist has an [illness](/wiki/Illness "Illness"). 

#### Sleeping conditions[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=80 "Edit section: Sleeping conditions")]

These thoughts occur when a humanoid sleeps in various unfavorable conditions. 

  * Slept outside: When a humanoid sleeps in an outdoor space, or an incomplete room. 
    * This thought does not occur in unroofed rooms.
  * Slept on ground: When a humanoid uses a sleeping spot or sleeps on the ground.
  * Slept in cold: When a humanoid sleeps when the temperature falls below 16 °C (60.8 °F). (clothing doesn't change value)
  * Slept in heat: When a humanoid sleeps when the temperature raises above 26 °C (78.8 °F). (clothing doesn't change value)



#### Someone's organ harvested[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=81 "Edit section: Someone's organ harvested")]

This thought occurs in every colonist, [except for the patient](/wiki/Thoughts_list#My_organ_harvested "Thoughts list") and the one performing the operation, when an organ is harvested from a prisoner, guest, or colonist. 

#### Strange feeling[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=82 "Edit section: Strange feeling")]

This thought occurs when a [psychic soothe pulser](/wiki/Psychic_soothe_pulser "Psychic soothe pulser") is used. 

#### Spacious interior[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=83 "Edit section: Spacious interior")]

This thought occurs when a humanoid has 41 or more tiles accessible within radius 7 in the current room (objects that can be walked over do not affect this - see [Space](/wiki/Space "Space")). 

#### Cramped interior[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=84 "Edit section: Cramped interior")]

This thought occurs when a humanoid has 4-10 tiles accessible within radius 7 in the current room (objects that can be walked over do not affect this - see [Space](/wiki/Space "Space")). 

#### Confined interior[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=85 "Edit section: Confined interior")]

This thought occurs when a humanoid has 3 or less tiles accessible within radius 7 in the current room (objects that can be walked over do not affect this - see [Space](/wiki/Space "Space")). 

#### Was imprisoned[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=86 "Edit section: Was imprisoned")]

This thought occurs after a [prisoner](/wiki/Prisoner "Prisoner") is released. It does not occur to newly recruited prisoners. 

#### Ratty apparel[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=87 "Edit section: Ratty apparel")]

This thought occurs when a humanoid is wearing apparel which has health between 21% and 50%. 

Quest-locked apparel, as well as certain apparels like [utility](/wiki/Utility "Utility") ones do not cause this thought. 

#### Tattered apparel[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=88 "Edit section: Tattered apparel")]

This thought occurs when a humanoid is wearing apparel which has health 20% or less. 

Quest-locked apparel, as well as certain apparels like [utility](/wiki/Utility "Utility") ones do not cause this thought. 

#### We butchered humanlike[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=89 "Edit section: We butchered humanlike")]

This thought occurs when a human corpse is processed at a butcher table. 

## Notes[[edit](/index.php?title=Thoughts&action=edit&section=90 "Edit section: Notes")]

If a colonist is resurrected using a [Resurrector mech serum](/wiki/Resurrector_mech_serum "Resurrector mech serum"), the various mood penalties from losing a spouse, friend, colonist and so forth are removed. If there were bonuses such as from a recent marriage, these will not return, either for the directly affected pawns or anybody else in the colony.[1]
