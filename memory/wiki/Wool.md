**Wools** are a class of [textiles](/wiki/Textile "Textile") obtained by [handlers](/wiki/Work#Handle "Work") shearing _tamed_ hairy [animals](/wiki/Animals "Animals"). Such animals will slowly grow wool, the progress of which can be tracked in the animal's infobox. Once they reach 100%, handlers will shear these animals and leave the wool on the ground where sheared. You can avoid excessive hauling by zoning or [penning](/wiki/Pen "Pen") your wool-bearing livestock close to your storage. You can keep track of wool development on the specimen infobox. 

Shearing an animal takes 1,700 [ticks](/wiki/Time "Time") (28.33 secs), regardless of the amount of wool produced, and only modified by the [Animal Gather Speed](/wiki/Animal_Gather_Speed "Animal Gather Speed") of the handler. 

Compared to other textiles, wools typically provide excellent cold insulation, modest heat insulation, and comparatively poor armor, while also being easy to acquire in large quantities. The wool stats, yield, and shearing interval vary between different animals.

All wools have the following modifiers in common: 

  * [Beauty Factor](/wiki/Property:Beauty_Factor "Property:Beauty Factor") of 1.5.
  * [Max Hit Points Factor](/wiki/Property:Max_Hit_Points_Factor "Property:Max Hit Points Factor") of 1.
  * [Flammability Factor](/wiki/Property:Flammability_Factor "Property:Flammability Factor") of 1.7.
  * [Work To Make Factor](/wiki/Property:Work_To_Make "Property:Work To Make") and [Work To Build Factor](/wiki/Property:Work_To_Build "Property:Work To Build") of 1.
  * [Armor - Blunt Factor](/wiki/Property:Armor_-_Blunt_Factor "Property:Armor - Blunt Factor") of 0.
  * [Armor - Heat Factor](/wiki/Property:Armor_-_Heat_Factor "Property:Armor - Heat Factor") of 1.1.



## Descriptions[[edit](/index.php?title=Wool&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Descriptions")]

[![Alpacawool.png](/images/8/8b/Alpacawool.png)](/wiki/Alpaca_wool "Alpaca wool")

**[Alpaca wool](/wiki/Alpaca_wool "Alpaca wool")**

_The remarkably soft wool of an alpaca. It is warm, protects well from heat, and fetches a high price._

**Alpaca wool** is a type of [fabric](/wiki/Fabric "Fabric") gathered by [animal handlers](/wiki/Skills#Animals "Skills") by shearing tamed [alpacas](/wiki/Alpaca "Alpaca"). Alpaca wool is a very good insulator, and is therefore recommended in clothing like [parkas](/wiki/Parka "Parka") and [tuques](/wiki/Tuque "Tuque").

[![Bisonwool.png](/images/a/a3/Bisonwool.png)](/wiki/Bison_wool "Bison wool")

**[Bison wool](/wiki/Bison_wool "Bison wool")**

_Thick and coarse wool of a bison. Quite warm._

**Bison wool** is a type of [fabric](/wiki/Fabric "Fabric") gathered by [animal handlers](/wiki/Skills#Animals "Skills") by shearing tamed [bison](/wiki/Bison "Bison").

[![Mastodonwool.png](/images/4/4e/Mastodonwool.png)](/wiki/Mastodon_wool "Mastodon wool")

**[Mastodon wool](/wiki/Mastodon_wool "Mastodon wool")[![Content added by the Odyssey DLC](/images/thumb/7/7b/OdysseyIcon.png/16px-OdysseyIcon.png)](/wiki/Odyssey_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Odyssey DLC")**

_Thick shaggy wool from a mastodon. Provides excellent insulation against the cold._

[![Megaslothwool.png](/images/3/37/Megaslothwool.png)](/wiki/Megasloth_wool "Megasloth wool")

**[Megasloth wool](/wiki/Megasloth_wool "Megasloth wool")**

_While somewhat coarse, megasloth wool's long fibers make it very strong. It is very warm._

**Megasloth wool** is a type of [fabric](/wiki/Fabric "Fabric") gathered by [animal handlers](/wiki/Skills#Animals "Skills") by shearing tamed [megasloth](/wiki/Megasloth "Megasloth"). Megasloth wool is the second warmest textile in the base game, after [guinea pig fur](/wiki/Guinea_pig_fur "Guinea pig fur") and tied with [thrumbofur](/wiki/Thrumbofur "Thrumbofur"), and is valuable for colonies in cold biomes like sea ice and ice sheet. It can be hard to obtain lots of it as it takes quite a while for megasloths to produce the wool (usually only enough to make one parka) and it is rarely sold by traders.

[![Muffalowool.png](/images/3/31/Muffalowool.png)](/wiki/Muffalo_wool "Muffalo wool")

**[Muffalo wool](/wiki/Muffalo_wool "Muffalo wool")**

_Soft, light and provides very good insulation. Muffalo wool clothes can keep the body warm even in very cold conditions._

**Muffalo wool** is a type of [fabric](/wiki/Fabric "Fabric") gathered by [animal handlers](/wiki/Skills#Animals "Skills") by shearing tamed [muffalos](/wiki/Muffalo "Muffalo").

[![Muskoxwool.png](/images/6/6e/Muskoxwool.png)](/wiki/Muskox_wool "Muskox wool")

**[Muskox wool](/wiki/Muskox_wool "Muskox wool")[![Content added by the Odyssey DLC](/images/thumb/7/7b/OdysseyIcon.png/16px-OdysseyIcon.png)](/wiki/Odyssey_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Odyssey DLC")**

_Itchy wool from a muskox. Good at keeping the cold out._

[![Sheepwool.png](/images/0/06/Sheepwool.png)](/wiki/Sheep_wool "Sheep wool")

**[Sheep wool](/wiki/Sheep_wool "Sheep wool")**

_Sheep's wool. It provides good insulation._

**Sheep wool** is a type of [fabric](/wiki/Fabric "Fabric") gathered by [animal handlers](/wiki/Skills#Animals "Skills") by shearing tamed [sheep](/wiki/Sheep "Sheep").

## Trivia[[edit](/index.php?title=Wool&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Trivia")]

Contrary to wool in real life, wool in [RimWorld](/wiki/RimWorld "RimWorld") is more flammable than [cotton](/wiki/Cloth "Cloth"), rather than less.
