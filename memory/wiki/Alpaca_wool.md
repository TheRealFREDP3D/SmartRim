**Alpaca wool** is a type of [fabric](/wiki/Fabric "Fabric") gathered by [animal handlers](/wiki/Skills#Animals "Skills") by shearing tamed [alpacas](/wiki/Alpaca "Alpaca"). Alpaca wool is a very good insulator, and is therefore recommended in clothing like [parkas](/wiki/Parka "Parka") and [tuques](/wiki/Tuque "Tuque").

## Acquisition[[edit](/index.php?title=Alpaca_wool&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Acquisition")]

Alpaca wool is acquired by shearing tamed [alpacas](/wiki/Alpaca "Alpaca") \- each alpaca can be sheared every 10 days for 45 wool, for an average of 4.5 per day. Note that the amount of wool sheared scales with the [Animal Gather Yield](/wiki/Animal_Gather_Yield "Animal Gather Yield") of the shearer. 

Shearing takes 1,700 [ticks](/wiki/Time "Time") (28.33 secs), regardless of the amount of wool produced, and only modified by the [Animal Gather Speed](/wiki/Animal_Gather_Speed "Animal Gather Speed") of the handler. 

It can also be purchased from [traders](/wiki/Trade "Trade"). 

## Usage[[edit](/index.php?title=Alpaca_wool&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Usage")]

Alpaca wool can be used as a material for [stuffable](/wiki/Stuff "Stuff") items with the [Fabric](/wiki/Fabric "Fabric") stuff tag.

## Analysis[[edit](/index.php?title=Alpaca_wool&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Analysis")]

Along with the other [wools](/wiki/Wool "Wool"), alpaca wool has the second worst protective stats of any [textile](/wiki/Textile "Textile") in the game, only beating [cloth](/wiki/Cloth "Cloth") by virtue of heat armor. But like other wools, alpaca wool excels against the cold, offering the 4th best cold insulation. In comparison, its heat insulation is around many [leathers](/wiki/Leather "Leather"), at 10th of all textiles. This makes alpaca clothes mostly used for cold insulation. Outside of the extreme cold, it may be wiser to use a less insulative, but more protective clothing. 

Where alpaca wool clothing shines is its ease of acquisition; the alpaca has the second fastest Per Day production rate for wool, while only eating as much as a [sheep](/wiki/Sheep "Sheep"). This means it can be useful for [parkas](/wiki/Parka "Parka") or other pieces of clothing which are situational. 

As [furniture](/wiki/Furniture "Furniture"), it only has a somewhat improved [beauty factor](/wiki/Property:Beauty_Factor "Property:Beauty Factor") over other common textiles, but its rapid redundancy as clothing, the ease of acquisition, and modest value makes it a solid choice for improving furniture [beauty](/wiki/Beauty "Beauty") and providing bulk textiles for construction. But like other wools in RimWorld, it is exceptionally flammable. 

## Trivia[[edit](/index.php?title=Alpaca_wool&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Trivia")]

Contrary to wool in real life, wool in [RimWorld](/wiki/RimWorld "RimWorld") is more flammable than [cotton](/wiki/Cloth "Cloth"), rather than less.
