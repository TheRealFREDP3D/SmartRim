**Ailments** are [health](/wiki/Health "Health") conditions that cannot be treated completely using medicine alone. 

"MTB" stands for "Mean Time Between", and is how it's presented in the game files. 

Some conditions can give rise to other conditions; the risk of this happening will diminish if the condition is treated. 

Most permanent ailments are curable with body part replacements or usage of the [healer mech serum](/wiki/Healer_mech_serum "Healer mech serum"). 

## Healing method table

Some injuries and health conditions will not naturally heal or cannot be immediately cured by regular treatment. In these cases, more advanced treatment options such as transplantation, artificial body parts, healer mech serums, or luciferium may help, but each has its own unique list of what it can and cannot cure.

### Asthma[[edit](/index.php?title=Ailments&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Asthma")]

A chronic health condition where inflammation causes the airways to narrow, restricting the flow of oxygen to the lungs. Unlike other chronic diseases, asthma can be contracted at almost any age, meaning it's not terribly uncommon to have an asthmatic person some time down the line. 

When not treated, it progresses by a rate of 0.25 per day, meaning it will take 2 days to reach full severity (50%). 

Good treatment will reduce the rate of progression by up to 0.8, meaning it will regress by 0.55 a day; it can be returned to its initial severity, but cannot be cured completely. 

Asthma's severity will fluctuate depending on the quality of treatment received, with a treatment quality of 32% beginning to regress the stages of asthma (the higher, the better). 

Stage | Begins at | Symptoms   
---|---|---  
**Asthma (minor)** | ≥ 0% severity | -10% part efficiency   
**Asthma (major)** | ≥ 30% severity | -30% part efficiency   
**Asthma (major)** | ≥ 45% severity | -50% part efficiency   
  
Treatment: 

  * The severity of asthma can be reduced with treatment, which can be given every 420,000 ticks (around 7 days). One treatment applies to both lungs.
  * Asthma can be cured by replacing each affected lung with a [healthy replacement](/wiki/Lung#Acquisition "Lung").



A human pawn can first get asthma at any age. 

Asthma chance   
---  
  
Asthma can also affect animals, domestic, tamed and wild. Domestic and tamed will notify you that they "need treatment", although there apparently is no downside for ignoring that request except slowing them down which may be bad for [trained](/wiki/Animals#Training "Animals") or [pack animals](/wiki/Pack_animal "Pack animal"). Similarly, asthmatic wild animals on the map can roam and eat indefinitely without treatment. In fact, wild animals with asthma are easier and slightly safer to hunt, because asthma will reduce their [move speed](/wiki/Move_speed "Move speed") to 81%, and their [manipulation](/wiki/Manipulation "Manipulation") to only 90%, which makes any counter-attack less effective.

### Carcinoma[[edit](/index.php?title=Ailments&action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: Carcinoma")]

[![StumpChoppedA \(cropped\).png](/images/thumb/6/6a/StumpChoppedA_%28cropped%29.png/40px-StumpChoppedA_%28cropped%29.png)](/wiki/Category:Stubs "Category:Stubs") | **This section is a[stub](/wiki/Category:Stubs "Category:Stubs").** You can help RimWorld Wiki by **[expanding it](https://rimworldwiki.com/index.php?title=Ailments&action=edit). Reason:** _How do the stages progress/occur and what chances and factors affects them?._  
---|---  
  
A carcinoma (or cancer) is where mutated cells uncontrollably divide to form tumors, which then 'crowd out' normal bodily cells and hinder bodily function in that area. Carcinomas can be surgically removed by a skilled doctor or, in some cases, the affected body part can be removed, either by amputation, transplantation of a healthy body part, or replacement by an [artificial body part](/wiki/Artificial_body_part "Artificial body part"). Ordinary treatment will prolong the development of a carcinoma or speed up remission, and can be done by doctors of any skill, though better treatment quality is more effective. 

A carcinoma typically starts out at 30% severity. There is a 30% chance that a carcinoma won't cause any pain whatsoever. 

A cancer has 3 stages; growing, stable and remission. 

  * When growing, it progresses by 0.003 per day, multiplied by a random factor of 0.45 - 1.65.
  * When stable, it neither grows nor regresses on its own.
  * When in remission, it regresses by 0.002 per day, multiplied by a random factor of 0.7 - 1.5.



Good treatment at around 100% tend quality can slow progression by 0.0027 per day, meaning that the carcinoma will: 

  * grow more slowly when in growing stage



    

  * some slow-growing carcinomas (something less than half) may stop or actually regress (very slowly) during their "growing" stage with good treatment



  * slowly regress when stable
  * regress quickly when in remission

Stage | Begins at | Symptoms   
---|---|---  
**Carcinoma (minor)** | ≥0% severity | 

  * Little pain (+10%)
  * -10% part efficiency

  
**Carcinoma (minor)** | ≥15% severity | 

  * Moderate pain (+20%)
  * -25% part efficiency

  
**Carcinoma (major)** | ≥40% severity | 

  * Moderate pain (+35%)
  * -50% part efficiency

  
**Carcinoma (major)** | ≥60% severity | 

  * Acute pain (+50%)
  * -80% part efficiency

  
**Carcinoma (extreme)** | ≥80% severity | 

  * Acute pain (+60%)
  * -90% part efficiency

  
**Carcinoma (extreme)** | 100% severity | 

  * Affected part is destroyed

  
  
Treatment: 

  * Treated every 240,000 [ticks](/wiki/Time "Time") (66.67 mins)
  * The carcinoma will disappear if severity reaches 0.
  * "Excise carcinoma" surgery; this needs 4 medicine of [industrial quality](/wiki/Medicine "Medicine") or above, 4,500 [ticks](/wiki/Time "Time") (1.25 mins) of work, and a doctor with a [medical](/wiki/Medical "Medical") skill of 10 or above. The surgery has a 100% base chance to succeed. If it fails, there is a 25% chance that the patient [dies](/wiki/Death "Death"). Thus, as the maximum [success chance](/wiki/Doctoring#Success_chance "Doctoring") is capped at 98%, there is always at least a 0.5% chance of death per attempt.



Carcinoma can be prevented by the [non-senescent](/wiki/Non-senescent "Non-senescent") gene[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC") or cured by [healer mech serums](/wiki/Healer_mech_serum "Healer mech serum"), [luciferium](/wiki/Luciferium "Luciferium"), the [scarless](/wiki/Scarless "Scarless") genes,[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC") [unnatural healing](/wiki/Unnatural_healing "Unnatural healing"),[![Content added by the Anomaly DLC](/images/thumb/3/32/AnomalyIcon.png/16px-AnomalyIcon.png)](/wiki/Anomaly_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Anomaly DLC") or [chronophagy](/wiki/Chronophagy "Chronophagy")[![Content added by the Anomaly DLC](/images/thumb/3/32/AnomalyIcon.png/16px-AnomalyIcon.png)](/wiki/Anomaly_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Anomaly DLC") if the pawn would de-age to 22 or below, regardless of the source of the carcinoma. 

A human pawn must be at least 22.4 years old to get carcinoma from aging, meaning it can first occur at their 23rd birthday. Carcinomas from other sources, including [toxic buildup](/wiki/Toxic_buildup "Toxic buildup") and [nuclear stomachs](/wiki/Nuclear_stomach "Nuclear stomach"),[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC") can happen at any age. ## Acute[[edit](/index.php?title=Ailments&action=edit&section=11 "Edit section: Acute")]

Some ailments arise as a result of an acute lack of food or exposure to extreme temperatures. These ailments can only be treated by addressing the underlying cause (e.g. providing food or moving to more comfortable temperatures). 

### Malnutrition[[edit](/index.php?title=Ailments&action=edit&section=12 "Edit section: Malnutrition")]

When a pawn's [food](/wiki/Eating "Eating") meter reaches 0%, they will begin to suffer from [malnutrition](/wiki/Malnutrition "Malnutrition"), shown on the health tab. When a colonist is starving they will prioritize eating over other activities, including firefighting and doctoring. 

Malnutrition severity without food will advance at an average of 17% per day. There is a variation for each pawn that will vary this by 20% in both directions, meaning a pawn may actually die of malnutrition between 4.9~7.4 days of first having symptoms. There is no stat indicating the specific rate that a pawn may die of malnutrition, but the modified rate is determined for each specific pawn in a given playthrough.

### Heatstroke[[edit](/index.php?title=Ailments&action=edit&section=14 "Edit section: Heatstroke")]

Heat stroke occurs when a pawn has prolonged exposure to [temperatures](/wiki/Temperature "Temperature") 10°C (18°F) above their [maximum comfortable temperature](/wiki/Maximum_comfortable_temperature "Maximum comfortable temperature"), and recovery occurs in temperatures less than the maximum comfortable temperature. Note that it is possible for a pawn to have both heatstroke and hypothermia at the same time if time is spent in both extreme heat and cold - their severities are unrelated. 

Pawns additionally take periodic burn damage in temperatures more than 150°C (270°F) above their maximum comfortable temperature. 

#### Severity Increase[[edit](/index.php?title=Ailments&action=edit&section=15 "Edit section: Severity Increase")]

The procedure for determining severity growth every 60-tick interval, **SG60** for short, is given by: 

  1. Take the amount by which the ambient temperature exceeds the pawn's maximum safe temperature (which is the maximum comfortable temperature +10°C).
  2. Pass the amount through the curve shown below to obtain the effective temperature excess. 
     1. Note that for amounts from 0 to 25 °C, this doesn't result in a change.
  3. Multiply the excess by `6.45e-5` to obtain the severity growth this interval (60 ticks, 1 second).
  4. If the growth is less than `0.000375`, set it to that number. This sets a minimum amount the severity increases by for temperatures in the range of 10 to 15.814°C above the maximum comfortable temperature.

    `_where_ effective_temperature_curve()_is a post-processing curve with points_ : (0, 0), (25, 25), (50, 40), (100, 60), (200, 80), (400, 100), (4000, 1000).`

#### Severity Decrease[[edit](/index.php?title=Ailments&action=edit&section=16 "Edit section: Severity Decrease")]

If the ambient temperature is less than the maximum comfortable temperature, the amount the severity will decrease every 60-tick interval is given by: 

    `Decrease in heatstroke severity every 60 ticks = max(0.0015, min(0.015, 0.027 × heatstroke_severity))`

This means that as long as the ambient temperature is less than the maximum comfortable temperature, heatstroke recovery is independent of temperature. Severity decreases at a constant rate of 1.5% when at 55.6% or above, slowing down until reaching its slowest (0.15%) at 5.6% and below. Full recovery from near-100% severity occurs after 151 real-time seconds, or 3.6 in-game hours.

### Hypothermia[[edit](/index.php?title=Ailments&action=edit&section=18 "Edit section: Hypothermia")]

Hypothermia occurs when a pawn has prolonged exposure to [temperatures](/wiki/Temperature "Temperature") 10°C (18°F) below their [minimum comfortable temperature](/wiki/Minimum_comfortable_temperature "Minimum comfortable temperature"). [Insectoids](/wiki/Insectoids "Insectoids") don't experience hypothermia, but instead get [hypothermic slowdown](/wiki/Hypothermic_slowdown "Hypothermic slowdown"). 

Note that it is possible for a pawn to have both heatstroke and hypothermia at the same time if time is spent in both extreme heat and cold - their severities are unrelated. 

The rate of severity growth depends on the difference below the pawn's minimum safe temperature and the ambient temperature. The specific algorithm is: 

  1. Take the amount by which the pawn's minimum safe temperature (which is the [minimum comfortable temperature](/wiki/Minimum_comfortable_temperature "Minimum comfortable temperature") -10°C) exceeds the ambient temperature.
  2. Multiply the excess by `6.45e-5` to obtain the severity growth this interval. Note that unlike hyperthermia, hypothermia calculations don't use a postprocessing curve.
  3. If the growth is less than `0.00075`, set it to that number. As a result, temperatures between 10°C and 21.63°C less than the minimum comfortable temperature all have the same severity growth.



Expressed as a formula, this is: 

    `Increase in hypothermia severity every 60 ticks = max(0.00075, (_degrees_below_comfortable_ - 10)*0.0000645)`

### Vacuum exposure[[edit](/index.php?title=Ailments&action=edit&section=20 "Edit section: Vacuum exposure")]

[![OdysseyIcon.png](/images/thumb/7/7b/OdysseyIcon.png/40px-OdysseyIcon.png)](/wiki/Odyssey_\(DLC\) "Odyssey \(DLC\)") | **This section relates to content added by[Odyssey (DLC)](/wiki/Odyssey_\(DLC\) "Odyssey \(DLC\)").** Please note that it will not be present without the [DLC](/wiki/Downloadable_content "Downloadable content") enabled.  
---|---  
  
Vacuum exposure occurs when a pawn is subjected to [hard vacuum](/wiki/Orbit "Orbit") without adequate protection. 

The rate of severity growth is directly proportional to the vacuum percentage of the pawn's current tile, and to the difference between 1 and a pawn's [vacuum resistance](/wiki/Vacuum_resistance "Vacuum resistance") stat. Vacuum will be 100% outside pressurized rooms; otherwise, it will vary depending on the presence of [oxygen pumps](/wiki/Oxygen_pump "Oxygen pump"), open connections to other rooms, and so on. The specific algorithm is: 

  1. Start with a default of 2% per second.
  2. Multiply the amount by the tile's current vacuum percentage.
  3. Multiply the product by the difference between 1 and the pawn's vacuum resistance.



Expressed as a formula, the increase of severity every 60 [ticks](/wiki/Time "Time") (1 sec) is: 

    `Increase in vacuum exposure severity = 0.02 * tile vacuum percentage * (1 - vacuum resistance)`

Vacuum exposure builds up extremely quickly in pawns without significant vacuum resistance. An unprotected pawn with no resistance will die in just 3,000 [ticks](/wiki/Time "Time") (50 secs) of exposure to a complete vacuum, making even brief spacewalks a risky prospect. 

Pawns recover quickly from vacuum exposure while inside a pressurized area; severity will simply fall at a flat 10% per second until the condition disappears entirely. Affected pawns that reach 100% vacuum resistance while still exposed (such as by equipping a [vacsuit](/wiki/Vacsuit "Vacsuit")) will keep their current level of exposure until they return to a pressurized tile, but its severity will not increase any further.

A [Ghoul](/wiki/Ghoul "Ghoul")[![Content added by the Anomaly DLC](/images/thumb/3/32/AnomalyIcon.png/16px-AnomalyIcon.png)](/wiki/Anomaly_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Anomaly DLC") or a humans with the [Scarless](/wiki/Scarless "Scarless")[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC") gene will not get brain scars from damage to their brain, but they still have a chance to become trauma savants. Trauma savant negates the [consciousness](/wiki/Consciousness "Consciousness") penalty from all brain damage, including scars that existed before the condition. On another hand, those brain scars will still lower the brain's health and can potentially still cause pain. Trauma savant can be healed with a [healer mech serum](/wiki/Healer_mech_serum "Healer mech serum") or the [Unnatural healing](/wiki/Unnatural_healing "Unnatural healing")[![Content added by the Anomaly DLC](/images/thumb/3/32/AnomalyIcon.png/16px-AnomalyIcon.png)](/wiki/Anomaly_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Anomaly DLC") ability (or possibly through other means as well). ### Food poisoning[[edit](/index.php?title=Ailments&action=edit&section=30 "Edit section: Food poisoning")]

Occurs after eating a contaminated meal or occasionally from raw food. The chance of contamination for cooked meals is determined by the [cleanliness of the room](/wiki/Cleanliness "Cleanliness") where it was prepared and cook's [food poison chance](/wiki/Food_poison_chance "Food poison chance") stat. 

[Corpses](/wiki/Corpse "Corpse") have a flat chance of 5% to give food poisoning. Other raw food has a flat chance in between 1% and 4% depending on the type of food — most raw food having a 2% chance. Cooked meals roll two separate probabilities to determine if the food is poisoned. The first checks the [cleanliness](/wiki/Cleanliness "Cleanliness") of the kitchen used. See the accompanying graph for specifics. Note that cleanliness above -2 prevents this roll from producing poisoned meals. If the cooking station is outdoors, the default chance is 2%. 

If the first roll fails to poison the meal, then a second roll is performed, this time based on the [food poison chance](/wiki/Food_poison_chance "Food poison chance") of the pawn, controlled entirely by their [cooking skill](/wiki/Cooking "Cooking"). If the second roll indicates that the meal is poisonous, the probability of poisoning the pawn is 100% for that individual meal. However, when the poisonous meal is part of a stack with other meals, the probability is distributed among all the meals in the stack. This distribution reduces the chance of food poisoning from 100% to the ratio of total meals to poisonous meals within the stack. For instance, if a poisonous meal is placed on top of a stack containing three non-poisonous meals, the probability for each meal to poison the pawn becomes 25%. Consequently, even if the original meal was poisonous, there is a possibility that no pawns will experience food poisoning. 

If food poisoning is applied when cooking a recipe that produces multiple items (such as 4×[![Simple meal](/images/thumb/8/84/Simple_meal_a.png/16px-Simple_meal_a.png)](/wiki/Simple_meal "Simple meal") or 16×[![Pemmican](/images/thumb/d/d0/Pemmican_c.png/16px-Pemmican_c.png)](/wiki/Pemmican "Pemmican")), every item cooked in that batch becomes poisoned.

Treatment and Prevention

Food poisoning can only be minimized by manipulating the factors that cause it mentioned above. Prevention can only be guaranteed by exclusively consuming meals purchased from traders or by making [Baby food](/wiki/Baby_food "Baby food") or [nutrient paste meals](/wiki/Nutrient_paste_meal "Nutrient paste meal") as they will never cause food poisoning, or by installing a [sterilizing](/wiki/Sterilizing_stomach "Sterilizing stomach"),[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC") [nuclear stomach](/wiki/Nuclear_stomach "Nuclear stomach"),[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC") or [fleshmass stomach](/wiki/Fleshmass_stomach "Fleshmass stomach"),[![Content added by the Anomaly DLC](/images/thumb/3/32/AnomalyIcon.png/16px-AnomalyIcon.png)](/wiki/Anomaly_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Anomaly DLC") or having the [strong stomach](/wiki/Strong_stomach "Strong stomach") gene[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC") which render pawns immune. Note that [bionic stomachs](/wiki/Bionic_stomach "Bionic stomach") reduce the chance of getting food poisoning by ×50% but cannot outright prevent it. 

The notification for a colonist being poisoned will explain the cause of the poisoning. This can be very helpful if an unattended kitchen was allowed to cross into the very dirty −2 cleanliness danger zone, as a nearby cleaner can simply be ordered to clean the kitchen and avoid more unnecessary poisoning. 

There is no practical treatment for food poisoning, and generally it should be left to run its course. In Core, only a [healer mech serum](/wiki/Healer_mech_serum "Healer mech serum") will cure it, assuming there is nothing worse to cure, but this costly measure is rarely worthwhile as food poisoning only affects the pawn for the 24-hour cycle and then leaves them as healthy as they were before. Keep in mind, however, that because of the penalty to [blood filtration](/wiki/Blood_filtration "Blood filtration") and [consciousness](/wiki/Consciousness "Consciousness"), food poisoning in combination with other ailments such as an [infection](/wiki/Infection "Infection") can become much more debilitating or even fatal, and this may make using a serum worthwhile. 

With the [Anomaly DLC](/wiki/Anomaly_DLC "Anomaly DLC"), [Unnatural healing](/wiki/Unnatural_healing "Unnatural healing")[![Content added by the Anomaly DLC](/images/thumb/3/32/AnomalyIcon.png/16px-AnomalyIcon.png)](/wiki/Anomaly_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Anomaly DLC") is the vastly more economical option, and can sometimes be worth using to get a useful pawn back on their feet even outside of life-threatening situations, though the 6-day cooldown carries a notable opportunity cost. 

Because of the high chance of [vomiting](/wiki/Vomiting "Vomiting") at any moment, which interrupts and resets the process of eating, combined with the reduction in eating speed, patients with food poisoning may be at risk of developing [malnutrition](/wiki/Malnutrition "Malnutrition"). With the already impaired consciousness and moving capacity from the poisoning, stacking malnutrition on top of that could lead to a [downed](/wiki/Downed "Downed") state, so it may be worth supervising poisoned pawns in case they need rescue. A diet of small unit foods such as [pemmican](/wiki/Pemmican "Pemmican") will not mitigate this issue, as there is no concept of incremental eating - a pawn takes the same time to consume one pemmican as it takes to consume 18 pemmican, and if interrupted part way through, all the pemmican pieces remain uneaten.

Alternatively, some attacks cause instantaneous increases in toxic buildup severity, such as [cobra](/wiki/Cobra "Cobra") and [waste rat](/wiki/Waste_rat "Waste rat") [![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC") bites, the [venom talon](/wiki/Venom_talon "Venom talon"),[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC") or [venom fangs](/wiki/Venom_fangs "Venom fangs").[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC")

A colonist under a roof is protected from toxic fallout and tox rain, and avoiding interaction with the other sources can prevent buildup from them. If they are exposed to toxic fallout, they will accumulate Toxic Buildup at a rate of 40% per day. Pollution will accumulate at the same rate, but cannot be mitigated through roofing. When walking through polluted terrain in [caravans](/wiki/Caravan "Caravan"), they will accumulate buildup at 20% per day in Moderately Polluted terrain (50%-75% polluted tiles) and 40% per day in Extremely Polluted terrain (above 75%). Buildup from both pollution as well as toxic fallout can stack, meaning a pawn standing in polluted terrain while a toxic fallout can gain 80%/day.[Rate from tox rain?]

There are two stats that reduce toxic build up stats: 

  * All sources of toxic buildup are affected by the [Toxic Resistance](/wiki/Toxic_Resistance "Toxic Resistance") [stat](/wiki/Stat "Stat"). [Humans](/wiki/Human "Human") buildup at the full rate, [animals](/wiki/Animal "Animal") at half, and [insects](/wiki/Insects "Insects") and [mechanoids](/wiki/Mechanoids "Mechanoids") are immune.[Body Size?] Some ways of increasing this stat are the [detoxifier kidney](/wiki/Detoxifier_kidney "Detoxifier kidney")[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC") and [tox resistance](/wiki/Tox_resistance "Tox resistance") [gene](/wiki/Gene "Gene").[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC")
  * [Toxic Environment Resistance](/wiki/Toxic_Environment_Resistance "Toxic Environment Resistance") protects against all sources of buildup except for direct attacks like cobra bites. Some ways of increasing this stat are the [face mask](/wiki/Face_mask "Face mask"), [gas mask](/wiki/Gas_mask "Gas mask"),[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC") and [detoxifier lung](/wiki/Detoxifier_lung "Detoxifier lung").[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC")

Toxic Resistance and Toxic Environment Resistance will multiplicatively stack with each other, so if a human has 50% in both stats, the effective rate of buildup from environmental sources is 25%. 

The buildup severity occurs in stages. The later stages cause permanent dementia which does not wear off even after toxic buildup subsides. When a pawn affected by toxic buildup [dies](/wiki/Death "Death"), there is a chance that its corpse will instantly rot - this chance is equal to the severity. 

Once a colonist returns to a safe area, such as a roofed area for toxic fallout, or otherwise stops taking severity increases, their buildup severity will gradually decrease. Once no longer exposed, severity reduces by 8% per day, meaning that it will take up to 12.5 days to eliminate all toxins accumulated in the body. However, colonists that reach high stages of toxic buildup are likely to develop further complications.

### Vomiting[[edit](/index.php?title=Ailments&action=edit&section=39 "Edit section: Vomiting")]

[![StumpChoppedA \(cropped\).png](/images/thumb/6/6a/StumpChoppedA_%28cropped%29.png/40px-StumpChoppedA_%28cropped%29.png)](/wiki/Category:Stubs "Category:Stubs") | **This section is a[stub](/wiki/Category:Stubs "Category:Stubs").** You can help RimWorld Wiki by **[expanding it](https://rimworldwiki.com/index.php?title=Ailments&action=edit). Reason:** _what does it interrupt, what happens when different tasks are underway - e.g. eating cancels the eating task but doesn't waste the meal, explanation that vomiting is expressed as mtb etc._  
---|---  
  
The body attempting to forcefully expunge toxins. Unfortunately for your pawns, this doesn't work in Rimworld. 

Vomiting is caused by a wide variety of ailments[List Needed?], some [Psycasts](/wiki/Psycasts "Psycasts"),[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC") or being hit with an EMP while having the [Sterilizing](/wiki/Sterilizing_stomach "Sterilizing stomach"), [Reprocessor](/wiki/Reprocessor_stomach "Reprocessor stomach"), or [Nuclear](/wiki/Nuclear_stomach "Nuclear stomach") stomachs[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC") installed. 

A pawn that is vomiting will stop in place, face the side, and begin vomiting. Vomiting lasts a random duration from 300 [ticks](/wiki/Time "Time") (5 secs) to 900 [ticks](/wiki/Time "Time") (15 secs), and every 150 interval ticks it reduces the pawn's food bar by 0.04 and generates 1 stack of [vomit](/wiki/Vomit "Vomit") filth on the tile they're facing. Note that this interval tick occurs independently from the beginning of the vomiting duration, and as such the number of times it occurs may not be the simple division of the chose duration divided by 150. As such, between one fewer and one more interval may occur than the the division would indicate. A pawn lying in a bed will not stand up to vomit[Details]. 

Vomiting will interrupt most tasks, including eating, walking, aiming a weapon, or working at a station, but they will resume the task once they stop vomiting[Mechanics]. For this reason, it is recommended that pawns who are afflicted with an ailment that causes frequent vomiting, such as [food poisoning](/wiki/Food_poisoning "Food poisoning"), eat foods like [pemmican](/wiki/Pemmican "Pemmican"), as a pawn who vomits while in the middle of eating a normal meal will lose all progress towards consuming it (note that the meal is not wasted), but a pawn eating pemmican will still consume part of the stack. Similarly, they should also avoid long tasks that have their progress reset on being interrupted, such as cooking 4x [lavish meals](/wiki/Lavish_meal "Lavish meal"). 

### Crumbling mind[[edit](/index.php?title=Ailments&action=edit&section=40 "Edit section: Crumbling mind")]

[![AnomalyIcon.png](/images/thumb/3/32/AnomalyIcon.png/40px-AnomalyIcon.png)](/wiki/Anomaly_\(DLC\) "Anomaly \(DLC\)") | **This section relates to content added by[Anomaly (DLC)](/wiki/Anomaly_\(DLC\) "Anomaly \(DLC\)").** Please note that it will not be present without the [DLC](/wiki/Downloadable_content "Downloadable content") enabled.  
---|---  
![](/images/thumb/0/06/Modal_Warning_Icon.png/40px-Modal_Warning_Icon.png)| **Spoiler warning:** This section may contain details about the gameplay that might be considered spoilers.  
If you wish to enjoy the content first hand, you probably shouldn't continue reading beyond this point.

The gradual breakdown of the mind. While technically non-fatal, it applies mounting consciousness penalties, and its final stage renders a pawn incapable of nearly all work. 

Crumbling mind is caused by two [Anomaly](/wiki/Anomaly "Anomaly") events - The [Corrupted obelisk](/wiki/Corrupted_obelisk "Corrupted obelisk") and the [Creepjoiner](/wiki/Creepjoiner "Creepjoiner"). It cannot affect pawns that existed before those events, only ones created by them. It can be detected early by a surgical inspection. 

Creepjoiners with this condition will begin showing symptoms within 2-3.33 days. Duplicated pawns will begin showing them within 12-48 hours. Severity progresses by 33% per day. Progress can be paused through the use of a [cryptosleep casket](/wiki/Cryptosleep_casket "Cryptosleep casket"). 

Crumbling mind can be cured by a [healer mech serum](/wiki/Healer_mech_serum "Healer mech serum") as well as the [unnatural healing](/wiki/Unnatural_healing "Unnatural healing") ability so long as it hasn't progressed to the final stage of **Crumbled mind**. Once it has, the condition is incurable, short of destroying the colonist's brain then resurrecting them with either a [resurrector mech serum](/wiki/Resurrector_mech_serum "Resurrector mech serum") or [death refusal](/wiki/Death_refusal "Death refusal"). However, consistently destroying the brain can be difficult. There are 5 main ways to achieve it: 

  * With the [Biotech DLC](/wiki/Biotech_DLC "Biotech DLC"), ordering a colonist into a loaded [Subcore ripscanner](/wiki/Subcore_ripscanner "Subcore ripscanner") will always cause total brain destruction.
  * With the [Ideology DLC](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Ideology DLC"), extracting the [skull](/wiki/Skull "Skull") from a dead pawn will destroy its head reliably and safely.
  * Fatal [Luciferium](/wiki/Luciferium "Luciferium") withdrawal kills via brain death.
  * Allow colonists or animals to eat sections of the corpse, risking consuming the entire body before the head is removed.



Furthermore, no matter how successful the head removal, the costs and downsides of the either the serum or refusal remain. If this strategy is used, remember not to euthanize them or they will be no longer of your faction after resurrection. 

Counterintuitively, [ghoul infusion](/wiki/Ghoul "Ghoul") will not cure crumbling mind and it will hinder ghoul's combat abilities. [Chronophagy](/wiki/Chronophagy "Chronophagy") healing is also useless against crumbling mind.

**Crumbling mind (moderate)** | ≥40% severity | 

  * ×75% [Consciousness](/wiki/Consciousness "Consciousness")

### Duplicate sickness[[edit](/index.php?title=Ailments&action=edit&section=41 "Edit section: Duplicate sickness")]

[![AnomalyIcon.png](/images/thumb/3/32/AnomalyIcon.png/40px-AnomalyIcon.png)](/wiki/Anomaly_\(DLC\) "Anomaly \(DLC\)") | **This section relates to content added by[Anomaly (DLC)](/wiki/Anomaly_\(DLC\) "Anomaly \(DLC\)").** Please note that it will not be present without the [DLC](/wiki/Downloadable_content "Downloadable content") enabled.

![](/images/thumb/0/06/Modal_Warning_Icon.png/40px-Modal_Warning_Icon.png)| **Spoiler warning:** This section may contain details about the gameplay that might be considered spoilers.

Psychic interference between this person and their duplicate is causing slow mental deterioration. As long as multiple copies of this person are alive, the condition will keep getting worse. However, the condition will not be lethal. 

Duplicate sickness is one of the potential outcomes from the duplication of a pawn by a [corrupted obelisk](/wiki/Corrupted_obelisk "Corrupted obelisk"). It affects both the original pawn and the duplicate[Wb other duplicates?] and is initially hidden. Severity increases by 10% per day that both pawns are alive. A pawn in [cryptosleep](/wiki/Cryptosleep "Cryptosleep") will not gain severity, but being in cryptosleep will _not_ prevent the other copy of the pawn from gaining severity. This gradually reduces the consciousness of both pawns and makes them more likely to have [mental breaks](/wiki/Mental_breaks "Mental breaks"), before eventually rendering both comatose. 

It cannot be treated,[Verify] but can be cured by one of three methods: [healer mech serum](/wiki/Healer_mech_serum "Healer mech serum"), the [unnatural healing](/wiki/Unnatural_healing "Unnatural healing") ability, or the death of one of the two pawns. Note that curing one pawn via healer mech serum[Verify] or unnatural healing will not cure the other. 

If one of the two connected pawns is killed, the other's duplication sickness severity immediately starts regressing at the rate of 50% per day, while the killed pawn's duplication sickness is instantly cured. The killed pawn can then be immediately resurrected with a [resurrector mech serum](/wiki/Resurrector_mech_serum "Resurrector mech serum") or [death refusal](/wiki/Death_refusal "Death refusal") without stopping the regression of the other pawn's sickness. Similarly, if one of the linked pawns is turned into a [ghoul](/wiki/Ghoul "Ghoul"), killed, and revived with a [ghoul resurrection serum](/wiki/Ghoul_resurrection_serum "Ghoul resurrection serum"), the same mechanics apply.

## Heart attack[[edit](/index.php?title=Ailments&action=edit&section=49 "Edit section: Heart attack")]

Heart attacks can randomly occur on any pawn/animal at any time, but they become more frequent as they pass half of their life expectancy (e.g. 40 years in humans), triggered by the [birthday event](/wiki/Events#Birthday "Events"). There must be 2 or more colonists on your colony for this to occur. 

The average interval between heart attacks is curved as follows (in days): 

  * 0-60% of life expectancy: 99,999,999 - 99,999,999
  * 60-80% of life expectancy: 99,999,999 - 2,500
  * 80-100% of life expectancy: 2,500 - 300



Examples of intervals and chances: 

  * 70% of life expectancy (i.e. 56 in humans): 50,001,249.5 days (approx. 0.00000002% per day)
  * 90% of life expectancy (i.e. 72 in humans): 1,400 days (approx. 0.000714% per day)



### Stages[[edit](/index.php?title=Ailments&action=edit&section=50 "Edit section: Stages")]

Stage | Begins at | Symptoms   
---|---|---  
**Painful** | 0% Severity | 

  * [Consciousness](/wiki/Consciousness "Consciousness") ×50%
  * [Pain](/wiki/Pain "Pain") +40%

  
**Debilitating** | 60% Severity | 

  * [Consciousness](/wiki/Consciousness "Consciousness") max. 10% (Unconsciousness)
  * [Pain](/wiki/Pain "Pain") +60%

  * [Death](/wiki/Death "Death")

  
  
#### Progression[[edit](/index.php?title=Ailments&action=edit&section=51 "Edit section: Progression")]

Heart attacks always start at 40% severity. Each interval the heart attack severity will randomly change by a value between −40% to +60%. This means that it is possible for a heart attack to recover on its own, but usually (~76% likelihood)[Detail needed] an untreated heart attack progresses to fatal severity. The interval between changes is random, and varies between 500 [ticks](/wiki/Time "Time") (8.33 secs) and 10,000 [ticks](/wiki/Time "Time") (2.78 mins). 

#### Treatment[[edit](/index.php?title=Ailments&action=edit&section=52 "Edit section: Treatment")]

Like injuries, medicine can be used to treat a heart attack. 

Doctors treating heart attacks will administer treatments rapidly, consuming medicine in the process. Each treatment has a chance to succeed, reducing the heart attack severity by −30%. Successfully reducing severity below 0% will completely treat the heart attack. 

The chance a particular treatment can succeed is 65% multiplied by the [tend quality](/wiki/Tend_quality "Tend quality"), for an effective maximum of 84.5% with [glitterworld medicine](/wiki/Glitterworld_medicine "Glitterworld medicine"). 

#### Prevention[[edit](/index.php?title=Ailments&action=edit&section=53 "Edit section: Prevention")]

Replacing a pawn's heart with either a [prosthetic](/wiki/Prosthetic_heart "Prosthetic heart") or [bionic heart](/wiki/Bionic_heart "Bionic heart") will completely prevent heart attacks. Note however that while the bionic heart improves the [blood pumping](/wiki/Blood_pumping "Blood pumping") capacity, the prosthetic heart decreases it, and thus all of the stats and capacities it affects.
