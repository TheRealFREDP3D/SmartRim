**Research** is the primary method through which players can enhance and expand the abilities of their [colony](/wiki/Colony "Colony"). Certain [buildings](/wiki/Buildings "Buildings") and [gear](/wiki/Gear "Gear") require research in order to be built. 

## Mechanics[[edit](/index.php?title=Research&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Mechanics")]

A [research bench](/wiki/Research_bench "Research bench") is required to conduct research, with some projects requiring more advanced research stations. Research is presented as a tech tree, with later projects placed further right. Most research projects require a prerequisite. Some research projects introduced in the [Royalty DLC](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Royalty DLC"),[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC") and [Biotech DLC](/wiki/Biotech_DLC "Biotech DLC")[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC") require additional unlocking using items such as [techprints](/wiki/Techprint "Techprint") and mechanoid chips respectively, and only then would research be allowed. Research projects from the [Anomaly DLC](/wiki/Anomaly_DLC "Anomaly DLC") [![Content added by the Anomaly DLC](/images/thumb/3/32/AnomalyIcon.png/16px-AnomalyIcon.png)](/wiki/Anomaly_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Anomaly DLC") require the study of [entities](/wiki/Entities "Entities") rather than standard research points. See the Anomaly section for more details on Anomaly research projects. 

Once a project is selected, a [researcher](/wiki/Work#Research "Work") will work at the bench to generate research points. [Research Speed](/wiki/Research_Speed "Research Speed") is primarily determined by the Intellectual skill. Only one research project can be studied at a time. However, you can freely switch between research projects, and all research progress is retained. Multiple researchers will all contribute towards the same project, with no penalties. In addition, multiple benches can be used simultaneously, again, without penalty. 

Your faction's [tech level](/wiki/Tech_level "Tech level") determines research point costs, and therefore time spent researching. [New Arrivals](/wiki/New_Arrivals "New Arrivals") start at an Industrial level, and have normal costs for all research. [New Tribes](/wiki/New_Tribe "New Tribe") start at a Neolithic tech level, so certain research costs are increased: 

  * Researching Medieval-level tech, like Long Swords, takes 1.5x the research points for tribes to complete.
  * Researching Industrial-level tech or higher takes 2x the research for tribes. Note that the majority of research topics are industrial or higher.



Both New Arrivals and New Tribes start with certain projects already researched. Specific [scenarios](/wiki/Scenario "Scenario") can add, but not remove, pre-researched topics. 

Research can also be conducted by pawns reading [schematics](/wiki/Schematic "Schematic"), which can be obtained through [trading](/wiki/Trading "Trading") or earned through [quests](/wiki/Quest "Quest"). The number of research points generated is based on the quality of the schematic. Research points from schematics are not affected by the faction's tech level, pawn's research speed, or the storyteller's research speed setting. Each schematic will contribute points to one or two research projects. These projects are chosen when the schematic item is generated and cannot be modified. Schematics will stop contributing research points once the associated project is completed. See [schematics](/wiki/Schematic "Schematic") for more detailed mechanics and analysis. 

Mods can add their own research projects; they can be manually placed, or automatically placed if not specified. Projects will be moved apart if they overlap. They can also add separate research tabs, independent of the main tree. 

### Research speed[[edit](/index.php?title=Research&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Research speed")]

The number of research points gained is based on the researcher's [research speed](/wiki/Research_speed "Research speed") factor, which is largely based on their [intellectual](/wiki/Intellectual "Intellectual") skill. The number of research points gained per [tick](/wiki/Tick "Tick") is calculated with the following equation: 
    
    
    0.00825 × Pawn Research Speed × Building Research Speed Factor × Difficulty Factor

Where: 

  * 0.00825 points/tick is the base research factor.[Note]
  * Pawn Research Speed is the [research speed](/wiki/Research_speed "Research speed") stat from the researcher. This depends on other factors, like [health capacities](/wiki/Capacity "Capacity") and [global work speed](/wiki/Global_work_speed "Global work speed").
  * Building Research Speed Factor is the multiplier from the research station. This depends on other factors, like whether the building is outdoors, the temperature, the cleanliness and reading bonus of the room, and if any [facilities](/wiki/Multi-analyzer "Multi-analyzer") are connected. The following are the base factors: 
    * [Simple research bench](/wiki/Simple_research_bench "Simple research bench"): ×0.75
    * [Hi-tech research bench](/wiki/Hi-tech_research_bench "Hi-tech research bench"): ×1.00
    * [Hi-tech research bench](/wiki/Hi-tech_research_bench "Hi-tech research bench") with [Multi-analyzer](/wiki/Multi-analyzer "Multi-analyzer"): ×1.10
    * Not inside laboratory: x0.8
  * Difficulty Factor is the multiplier from the chosen [difficulty settings](/wiki/AI_Storytellers#Difficulty "AI Storytellers"). The factors for the default difficulties are: 
    * Peaceful and Community Builder (Easy) - x1.2
    * Adventure Story (Normal) and Strive to Survive (Rough) - x1
    * Blood and Dust (Hard) - x0.95
    * Losing is Fun (Extreme) - x0.9

## Anomaly[[edit](/index.php?title=Research&action=edit&section=9 "Edit section: Anomaly")]

[![AnomalyIcon.png](/images/thumb/3/32/AnomalyIcon.png/40px-AnomalyIcon.png)](/wiki/Anomaly_\(DLC\) "Anomaly \(DLC\)") | **This section relates to content added by[Anomaly (DLC)](/wiki/Anomaly_\(DLC\) "Anomaly \(DLC\)").** Please note that it will not be present without the [DLC](/wiki/Downloadable_content "Downloadable content") enabled.  
---|---  
  
Dark study is the primary method through which players can unlock the abilities in the Anomaly DLC for their [colony](/wiki/Colony "Colony"). It is unlocked by investigating the "fallen monolith". Certain [buildings](/wiki/Buildings "Buildings") and [gear](/wiki/Gear "Gear") require dark study in order to be built. 

### Mechanics[[edit](/index.php?title=Research&action=edit&section=10 "Edit section: Mechanics")]

[![StumpChoppedA \(cropped\).png](/images/thumb/6/6a/StumpChoppedA_%28cropped%29.png/40px-StumpChoppedA_%28cropped%29.png)](/wiki/Category:Stubs "Category:Stubs") | **This section is a[stub](/wiki/Category:Stubs "Category:Stubs").** You can help RimWorld Wiki by **[expanding it](https://rimworldwiki.com/index.php?title=Research&action=edit). Reason:** _This System has way more to it than just the projects itself. Also see[containment](/wiki/Containment "Containment") for impact of strength on study. [Entity Study Rate](/wiki/Entity_Study_Rate "Entity Study Rate"). [Study Efficiency](/wiki/Study_Efficiency "Study Efficiency")._  
---|---  
  
While regular research is a kind of stationary work, performed in a single room inside the colony, dark study must be done where the studied entity is located. Captured entities will be studied in their containment room, [void monolith](/wiki/Void_monolith "Void monolith") and [obelisks](/wiki/Obelisk "Obelisk") can't be moved and must be studied right where they spawn, while anomaly items such as [golden cube](/wiki/Golden_cube "Golden cube") or [unnatural corpse](/wiki/Unnatural_corpse "Unnatural corpse") can be moved to wherever you want to study them. Each anomaly entity gives either basic or advanced study points when studied, which go to the active basic or advanced study project. At first, the [void monolith](/wiki/Void_monolith "Void monolith") and all encountered entities will give basic study points. But eventually entities that give advanced study points will appear, and the monolith can also be attuned to give advanced points instead of basic points. Advanced study points convert into basic research if no advanced research is active, but basic study points never convert into advanced study points. 

Unlike researching, studying speed is not determined by the researcher's intellectual skill, but by their studying efficiency, which can be improved by the [voidsight serum](/wiki/Voidsight_serum "Voidsight serum"). Studying speed also depend on the condition of the entity being studied. Captured entities give more study points if the containment strength is high, and obelisks give more points if their activity level is high. Different types of captured entities also give different amount and type of study points. 

#### Void study[[edit](/index.php?title=Research&action=edit&section=11 "Edit section: Void study")]

[![IdeologyIcon.png](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/40px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_\(DLC\) "Ideology \(DLC\)") | **This section relates to content added by[Ideology (DLC)](/wiki/Ideology_\(DLC\) "Ideology \(DLC\)").** Please note that it will not be present without the [DLC](/wiki/Downloadable_content "Downloadable content") enabled.  
---|---  
  
The [void study](/wiki/Void_study_\(precept\) "Void study \(precept\)") precept multiplies the amount of knowledge gained from studying by 50%, 75%, 125%, or 150%, depending on which level of the precept you have. For example, a [sightstealer](/wiki/Sightstealer "Sightstealer") provides 1.7 basic study every 2 days, and with a 150% study rate, that would become 2.55 basic study every 2 days.

### Analysis[[edit](/index.php?title=Research&action=edit&section=15 "Edit section: Analysis")]

As the speed of dark study is not affected by intellectual skill, it should not be assigned to you main researcher to let them focus on regular researching. The studying pawn should still have some intellectual skill to reduce the time spent studying. 

## Analysis[[edit](/index.php?title=Research&action=edit&section=16 "Edit section: Analysis")]

This is a rough suggestion of what technologies to research. Variations exist, and research order can change depending on current map and [biome](/wiki/Biome "Biome"), colonist skill, personal playstyle/goals, and [difficulty](/wiki/Difficulty "Difficulty"). 

The key criterion is what is most likely to affect your survivability right now. For example, for a [New Tribe](/wiki/New_Tribe "New Tribe") [ice sheet](/wiki/Ice_sheet "Ice sheet") colony, that may be getting Complex clothing for [parkas](/wiki/Parka "Parka") to let you function outside for extended periods. 

Generally however, the primary risk is from combat, either directly or the downward spirals that can result of the damage to colonists and infrastructure that results. This makes rushing Flak armor and Gas operation a solid option as not only will they give you equipment that can carry you into the end game, the prerequisite researches also unlock options while you're building towards for that end-game load out. The most obvious examples are the early guns which can stand in for the later ones that you might eventually standarize on, however the other options are also very useful - Smithing unlocks [maces](/wiki/Mace "Mace") for good early melee, Machining gives you [frag grenades](/wiki/Frag_grenades "Frag grenades") to dominate melee threats etc. 

Once survivability is reached, projects can be selected by preference or prioritized based on what allows for fastest progression. 

This may look something like this: 

  1. One of:[1]
     * [Batteries](/wiki/Batteries "Batteries") and [solar generators](/wiki/Solar_generator "Solar generator")
     * [Watermill generators](/wiki/Watermill_generator "Watermill generator")
     * [Geothermal generator](/wiki/Geothermal_generator "Geothermal generator")
  2. Microelectronics [2]
  3. [Multi-analyzer](/wiki/Multi-analyzer "Multi-analyzer") [3]
  4. [Fabrication](/wiki/Fabrication_bench "Fabrication bench") [3]



**Notes:**

    1 You'll want some better source of [power](/wiki/Power "Power"). Solar generators are easy to use, and with batteries, are a consistent source of power. [Watermill generators](/wiki/Watermill_generator "Watermill generator") can only be used if a [river](/wiki/World_generation#Rivers "World generation") (not ocean) is nearby. Water is more reliable than solar, for less research than batteries + solar.
    In theory, [geothermal generators](/wiki/Geothermal_generator "Geothermal generator") provide much more power than either option, but its 3200 research cost makes it more difficult for newer players. Rushing to geothermal is still possible, especially if you rely on [wind turbines](/wiki/Wind_turbine "Wind turbine") or [wood-fired generators](/wiki/Wood-fired_generator "Wood-fired generator") initially.
    2 Microelectronics gives access to [hi-tech research bench](/wiki/Hi-tech_research_bench "Hi-tech research bench"), which makes research 33% faster. It _also_ gives access to the [comms console](/wiki/Comms_console "Comms console"), an easy source for tradable items, and [EMP grenades](/wiki/EMP_grenades "EMP grenades"), which are very powerful against [mechanoids](/wiki/Mechanoid "Mechanoid").
    3 A [multi-analyzer](/wiki/Multi-analyzer "Multi-analyzer") boosts research by a further 10%, though you may not have the materials to create one. If you can't build a multi-analyzer or Fabrication's [fabrication bench](/wiki/Fabrication_bench "Fabrication bench") yet, then you'll gain the opportunity to chase some other tribal/industrial research projects. The ultimate goal is to reach Fabrication as soon as possible, so you can make your own [components](/wiki/Component "Component").
