A **simple meal** is a [meal](/wiki/Meal "Meal") and the most basic way to prepare [raw food](/wiki/Raw_food "Raw food") for consumption.

## Acquisition[[edit](/index.php?title=Simple_meal&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Acquisition")]

Simple meals can be [cooked](/wiki/Work#Cook "Work") at a [Campfire](/wiki/Campfire "Campfire"), [Fueled stove](/wiki/Fueled_stove "Fueled stove"), or [Electric stove](/wiki/Electric_stove "Electric stove"). A simple meal requires 0.5 units of [nutrition](/wiki/Nutrition "Nutrition") from [raw food](/wiki/Raw_food "Raw food") \- this is equal to 10 units of [rice](/wiki/Rice "Rice"), [potatoes](/wiki/Potatoes "Potatoes"), [meat](/wiki/Meat "Meat"), and most foods. They require 300 [ticks](/wiki/Time "Time") (5 secs) of work to make, and have no minimum cooking skill. Cooking at a campfire doubles the work required, to 600 [ticks](/wiki/Time "Time") (10 secs) of work. 4 Simple meals can be cooked at once, which require 2 units of [nutrition](/wiki/Nutrition "Nutrition") from raw food and quadruple the time compared to 1 simple meal to make. 

## Summary[[edit](/index.php?title=Simple_meal&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Summary")]

A simple meal can be eaten by humans or animals for 0.9 [nutrition](/wiki/Nutrition "Nutrition"), or 90% of a human's hunger meter. As it takes 0.5 nutrition of [raw food](/wiki/Raw_food "Raw food") to create, simple meals provide 180% nutrition efficiency. 

Eating a simple meal has no intrinsic effect on [mood](/wiki/Mood "Mood") like some other meals, however if it was made out of [insect meat](/wiki/Insect_meat "Insect meat"), [human meat](/wiki/Human_meat "Human meat"), or [twisted meat](/wiki/Twisted_meat "Twisted meat"),[![Content added by the Anomaly DLC](/images/thumb/3/32/AnomalyIcon.png/16px-AnomalyIcon.png)](/wiki/Anomaly_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Anomaly DLC") their respective moodlets will be applied. If [Ideology DLC](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Ideology DLC") is active and the meal was made out of [raw fungus](/wiki/Raw_fungus "Raw fungus"), its normal moodlet will also be applied. 

## Analysis[[edit](/index.php?title=Simple_meal&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Analysis")]

Going from eating raw food to simple meals is a huge upgrade, since: 

  * Simple meals give ×180% the nutrition of raw food.
  * Simple meals avoid the −7 [_Ate raw food_](/wiki/Thoughts#Ate_raw_food "Thoughts") [moodlet](/wiki/Mood "Mood").
  * Simple meals have a lower chance of giving [food poisoning](/wiki/Food_poisoning "Food poisoning") than raw food, assuming a [clean](/wiki/Clean "Clean") cooking area and a chef with a [cooking](/wiki/Cooking "Cooking") skill of 3 or better.
  * Simple meals last for 4 days compared to Raw Meat lasting for 2 days. However, raw vegetables last about 30 days longer.



Compared to other meals: 

  * [Fine meals](/wiki/Fine_meal "Fine meal") give a +5 _Ate fine meal_ mood boost and have the same nutrition efficiency, but require both meat and vegetables to create. They take more work and require a Cooking skill of 6. [Vegetarian fine meals](/wiki/Vegetarian_fine_meal "Vegetarian fine meal") and [carnivore fine meals](/wiki/Carnivore_fine_meal "Carnivore fine meal") can be made from 1 type of food, but waste food in the process.


  * [Nutrient paste meals](/wiki/Nutrient_paste_meal "Nutrient paste meal") give a −4 moodlet, require [Nutrient paste](/wiki/Research#Nutrient_paste "Research") researched (which [New Arrivals](/wiki/New_Arrivals "New Arrivals") start with), and it takes [power](/wiki/Power "Power") to operate a [dispenser](/wiki/Nutrient_paste_dispenser "Nutrient paste dispenser"). However, nutrient paste meals give ×166% the nutrition (300% the nutrition from raw food), never give [food poisoning](/wiki/Food_poisoning "Food poisoning"), and take no work to create. Paste becomes relatively better if you do not have a skilled cook, or are limited on food.
