**Rice** is a vegetable [raw food](/wiki/Raw_food "Raw food") obtained by harvesting [rice plants](/wiki/Rice_plant "Rice plant"). Rice plants grow very quickly but with a modest yield. 

## Acquisition[[edit](/index.php?title=Rice&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Acquisition")]

Rice is harvested from [rice plants](/wiki/Rice_plant "Rice plant"). Each plant requires 5.54 full days at optimal light and temperature range to reach maturity in normal, 100% fertile soil and yields 6 units per plant, before modifiers for [Difficulty](/wiki/Difficulty "Difficulty") and [Plant Harvest Yield](/wiki/Plant_Harvest_Yield "Plant Harvest Yield"). 

## Summary[[edit](/index.php?title=Rice&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Summary")]

As a [raw food](/wiki/Raw_food "Raw food"), rice can be be eaten raw, with a flat 2% chance of giving [food poisoning](/wiki/Food_poisoning "Food poisoning"), or cooked into a [meal](/wiki/Meal "Meal") with a food poisoning chance dependent on the skill of the cook and the [cleanliness](/wiki/Cleanliness "Cleanliness") of the kitchen. [Animals](/wiki/Animals "Animals") are immune to food poisoning from raw food but not from meals. When used in food recipes that require specific types of [nutrition](/wiki/Nutrition "Nutrition"), such as [fine meals](/wiki/Fine_meal "Fine meal"), rice is classified as a vegetable. 

As with most raw food, eating rice directly results in a −7 _Ate raw food_ [thought](/wiki/Thought "Thought") for 1 [day](/wiki/Time "Time"). This mood penalty is negated if the pawn has the [ascetic](/wiki/Ascetic "Ascetic") trait or the [robust digestion](/wiki/Robust_digestion "Robust digestion") [![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC") gene. 

## Analysis[[edit](/index.php?title=Rice&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Analysis")]

Rice has a shelf life of 40 days, second only to [corn](/wiki/Corn "Corn") and [toxipotatoes](/wiki/Toxipotatoes "Toxipotatoes"). While only relevant while a freezer is not available, this should give enough time to set up an appropriate storage facility. 

With a Nutrition of 0.05 per unit and a deterioration rate of 6, there is no difference with other raw vegetables for the purpose of cooking meals. While direct consumption is possible, this is inefficient from a nutrition point of view compared to cooking a [simple meal](/wiki/Simple_meal "Simple meal") even without considering the mood penalty and food poisoning chance. 

Feeding rice to [herbivores](/wiki/Herbivore "Herbivore") and [omnivores](/wiki/Omnivore "Omnivore") has no ill effects, but is inefficient in terms of work per nutrition compared to farming [Haygrass](/wiki/Haygrass "Haygrass") or making [kibble](/wiki/Kibble "Kibble"). 

Rice is often seen in large quantities in new colonies due to the [rice plant](/wiki/Rice_plant "Rice plant") being a superb starting plant for colonies with access to rich soil. See [Rice plant](/wiki/Rice_plant#Analysis "Rice plant") for more details.
