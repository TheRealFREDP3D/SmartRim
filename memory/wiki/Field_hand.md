Field hand

[![Field hand](/images/7/7a/Health_item_prosthetic.png)](/wiki/File:Health_item_prosthetic.png "Field hand")

This mechanical hand replacement is made to speed up the sowing and harvesting tasks of a farmer. Multiple telescoping finger-like protrusions can simultaneously harvest from several points on the same plant, or precisely sow several holes at once. It also has a general-purpose manipulator. It is somewhat ungainly, which slows down movement slightly.

Field hands can be crafted at a [machining table](/wiki/Machining_table "Machining table") once the [Specialized limbs](/wiki/Research#Specialized_limbs "Research") research project has been completed. Note that this research requires a [techprint](/wiki/Techprint "Techprint"). Each requires [![Steel](/images/thumb/9/90/Steel_c.png/16px-Steel_c.png)](/wiki/Steel "Steel") 60 [Steel](/wiki/Steel "Steel"), [![Component](/images/thumb/4/40/Component.png/16px-Component.png)](/wiki/Component "Component") 8 [Components](/wiki/Component "Component"), 15,000 [ticks](/wiki/Time "Time") (4.17 mins) of work modified by the [general labor speed](/wiki/General_Labor_Speed "General Labor Speed") of the crafter, and a [crafting](/wiki/Crafting "Crafting") skill of 5.   
Field hands can be bought from the following traders: [Empire base trader](/wiki/Trader/Base_Empire_Standard "Trader/Base Empire Standard")[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC"), [Imperial trader caravan](/wiki/Trader/Empire_Caravan_Trader_General "Trader/Empire Caravan Trader General")[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC"), [Orbital imperial trader](/wiki/Trader/Orbital_Empire "Trader/Orbital Empire")[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC"). 

They can also be [bought](/wiki/Trade "Trade") from [Outlanders](/wiki/Outlanders "Outlanders") and [Empire](/wiki/Empire "Empire") settlements. 

The pawns that populate Farming [work sites](/wiki/Work_site "Work site") owned by an Outlander or [Pirate](/wiki/Pirate "Pirate") factions, including minor factions, very commonly spawn with field hands. 

## Summary[[edit](/index.php?title=Field_hand&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Summary")]

### Body part[[edit](/index.php?title=Field_hand&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Body part")]

Field hands replace the user's organic hand. They are universal and can be installed on either the left or right. This is a hand replacement, and cannot be installed on an artificial or missing arm. 

The field hand acts as a fully functional hand, providing **100%** [Manipulation](/wiki/Manipulation "Manipulation"). This fully replaces the functionality of a regular arm, but with no enhancement. Manipulation, in turn, affects a great many stats. For a full list of stats, see [Manipulation](/wiki/Manipulation "Manipulation"). 

### Other effects[[edit](/index.php?title=Field_hand&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Other effects")]

Each field hand installed gives +160% [Plant Work Speed](/wiki/Plant_Work_Speed "Plant Work Speed"). Note that this is an offset to the base value of the stat, which is then multiplied by factors like [Global Work Speed](/wiki/Global_Work_Speed "Global Work Speed") and [Manipulation](/wiki/Manipulation "Manipulation"). This means that the final value can be significantly higher. The effect of multiple field hands will stack. 

Each field hand also inflicts a −8% [Moving](/wiki/Moving "Moving") penalty. Moving, in turn, affects the following [stats](/wiki/Stats "Stats"): [Hunting Stealth](/wiki/Hunting_Stealth "Hunting Stealth"), [Melee Dodge Chance](/wiki/Melee_Dodge_Chance "Melee Dodge Chance"), [Move Speed](/wiki/Move_Speed "Move Speed"). This results in a directly proportional change to a pawn's [move speed](/wiki/Move_speed "Move speed"), and a penalty to [melee dodge chance](/wiki/Melee_dodge_chance "Melee dodge chance") equivalent to an losing −1.44 levels of [Melee](/wiki/Melee "Melee") skill per arm replaced.

### Installation[[edit](/index.php?title=Field_hand&action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: Installation")]

Installing the part requires 2,500 [ticks](/wiki/Time "Time") (41.67 secs) of work, 2x medicine of [herbal](/wiki/Herbal_medicine "Herbal medicine") quality or better, and a [Medical skill](/wiki/Skills#Medical "Skills") of 4. 

Removing the part requires 2,000 [ticks](/wiki/Time "Time") (33.33 secs) of work, 1x medicine of [herbal](/wiki/Herbal_medicine "Herbal medicine") quality or better, and has no [Medical skill](/wiki/Skills#Medical "Skills") requirements. 

If the operation fails, the part will be destroyed. 

## Analysis[[edit](/index.php?title=Field_hand&action=edit&section=7 "Edit section: Analysis")]

Field hands offer an extreme boost to planting speed. With 1 field hand, a Plants 0 pawn sows and harvests as fast as a baseline Plants 13 pawn. With 2 field hands, this same pawn works _faster_ than a Plants 20 pawn. While the field hand disables the use of a work-enhancing [bionic arm](/wiki/Bionic_arm "Bionic arm") or [archotech arm](/wiki/Archotech_arm "Archotech arm"), if a pawn's primary job is to grow, then a field hand offers a clear benefit. (Note that this implant does not increase [Plant Harvest Yield](/wiki/Plant_Harvest_Yield "Plant Harvest Yield"), so your Plants 0 pawn will not get much per harvest). 

Plant work speed is calculated as 

(100% + Offsets) * [Plant work speed](/wiki/Plant_Work_Speed "Plant Work Speed") skill modifier * Manipulation * max(100%, 100% + (Sight - 100%) * 30%) * Global Work Speed   
---  
  
Harvest yield is calculated as 

(100% + Offsets) * [Plant Harvest Yield](/wiki/Plant_Harvest_Yield "Plant Harvest Yield") skill modifier * (100% + (Manipulation - 100%) * 30%) * max(100%, 100% + (Sight - 100%) * 20%)   
---  
  
The effective plant work speed (yield per unit time spent working) is equal to the product of the work speed and the yield. The data above shows that using a pair of field hands is 20% faster than using a bionic arm alongside a field hand. Whenever or not this increase is worth the additional -8% [Moving](/wiki/Moving "Moving") penalty of the second field hand and the opportunity cost of not using a bionic arm (or archotech arm) is something to be considered. 

Planting is essential to virtually every colony. Crops are a mostly stable form of food, and this skill can also be used to grow useful products like [drugs](/wiki/Drug "Drug"), [cloth](/wiki/Cloth "Cloth"), and [devilstrand](/wiki/Devilstrand "Devilstrand"). Even on the [sea ice](/wiki/Sea_ice "Sea ice"), (successful) colonies will eventually reach [hydroponics](/wiki/Hydroponics "Hydroponics") and be able to farm. You may want to consider planting [rice](/wiki/Rice_plant "Rice plant"); since the field hand is _so_ efficient, the workload of rice is easily managed. 

This comes at a cost of movement speed. In addition to the reduced efficiency in regards to day-to-day work, installing a field hand will hinder a pawn's performance in combat. For ranged fighters, slower movement means that [kiting](/wiki/Kiting "Kiting") tactics are no longer as viable. For melee fighters, the Moving penalty directly encumbers a pawn's [melee dodge chance](/wiki/Melee_dodge_chance "Melee dodge chance"). This can be eventually compensated, partially, by using [bionic legs](/wiki/Bionic_leg "Bionic leg"). 

Field hands can also be used to replace a missing hand. Compared to the [wooden hand](/wiki/Wooden_hand "Wooden hand"), the field hand offers +10% [Manipulation](/wiki/Manipulation "Manipulation") for −8% [Moving](/wiki/Moving "Moving").
