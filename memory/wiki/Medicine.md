**Medicine** , also called **[industrial-tech](/wiki/Lore#Tech_levels "Lore") medicine** in the [Health tab](/wiki/Health#Health_tab "Health"), is an expendable item used in [doctoring](/wiki/Doctoring "Doctoring").

## Acquisition[[edit](/index.php?title=Medicine&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Acquisition")]

Medicine can be crafted at a [drug lab](/wiki/Drug_lab "Drug lab") once the [Medicine production](/wiki/Research#Medicine_production "Research") research project has been completed. Each requires [![Cloth](/images/thumb/8/80/Cloth_b.png/16px-Cloth_b.png)](/wiki/Cloth "Cloth") 3 [Cloth](/wiki/Cloth "Cloth"), [![Herbal medicine](/images/thumb/a/af/Herbal_medicine.png/16px-Herbal_medicine.png)](/wiki/Herbal_medicine "Herbal medicine") 1 [Herbal medicine](/wiki/Herbal_medicine "Herbal medicine"), [![Neutroamine](/images/thumb/3/3c/Neutroamine_c.png/16px-Neutroamine_c.png)](/wiki/Neutroamine "Neutroamine") 1 [Neutroamine](/wiki/Neutroamine "Neutroamine") and 700 [ticks](/wiki/Time "Time") (11.67 secs) of work modified by the [drug synthesis speed](/wiki/Drug_Synthesis_Speed "Drug Synthesis Speed") of the crafter.   
Medicine can be bought from the following traders: [Orbital bulk goods trader](/wiki/Trader/Orbital_Bulk_Goods "Trader/Orbital Bulk Goods"), [Orbital combat supplier](/wiki/Trader/Orbital_Combat_Supplier "Trader/Orbital Combat Supplier"), [Outlander base trader](/wiki/Trader/Base_Outlander_Standard "Trader/Base Outlander Standard"), [Outlander combat supplier](/wiki/Trader/Caravan_Outlander_Combat_Supplier "Trader/Caravan Outlander Combat Supplier"), [Outlander slaver](/wiki/Trader/Caravan_Outlander_Pirate_Merchant "Trader/Caravan Outlander Pirate Merchant"), [Outlander visitor](/wiki/Trader/Visitor_Outlander_Standard "Trader/Visitor Outlander Standard"), [Slave ship](/wiki/Trader/Orbital_Pirate_Merchant "Trader/Orbital Pirate Merchant"). 

Medicine can also be purchased from [traders](/wiki/Traders "Traders") and looted from [raiders](/wiki/Raider "Raider"). 

## Summary[[edit](/index.php?title=Medicine&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Summary")]

[![StumpChoppedA \(cropped\).png](/images/thumb/6/6a/StumpChoppedA_%28cropped%29.png/40px-StumpChoppedA_%28cropped%29.png)](/wiki/Category:Stubs "Category:Stubs") | **This article is a[stub](/wiki/Category:Stubs "Category:Stubs").** You can help RimWorld Wiki by **[expanding it](https://rimworldwiki.com/index.php?title=Medicine&action=edit). Any form of [medicine](/wiki/Medicine_\(disambiguation\) "Medicine \(disambiguation\)") can be used whenever a [doctor](/wiki/Doctoring "Doctoring") tends to an [injury](/wiki/Injury "Injury") or [disease](/wiki/Disease "Disease"). In most cases, medicine does not directly cause healing. Instead, using medicine and better medicine will increase [tend quality](/wiki/Tend_quality "Tend quality"), increasing the [rate of healing](/wiki/Injury#Healing_rate "Injury") for injuries, or slowing down progression of a disease. For [gut worms](/wiki/Gut_worms "Gut worms") and [muscle parasites](/wiki/Muscle_parasites "Muscle parasites"), tend quality is directly used to cure the disease. 

With a [Medical Potency](/wiki/Medical_Potency "Medical Potency") of 100%, medicine has a 100% multiplier on both [tend quality](/wiki/Tend_quality "Tend quality") and [surgery success](/wiki/Doctoring#Operation_Success_Rate "Doctoring"). 

Medicine does not spoil, but will [deteriorate](/wiki/Deteriorate "Deteriorate") when left outside and unprotected by a [shelf](/wiki/Shelf "Shelf"). 

### Experience[[edit](/index.php?title=Medicine&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Experience")]

The amount of XP gained from tending depends on the type of medicine used, and what it is used on (human or animal). The equation for this is: 

XP = Patient XP Factor × Medicine XP Factor × Doctor's Learning Rate   
---  
  
Where: 

  * Patient XP Factor is a multiplier from the patient type. ×175 for animal patients, and ×500 for human patients.
  * Medicine XP Factor is the medicine's potency multiplied by 0.7, and clamped in the range of 0.5 and 1.
  * Doctor's Learning Rate is the doctor's own multiplier on XP gained for the medicine skill. See "[Improving Skills](/wiki/Skills#Improving_skills "Skills")" for more details.

## Analysis[[edit](/index.php?title=Medicine&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Analysis")]

Superior to [herbal medicine](/wiki/Herbal_medicine "Herbal medicine") but inferior to [glitterworld medicine](/wiki/Glitterworld_medicine "Glitterworld medicine"). 

Industrial medicine is best used for [diseases](/wiki/Disease "Disease") and [surgery](/wiki/Surgery "Surgery"), where Medical Potency has the greatest impact. A dose or two of medicine can save a colonist's life from the [plague](/wiki/Plague "Plague"). So long as patients are constantly treated by a decent doctor, resting full-time, and well-fed, this medicine should be enough against any disease. 

It is generally misused for bruises, cuts, and other [injuries](/wiki/Injuries "Injuries"). These are usually too common to waste high-quality medicine on; use herbal medicine or no medicine instead. But if a colonist is bleeding out rapidly, you may want to use better medicine for its increased tend speed and quality. Note that colonists are set to use the best quality medicine by default. In the [assign](/wiki/Assign "Assign") tab, you can set colonists and other pawns to [herbal medicine](/wiki/Herbal_medicine "Herbal medicine") or worse. You can also alter this in a colonist's Health tab. 

Regular medicine is enough to reach the 98% [surgery success chance cap](/wiki/Doctoring#Operation_Success_Rate "Doctoring"), under the right circumstances. A _healthy_ doctor with Medical 8 can reach the surgery cap (for most surgeries) in a [lit](/wiki/Light "Light"), [clean](/wiki/Clean "Clean") room, using a normal quality [hospital bed](/wiki/Hospital_bed "Hospital bed"). Without a hospital bed (or [sterile tile](/wiki/Sterile_tile "Sterile tile")), Medical 11 is required for the same thing. Note that many factors, such as a dirty room or a surgeon's damaged [Manipulation](/wiki/Manipulation "Manipulation"), will quickly lower your success rates.
