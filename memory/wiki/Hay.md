 Hay

Nutrient-rich grasses and shoots, harvested and compacted for storage. Hay is good animal feed, but inedible for humans.

Hay is the fastest produced food source, acquired by growing and harvesting haygrass. It's great for efficiently feeding animals, and can be used for straw matting. It cannot, however be used for training animals.

It is inedible to humans unless prepared as an ingredient in kibble, and doing so gives your colonists a brutal −12 Ate kibble thought for 1 day.
