Hand talon

[![Hand talon](/images/7/7a/Health_item_prosthetic.png)](/wiki/File:Health_item_prosthetic.png "Hand talon")

A razor-sharp talon which extends from a hidden opening between the middle and ring fingers. The talon is longer than the palm, but it becomes flexible upon retracting, allowing the user to curl it up and conceal it inside the hand. It can perform rapid slashing attacks, but is poor at penetrating armor.

## Acquisition[[edit](/index.php?title=Hand_talon&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Acquisition")]

Hand talons can be crafted at a [machining table](/wiki/Machining_table "Machining table") once the [Compact weaponry](/wiki/Research#Compact_weaponry "Research") research project has been completed. Note that this research requires a [techprint](/wiki/Techprint "Techprint"). Each requires [![Steel](/images/thumb/9/90/Steel_c.png/16px-Steel_c.png)](/wiki/Steel "Steel") 40 [Steel](/wiki/Steel "Steel"), [![Component](/images/thumb/4/40/Component.png/16px-Component.png)](/wiki/Component "Component") 7 [Components](/wiki/Component "Component"), 15,000 [ticks](/wiki/Time "Time") (4.17 mins) of work modified by the [general labor speed](/wiki/General_Labor_Speed "General Labor Speed") of the crafter, and a [crafting](/wiki/Crafting "Crafting") skill of 5.   
Hand talons can be bought from the following traders: [Empire base trader](/wiki/Trader/Base_Empire_Standard "Trader/Base Empire Standard")[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC"), [Imperial trader caravan](/wiki/Trader/Empire_Caravan_Trader_General "Trader/Empire Caravan Trader General")[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC"), [Orbital imperial trader](/wiki/Trader/Orbital_Empire "Trader/Orbital Empire")[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC"). 

The item can also be traded from exotic [traders](/wiki/Traders "Traders"), and can be earned as a [quest](/wiki/Quests "Quests") reward. 

## Summary[[edit](/index.php?title=Hand_talon&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Summary")]

The hand talon is a implant that is installed into a pawn's existing, organic hand. They are universal and can be installed on either the left or right hand but cannot be implanted into artificial body parts such as [wooden hands](/wiki/Wooden_hand "Wooden hand") or [bionic arms](/wiki/Bionic_arm "Bionic arm"). It adds an extra attack to implantee's melee verb pool. This attack deals 15 [Scratch](/wiki/Scratch "Scratch") damage, with an AP of 15. The true DPS will depend on the [specific melee verbs](/wiki/Melee_verb_selection "Melee verb selection") available to the pawn. The implant does not affect [manipulation](/wiki/Manipulation "Manipulation") nor does it replace the function of any body part. Hand talons have no [quality](/wiki/Quality "Quality"). 

When [social fighting](/wiki/Social_fight "Social fight"), colonists will not use the hand talon to attack their opponent.

### Installation[[edit](/index.php?title=Hand_talon&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Installation")]

Installing the part requires 2,500 [ticks](/wiki/Time "Time") (41.67 secs) of work, 2x medicine of [herbal](/wiki/Herbal_medicine "Herbal medicine") quality or better, and a [Medical skill](/wiki/Medical "Medical") of 5. 

Removing the part requires 2,500 [ticks](/wiki/Time "Time") (41.67 secs) of work, 1x medicine of [herbal](/wiki/Herbal_medicine "Herbal medicine") quality or better, and a [Medical skill](/wiki/Medical "Medical") of 5. 

If the operation fails, the part will be destroyed. 

## Analysis[[edit](/index.php?title=Hand_talon&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Analysis")]

The hand talon is a relatively powerful [body part weapon](/wiki/Body_part_weapon "Body part weapon"), having the second highest DPS of all options second only the [power claw](/wiki/Power_claw "Power claw"). And unlike the [power claw](/wiki/Power_claw "Power claw"), it inflicts no [stat](/wiki/Stat "Stat") malus. Its primary failing is in AP and single attack damage with the lowest of the all the damage-only body part weapons. Against unarmored enemies, the hand talon is slightly superior to a [good quality](/wiki/Quality "Quality") [steel](/wiki/Steel "Steel") [longsword](/wiki/Longsword "Longsword") but is inferior to normal quality [plasteel](/wiki/Plasteel "Plasteel") [longsword](/wiki/Longsword "Longsword") or [uranium](/wiki/Uranium "Uranium") [mace](/wiki/Mace "Mace"). As with all body part weapons, the hand talon is not particularly useful for a dedicated melee pawn if melee weapons of decent material and quality are available. 

Its greatest benefit comes in fitting it to ranged pawns, as it gives them the ability to be effective in melee combat while still being able to hold a gun. Being able to give a ranged pawn the melee capabilities of a pawn with a [steel](/wiki/Steel "Steel") [longsword](/wiki/Longsword "Longsword") means that if an enemy raider closes to melee range, your pawn isn't as defenseless as they would be otherwise. It also makes you pawns more versatile as they can switch from a ranged to melee role in an instant, and still be effective. This is useful if you need another melee fighter immediately for any reason, whether that be to melee block a choke point but no undowned melee pawn is available, or simply see that melee locking an enemy is a more effective way to combat them as they can't shoot back. 

Although a dedicated melee pawn will almost certainly benefit more from increased manipulation, the hand talon can still potentially be useful on such a pawn. Since pawns use their strong attack 75% of the time and a "mid" attack 25% of the time, the hand talon can be used to slightly increase the damage of the "mid" attack but, more importantly, it potentially has a shorter cooldown than other mid attacks, allowing for the next strong attack to come slightly faster. For example, a human fist has a cooldown time of 2.0 seconds, and a hand talon has a cooldown time of 1.5 seconds. Determining if the melee pawn's current mid attack has a shorter or longer cooldown than that of the hand talon is important for deciding if that pawn may enjoy a slight boost to their DPS from a hand talon. 

### Comparison to Power Claw[[edit](/index.php?title=Hand_talon&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Comparison to Power Claw")]

The hand talon has slightly lower DPS and significantly lower AP compared to the power claw, has a faster attack speed, and it does not slow the pawn like the power claw. The hand talon hits faster than the power claw but does less damage per hit, making it less likely to destroy body parts, which may be desirable or undesirable depending on the circumstances. This also means it "wastes" less damage by "overkilling" body parts; and allows the pawn to shorten the time to another, potentially stronger melee attack if they have one or retreat from melee combat if they do not. Depending on one's playstyle, the difference in AP may be less significant than it initially appears, as enemy types that favor melee, such as animals and tribals, often lack the high armor that enemies that favor ranged combat, such as centipedes, often possess. The notable exception to this is the scyther, which has a moderate sharp armor of 40%. However, if engaging any creature that is heavily armored, ranged or melee, in melee combat, the power claw will do significantly more damage than the hand talon. Another consideration is the movement penalty to the power claw, which may be less significant to dedicated shooting pawns compared to melee pawns. [Moving](/wiki/Moving "Moving") also affects derived stats including [melee dodge chance](/wiki/Melee_dodge_chance "Melee dodge chance") and [hunting stealth](/wiki/Hunting_stealth "Hunting stealth"). The increased DPS of the power claw, while modest, could also a significant factor, as both parts close to the average DPS you could expect from a raider: Using a power claw will mean the pawn is fighting with a slight advantage more often than not, and with the hand talon they will be fighting at a slight disadvantage more often than not. 

Combining a power claw and a hand talon, or using two of either, on a single pawn, is notably rarely useful. This is due to the [melee verb selection](/wiki/Melee_verb_selection "Melee verb selection") mechanic, the pawn will use their strongest melee attack 75% of the time regardless of how many are installed; making a second bionic significantly less useful (whether they have a stronger melee weapon equipped or not.) Whether the penalty to damage from the hand talon is worth avoiding the penalties to [moving](/wiki/Moving "Moving") (and stats derived from moving) is worth the reduced AP and DPS compared to the power claw for the individual pawn in question should be the primary difference considered when making the choice between the two. 

### Part incompatibly[[edit](/index.php?title=Hand_talon&action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: Part incompatibly")]

A significant drawback of the hand talon is that it can not be simultaneously used with two [bionic](/wiki/Bionic_arm "Bionic arm") or [archotech arms](/wiki/Archotech_arm "Archotech arm"). [Manipulation](/wiki/Manipulation "Manipulation") above 100% does not affect [Shooting Accuracy](/wiki/Shooting_Accuracy "Shooting Accuracy"), and so for a dedicated gunfighter this may not be a significant drawback. However, [manipulation](/wiki/Manipulation "Manipulation") increases [general labor speed](/wiki/General_labor_speed "General labor speed"), meaning that if they have a role within your colony beyond fighting affected by [manipulation](/wiki/Manipulation "Manipulation"), such as crafting or constructing, they will be less effective at these roles than they otherwise could be. A pawn can still be equipped with one [bionic](/wiki/Bionic_arm "Bionic arm") or [archotech arm](/wiki/Archotech_arm "Archotech arm"), and one hand talon without it negatively affecting their melee capabilities after [1.1.2610](/wiki/Version/1.1.2610 "Version/1.1.2610") changed melee verb selection. 

If you choose two [bionic](/wiki/Bionic_arm "Bionic arm") or [archotech arms](/wiki/Archotech_arm "Archotech arm") over one arm and a hand talon, you also have the option of the [knee spike](/wiki/Knee_spike "Knee spike"). The [knee spike](/wiki/Knee_spike "Knee spike") is not as effective in combat as the hand talon however, and has the significant drawback that you then can't install two [bionic](/wiki/Bionic_leg "Bionic leg") or [archotech legs](/wiki/Archotech_leg "Archotech leg"), which are arguably more important than the arms. In this case you can install [venom fangs](/wiki/Venom_fangs "Venom fangs") which, while the weakest implant of all those mentioned, does not conflict with anything. 

## Trivia[[edit](/index.php?title=Hand_talon&action=edit&section=7 "Edit section: Trivia")]

The stats page of the hand talon claims it replaces the hand and fingers, but this is incorrect. The hand talon is an implant and does not replace any body parts. The [elbow blade](/wiki/Elbow_blade "Elbow blade") and [knee spike](/wiki/Knee_spike "Knee spike") share this mistake.
