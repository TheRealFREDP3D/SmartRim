The **psychic emanator** is a special building only received as a [quest](/wiki/Quest "Quest") reward that provides a [mood](/wiki/Mood "Mood") bonus to all [human](/wiki/Human "Human") pawns around it when powered.

## Summary[[edit](/index.php?title=Psychic_emanator&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Summary")]

[![](/images/thumb/1/1a/Psychic_emanator_radius.png/200px-Psychic_emanator_radius.png)](/wiki/File:Psychic_emanator_radius.png)

[](/wiki/File:Psychic_emanator_radius.png "Enlarge")

Psychic emanator radius of effect

When provided with 200W of [power](/wiki/Power "Power"), a psychic emanator projects an aura in a 14.9-tile radius around itself. This radius is not blocked by any structure, including [walls](/wiki/Walls "Walls"). All human pawns within the radius experience a +5 _Psychic emanator soothe_ [thought](/wiki/Thought "Thought") bonus, directly scaled to the [psychic sensitivity](/wiki/Psychic_sensitivity "Psychic sensitivity") of the pawn. That is, a [psychically hypersensitive](/wiki/Psychically_hypersensitive "Psychically hypersensitive") pawn, with the resulting 180% sensitivity, will experience a +9 mood bonus, while a [psychically deaf](/wiki/Psychically_deaf "Psychically deaf") pawn with 0% sensitivity will have no change to their mood at all. All humans in range experience the thought - [colonist](/wiki/Colonist "Colonist"), [raider](/wiki/Raider "Raider"), and [guest](/wiki/Guest "Guest"); adult, child and [baby](/wiki/Baby "Baby")[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC") alike. 

The mood bonus only lasts for as long as a pawn remains within its area of effect. 

The effects of multiple emanators do not stack. 

## Analysis[[edit](/index.php?title=Psychic_emanator&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Analysis")]

The mood bonus from the emanator immediately ends once the pawn moves out of its somewhat short range, however Mood [moves gradually](/wiki/Mood#Mechanics "Mood") in response to changes. Having the mood stabilize at the higher level obviously helps while inside the radius, but the mood will also take time to tick down to baseline after leaving the area. The higher the mood bonus, the longer it will be in effect - a psychically hypersensitive pawn will have over an hour of time before their mood stabilizes. As such, it is advisable to place the emanator in places where pawns stay for long periods of time, or frequently spend time in. 

Suggestions include: 

  * Hospitals to help negate some of the negative effects of being bedridden with an illness. Note that pawns that are immobile or unconscious cannot break and are therefore a low priority for mood management.
  * Near prison cells to reduce mental breaks.
  * Near work spaces to provide a mood bonuses throughout the day - a pawn that spends all of its time in the field and then goes out of it to eat and sleep effectively has it 24/7. Ideal places include workshops, kitchens, [meditation](/wiki/Meditation "Meditation") locations,[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC") and any worksite that does not require the pawn to range over a large area.
  * In shared spaces such as dining and rec rooms - this is less efficient on a per pawn basis but will increase the happiness of the colony as a whole more effectively.
  * In Nurseries, due to [children](/wiki/Children "Children")[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC") causing either a −2 _Sad youngsters_ [thought](/wiki/Thought "Thought") or +2 _Happy youngsters_ [thought](/wiki/Thought "Thought") to colonists without the [Psychopath](/wiki/Psychopath "Psychopath") trait, babies do not have many mood offsets and frequently have their moods decrease due to their play need not being satisfied or just not going to sleep.
  * In areas frequented by particularly break-prone pawns, such as those with the [Depressive](/wiki/Depressive "Depressive") trait or that have recently lost family members.
  * To negate the effects of the [Sleep suppressor](/wiki/Sleep_suppressor "Sleep suppressor")[![Content added by the Anomaly DLC](/images/thumb/3/32/AnomalyIcon.png/16px-AnomalyIcon.png)](/wiki/Anomaly_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Anomaly DLC") and [Frenzy inducer](/wiki/Frenzy_inducer "Frenzy inducer").[![Content added by the Anomaly DLC](/images/thumb/3/32/AnomalyIcon.png/16px-AnomalyIcon.png)](/wiki/Anomaly_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Anomaly DLC")



As mood does not change while sleeping, it should not be placed near bedrooms. 

Because of the rarity and high value of the emanator, you can wall to prevent angry pawns from destroying it during a [tantrum](/wiki/Tantrum "Tantrum"), or raiders from damaging or stealing it. 

If targeted at improving the mood of a specific pawn or pawns, consider enhancing their [psychic sensitivity](/wiki/Psychic_sensitivity "Psychic sensitivity") to maximize the effect on their mood. [Eltex](/wiki/Eltex "Eltex") clothing[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC") is ideal, especially if the mood boost is not intended to be permanent, but a variety of methods exist. For a full list of potential enhancers, see the [Psychic Sensitivity](/wiki/Psychic_Sensitivity "Psychic Sensitivity") page.
