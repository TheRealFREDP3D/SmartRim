## Summary[[edit](/index.php?title=Prisoner&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Summary")]

Prisoners are ordinary pawns under restraint. They have no [Recreation](/wiki/Recreation "Recreation") needs, but all other needs and most [moodlets](/wiki/Mood "Mood") apply to them. This includes a need for [food](/wiki/Food "Food") and the chance for [mental breaks](/wiki/Mental_break "Mental break"). They are confined to their cell and will not do work. Prisoners retain their faction allegiance; imprisoned colonists who are released will immediately rejoin the colony, while other prisoners will try and leave the map. Prisoner restraints reduce their [move speed](/wiki/Move_speed "Move speed") by 65%. 

Prisoners cannot open doors if not undergoing a prison break. While inside your colony, they will try to leave if there is an opening, such as a hole in the wall or a held open door, including if it is held open because of an item inside. A prisoner that can move will wear the most comfortable[Verify/Detail] clothing placed in their cell. They eat the food with the best mood buff that they can reach, ignoring food restrictions. Mobile prisoners can use a [nutrient paste dispenser](/wiki/Nutrient_paste_dispenser "Nutrient paste dispenser") that faces into their prison. Otherwise, wardens will bring any food allowed by their [food restrictions](/wiki/Food_restriction "Food restriction"). A prisoner's medical settings, as well as defaults for all prisoners, can be adjusted in their Health tab. 

Colonists assigned to Warden can interact with a prisoner in multiple different ways, including delivering food and recruitment. Colonists assigned to either Warden or Doctoring can feed a prisoner in medical rest. See #Interaction for more details. 

### Unwaveringly loyal[[edit](/index.php?title=Prisoner&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Unwaveringly loyal")]

Unwaveringly loyal prisoners are unable to be recruited to your colony, and do not have resistance to lower. They are otherwise the same as regular prisoners, including the ability to become a [slave](/wiki/Slave "Slave").[![Content added by the Ideology DLC](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/16px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Content added by the Ideology DLC") Unwaveringly loyal pawns without a faction will be described as "not recruitable". 

Unwaveringly loyal prisoners can be recruited after undergoing the [Brainwipe](/wiki/Brainwipe "Brainwipe") psychic ritual.[![Content added by the Anomaly DLC](/images/thumb/3/32/AnomalyIcon.png/16px-AnomalyIcon.png)](/wiki/Anomaly_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Anomaly DLC") Additionally, if an unwaveringly loyal pawn joins your colony through any means, they will lose their unwavering loyalty. Thus, unwaveringly loyal pawns from a temporary faction (such as the [refugee faction](/wiki/Factions#Refugees "Factions") [![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC")) or [no faction](/wiki/Factions#Factionless_humans "Factions") can be recruited by [enslaving](/wiki/Slavery "Slavery") [![Content added by the Ideology DLC](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/16px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Content added by the Ideology DLC") them, which makes them a member of the player's faction, and then emancipating them, which makes them a normal member of the player's colony. Friendly [shamblers](/wiki/Shamblers "Shamblers") [![Content added by the Anomaly DLC](/images/thumb/3/32/AnomalyIcon.png/16px-AnomalyIcon.png)](/wiki/Anomaly_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Anomaly DLC") also automatically join the player's faction, which will remove the unwavering loyalty of a prisoner, though they will have to brought back to life with a [resurrector mech serum](/wiki/Resurrector_mech_serum "Resurrector mech serum") to be recruited as a normal colonist. 

Unwavering loyalty can be disabled in the [storyteller settings](/wiki/Storyteller_settings "Storyteller settings"). Note that the enabling unwaveringly loyal pawns decreases the percentage of raiders that are automatically killed on [downing](/wiki/Down "Down") to almost exactly make up for the percentage of pawns that are now unrecruitable - this means that the number of recruitable prisoners remains constant with either option, but the total number of prisoners increases with unwaveringly loyal enabled. This is pure benefit if the player only intends to recruit from the pool of randomly downed raiders, however if the player was willing to specifically target good pawns for safe-downing and recruitment, such as through [bleed](/wiki/Blood_loss "Blood loss") kiting or [psychic shock lance](/wiki/Psychic_shock_lance "Psychic shock lance"), then some portion of those previously recruitable pawns will instead be loyal and thus unavailable. 

### Caravans[[edit](/index.php?title=Prisoner&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Caravans")]

Prisoners can be loaded into a [caravan](/wiki/Caravan "Caravan"). If they can walk, they can carry 35 kg worth of items, like your colonists. When inside a caravan, the prisoner will never try and escape unless its part of a [mental break](/wiki/Mental_break "Mental break"). 

If the caravan enters a loaded map (such as an ambush), the prisoner will wander around idly, and may walk into the crossfire. Enemies will also target prisoners if their factions are enemies. 

## Capturing prisoners[[edit](/index.php?title=Prisoner&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Capturing prisoners")]

In order to capture any prisoner, you need a valid prison, which is enclosed and has an open [bed](/wiki/Bed "Bed") or [sleeping spot](/wiki/Sleeping_spot "Sleeping spot") (See #Prisons below). 

The gameplay steps required to capture a pawn depends on their state: 

  * [Downed](/wiki/Downed "Downed") pawns can be captured with 100% success, without drafting a pawn. Select a colonist, and right click the future prisoner. 
    * [Raiders](/wiki/Raider "Raider"), [berserk](/wiki/Berserk "Berserk") pawns, and former prisoners in a [prison break](/wiki/Prison_break "Prison break") are unable to be captured directly. They must first be [downed](/wiki/Downed "Downed").
    * NOTE: Raiders in particular have a [chance to die](/wiki/AI_Storytellers#Enemy_death_on_downed "AI Storytellers") whenever they are downed from [pain](/wiki/Pain "Pain") \- such as from being injured. This does not apply if the pawn is downed from other causes; some ways to get around this include: 
      * [Psychic shock lance](/wiki/Psychic_shock_lance "Psychic shock lance"), although there is a risk of burning the brain
      * [Blood loss](/wiki/Blood_loss "Blood loss")
      * [Tox gas](/wiki/Tox_gas "Tox gas"),[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC") although not all xenotypes are affected, and [Toxic buildup](/wiki/Toxic_buildup "Toxic buildup") also has risks
  * To _arrest_ a colonist, guest, ally, or [slave](/wiki/Slave "Slave"),[![Content added by the Ideology DLC](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/16px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Content added by the Ideology DLC") you need to [draft](/wiki/Draft "Draft") a colonist and select the target to capture. The colonist will then walk up to the target and attempt a capture. The chance of success depends on their [Arrest Success Chance](/wiki/Arrest_Success_Chance "Arrest Success Chance"), and will be displayed on the prompt. Arrest success chance largely depends on [Social skill](/wiki/Social_skill "Social skill"), but is negatively affected by [Manipulation](/wiki/Manipulation "Manipulation") penalties. If the arrest fails, the target will go [berserk](/wiki/Berserk "Berserk"). Pawns with the [dead calm](/wiki/Dead_calm "Dead calm") gene[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC") always have a 100% success chance, regardless of arrester stats, and thus will never go berserk when arrested. 
    * Colonists can be captured even during a [mental break](/wiki/Mental_break "Mental break"), except for [berserk](/wiki/Berserk "Berserk"). This immediately ends most mental breaks, without catharsis. When released, colonists will get the −6 _Was imprisoned_ [moodlet](/wiki/Mood "Mood") for 12 days. This mood penalty will not occur if the colonist is instead re-recruited. Arrests will not end the [run wild](/wiki/Mental_break#Run_wild "Mental break") or [catatonic breakdown](/wiki/Mental_break#Catatonic_breakdown "Mental break") mental breaks.



If you attempt to capture a pawn from a neutral or allied [faction](/wiki/Faction "Faction"), the faction will immediately turn hostile. This includes [transport pod](/wiki/Transport_pod "Transport pod") crashes, if they happen to be part of an existing faction. 

If you need to capture a pawn, then [sleeping spots](/wiki/Sleeping_spot "Sleeping spot") can be used to instantly create a prison and bed space anywhere indoors. You can also turn a colonist bedroom into a prison. 

### Reasons to imprison[[edit](/index.php?title=Prisoner&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Reasons to imprison")]

  * To recruit raiders, guests, or transport pod crashes into a colony.
  * To use raiders for their [human resources](/wiki/Human_resources "Human resources").
  * To end most [mental breaks](/wiki/Mental_break "Mental break"). Colonists can immediately be released afterwards.
  * To keep a colonist restrained in order to overcome [drug](/wiki/Drug "Drug") withdrawal.
  * To convert a colonist or [slave](/wiki/Slave "Slave") to a desired [ideoligion](/wiki/Ideoligion "Ideoligion").[![Content added by the Ideology DLC](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/16px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Content added by the Ideology DLC")
  * To act as sources of [bloodfeeding](/wiki/Bloodfeeder "Bloodfeeder") or [hemogen packs](/wiki/Hemogen_pack "Hemogen pack").[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC")
  * To sell the prisoner.
  * For raiders, to release them for a goodwill gain of the enemy faction (faction must not be permanently hostile and the released prisoner must exit the map healthy).



## Prisons[[edit](/index.php?title=Prisoner&action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: Prisons")]

[![](/images/thumb/5/56/Thank_you_Megasloth_for_letting_my_prisoners_run.png/350px-Thank_you_Megasloth_for_letting_my_prisoners_run.png)](/wiki/File:Thank_you_Megasloth_for_letting_my_prisoners_run.png)

[](/wiki/File:Thank_you_Megasloth_for_letting_my_prisoners_run.png "Enlarge")

Megasloth getting sheared at the exit of a prison door, holding it open for a prisoner to escape.

Prisons are required to hold prisoners. 

### Creation[[edit](/index.php?title=Prisoner&action=edit&section=7 "Edit section: Creation")]

A prison must be designated in a fully enclosed [room](/wiki/Room "Room"), closed off with [walls](/wiki/Wall "Wall") and other impassable objects like [doors](/wiki/Door "Door"), [coolers](/wiki/Cooler "Cooler") and [nutrient paste dispensers](/wiki/Nutrient_paste_dispenser "Nutrient paste dispenser"). Prisoners cannot be captured unless the prison is enclosed, and mobile prisoners will walk out of the map if an opening exists. 

In order to actually form a prison, at least 1 [bed](/wiki/Bed "Bed") or [sleeping spot](/wiki/Sleeping_spot "Sleeping spot") must be placed. Selecting the bed allows you to set "For Prisoners". Colonists and [slaves](/wiki/Slaves "Slaves")[![Content added by the Ideology DLC](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/16px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Content added by the Ideology DLC") may not share the same room as a prisoner; when 1 bed is set to prisoners, all beds will be converted to prison beds. The beds will turn orange to signify this, and the whole room gains an orange outline when selected. In order to actually capture a prisoner, there must be an open sleeping area for them to stay. 

### General[[edit](/index.php?title=Prisoner&action=edit&section=8 "Edit section: General")]

A prison is called a _prison cell_ if there is 1 bed available, or a _prison barracks_ if there are multiple beds. A prison is otherwise treated as a bed room or barracks, including [mood](/wiki/Mood "Mood") impacts for its Impressiveness. 

Prisoners can only use items that are in their cell. Food, beds, and [nutrient paste dispensers](/wiki/Nutrient_paste_dispenser "Nutrient paste dispenser") placed inside a prison are reserved for prisoner use. While a colonist can be ordered to eat food placed in a prison cell, they will not automatically do so. Colonists on the food binge [mental break](/wiki/Mental_break "Mental break"), as well as [animals](/wiki/Animal "Animal"), will ignore a prison for this purpose. 

Unless you want your prisoners to die, make sure that it is hospitable. Regulate the [temperature](/wiki/Temperature "Temperature"), or give prisoners sufficiently insulating [clothing](/wiki/Clothing "Clothing"), such as [tribalwear](/wiki/Tribalwear "Tribalwear"). Make sure that food gets to a prisoner (either via a storage zone, warden delivery, or [nutrient paste dispenser](/wiki/Nutrient_paste_dispenser "Nutrient paste dispenser")). Otherwise, see #Mood regulation for how to make your prison better for the inmates... or worse. 

## Interaction[[edit](/index.php?title=Prisoner&action=edit&section=9 "Edit section: Interaction")]

Players can assign wardens to do the following tasks: 

  * **Recruit** \- Reduces the prisoner's resistance, then attempts to recruit.
  * **Reduce resistance** \- Reduces the prisoner's resistance, does not recruit.
  * **Release** \- Releases the prisoner. Imprisoned colonists will rejoin the colony, while other factions will attempt to leave the map.
  * **Execute** \- Order wardens to kill the prisoner.



The following options are added by [DLC](/wiki/DLC "DLC"): 

  * **Enslave**[![Content added by the Ideology DLC](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/16px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Content added by the Ideology DLC") \- Reduces the prisoner's will, then forces them into [slavery](/wiki/Slave "Slave").
  * **Reduce will**[![Content added by the Ideology DLC](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/16px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Content added by the Ideology DLC") \- Reduces the prisoner's will, does not enslave them.
  * **Convert**[![Content added by the Ideology DLC](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/16px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Content added by the Ideology DLC") \- Attempts to convert the prisoner into a warden's [ideoligion](/wiki/Ideoligion "Ideoligion").
  * **Bloodfeed**[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC") \- [Sanguophages](/wiki/Sanguophage "Sanguophage") and other pawns with the [Bloodfeeder](/wiki/Bloodfeeder "Bloodfeeder") [gene](/wiki/Gene "Gene") will drink the prisoner's blood.
  * **Hemogen Farm**[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC") \- Pawns assigned to the [Doctor](/wiki/Work#Doctor "Work") worktype will automatically extract [hemogen packs](/wiki/Hemogen_pack "Hemogen pack") from the prisoner.



### Resistance[[edit](/index.php?title=Prisoner&action=edit&section=10 "Edit section: Resistance")]

See also: Recruitment

Prisoners start with **resistance** , or unwillingness to join the colony. This must be lowered to 0 for recruitment attempts to begin. Unwaveringly loyal prisoners do not have a resistance stat, and can't be recruited. 

Under the _Recruit_ or _Reduce Resistance_ orders, wardens will attempt to lower the resistance. How much is reduced depends on a prisoner's [mood](/wiki/Mood "Mood"), the warden's [Negotiation Ability](/wiki/Negotiation_Ability "Negotiation Ability"), and the prisoner's [opinion](/wiki/Opinion "Opinion") of the warden. Wardens with more [Social skill](/wiki/Social_skill "Social skill") have a greater Negotiation Ability. There is a delay between each conversation. 

Once resistance hits 0, wardens will try to convince the prisoner to join the colony, with a chance to succeed, depending on each prisoner's recruitment difficulty percentage, their current mood, faction, how many colonists you currently have, as well as your [storyteller](/wiki/Storyteller "Storyteller"). If the prisoner is assigned to _Reduce Resistance_ , then wardens will continue to converse without actually recruiting. 

For exact mechanics and chances of recruitment, see #Recruitment. 

#### Will[[edit](/index.php?title=Prisoner&action=edit&section=11 "Edit section: Will")]

Enslave[![Content added by the Ideology DLC](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/16px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Content added by the Ideology DLC") and Reduce Will[![Content added by the Ideology DLC](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/16px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Content added by the Ideology DLC") work analogously to Recruit and Reduce Resistance, except that the end result is that the prisoner becomes a [slave](/wiki/Slave "Slave").[![Content added by the Ideology DLC](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/16px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Content added by the Ideology DLC") A prisoner starts with much less will than they do resistance. 

#### Tame[[edit](/index.php?title=Prisoner&action=edit&section=12 "Edit section: Tame")]

A [wild man](/wiki/Wild_man "Wild man") can be arrested and imprisoned. However, they must be [tamed](/wiki/Tame "Tame") using the usual [animal](/wiki/Animal "Animal") mechanics, rather than being recruited as a prisoner. Once tamed, they will join the colony as a colonist. 

### Release[[edit](/index.php?title=Prisoner&action=edit&section=13 "Edit section: Release")]

Wardens will take the prisoner outside your colony, and free the prisoner. "Outside the colony" means beyond your outermost line of connected walls, so it could be just out the door, or across most of the map if your defenses stretch that far. 

Releasing a prisoner of another faction will give a [goodwill](/wiki/Goodwill "Goodwill") boost of +12. In order to count towards goodwill, the prisoner must successfully leave the map healthy, with any wounds tended to. Prisoners that can't walk can't be released. Pirates and savage tribes do not interact with goodwill, so releasing them has no effect. 

Releasing a colonist will have them rejoin your colony. [Slaves](/wiki/Slave "Slave") retain their home faction. 

### Execute[[edit](/index.php?title=Prisoner&action=edit&section=14 "Edit section: Execute")]

Wardens will cut a prisoner's neck immediately, killing them without fail. This is a different action from the _Prisoner execution_ [ritual](/wiki/Ritual "Ritual"). 

Executing guilty prisoners - those who have harmed the colony in 24 hours - give a −2 [moodlet](/wiki/Mood "Mood") to non-[Psychopath](/wiki/Psychopath "Psychopath") colonists. Executing a non-guilty prisoner increases the penalty to −5. 

Colonists following an [ideoligion](/wiki/Ideoligion "Ideoligion")[![Content added by the Ideology DLC](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/16px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Content added by the Ideology DLC") with the [Execution: Respected if Guilty](/wiki/Ideoligion#Execution "Ideoligion") precept instead gain a +3 [moodlet](/wiki/Mood "Mood") for executing a guilty prisoner, with the executioner gaining +10 mood instead. The [Execution: Required](/wiki/Ideoligion#Execution "Ideoligion") precept gains the same moodlets (but shorter) for executing anyone. If the execution was desired by precept, then fluid ideoligions gain a development point. 

Executed prisoners who are then [resurrected](/wiki/Resurrector_mech_serum "Resurrector mech serum") will have the execute toggle deselected to prevent their immediate re-execution. 

### Convert[[edit](/index.php?title=Prisoner&action=edit&section=15 "Edit section: Convert")]

[![IdeologyIcon.png](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/40px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_\(DLC\) "Ideology \(DLC\)") | **This section relates to content added by[Ideology (DLC)](/wiki/Ideology_\(DLC\) "Ideology \(DLC\)").** Please note that it will not be present without the [DLC](/wiki/Downloadable_content "Downloadable content") enabled.

This is functionally the same as [regular conversion](/wiki/Ideoligion#Conversion "Ideoligion") that can occur randomly between colonists, except that it is done on a regular basis.

#### Bloodfeed[[edit](/index.php?title=Prisoner&action=edit&section=17 "Edit section: Bloodfeed")]

Pawns with the [Bloodfeeder](/wiki/Bloodfeeder "Bloodfeeder") [gene](/wiki/Gene "Gene") will automatically drink the blood from a prisoner, directly. This happens when a bloodfeeder is below their desired Hemogen and if the bloodfeeding would not kill the prisoner. 

#### Hemogen farm[[edit](/index.php?title=Prisoner&action=edit&section=18 "Edit section: Hemogen farm")]

Automatically orders the operation extract [hemogen pack](/wiki/Hemogen_pack "Hemogen pack") when a prisoner's [blood loss](/wiki/Blood_loss "Blood loss") level reaches 0. In addition to creating drinkable hemogen, it also trains the surgeon's Medical skill. 

### Other[[edit](/index.php?title=Prisoner&action=edit&section=19 "Edit section: Other")]

  * Prisoners can be escorted by a warden to a prisoner bed. Prisoners can be escorted to a prisoner medical bed, if needed, without a risk of escape.
  * Prisoners can initiate social fights with other prisoners.
  * Prisoners can be given any number of medical [operations](/wiki/Operation "Operation"). This includes injecting [drugs](/wiki/Drug "Drug"), installing [artificial body parts](/wiki/Artificial_body_parts "Artificial body parts"), [organ harvesting](/wiki/Organ_harvesting "Organ harvesting"), and more.
  * Prisoners are valid targets for a [gene extractor](/wiki/Gene_extractor "Gene extractor"),[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC") [subcore softscanner](/wiki/Subcore_softscanner "Subcore softscanner"),[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC") or [subcore ripscanner](/wiki/Subcore_ripscanner "Subcore ripscanner").[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC")
  * Prisoners can be sold to a [faction base](/wiki/Faction_base "Faction base") or slaver for [silver](/wiki/Silver "Silver"), or a royal tribute collector[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC") for [honor](/wiki/Honor "Honor"). Colonists who do not like slavery will get a -3 moodlet, modified by [ideoligion](/wiki/Ideoligion "Ideoligion").[![Content added by the Ideology DLC](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/16px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Content added by the Ideology DLC")
  * If a prisoner dies, the mood penalty depends on their cause of death. "Intentional" means, like violent combat, count as an execution.



## Prison break[[edit](/index.php?title=Prisoner&action=edit&section=20 "Edit section: Prison break")]

    "_Prisoners have staged a breakout! They've somehow defeated the door lock and are making their way out. They may seek weapons, fight, or try to escape._ "  


Prisoners can escape via opened [doors](/wiki/Door "Door") or gaps in the walls in their cell. In addition, prisoners also have a small chance to actively break out and escape. The time between escape attempts is affected by the following factors: 

  * The number and type of [doors](/wiki/Door "Door") in their cell. Having all doors to a cell be [security doors](/wiki/Security_door "Security door")[![Content added by the Anomaly DLC](/images/thumb/3/32/AnomalyIcon.png/16px-AnomalyIcon.png)](/wiki/Anomaly_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Anomaly DLC") increases prison break interval compared to other doors, while having more than one door of any type for a cell decreases prison break interval.
  * The [Moving](/wiki/Moving "Moving") capacity of the individual prisoner. The effect varies by the capacity value and pawns with 0% moving will never attempt to escape.
  * [Genes relating to aggression](/wiki/Genes#Violence "Genes").[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC")
  * Number of prisoners. Prison break instances are rolled per prisoner, meaning that the more prisoners you have, the more likely a prison break is to happen in a given period, despite not directly affecting each individual prisoner's mean time between prison breaks.
  * The map must have an exit for a prison break to occur. If there is no traditional exit- such as maps that are in [orbit](/wiki/Orbit "Orbit")[![Content added by the Odyssey DLC](/images/thumb/7/7b/OdysseyIcon.png/16px-OdysseyIcon.png)](/wiki/Odyssey_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Odyssey DLC") or [underground](/wiki/Underground "Underground")\- a prison break cannot occur.
  * A prisoner after a [bliss lobotomy](/wiki/Bliss_lobotomy "Bliss lobotomy")[![Content added by the Anomaly DLC](/images/thumb/3/32/AnomalyIcon.png/16px-AnomalyIcon.png)](/wiki/Anomaly_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Anomaly DLC") surgery will have increased prison break interval



Once it happens, all rooms within 20 tiles of the prisoner who started the prison break have a chance to join it. The original room will always join. Other rooms have a 50% chance to join. If a room does join, all prisoners in that room are affected too. 

During a prison break, prisoners are able to open all doors. They will attempt[Under what conditions?] to equip and use [weapons](/wiki/Weapons "Weapons") and weapon-like [utility](/wiki/Utility "Utility") items such as [orbital bombardment targeters](/wiki/Orbital_bombardment_targeter "Orbital bombardment targeter").[Which?] Because of this, it is recommended to set armories far away from your prisons and watch out for any weapon dropped by your own pawns. Additionally, placing the exits away from the edge of the map will increase the time it takes for the escapees to exit the map. 

Since prisoners are technically part of an enemy faction, any friendlies[Same faction?] on the map will engage them and your own pawns will react to them as specified by their [threat response](/wiki/Threat_response "Threat response"). While these reactions can be helpful if you need to contain a larger break, the use of higher-powered weapons means they have a higher chance of outright killing the escapees. Recapturing escaped prisoners from factions that have turned friendly since that prisoner was originally captured will not affect relations with that faction. 

To contain a prison break while reducing the risk of killing the escaping prisoners, low damage weapons are recommended. Alternatively, brain implants that cause [brain shock](/wiki/Brain_shock "Brain shock")[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC") can be implanted in long term or particularly unruly prisoners, and [EMP](/wiki/EMP "EMP") weapons used to down them instantly and safely. 

## Recruitment[[edit](/index.php?title=Prisoner&action=edit&section=21 "Edit section: Recruitment")]

In order to start recruitment, a target pawn must first be captured, and become a prisoner. Prisoners then must be set to "Recruit" (or "Reduce Resistance" and then "Recruit") in the "Prisoner" tab of their personal info box (bottom left of screen when pawn is selected). 

Then colonists assigned to wardening will "chat" with the target prisoner. This can be repeated every several hours, each one reducing Resistance by a predictable amount (based on the Social pawn's Social skill and the target's Mood). 

### Starting resistance[[edit](/index.php?title=Prisoner&action=edit&section=22 "Edit section: Starting resistance")]

Each prisoner has their own recruitment difficulty based on their "Pawn Kind". See the [Raider](/wiki/Raider "Raider") page for all pawn kinds and resistance intervals. A random value is chosen based on the Pawn Kind's resistance interval. For example, a "Warrior" will have a prisoner resistance between 5 and 9, chosen randomly. 

### Resistance reduction[[edit](/index.php?title=Prisoner&action=edit&section=23 "Edit section: Resistance reduction")]

A warden will reduce a base of 1.0 resistance per conversation. This is multiplied by: 

**Prisoner's[opinion](/wiki/Opinion "Opinion") of the warden:**

  * At -100 opinion, the resistance reduction factor is 50%.
  * At 0 opinion, the resistance reduction factor is 100%.
  * At 100 opinion, the resistance reduction factor is 150%.



**Prisoner[mood](/wiki/Mood "Mood"):**

  * At 0% current mood, the resistance reduction factor is 20%.
  * At 50% mood, the resistance reduction factor is 100%.
  * At 100% mood, the resistance reduction factor is 150%.



**Negotiation Ability:**

  * See [Negotiation Ability](/wiki/Negotiation_Ability "Negotiation Ability").



#### Word of Trust[[edit](/index.php?title=Prisoner&action=edit&section=24 "Edit section: Word of Trust")]

The [Word of trust](/wiki/Word_of_trust "Word of trust") psycast [![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC") will reduce resistance by 12, multiplied directly by the prisoner's [Psychic Sensitivity](/wiki/Psychic_Sensitivity "Psychic Sensitivity") and no other factors. 

## Tips[[edit](/index.php?title=Prisoner&action=edit&section=25 "Edit section: Tips")]

### Mood regulation[[edit](/index.php?title=Prisoner&action=edit&section=26 "Edit section: Mood regulation")]

[![StumpChoppedA \(cropped\).png](/images/thumb/6/6a/StumpChoppedA_%28cropped%29.png/40px-StumpChoppedA_%28cropped%29.png)](/wiki/Category:Stubs "Category:Stubs") | **This section is a[stub](/wiki/Category:Stubs "Category:Stubs").** You can help RimWorld Wiki by **[expanding it](https://rimworldwiki.com/index.php?title=Prisoner&action=edit). Reason:** _Only some mental breaks are available to prisoners. Which? Expand on how that affects strategy and options._  
---|---  
  
Increasing [mood](/wiki/Mood "Mood") makes prisoners easier to recruit, and less likely to go [berserk](/wiki/Berserk "Berserk"). If you want to increase prisoner mood, you can do the following: 

  * Give prisoners separate rooms, preferably one that isn't cramped.
  * Give a prisoners a source of [light](/wiki/Light "Light"), a [table](/wiki/Table "Table") and [chair](/wiki/Chair "Chair"), and a decent [bed](/wiki/Bed "Bed").
  * Increase the impressiveness of a room with [sculptures](/wiki/Sculpture "Sculpture"), [stone tiles](/wiki/Stone_tile "Stone tile"), etc.
  * Give the prisoner [fine meals](/wiki/Fine_meal "Fine meal"), [lavish meals](/wiki/Lavish_meal "Lavish meal"), or even [drugs](/wiki/Drug "Drug").



### Changing ideoligion[[edit](/index.php?title=Prisoner&action=edit&section=27 "Edit section: Changing ideoligion")]

[![IdeologyIcon.png](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/40px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_\(DLC\) "Ideology \(DLC\)") | **This section relates to content added by[Ideology (DLC)](/wiki/Ideology_\(DLC\) "Ideology \(DLC\)").** Please note that it will not be present without the [DLC](/wiki/Downloadable_content "Downloadable content") enabled.  
---|---  
  
If you want to change the target's [ideoligion](/wiki/Ideoligion "Ideoligion"), then instead try and make the prisoner upset. Lower mood reduces [certainty](/wiki/Certainty "Certainty") gain, and may cause a [Crisis of Belief](/wiki/Mental_break#Crisis_of_belief "Mental break") extreme mental break, which reduces certainty by 50%. The following can help with decreasing mood: 

  * Keep prisoners in a confined, dark space, without a bed or furniture. Even if there's only 1 prisoner, placing 2 [sleeping spots](/wiki/Sleeping_spot "Sleeping spot") will create a prison barracks.
  * Feed the pawn [human meat](/wiki/Human_meat "Human meat"), [insect meat](/wiki/Insect_meat "Insect meat"), [raw food](/wiki/Raw_food "Raw food"), or [kibble](/wiki/Kibble "Kibble"). Certain ideoligions can make some of these foods more desirable, but kibble is never desirable to eat.
  * Keep them in [pain](/wiki/Pain "Pain"). Inflicting [injury](/wiki/Injury "Injury") comes at the risk of scarring or permanent damage, however, and a [mindscrew](/wiki/Mindscrew "Mindscrew") [![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC") cannot be removed by traditional methods once the pawn is converted. A [torture crown](/wiki/Torture_crown "Torture crown") is a risk free way of inflicting pain, but dressing prisoners has its own difficulties.



Keep in mind that crises of belief that would drop a pawn's certainty to or below 0%, will instead convert the pawn to a weighted randomly selected ideoligion with a new certainty. While this process might select the intended ideoligion, it may not while also requiring their certainty be reduced again. If a pawn's certainty is below 50%, consider improving their mood above their extreme [mental break threshold](/wiki/Mental_break_threshold "Mental break threshold") to prevent this. 

It is also worth considering the individual pawn when deciding on an approach. The first concern are the [traits](/wiki/Traits "Traits") \- many traits affect the [Global Certainty Loss Factor](/wiki/Global_Certainty_Loss_Factor "Global Certainty Loss Factor") of a pawn, which in turn affect the efficacy of conversion attempts by wardens. For pawns with a high factor, traditional conversion will be effective, while forcing a crisis of belief may be all but necessary. For further details, including a list of traits that affect the factor, see [Certainty](/wiki/Certainty "Certainty") and [Global Certainty Loss Factor](/wiki/Global_Certainty_Loss_Factor "Global Certainty Loss Factor"). 

The second concern are [xenotypes](/wiki/Xenotypes "Xenotypes")[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC") with the [Aggressive](/wiki/Aggressive "Aggressive") and [Hyper-aggressive](/wiki/Hyper-aggressive "Hyper-aggressive") genes.[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC") Those with aggressive, including all [yttakin](/wiki/Yttakin "Yttakin"),[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC") [wasters](/wiki/Wasters "Wasters"),[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC") [neanderthals](/wiki/Neanderthals "Neanderthals"),[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC") and [sanguophages](/wiki/Sanguophages "Sanguophages"),[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC") are much more likely to have a berserk mental break, proportionally reducing the chances of a crisis of belief. Meanwhile those with hyper-aggressive will essentially never select the crisis, making pawns such as [hussars](/wiki/Hussars "Hussars")[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC") all but impossible to convert by crisis. 

### Prisoner suppression[[edit](/index.php?title=Prisoner&action=edit&section=28 "Edit section: Prisoner suppression")]

Whenever they are [berserk](/wiki/Berserk "Berserk"), under a prison break, or just escaping, sometimes you will need to subdue a prisoner. When berserk, prisoners will fight other prisoners, then will punch the weakest area (usually a [door](/wiki/Door "Door")) to escape. When under a prisoner break, they can just open the door. 

If you need to keep the prisoner alive, it is best to use low damage weapons and attacks - even just melee attacks with a gun or bare fists. Several unskilled pawns work better than 1 skilled one. For berserkers, you can have a skilled [Construction](/wiki/Construction "Construction") pawn constantly repair the door. This trains Construction but is labor intensive. You can also wall the prisoner in, which will cause them to stop attacking the door and just wander around in their cell instead. 

If free roaming [animals](/wiki/Animal "Animal") are within the prison, then [berserking](/wiki/Berserk "Berserk") prisoners will prioritize them over breaking out. You can place [animal sleeping spots](/wiki/Animal_sleeping_spot "Animal sleeping spot") inside the prison for an automatic form of prisoner suppression. Note that animals are slightly more dangerous than humans. In addition, most animals will create a large amount of [filth](/wiki/Filth "Filth"), quickly lowering the room's quality. You may want to use [straw matting](/wiki/Straw_matting "Straw matting"). 

### Unwanted prisoners[[edit](/index.php?title=Prisoner&action=edit&section=29 "Edit section: Unwanted prisoners")]

Enemies who have fallen in battle may not always be worth capturing in the first place. For example, a hostile with a shattered spine will require a [bionic spine](/wiki/Bionic_spine "Bionic spine") to replace, which may not be worth it. Unwavering prisoners can't be recruited normally, so fall under the same boat. 

There are several ways to dispose of these prisoners: 

  * **Release:** The least upsetting way to remove a prisoner. Colonists will not become upset if an enemy prisoner is released, and it may increase [goodwill](/wiki/Goodwill "Goodwill") by a small amount.
  * **Expendables:** This is not exactly "getting rid of them", as much as making best (and ultimately temporary) use of them. Recruit them, and send them out for a long [caravan](/wiki/Caravan "Caravan") that you wouldn't expend another colonist for. You can set them on a trading expedition, or scout a base/map. Or, you can use them as a meatshield. A "colonist" dying still gives a negative moodlet (-3, same as incidental death or non-guilty euthanasia), which is affected by [opinion](/wiki/Opinion "Opinion"). Cut off their tongue to prevent social interaction from happening. Of course, unwavering prisoners can't be recruited.
  * **Euthanasia:** Execute in a more humane fashion by neck cut. This costs 1x [herbal medicine](/wiki/Herbal_medicine "Herbal medicine") (or better) and trains Medical experience. An 'innocent' prisoner going through euthanasia is less upsetting than an innocent execution.
  * **Execution:** Execute a prisoner in a raw manner. Those with an [ideoligion](/wiki/Ideoligion "Ideoligion") favoring execution will enjoy it. With a fluid ideoligion, execution is a very easy way to gain development points (so long as it is Respected if Guilty).
  * **[Human resources](/wiki/Human_resources "Human resources"):** Use prisoners for their parts. Artificial limbs can be removed without penalty. 
    * [Organ harvesting](/wiki/Organ_harvesting "Organ harvesting"): A [kidney](/wiki/Kidney "Kidney") and [lung](/wiki/Lung "Lung") can be harvested without death, and a [heart](/wiki/Heart "Heart"), [liver](/wiki/Liver "Liver"), or second kidney/lung can be taken to kill. Unless Organ Use is acceptable by [ideoligion](/wiki/Ideoligion "Ideoligion"), this comes with a -6 [mood](/wiki/Mood "Mood") penalty (stacks, but diminishing) for the colony, and even more if the prisoner was killed.
    * Used for a [gene extractor](/wiki/Gene_extractor "Gene extractor")[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC") or [subcore ripscanner](/wiki/Subcore_ripscanner "Subcore ripscanner"),[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC") the latter of which is fatal.
  * **Selling:** Prisoners can be sold for money at a [faction base](/wiki/Faction_base "Faction base") or slaver, or exchanged for [honor](/wiki/Honor "Honor")[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC") from a royal tribute collector. Each sold prisoner gives -3 [mood](/wiki/Mood "Mood").
