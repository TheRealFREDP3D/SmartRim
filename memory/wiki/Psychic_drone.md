For a guide to survive events, see [Events Guide](/wiki/Events_Guide "Events Guide").

**Events** are driven by the currently selected [AI storyteller](/wiki/AI_Storytellers "AI Storytellers"). 

Most events that occur create a [Letter](/wiki/Menu#Letters "Menu"), an envelope icon on the right side of the screen. Other events will pause the game and pop up a window where the player must make a choice. Usually, the color of the envelope will suggest the severity of the event; blue envelopes are good events, grey is neutral, yellow is bad, and red denotes direct threats. 

Many event titles are variable. They can mention location, pawn and faction names, specific buffs or debuffs, or even have countdowns for the time variable events. 

There are special kinds of events called [Quests](/wiki/Quests "Quests") that work under different rules.

## Major threats[[edit](/index.php?title=Events&action=edit&section=7 "Edit section: Major threats")]

Major threats can appear from Base Builder difficulty onwards. They mostly consist of direct attacks to your colony. Their size depends on the [raid points](/wiki/Raid_points "Raid points") mechanic; generally increasing in strength with wealth, colonist count, and [difficulty](/wiki/Difficulty "Difficulty"). 

[Cassandra Classic](/wiki/Cassandra_Classic "Cassandra Classic") and [Phoebe Chillax](/wiki/Phoebe_Chillax "Phoebe Chillax") both treat major threats separately from other events; they cycle between "OnDays" and "OffDays", where a certain amount of major events _will_ occur during each OnDay cycle. [Randy Random](/wiki/Randy_Random "Randy Random") simply treats a major threat as an event that's less likely to happen.

### Major threats generation[[edit](/index.php?title=Events&action=edit&section=8 "Edit section: Major threats generation")]

Major threats can be fired via 3 different methods: storyteller, quest, and special fired. Storyteller major threats are fired according to the Storyteller's schedule. Randy's "schedule" is a random firing mechanic. Quest major threats must be explicitly accepted by the player. Special fired major threats are limited to Wastepack Infestations that can spawn when a wastepack deteriorates ([Biotech DLC](/wiki/Biotech "Biotech")[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC")).

### Raid Generation[[edit](/index.php?title=Events&action=edit&section=9 "Edit section: Raid Generation")]

After the storyteller has chosen to fire a major threat, and chosen to fire a raid, first the Faction of the Raid is chosen. Each faction is given a weight and all hostile factions are considered separately. Human factions each have a weight of 1. The mechanoid faction's weight changes with raid Points. As Raid Points go up the weight of the Mechanoid Faction Increases. Mechanoids weight is 0 until 300 raid points. The weight of human factions can become 0 at extreme temperatures. 

After the faction is chosen, the attack type is chosen. Each attack type has a weight. Some factions do not have all possible attack types. For example, Tribals cannot Siege. After the attack type is chosen, arrival method is chosen. Each arrival method has a weight. Some weights change based on raid points. Some arrival methods are not possible with some factions. Some attack types only arrive via certain methods. 

Once Rimworld has determined the faction, attack type and arrival type, a Raid Composition is selected. Each faction has defined raid compositions like the "all sniper" or "all melee attack". 

### Enemy attack[[edit](/index.php?title=Events&action=edit&section=10 "Edit section: Enemy attack")]

* * *

Main article: [Raids](/wiki/Raids "Raids")

Commonly known as a **raid** , this event comes in several types. It is possible for multiple raids to happen at the same time, even of opposing factions. [Raiders](/wiki/Raider "Raider") will come and assault your colony, first attacking adult colonists that they can walk to, then animals and buildings. Human raiders flee if enough of them have been [downed](/wiki/Downed "Downed") or killed, or if enough damage to your colony has been done. [Mechanoids](/wiki/Mechanoid "Mechanoid") will persist indefinitely. 

##### Arrival methods[[edit](/index.php?title=Events&action=edit&section=11 "Edit section: Arrival methods")]

Tribal-level factions can't use drop pods, so are limited to assaults. 

  * **Assaults** : Arrive at the edge of the map, either by walking or by [drop pod](/wiki/Drop_pod "Drop pod"). They may assault the colony immediately, or stage for a time before launching their attack, giving you time to prepare.
  * **Center drop** : Raiders immediately attack via drop pod, centered on one of your colonists. There is 40% to drop on an un[roofed](/wiki/Roof "Roof") [orbital trade beacon](/wiki/Orbital_trade_beacon "Orbital trade beacon") if one is present. Drop pods can go through constructed and thin rock roofs, but not overhead mountain.
  * **Scatter drop** : Raiders immediately attack via drop pod, which have gone "haywire" and will disperse throughout the map.



##### Types[[edit](/index.php?title=Events&action=edit&section=12 "Edit section: Types")]

  * **Smart** : "Unusually clever" raiders will seek to enter your base while avoiding [traps](/wiki/Trap "Trap") and [turrets](/wiki/Turret "Turret").
  * **Sappers** : Sappers will mine or destroy walls to create the shortest route to a colonist's bedroom. Groups of sappers are smaller in number than the other raid types.
  * **Breachers** : Appear with breach-type raiders, such as tribals with [breach axes](/wiki/Breach_axe "Breach axe") or [termite](/wiki/Termite "Termite") [mechanoids](/wiki/Mechanoid "Mechanoid"). Breachers will attack walls in their path indiscriminately, as opposed to sappers having a target.
  * **Siege** : See below.

#### Siege[[edit](/index.php?title=Events&action=edit&section=13 "Edit section: Siege")]

Sieges are a special type of raid. They arrive in drop pods, along with supplies. They will construct [sandbags](/wiki/Sandbag "Sandbag") and [mortars](/wiki/Mortar "Mortar"), and will continuously be supplied with food and mortar shells. They will fire at colonists, colony structures, and other pawns they are hostile to. Sieges will directly assault the colony after a long time (a few days), if their mortars are destroyed or unbuildable, or after sufficient casualties. 

It is possible to steal a critical material (like [components](/wiki/Component "Component")) and prevent them from completing construction. This is largely possible with a [psycaster](/wiki/Psycast "Psycast")[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC") using both Invisibility and Skip, with enough heat capacity to cast both 2-3 times in a row. 

Strategies to force sieges to attack include the [sniper rifle](/wiki/Sniper_rifle "Sniper rifle") (watch out for other snipers), a [psychic animal pulser](/wiki/Psychic_animal_pulser "Psychic animal pulser"), or your own [mortars](/wiki/Mortar "Mortar"). 

### Infestation[[edit](/index.php?title=Events&action=edit&section=14 "Edit section: Infestation")]

* * *

Main article: [Infestation](/wiki/Infestation "Infestation")

Infestations come with [hives](/wiki/Hive "Hive") and [insectoids](/wiki/Insectoid "Insectoid"). Hives will produce [insect jelly](/wiki/Insect_jelly "Insect jelly"), but will rapidly create insects, too. If you don't want it to infest the whole area, muster your forces and destroy it. But beware - the bugs will defend their hive. 

In order for a regular infestation to spawn, there must be an open area with the [overhead mountain](/wiki/Overhead_mountain "Overhead mountain") roof, within 30 tiles of a colony [structure](/wiki/Structure "Structure"), and a temperature above -17 °C (1.4 °F). Light, and temperatures below -8 °C (17.6 °F) reduce the chance of infestations. 

The [Too Deep: Infestation](/wiki/Infestation#Too_Deep:_Infestation "Infestation") is a separate event. [Insects](/wiki/Insect "Insect") can still arrive, but this event has separate mechanics. 

The [Wastepack infestations](/wiki/Infestation#Wastepack_infestation "Infestation") is a separate event available if [Biotech](/wiki/Biotech "Biotech")[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC") is active. The end effect is similar, but the mechanics are different. 

### Manhunter pack[[edit](/index.php?title=Events&action=edit&section=15 "Edit section: Manhunter pack")]

* * *

For the mental state that makes these animals hostile, see [Manhunter](/wiki/Manhunter "Manhunter").

A pack of [scaria](/wiki/Scaria "Scaria")-infested animals have arrived, [hunting](/wiki/Manhunter "Manhunter") for human flesh. A manhunter pack has 40% more points (i.e. 40% more "raiders") than a regular raid. A single animal can arrive and the event will still be called a "pack". 

The species of animal is randomly chosen from the list of possible wildlife species for the map's [biome](/wiki/Biome "Biome"), however only animals capable of passing [fences](/wiki/Fence "Fence") may be chosen - this effectively excludes all [pen animals](/wiki/Pen_animals "Pen animals"). If the map is an [Animal Habitat](/wiki/Landmarks#Animal_Habitat "Landmarks")[![Content added by the Odyssey DLC](/images/thumb/7/7b/OdysseyIcon.png/16px-OdysseyIcon.png)](/wiki/Odyssey_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Odyssey DLC"), and the species associated with the habitat can pass fences, that species has a 50% chance of being chosen for a manhunter pack, before considering any other options. Finally, the [pollution](/wiki/Pollution "Pollution")[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC") level on the map determines the likelihood of a pollution-type species being chosen. 

Manhunters will roam the region, attacking any human they can path to. The animals can't enter doors, but if they see a colonist run behind a door, they will attempt to beat down the door. If left alive, they will congregate around your base for anywhere from 24 to 54 in-game hours before they all leave. 

When killed, scaria has a chance to instantly rot a [corpse](/wiki/Corpse "Corpse"), which makes them inedible and creates [rot stink](/wiki/Rot_stink "Rot stink"). This limits the amount of [meat](/wiki/Meat "Meat") the colony can obtain from the pack.

### Psychic wave[[edit](/index.php?title=Events&action=edit&section=16 "Edit section: Psychic wave")]

* * *

Appears as Mad Animals: (Animal name). Immediately drives local [wildlife](/wiki/Animals "Animals") insane, becoming [manhunter](/wiki/Manhunter "Manhunter"). As opposed to a manhunter pack, these animals do not have [scaria](/wiki/Scaria "Scaria"), and so won't immediately rot. No new animals are created - only animals already on the map can be affected. This fact means that psychic waves scale poorly with raid points; advanced colonies will find these events much smaller than any other major threat. 

As manhunters, the selected animals will roam the region, attacking any human they can path to. The animals can't enter doors, but if they see a colonist run behind a door, they will attempt to beat down the door. Unlike with scaria, the manhunter status will end when the animals need to sleep. 

This event has a variation that only affects a single animal, which is not considered a major threat.

### Toxic fallout[[edit](/index.php?title=Events&action=edit&section=24 "Edit section: Toxic fallout")]

* * *

"_A distant chemical fire has released a plume of poison over this entire region. Any person or creature not under a roof will be slowly sickened by the toxic dust settling out of the atmosphere. It will last for anywhere between a few days to nearly a quadrum._ " 

**Toxic fallout** is a dangerous event which causes [toxic buildup](/wiki/Toxic_buildup "Toxic buildup") to any pawn not under a [roof](/wiki/Roof "Roof"). The fallout also gives a −5 [moodlet](/wiki/Mood "Mood") for colonists when outside. It lasts between 2.5 and 10.5 days, will not occur before Day 60 of the colony, and will not reoccur for 90 days. 

Toxic buildup from toxic fallout is proportional to both [Toxic Resistance](/wiki/Toxic_Resistance "Toxic Resistance") and the [Toxic Environment Resistance](/wiki/Toxic_Environment_Resistance "Toxic Environment Resistance") of the pawn. Animals have 50% [Toxic Resistance](/wiki/Toxic_Resistance "Toxic Resistance"), so buildup increases at half the rate, but exposed animals will eventually die. When animals die with toxic buildup, there is a chance, scaling with severity, for their [corpse](/wiki/Corpse "Corpse") to immediately rot. 

By default, toxic buildup increases at a rate of 40% per day. For humans, a moderate buildup - 40% severity, or 24 hours left outside - is enough to potentially cause permanent damage, from either [dementia](/wiki/Dementia "Dementia") or [carcinoma](/wiki/Carcinoma "Carcinoma"). Death happens at 100% severity. In [Biotech](/wiki/Biotech "Biotech"),[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC") both resistances can be gained in a number of ways, allowing counters to the effects of the fallout. These include the [face mask](/wiki/Face_mask "Face mask"), [gas mask](/wiki/Gas_mask "Gas mask"), [detoxifier kidney](/wiki/Detoxifier_kidney "Detoxifier kidney"), [detoxifier lung](/wiki/Detoxifier_lung "Detoxifier lung") and several [genes](/wiki/Genes "Genes"). 

Excluding some special cases ([Anima tree](/wiki/Anima_tree "Anima tree")[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC"), [Anima grass](/wiki/Anima_grass "Anima grass")[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC"), [Gauranlen tree](/wiki/Gauranlen_tree "Gauranlen tree")[![Content added by the Ideology DLC](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/16px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Content added by the Ideology DLC"), [Gauranlen pod](/wiki/Gauranlen_pod "Gauranlen pod")[![Content added by the Ideology DLC](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/16px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Content added by the Ideology DLC"), [Polux tree](/wiki/Polux_tree "Polux tree")[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC")), all plants will wither during the fallout, trees included. Crops can be protected under a roof, but without a [sun lamp](/wiki/Sun_lamp "Sun lamp"), they'll rot anyways. [Insectoids](/wiki/Insectoid "Insectoid") and [mechanoids](/wiki/Mechanoid "Mechanoid") are fully immune.

### Volcanic winter[[edit](/index.php?title=Events&action=edit&section=25 "Edit section: Volcanic winter")]

* * *

"_A distant supervolcano has erupted and begun spewing millions of cubic kilometers of ash into the atmosphere. Ash in the atmosphere will obscure the sun. Temperatures will drop and plants will suffer for lack of sunlight. It could pass in a few weeks, or it might last many months._ " 

**Volcanic winter** lowers the [temperature](/wiki/Temperature "Temperature") and natural [light](/wiki/Light "Light"),which in turn weakens [solar generators](/wiki/Solar_generator "Solar generator") and slows plant growth. They also appear to significantly reduce the inflow of wildlife (observed in a Temperate Forest, 300x300, very few animals came in during Spring-Summer when there would normally be many). This can easily lead to meat shortage and famine if you do not have sufficiently large farms for your colony. Volcanic winters will last between 7.5 and 40 days, will not occur before Day 60 of the colony, and will not reoccur for 140 days. 

To be specific, the temperature is reduced by 7 °C (12.6 °F) and the light by 30% over the course of 50,000 [ticks](/wiki/Time "Time") (20 in-game hours). The wildlife density is cut by half, and pawns can no longer enjoy their time outside.[Mechanic detail]

Note that volcanic winters won't kill plants outright, but they still have the potential to kill plants should the temperature drop to a sufficiently cold point. Like cold snaps, building warmed and lit greenhouses can help with crop growth during volcanic winters.

[![](/images/thumb/0/0e/Ambrosia_sprout.png/300px-Ambrosia_sprout.png)](/wiki/File:Ambrosia_sprout.png)

[](/wiki/File:Ambrosia_sprout.png "Enlarge")

An ambrosia sprout among the trees

[![Ambrosia bush.png](/images/thumb/c/c6/Ambrosia_bush.png/48px-Ambrosia_bush.png)](/wiki/File:Ambrosia_bush.png)

> A grove of the mysterious [ambrosia bush](/wiki/Ambrosia_bush "Ambrosia bush") has sprouted nearby! If you wait for the plants to grow, you'll be able to harvest the delicious, pleasurable, and slightly addictive [fruit](/wiki/Ambrosia "Ambrosia").

Ambrosia bushes are the only way to harvest [ambrosia](/wiki/Ambrosia "Ambrosia"), a safe [mood](/wiki/Mood "Mood") boosting [social drug](/wiki/Social_drug "Social drug"). A good way to harvest them is to draw a growing zone over them, but forbid sowing. Colonists will then automatically harvest the bushes when they are fully grown. 

The ambrosia event must initially spawn in the growing season of the map tile. 

It can only happen in the following [biomes](/wiki/Biomes "Biomes"): 

    * [Tropical rainforest](/wiki/Tropical_rainforest "Tropical rainforest")
    * [Tropical swamp](/wiki/Tropical_swamp "Tropical swamp")
    * [Temperate forest](/wiki/Temperate_forest "Temperate forest")
    * [Temperate swamp](/wiki/Temperate_swamp "Temperate swamp")
    * [Boreal forest](/wiki/Boreal_forest "Boreal forest")
    * [Arid shrubland](/wiki/Arid_shrubland "Arid shrubland")



#### Animals join[[edit](/index.php?title=Events&action=edit&section=30 "Edit section: Animals join")]

Tamed [dogs](/wiki/Dog "Dog") or [farm animals](/wiki/Farm_animals "Farm animals") from off-map join your colony. 

#### Animal self-tamed[[edit](/index.php?title=Events&action=edit&section=31 "Edit section: Animal self-tamed")]

[![Thrumbo self-tamed.png](/images/thumb/1/19/Thrumbo_self-tamed.png/300px-Thrumbo_self-tamed.png)](/wiki/File:Thrumbo_self-tamed.png)

A random animal on the map becomes tame, and immediately joins the colony. The frequency of self-taming events is unrelated to the number of animals on the map, provided there is at least one. This means in a tundra biome with one snowhare you're just as likely to get a self-taming event as in a tropical swamp populated with dozens of animals. Previously tamed animals can self-tame, and will retain the name and all training they previously had. 

For pen animals, you must rope them into a pen or to a caravan hitching spot or the animal will eventually run off the map, though usually this is not urgent. For zoneable animals, you should check the "Animals" tab and assign the animal to an appropriate zone, unless you want to let it move unrestricted. If you are dangerously low on food, be aware that if a zoneable animal self-tames and there is no food in the environment, it will run inside your base and eat your food stores when it gets hungry. 

#### Aurora[[edit](/index.php?title=Events&action=edit&section=32 "Edit section: Aurora")]

An aurora lights up the night sky, giving a +14 _Beautiful aurora_ [thought](/wiki/Thought "Thought") boost to anyone who is not [blind](/wiki/Sight "Sight") and is outside watching. While in real life it only happens near the poles, on the rimworlds it can happen anywhere regardless of latitude. It ends at day and otherwise lasts from 0.125 to 0.35 days. 

Despite the event text stating that it will "make the night brighter", it does not actually affect the [light](/wiki/Light "Light") level. 

During an aurora a pawn looking for a recreation source is 8 times more likely to choose [skygazing](/wiki/Recreation#Skygazing "Recreation"), and it restores 5 times more recreation. 

#### Cargo pods[[edit](/index.php?title=Events&action=edit&section=33 "Edit section: Cargo pods")]

[![Cargo pods.png](/images/thumb/0/03/Cargo_pods.png/300px-Cargo_pods.png)](/wiki/File:Cargo_pods.png)

From time to time, [cargo pods](/wiki/Transport_pod "Transport pod") will fall out of orbit, containing a random amount of single type of resource. The remains of the pods become [steel slag chunks](/wiki/Steel_slag_chunk "Steel slag chunk"), which can be refined into usable metal at an [electric smelter](/wiki/Electric_smelter "Electric smelter"). The pods have a chance of crashing through the roofs of any buildings, damaging any nearby furniture, structures or any pawn they land on.

#### Party[[edit](/index.php?title=Events&action=edit&section=35 "Edit section: Party")]

One of your colonists will throw a party around a [party spot](/wiki/Party_spot "Party spot"), or if unavailable, a gather spot ([table](/wiki/Table "Table") or [campfire](/wiki/Campfire "Campfire")). Colonists will continually get mood buffs the longer they attend a party.[Detail] Each stack adds +0.8 to mood, lasts 10 days and stacks up to 10 times, for a max buff of +8 mood. In addition, a party provides many social opportunities. They will usually have [beers](/wiki/Beer "Beer") and [smokeleaf joints](/wiki/Smokeleaf_joint "Smokeleaf joint"), if available, and eat [meals](/wiki/Meal "Meal") even when not hungry. 

Pawns that don't attend won't get mood buffs. Players may have to [draft](/wiki/Draft "Draft") and undraft pawns to get them out of work. If too many pawns, or the hosting pawn are drafted at once, or if a [raid](/wiki/Raid "Raid") happens, then the party will be called off. 

#### Psychic soothe[[edit](/index.php?title=Events&action=edit&section=36 "Edit section: Psychic soothe")]

[![Psychic soothe.png](/images/thumb/2/20/Psychic_soothe.png/300px-Psychic_soothe.png)](/wiki/File:Psychic_soothe.png)

When this event triggers, the soothe gives a positive mood modifier (+16 for normal colonists, more or less depending on the [Psychic Sensitivity](/wiki/Psychic_Sensitivity "Psychic Sensitivity")) to all colonists of a randomly chosen sex. It can be considered the opposite of the Psychic Drone, but does not have different levels of severity. It lasts between 1.5 and 3.0 days and will not reoccur for 15 days. 

#### (Trader type)[[edit](/index.php?title=Events&action=edit&section=37 "Edit section: \(Trader type\)")]

This event, without a faction mentioned, occurs when an orbital [trade](/wiki/Trade "Trade") ship passes near the colony and you have a powered [comms console](/wiki/Comms_console "Comms console"). Players may trade to obtain resources or weapons, or alternatively sell excess items for silver, which is always accepted by traders. Note that orbital trade ships can appear even when the comms console is turned off, so check the console during a solar flare. 

You need to build [orbital trade beacons](/wiki/Orbital_trade_beacon "Orbital trade beacon") before contacting them so you can beam [silver](/wiki/Silver "Silver") and items up to the ship to actually carry out transactions. You can still check the items for sale even without them however. 

#### (Trader type) from (Faction)[[edit](/index.php?title=Events&action=edit&section=38 "Edit section: \(Trader type\) from \(Faction\)")]

This event with the faction name mentioned means the traders are terrestrial. Trader caravans from other non-hostile factions can stop by your colony, whether specifically requested via the [comms console](/wiki/Comms_console "Comms console") at a hit to good will or randomly on their own with no penalty.

#### Wild (wo)man wanders in[[edit](/index.php?title=Events&action=edit&section=42 "Edit section: Wild \(wo\)man wanders in")]

A random person with no faction wanders into your colony and will mill about the map. You have the option to [Tame](/wiki/Tame "Tame") this person with a minimum [Animals skill](/wiki/Skills#Animals "Skills") of 7. Doing so will convert them to into a completely normal colonist. Sometimes, they may not be able to withstand the harshness of nature (e.g. extreme temperatures) and may fall unconscious, allowing you to rescue them like transport pod crash victims. They also have the same hunger needs as regular humans, and if they do not find animals to hunt or wild plants (or simply do not want to) they can walk into your base and eat your food, which can be an annoyance. Arresting them will not allow you to recruit or [enslave](/wiki/Slavery "Slavery")[![Content added by the Ideology DLC](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/16px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Content added by the Ideology DLC") them like regular prisoners, they must be tamed or rescued. 

Wild humans have a 50% chance of attacking when harmed, and 10% if taming fails. 

These wild (wo)men may have random Ideoligion with [Ideology](/wiki/Ideology "Ideology"). 

Unlike other animals, wild people will not flee from gunfire. Wild people can hunt human pawns for food like a predator, regardless of whether they are cannibals or not. Wild people who are incapable of violence will not hunt at all, but may still become manhunter when provoked. 

Wild (wo)men will not appear during [toxic fallout](/wiki/Events#Toxic_fallout "Events"), during [acidic smog](/wiki/Pollution#Acidic_smog "Pollution"), or if the expected outdoor temperature based on the current season is outside their comfortable range (16 °C – 26 °C (60.8 °F – 78.8 °F)).

#### Traveler Visit[[edit](/index.php?title=Events&action=edit&section=43 "Edit section: Traveler Visit")]

Occasionally, one or more members of a friendly [faction](/wiki/Factions "Factions") will arrive, just passing through. They'll enter at one edge of the map and proceed across the map. They'll exit the map upon reaching another edge.

#### Visitor(s)[[edit](/index.php?title=Events&action=edit&section=44 "Edit section: Visitor\(s\)")]

Sometimes there will be a singular visitor. In this case, the game will notify you of the backstory of that visitor.

#### Blight[[edit](/index.php?title=Events&action=edit&section=50 "Edit section: Blight")]

Main article: [Blight](/wiki/Blight "Blight")

[![](/images/thumb/1/1f/Crop_blight.png/200px-Crop_blight.png)](/wiki/File:Crop_blight.png)

[](/wiki/File:Crop_blight.png "Enlarge")

Blighted crops with an indicator above them.

Blight is a disease that infects [domesticated plants](/wiki/Domesticated_plants "Domesticated plants") with a growing period shorter than 15 days. Blighted crops will not grow or yield anything when harvested, and the blight will spread to other nearby plants within 3 tiles. These plants must be cut in order to avoid further spread. [Trees](/wiki/Tree "Tree"), [wild](/wiki/Wild_Plants "Wild Plants"), [decorative](/wiki/Decorative_Plants "Decorative Plants") plants, and [devilstrand mushrooms](/wiki/Devilstrand_mushroom "Devilstrand mushroom") are immune to blight. 

Blight starts on one of your crops, at 10% severity, affecting 20% of your plants (40% initial plants affected has been witnessed in December 2020). It will progress slowly once plants are infected, and once it reaches 28% it can spread to nearby plants within a 4-tile radius. 

It will not reoccur for 30 days.

#### Heat wave[[edit](/index.php?title=Events&action=edit&section=52 "Edit section: Heat wave")]

[![StumpChoppedA \(cropped\).png](/images/thumb/6/6a/StumpChoppedA_%28cropped%29.png/40px-StumpChoppedA_%28cropped%29.png)](/wiki/Category:Stubs "Category:Stubs") | **This section is a[stub](/wiki/Category:Stubs "Category:Stubs").** You can help RimWorld Wiki by **[expanding it](https://rimworldwiki.com/index.php?title=Events&action=edit). Reason:** _Needs details. What's the chance for a fire to occur?._  
---|---  
  
A heat wave sweeps the colony, driving outdoor [temperatures](/wiki/Temperature "Temperature") unusually high for 1.5 and 3.5 days. To be precise, the temperature will increase by 17 °C (30.6 °F) over 12000 game ticks (4.8 in-game hours). There is also a chance for a [fire](/wiki/Fire "Fire") to randomly appear on the map, which can quickly spread. It only occurs when the map's summer temperature is at least 20 °C (68 °F), and will not reoccur for 30 days. 

The major risks are [coolers](/wiki/Cooler "Cooler") failing to freeze food, and [heat stroke](/wiki/Heat_stroke "Heat stroke") to your colonists and animals. Summer clothing like [cowboy hats](/wiki/Cowboy_hat "Cowboy hat") and [tribalwear](/wiki/Tribalwear "Tribalwear") can mitigate the heat, and [passive coolers](/wiki/Passive_cooler "Passive cooler") can help make indoor temperatures bearable. 

Do note that an while this event remains active, the colony will not trigger the events Cold Snaps, Volcanic Winter, nor additional Heat Waves. The one exception are events triggered by quest, which will apply regardless. 

#### Cold snap[[edit](/index.php?title=Events&action=edit&section=53 "Edit section: Cold snap")]

A cold snap strikes the colony, driving outdoor [temperature](/wiki/Temperature "Temperature") unusually low for 1.5 and 3.5 days. To be precise, the temperature decreases by 20 °C (36 °F) over 12000 game ticks (4.8 in-game hours). It only occurs when the map's summer temperature is in the range of 0 °C – 15 °C (32 °F – 59 °F), and will not reoccur for 30 days. Colonists will complain about the cold, especially sleeping in it if their rooms are not properly heated. 

While this is generally good for item preservation, it can kill off crops, influence colonist moods and cause [hypothermia](/wiki/Ailments#Hypothermia "Ailments") if colonists or animals stay in non-heated areas for a prolonged time. It also renders most of the plants in the area inedible (unless when it happens in warmer areas in which some plants are still edible), potentially causing tame animals to starve to death and wild ones to leave the map. With no other food source, wild predators may attack colonists and tame animals while they are outdoors. Snowing may also happen, depositing thick snow that can slow down outdoor movement significantly. 

[Jackets](/wiki/Jacket "Jacket"), [dusters](/wiki/Duster "Duster"), [parkas](/wiki/Parka "Parka") and [tuques](/wiki/Tuque "Tuque") provide cold insulation for colonists, protecting them while they're awake, though they don't prevent mood loss caused by sleeping in cold areas. 

Do note that an while this event remains active, the colony will not trigger the events Heat Waves, Volcanic Winter, nor additional Cold Snaps. The one exception are events triggered by quest, which will apply regardless. 

#### Mad animal[[edit](/index.php?title=Events&action=edit&section=54 "Edit section: Mad animal")]

A single random animal on the map turns [manhunter](/wiki/Manhunter "Manhunter"), like a psychic wave. The insane animal will charge toward the nearest human anywhere on the map, attacking any other animal or obstacle (doors, fences, barricades, etc.) blocking its way. If the "nearest" colonist changes, the animal may change its focus to that target. 

Any animal can be dangerous to a colonist, but multiple colonists can usually take them out. Large animals, as well as the explosive [boomalope](/wiki/Boomalope "Boomalope") and [boomrat](/wiki/Boomrat "Boomrat"), are bigger threats. 

#### Psychic drone[[edit](/index.php?title=Events&action=edit&section=55 "Edit section: Psychic drone")]

For the moodlet, see [Thought#Exotic](/wiki/Thought#Exotic "Thought").

'Drone' in this case does not refer to "Unmanned Aerial Vehicle" but instead "To produce a low-pitched hum or buzz." A psychic drone affects all colonists of a randomly chosen gender, giving a [strong negative moodlet](/wiki/Mood#Exotic "Mood"). The drone can vary in severity, from low (-12 mood) to extreme (-40 mood). [Psychic Sensitivity](/wiki/Psychic_Sensitivity "Psychic Sensitivity") will multiply the effect on the drone. It lasts between 0.75 and 1.75 days and will not reoccur for 15 days. Below 800 raid points, a Psychic Drone is always Low level. Above 2000 Raid Points, a Psychic Drone is High level. Between 800 and 2000 Psychic drones are Medium level. 

Colonists have no choice but to endure it, as the point of origin is off-map. [Psychic foil helmets](/wiki/Psychic_foil_helmet "Psychic foil helmet"), [drugs](/wiki/Drug "Drug"), and other mood-enhancers can help deal with the effects. Animals are not affected. 

#### Solar flare[[edit](/index.php?title=Events&action=edit&section=56 "Edit section: Solar flare")]

Solar flares cause all electrical devices to stop working for its duration, from 0.15 to 0.5 days. [Wood-fired generators](/wiki/Wood-fired_generator "Wood-fired generator") and [chemfuel generators](/wiki/Chemfuel_generator "Chemfuel generator") will still burn fuel during this time. 

  * Colonies that rely on turrets for defense will become vulnerable to raids.
  * Heaters and coolers will stop working. In extreme temperatures, crops will die and colonists can be at risk of heatstroke or hypothermia. [Campfires](/wiki/Campfire "Campfire") and [passive coolers](/wiki/Passive_cooler "Passive cooler") are low-tech options that work even in a solar flare.
  * Crops in a [hydroponics basin](/wiki/Hydroponics_basin "Hydroponics basin") will rapidly degrade, regardless of the temperature. It's recommended to quickly harvest any crops that can't be saved before they die.
  * Occasionally an [orbital trade ship](/wiki/Trade#Orbital "Trade") may arrive during the event, so it is wise to check out the Comms Console as soon as the event ends or risk getting the ''_< trader> has left comms range"_ notification while unaware of their presence. If a ship leaves _during_ a solar flare, you _will_ be notified that the trader has left, although this is only a minor message in the upper right of the screen.
  * The colony is immune to the effects of a solar flare while also undergoing [unnatural darkness](/wiki/Unnatural_darkness "Unnatural darkness"). [![Content added by the Anomaly DLC](/images/thumb/3/32/AnomalyIcon.png/16px-AnomalyIcon.png)](/wiki/Anomaly_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Anomaly DLC")
  * Shuttles ([imperial](/wiki/Imperial_shuttle "Imperial shuttle") and [passenger](/wiki/Passenger_shuttle "Passenger shuttle") [![Content added by the Odyssey DLC](/images/thumb/7/7b/OdysseyIcon.png/16px-OdysseyIcon.png)](/wiki/Odyssey_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Odyssey DLC")) cannot take off during a solar flare.



Due to its length, most of the adverse effects are manageable. 

#### Zzztt[[edit](/index.php?title=Events&action=edit&section=57 "Edit section: Zzztt")]

A short-circuit has occured, causing an explosion at a [power conduit](/wiki/Power_conduit "Power conduit") or electrical appliance under [weather](/wiki/Weather "Weather"). There are two different types of short-circut events: 

First, any regular [power conduit](/wiki/Power_conduit "Power conduit") with power flowing through it may suffer a fault, causing a short circuit. This variant of the event has a minimum cooldown of 8 days. [Hidden conduits](/wiki/Hidden_conduit "Hidden conduit") and [waterproof conduits](/wiki/Waterproof_conduit "Waterproof conduit") are _not_ valid targets for this variant of short circuit, meaning it can be avoided by building the entire base out of hidden/waterproof conduits. 

Also, if certain buildings are left unroofed and are exposed to [rain or snow](/wiki/Weather "Weather"), a short circuit may occur on the building. This can be avoided by simply roofing in all applicable buildings. 

The following buildings can short circuit due to weather: 

  * [Sun lamp](/wiki/Sun_lamp "Sun lamp")
  * [Tube television](/wiki/Tube_television "Tube television")
  * [Flatscreen television](/wiki/Flatscreen_television "Flatscreen television")
  * [Megascreen television](/wiki/Megascreen_television "Megascreen television")
  * [Comms console](/wiki/Comms_console "Comms console")
  * [Multi-analyzer](/wiki/Multi-analyzer "Multi-analyzer")
  * [Vitals monitor](/wiki/Vitals_monitor "Vitals monitor")
  * [Battery](/wiki/Battery "Battery")
  * [Electric tailor bench](/wiki/Electric_tailor_bench "Electric tailor bench")
  * [Electric smithy](/wiki/Electric_smithy "Electric smithy")
  * [Machining table](/wiki/Machining_table "Machining table")
  * [Electric stove](/wiki/Electric_stove "Electric stove")
  * [Electric smelter](/wiki/Electric_smelter "Electric smelter")
  * [Biofuel refinery](/wiki/Biofuel_refinery "Biofuel refinery")
  * [Fabrication bench](/wiki/Fabrication_bench "Fabrication bench")
  * [Hi-tech research bench](/wiki/Hi-tech_research_bench "Hi-tech research bench")
  * [Electric crematorium](/wiki/Electric_crematorium "Electric crematorium")
  * [Heater](/wiki/Heater "Heater")
  * [Lightball](/wiki/Lightball "Lightball")[![Content added by the Ideology DLC](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/16px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Content added by the Ideology DLC")
  * [Loudspeaker](/wiki/Loudspeaker "Loudspeaker")[![Content added by the Ideology DLC](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/16px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Content added by the Ideology DLC")
  * [Autobong](/wiki/Autobong "Autobong")[![Content added by the Ideology DLC](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/16px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Content added by the Ideology DLC")



Short circuits will either result in a [fire](/wiki/Fire "Fire"), or, if any [batteries](/wiki/Battery "Battery") are connected to the circuit and contains at least 20Wd, a fiery explosion centered on the tile, that increases with energy stored. The explosion deals 10 [Flame damage](/wiki/Damage_Types#Flame "Damage Types") and setting objects on fire. If the explosion radius is greater than 3.5 tiles, a second explosion of 30% radius deals 50 [Bomb damage](/wiki/Damage_Types#Bomb "Damage Types"), which can extinguish fires. The fault will also discharge all stored power in batteries in the process. 

The explosion radius is controlled by the following equation: 

**Explosion Radius** = **clamp**( **sqrt**(Sum of all stored power) * 0.05, 1.5, 14.9)   
---  
  
Or in other words, the radius will be at least 1.5 tiles, and at most 14.9 tiles, with a bomb explosion of 4.5 tiles. This occurs when a total of 90,000 Wd is stored. Bomb explosions can occur beyond once 4,900 Wd are stored. 

Explosion radii per number of [batteries](/wiki/Batteries "Batteries") filled   
---  
  
Unconnected batteries, such as through an open switch or on separate power network, will not be affected. Power networks which contain only [batteries](/wiki/Battery "Battery") and no conduits will not experience this event. Power networks which contain only conduits will only have a single tile on fire, as there's no stored power to be discharged.

##### Outcome chances[[edit](/index.php?title=Events&action=edit&section=72 "Edit section: Outcome chances")]

The actual outcome chances are dependent on the negotiator's [Negotiation Ability](/wiki/Negotiation_Ability "Negotiation Ability") stat and, if the [Ideology DLC](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Ideology DLC") is active, the presence/absence of your [leader](/wiki/Leader "Leader").[![Content added by the Ideology DLC](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/16px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Content added by the Ideology DLC")

First, the negotiator's [Negotiation Ability](/wiki/Negotiation_Ability "Negotiation Ability") stat goes through a simple curve to determine the **bad outcome factor**. In this formula, X is the decimal equivalent to the negotiation ability. As an example, a negotiation ability of 130% gives us an X value of 1.3. 

Negotiation Ability | Bad outcome factor | Observations   
---|---|---  
**0%** | 4 | Not possible in practice.   
**0% - 100%** | 4 - 3*X |   
**100%** | 1 | Equivalent to lvl 8 Social   
**100% - 150%** | 1 - 1.2 * (X-1) |   
**150%** | 0.4 | Equivalent to lvl 15+ Social   
  
If the [Ideology DLC](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Ideology DLC") is active, the **bad outcome factor** is then multiplied by: 

  * 0.95, if the colony leader is present on this caravan.
  * 1.05, if he's not.



The new weight of each outcome is calculated afterwards. 

  * Triumph = 0.1 * ( 1 / Bad Outcome Factor)
  * Success = 0.55 * ( 1 / Bad Outcome Factor)
  * Flounder = 0.2
  * Backfire = 0.1 * Bad Outcome Factor
  * Disaster = 0.05 * Bad Outcome Factor



The sum total of the new weights is then calculated. 

Finally, the probability of each outcome is: 

    Probability of each outcome = New Weight ÷ Sum Total of Weights

  


Example

Take a level 12 Social negotiator as an example: 

Without [Ideology DLC](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Ideology DLC") active: 

  1. The Negotiation Ability is 130%, corresponding to a bad outcome factor of 0.64.
  2. The new weight of each outcome: 
     * Triumph = 0.1 * ( 1 / 0.64) = 0.15625
     * Success = 0.55 * ( 1 / 0.64) = 0.859375
     * Flounder = 0.2
     * Backfire = 0.1 * 0.64 = 0.064
     * Disaster = 0.05 * 0.64 = 0.032
  3. The sum total of new weights is calculated: 
     * 0.15625 + 0.859375 + 0.2 + 0.064 + 0.032 = 1.311625
  4. Finally, the probability of each event, to 2 d.p.: 
     * Triumph = 0.15625 / 1.311625 = 11.91%
     * Success = 0.859375 / 1.311625 = 65.52%
     * Flounder = 0.2 / 1.311625 = 15.25%
     * Backfire = 0.064 / 1.311625 = 4.88%
     * Disaster = 0.032 / 1.311625 = 2.44%



With [Ideology DLC](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Ideology DLC") active: 

  1. The Negotiation Ability is 130%, corresponding to a bad outcome factor of 0.64.
  2. Assuming the leader is present, the bad outcome factor gets reduced to 0.608.
  3. The new weight of each outcome, to 3 d.p.: 
     * Triumph = 0.1 * ( 1 / 0.608) = 0.164
     * Success = 0.55 * ( 1 / 0.608) = 0.905
     * Flounder = 0.2
     * Backfire = 0.1 * 0.608 = 0.0608
     * Disaster = 0.05 * 0.608 = 0.0304
  4. The sum total of new weights is calculated: 
     * 0.164 + 0.905 + 0.2 + 0.0608 + 0.0304 = 1.3602
  5. Finally, the probability of each event, to 2 d.p.: 
     * Triumph = 0.164 / 1.3602 = 12.05%
     * Success = 0.905 / 1.3602 = 66.53%
     * Flounder = 0.2 / 1.3602 = 14.70%
     * Backfire = 0.0608 / 1.3602 = 4.46%
     * Disaster = 0.0304 / 1.3602 = 2.23%

## Legacy events[[edit](/index.php?title=Events&action=edit&section=81 "Edit section: Legacy events")]

### Bonded animal's death[[edit](/index.php?title=Events&action=edit&section=82 "Edit section: Bonded animal's death")]

[![](/images/thumb/5/58/Bonded_animals_death.png/300px-Bonded_animals_death.png)](/wiki/File:Bonded_animals_death.png)

Bonded animal dies affecting the owner's mood.

Both of these bonded death events were simplified into the [Death: (Pawn)](/wiki/Events/Incidental#Death "Events/Incidental") event. 

An animal with a bond to a colonist dies, affecting that colonist's mood negatively. Enemies may directly target your animals during attacks. Enemies, turrets or even your own colonists can even hit them unintentionally during shootouts, especially animals trained for Release and checked to follow your drafted colonists in the Animals tab. Wild predators may hunt and kill your animals, including bonded ones. Bonded animals can also fall into traps. It can even occur intentionally by slaughtering, euthanizing or manually drafting and attacking the bonded animal. 

### Bonded master's death[[edit](/index.php?title=Events&action=edit&section=83 "Edit section: Bonded master's death")]

Both of these bonded death events were simplified into the [Death: (Pawn)](/wiki/Events/Incidental#Death "Events/Incidental") event. 

The inverse of a bonded animal's death. This time, it's the master who dies, through causes such as enemy raids, disease or large wild predators getting hungry. 

When this happens, any animal that is bonded to the master will have a mental break. It can be a less dangerous one such as a dazed wander, or a more dangerous one such as a berserk rage. 

This is one of the only situations where animals can have non-manhunter mental breaks.

### Tornado[[edit](/index.php?title=Events&action=edit&section=85 "Edit section: Tornado")]

A tornado has touched down in this region, and will proceed to wreak havoc on anything in its way. There is small chance it may spawn at the map borders and eventually [leave the map](/wiki/File:The_tornado_has_left_the_map.png "File:The tornado has left the map.png"). 

Tornadoes rapidly deal damage to any structure or item that is right beneath it. Any pawn underneath will rapidly receive scratches from the random objects blowing about in the wind. 

Naturally forming tornadoes are exclusive to Beta 18. You can still use the [Tornado generator](/wiki/Tornado_generator "Tornado generator") to spawn them in for defense purposes.
