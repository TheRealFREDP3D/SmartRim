**Guinea pigs** are tameable [herbivores](/wiki/Herbivore "Herbivore") with a fast movement speed. Guinea Pigs come in three colors: Cream, Black and Brown.

## Acquisition[[edit](/index.php?title=Guinea_pig&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Acquisition")]

Guinea pigs can be found in [temperate forests](/wiki/Temperate_forest "Temperate forest"), [temperate swamps](/wiki/Temperate_swamp "Temperate swamp"), [tropical rainforests](/wiki/Tropical_rainforest "Tropical rainforest"), [tropical swamps](/wiki/Tropical_swamp "Tropical swamp"), [arid shrublands](/wiki/Arid_shrubland "Arid shrubland"), [deserts](/wiki/Desert "Desert"), [extreme deserts](/wiki/Extreme_desert "Extreme desert"), [cold bogs](/wiki/Cold_bog "Cold bog"), and [lava fields](/wiki/Lava_field "Lava field")[![Content added by the Odyssey DLC](/images/thumb/7/7b/OdysseyIcon.png/16px-OdysseyIcon.png)](/wiki/Odyssey_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Odyssey DLC"). They can either be tamed by a [handler](/wiki/Work#Handle "Work") or self-tame in a random event. 

Guinea Pigs can be bought and sold in other [faction bases](/wiki/Trade#Faction_base "Trade") and from [bulk goods traders](/wiki/Trade#Bulk_goods_trader_2 "Trade") or [exotic goods traders](/wiki/Trade#Exotic_goods_trader_2 "Trade"). Guinea Pigs purchased from traders will be already tamed. 

One guinea pig can be selected as a [starting pet](/wiki/Animal_husbandry#Starting_pets "Animal husbandry") in some [scenarios](/wiki/Scenario "Scenario") if a pawn with [Animals skill](/wiki/Animals_skill "Animals skill") of 6 or higher is selected as a starting colonist. This animal will be tamed by the colony, have a random name, and have a chance to be [bonded](/wiki/Bond "Bond") with a pawn. Which animal is selected is random, with selection chance depending on the highest available Animals skill and the petness of available options for that skill. See [Starting pets](/wiki/Animal_husbandry#Starting_pets "Animal husbandry") for further detail. 

## Analysis[[edit](/index.php?title=Guinea_pig&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Analysis")]

Guinea pigs can be farmed for [meat](/wiki/Meat "Meat") and [fur](/wiki/Guinea_pig_fur "Guinea pig fur"). While each guinea pig is small, they reproduce rapidly, so a large pack of guinea pigs can produce a lot of material. Compared to [chinchillas](/wiki/Chinchilla "Chinchilla"), guinea pigs are not [pen animals](/wiki/Pen_animal "Pen animal"), so they will need to constantly be re-tamed. However, guinea pigs can [nuzzle](/wiki/Nuzzle "Nuzzle") and can be controlled via [allowed areas](/wiki/Allowed_area "Allowed area"). 

Due to their high reproductive rate, guinea pigs can be used as bait animals. Set an [allowed area](/wiki/Allowed_area "Allowed area") towards the direction of raiders. This allows you to direct where the guinea pigs go, and they will be shot at rather than your colonists. This results in less meat & leather than slaughtering them would, but can be useful in combat. Compared to [rats](/wiki/Rat "Rat") and [hares](/wiki/Hare "Hare"), guinea pigs offer a more valuable fur and can nuzzle colonists, but are larger targets and reproduce less. 

They are one of the few animals that can [nuzzle](/wiki/Nuzzle "Nuzzle"), occasionally giving a +4 _Nuzzled_ moodlet to colonists. Guinea pigs and [monkeys](/wiki/Monkey "Monkey") are the only 2 wild animals that can nuzzle. 

### Leather[[edit](/index.php?title=Guinea_pig&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Leather")]

Guinea pigs have valuable [fur](/wiki/Guinea_pig_fur "Guinea pig fur"). As clothing and building material, it has a few merits, but is generally lacking: 

  * Guinea pig fur may not have the best physical protection, but it has the best cold insulation in the game, protecting your colonists against cold temperatures. Guinea pig [parkas](/wiki/Parka "Parka") are the most insulative gear in the game. In less extreme biomes, [tribalwear](/wiki/Tribalwear "Tribalwear") can be used to protect against heat and cold, potentially letting you wear a [jacket](/wiki/Jacket "Jacket") instead of a parka. However, guinea pigs are only native to tropical biomes, which ultimately limits this use.
  * Guinea pig fur is very beautiful. However, only a select few objects can be made out of leather - a single [armchair](/wiki/Armchair "Armchair")'s beauty is unlikely to have much impact.



Otherwise, the best use for their fur is for trade. It is a valuable resource, worth more than even [devilstrand](/wiki/Devilstrand "Devilstrand"). 

Female guinea pigs produce: 

  * Baby slaughter: [![Guinea pig fur](/images/thumb/e/e0/Guineapigfur_b.png/16px-Guineapigfur_b.png)](/wiki/Guinea_pig_fur "Guinea pig fur") 1.05 fur per day, or [![Guinea pig fur](/images/thumb/e/e0/Guineapigfur_b.png/16px-Guineapigfur_b.png)](/wiki/Guinea_pig_fur "Guinea pig fur") 6.569 fur per 1 unit nutrition.
  * Adult slaughter: [![Guinea pig fur](/images/thumb/e/e0/Guineapigfur_b.png/16px-Guineapigfur_b.png)](/wiki/Guinea_pig_fur "Guinea pig fur") 4.2 fur per day, or [![Guinea pig fur](/images/thumb/e/e0/Guineapigfur_b.png/16px-Guineapigfur_b.png)](/wiki/Guinea_pig_fur "Guinea pig fur") 10.357 fur per 1 unit nutrition.



When considering a 1:1 ratio of males:females, fur lowers to [![Guinea pig fur](/images/thumb/e/e0/Guineapigfur_b.png/16px-Guineapigfur_b.png)](/wiki/Guinea_pig_fur "Guinea pig fur")3.285 per 1 unit nutrition (baby), or [![Guinea pig fur](/images/thumb/e/e0/Guineapigfur_b.png/16px-Guineapigfur_b.png)](/wiki/Guinea_pig_fur "Guinea pig fur") 7.429 per 1 unit nutrition (adult). A higher ratio of females will increase efficiency from there. 

For the sole purpose of farming leather, [chinchillas](/wiki/Chinchilla "Chinchilla") are superior as they do not need to be retamed. Guinea pigs have some non-leather advantages, as listed above.
