## Acquisition[[edit](/index.php?title=Lavish_meal&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Acquisition")]

Lavish meals can be [cooked](/wiki/Work#Cook "Work") at a [fueled stove](/wiki/Fueled_stove "Fueled stove") or [electric stove](/wiki/Electric_stove "Electric stove"). A lavish meal requires 0.5 units of [nutrition](/wiki/Nutrition "Nutrition") from vegetarian items, 0.5 units of nutrition from meat or animal products ([milk](/wiki/Milk "Milk") or [eggs](/wiki/Egg "Egg")), 800 [ticks](/wiki/Time "Time") (13.33 secs) of work to make, and a [cooking](/wiki/Cooking "Cooking") skill of 8. 

## Summary[[edit](/index.php?title=Lavish_meal&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Summary")]

A lavish meal can be eaten by humans or animals for 1 [nutrition](/wiki/Nutrition "Nutrition"). As it takes 1.0 nutrition of [raw food](/wiki/Raw_food "Raw food") to create, lavish meals provide only 100% nutrition efficiency. Contrast this with 180% nutrition efficiency from [fine meals](/wiki/Fine_meal "Fine meal") or [simple meals](/wiki/Simple_meal "Simple meal"), which provide more nutrition than their individual ingredients. Note that, because pawns will automatically eat when they are hungry (0.25/1 nutrition), some food is usually lost from [overeating](/wiki/Overeating "Overeating"). 

Eating a lavish meal or its variants gives the +12 _Ate lavish meal_ [thought](/wiki/Thought "Thought") for 1 [day](/wiki/Time "Time"). This replaces the +5 _Ate fine meal_ [thought](/wiki/Thought "Thought") for 1 [day](/wiki/Time "Time") from [fine meal](/wiki/Fine_meal "Fine meal") if present. If the meal was made out of [insect meat](/wiki/Insect_meat "Insect meat"), [human meat](/wiki/Human_meat "Human meat"), or [twisted meat](/wiki/Twisted_meat "Twisted meat")[![Content added by the Anomaly DLC](/images/thumb/3/32/AnomalyIcon.png/16px-AnomalyIcon.png)](/wiki/Anomaly_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Anomaly DLC") their respective moodlets will be applied. If [Ideology DLC](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Ideology DLC") [![Content added by the Ideology DLC](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/16px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Content added by the Ideology DLC") is active and the meal was made out of [raw fungus](/wiki/Raw_fungus "Raw fungus"), its normal moodlet will also be applied. 

Lavish meals will also satisfy the food requirements of any [conceited noble](/wiki/Conceited_noble "Conceited noble")[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC"). 

## Analysis[[edit](/index.php?title=Lavish_meal&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Analysis")]

Lavish meals consume twice as many ingredients and require 75% more work than [fine meals](/wiki/Fine_meal "Fine meal") to produce one meal. Most of the time, you should wait to switch to lavish meals until the mid-late game, when food is rarely an issue and your [cooks](/wiki/Cooking "Cooking") are more proficient in their duties. The mood boost lasts for 24 hours, so colonists can be manually directed to eat one per day. 

Lavish meals can be prepared and reserved for emergencies, such as a [psychic drone](/wiki/Psychic_drone "Psychic drone"), in order to avoid [mental breaks](/wiki/Mental_break "Mental break"). Colonists can eat meals even if they are at full [saturation](/wiki/Saturation "Saturation"), so lavish meals can be used like a drug. Wealthier colonies can use them to encourage [mental inspiration](/wiki/Mental_inspiration "Mental inspiration"), or help mood during the [ship launch](/wiki/Ship_launch "Ship launch").
