[](/wiki/File:Quests_available.png "Enlarge")

Quests UI on the 'Available' tab

Quests are found in the Quests tab. Most quests are offered by a [faction](/wiki/Faction "Faction"), but they can be offered by individual pawns or unaligned AI's. Most quests have to be accepted, but some are automatically given. Some quests have requirements to be accepted, and others have a time limit for acceptance. Quests can be "trashed" by clicking on the trash can icon, but is purely visual and can be reversed (Quests/Historical). 

Most quests are time sensitive, and have one or both of these timers: 

  * The first timer is the limit to _accept_ the quest. If not accepted, the quest will be rejected.
  * The second timer is the time to _do_ the quest, or alternatively, how long a quest lasts for.



Not all quests have a time limit. The [endgame](/wiki/Endings "Endings") quests in particular (Ship to the Stars, Royal Ascent,[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC") Archonexus,[![Content added by the Ideology DLC](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/16px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Content added by the Ideology DLC") The Gravship[![Content added by the Odyssey DLC](/images/thumb/7/7b/OdysseyIcon.png/16px-OdysseyIcon.png)](/wiki/Odyssey_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Odyssey DLC") and its subquests) can be done at any time. 

Some Quests, such as the beggars quest, are accepted automatically 

Without the [Royalty DLC](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Royalty DLC"), you will receive 1 quest every 10 day interval - this means that quests can be up to 19.9 days apart. With the Royalty DLC active, you will get 2 quests every 12 day interval, as this DLC introduces many more possible quests.[Verify]

### Difficulty[[edit](/index.php?title=Quests&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Difficulty")]

Quests use the [raid points](/wiki/Raid_points "Raid points") mechanic to determine quest size. In general, the larger the colony, the more difficult and more rewarding quests will be. Unlike standard Major Threats that only use the raid points of that map tile or caravan, quests use the total raid point value from all colonies and caravans. If multiple colonies are open simultaneously, carefully read which colony will encounter the quest threat. 

Quests can be from 1 to 3 stars. The more stars, the higher the raid point multiplier, and the harder the quest will be. One star quests are standard raid points. Two star quests are double Raid Points and Three Star is triple raid points. Quests also have a 30% raid point variance that causes quest threats to be up to 30% smaller or larger. These variation takes place on all Storytellers and the 50% Randy Random Raid Point multiplier does not apply to quests. 

The [endgame](/wiki/Endings "Endings") quests, exclusively, are listed as 4 stars. However, they do not scale in the same way as with other quests.

There are a few reward types - from simple rewards such as [plasteel](/wiki/Plasteel "Plasteel") and [hyperweave](/wiki/Hyperweave "Hyperweave"), to quest-exclusive items like [tornado generators](/wiki/Tornado_generator "Tornado generator") and [vanometric power cells](/wiki/Vanometric_power_cell "Vanometric power cell"), to factional [goodwill](/wiki/Goodwill "Goodwill") and new colonists. Sometimes, the quest is its own reward, such as solving a [condition causer](/wiki/Condition_causer "Condition causer")[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC") or escaping the planet. Quests may only have 1 reward, or they can have a pool of 3 rewards to choose from. 

If you accept a quest for [goodwill](/wiki/Goodwill "Goodwill") or [honor](/wiki/Honor "Honor"),[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC") those will be the only quest rewards, so you cannot receive both honor, goodwill and items from the same quest, excluding hospitality, bandit camp and crashed shuttle quests. 

Reward preferences can be altered at the bottom of the quest menu. This allows you to choose if you want [goodwill](/wiki/Goodwill "Goodwill") or [honor](/wiki/Honor "Honor")[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC") from a specific faction. Disabling these rewards means you'll get another pool of rewards to choose from, but it is still possible to receive faction goodwill through keeping high moods during hospitality quests. 

Certain pawn join rewards - typically the ones offered by the pawn themselves, but all on console - will not give detailed information about the pawn themselves. You _will_ be able to see their age and [backstory](/wiki/Backstory "Backstory") before accepting. For example, a _Nurse_ will always have some amount of Medical skill, but will almost always be incapable of Violence. However, others may allow you to preview them as if viewing their info tab. 

Main reward options:

  * [Goodwill](/wiki/Goodwill "Goodwill")
  * New [colonists](/wiki/Colonists "Colonists")

## Failure[[edit](/index.php?title=Quests&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Failure")]

Most quests can be failed in a couple of general ways: 

  1. Not accepting it / Not completing it in time.
  2. Becoming hostile with the quest giver's faction.
  3. Becoming neutral with an enemy faction you were supposed to fight.



Quests can be failed in other ways, depending on the type of quest. No matter what, if you've gotten the quest reward, then you have the reward. 

# List of quests[[edit](/index.php?title=Quests&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: List of quests")]

This section covers all the quests available in Core RimWorld. For DLC content, see #Royalty, #Ideology, #Biotech, #Anomaly, and #Odyssey. 

## Bandit Camp[[edit](/index.php?title=Quests&action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: Bandit Camp")]

A faction has contacted you about an outpost of a mutual enemy. Destroy it to be rewarded. 

When accepting this quest: 

  * An event space generates somewhere near your colony, with enemies inside. 
    * Timer starts as soon as the base is generated, and will remain the same regardless of when it is accepted.



As soon as the enemies are "fleeing" (>50% of enemies [downed](/wiki/Downed "Downed") or [killed](/wiki/Death "Death")), you will be rewarded at your colony. Plus, the camp itself can generate with some loot. 

## Downed Refugee[[edit](/index.php?title=Quests&action=edit&section=7 "Edit section: Downed Refugee")]

A refugee has contacted you for help. They are [downed](/wiki/Downed "Downed") and need your help. As a pawn join event, it will be less common as you gain more people. 

When this quest appears: 

  * You have a certain time frame to reach the downed refugee. The refugee won't starve or bleed out before you reach the event space.
  * Any specified threats will be present. An unknown threat may appear as you approach the refugee.
  * Generally, the refugee won't have any bleeding wounds at all, meaning you have time to deal with any threats that appear. When rescued, they will gain the +18 _Rescued_ [moodlet](/wiki/Mood "Mood") for 30 days.



Select a colonist, right click on the refugee, and click "offer help" in order to accept them into your colony. You can see the pawn's bio and stats before accepting them. If you don't like the pawn, then you can leave with no penalty. 

## Item Stash[[edit](/index.php?title=Quests&action=edit&section=8 "Edit section: Item Stash")]

You have been contacted about items nearby your colony. 

When this quest appears: 

  * You have a certain time frame to reach the item stash.
  * Any specified threats will be present. An unknown threat may appear as you approach the stash.



## Peace Talks[[edit](/index.php?title=Quests&action=edit&section=9 "Edit section: Peace Talks")]

An enemy faction is willing to talk. Depending on your negotiator, they may become ease relations, or immediately attack you. This needs a hostile faction that is willing to become allies. 

When this quest appears: 

  * You have a certain, random time frame to reach the peace talk location. This time, which is not listed in the quest description, will be between 12 and 28 days. As soon as you reach the event tile, the event will trigger.
  * This quest has various outcomes, increasing or decreasing [goodwill](/wiki/Goodwill "Goodwill"). The chance of each outcome is determined randomly, influenced by the [Negotiation Ability](/wiki/Negotiation_Ability "Negotiation Ability") of the best pawn you set into the caravan, who will act as a negotiator for this quest. 
    * **Triumph** \- Goodwill increases by 100-110, faction leaves a gift with total 500 - 1200 market value, [![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC")negotiator get 1-4 [Honor](/wiki/Honor "Honor") if Peace Talks were with the [Empire](/wiki/Empire "Empire").
    * **Success** \- Goodwill increases by 60-70, [![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC")negotiator get 1-4 [Honor](/wiki/Honor "Honor") if Peace Talks were with the [Empire](/wiki/Empire "Empire").
    * **Flounder** \- No change in relations.
    * **Backfire** \- Goodwill decreases by 10-20.
    * **Disaster** \- Goodwill decreases by 40-50, _immediate attack_ on caravan party.

Even if peace talks were a "Triumph", the faction may still be hostile to you. You can send gifts to increase goodwill to 0, where they will become neutral. You cannot ever gain goodwill with [pirate](/wiki/Pirate "Pirate") factions or any faction listed as "hostile" in the Factions screen, so they will never offer you this quest. 

## Prisoner Rescue[[edit](/index.php?title=Quests&action=edit&section=10 "Edit section: Prisoner Rescue")]

A prisoner has called you for help. If you can free them, then they will join the colony. As a pawn join event, it will be less common as you gain more people. 

When this quest appears: 

  * You have a certain timeframe to reach the prisoner camp. When you arrive, you'll need to defeat all enemies.
  * The prisoner starts with some [food](/wiki/Food "Food") in their cell. When rescued, they will gain the +18 _Rescued_ [moodlet](/wiki/Mood "Mood") for 30 days.



In order to recruit the prisoner: first, defeat enemies. Then, claim the prison cell's [door](/wiki/Door "Door"), or just destroy the cell's walls. Select a colonist, right click on the prisoner, and click "Free Prisoner". You can see the pawn's bio and stats before accepting them. If you don't like the pawn, then you can leave with no penalty. 

## Threatened Joiner[[edit](/index.php?title=Quests&action=edit&section=11 "Edit section: Threatened Joiner")]

A pawn is threatened by some sort of mutual enemy. As a pawn join event, it will be less common as you gain more people. 

When accepting this quest: 

  * The specified pawn will immediately join your colony.
  * A [major threat](/wiki/Major_threat "Major threat") will arrive at the colony within a few hours. 
    * This can be enemy [raiders](/wiki/Raider "Raider"), a [manhunter pack](/wiki/Manhunter_pack "Manhunter pack"), a [mech cluster](/wiki/Mech_cluster "Mech cluster"),[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC") an world event [condition causer](/wiki/Condition_causer "Condition causer"),[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC") or a forced condition (like [rain](/wiki/Rain "Rain"), [heat wave](/wiki/Heat_wave "Heat wave"), or [volcanic winter](/wiki/Volcanic_winter "Volcanic winter")) for a few days[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC"). This threat is usually specified.



## Trade Request[[edit](/index.php?title=Quests&action=edit&section=12 "Edit section: Trade Request")]

A [faction base](/wiki/Faction_base "Faction base") is requesting a special offer. You have a certain amount of time to get to the faction base and deliver the items. 

When accepting this quest: 

  * You will have to deliver the requested items. All items must be of normal [quality](/wiki/Quality "Quality") or higher and cannot be tainted, but item HP does not matter. 
    * You can't send the items directly via [transport pod](/wiki/Transport_pod "Transport pod"), but you can transport pod a pawn and the items to the location.
  * A timer starts as soon as you accept this quest. The time limit does not compensate you if you accept the quest early; accepting the quest later is advantageous as it gives more time.



Rewards are received as soon as items are delivered. Item rewards are inserted into the [caravan](/wiki/Caravan "Caravan") that fulfilled the offer in the first place; make sure you can carry it back. 

## Ore Lump[[edit](/index.php?title=Quests&action=edit&section=13 "Edit section: Ore Lump")]

A [long-range mineral scanner](/wiki/Long-range_mineral_scanner "Long-range mineral scanner") has found a lump of ore. You have a certain amount of time to get the ore before others retrieve it. Note: this quest _only_ appears when using the long-range mineral scanner. 

When this quest appears: 

  * You have a certain timeframe (30 days) to reach the ore lump. You have 10 days to mine the ore before raids start arriving.
  * Any specified threats will be present. An additional unknown threat may appear as you approach the ore lump.



The reward is that you get to mine the minerals you've scanned for. ## Royalty Intro Quests[[edit](/index.php?title=Quests&action=edit&section=15 "Edit section: Royalty Intro Quests")]

These quests appear only once, near the start of the game. These offer distinct interaction with the [empire](/wiki/Empire "Empire") faction. 

### Noble Wimp[[edit](/index.php?title=Quests&action=edit&section=16 "Edit section: Noble Wimp")]

[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC") A [noble](/wiki/Noble "Noble"), either a knight or praetor, is being chased by a small, manhunting animal. This quest appears after 8 days, counting from the moment you've reached 3 colonists for the first time (including if you've started with more). This quest won't appear if you have under 3 colonists, any of your colonists reached the title of a [Yeoman](/wiki/Title#Titles "Title"), if you have disabled honor in your Reward Preferences, or if you are in an extreme biome. 

When accepting this quest: 

  * An empire noble, always with the [Wimp](/wiki/Wimp "Wimp") trait, joins your colony as a quest lodger. A single [manhunter](/wiki/Manhunter "Manhunter") animal - [rat](/wiki/Rat "Rat"), [squirrel](/wiki/Squirrel "Squirrel"), or [raccoon](/wiki/Raccoon "Raccoon") at largest, will be following them.
  * After 3 hours from accepting the quest, an [imperial shuttle](/wiki/Imperial_shuttle "Imperial shuttle") will arrive to pick up the noble. The noble can be carried to the shuttle if they are [downed](/wiki/Downed "Downed").



This quest always gives 8 [honor](/wiki/Honor "Honor"), enough to get a pawn to the [Novice](/wiki/Title#Tiles "Title") rank. 

**Notes:** You can [arrest](/wiki/Arrest "Arrest") the noble wimp, if so desired. The noble will have a _massive_ amount of [resistance](/wiki/Resistance "Resistance") due to their title (+75 for count), which makes it not worth it - unless you have a recruitment inspiration available. 

  * However, with the [Ideology DLC](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Ideology DLC"), they can be [enslaved](/wiki/Slave "Slave")[![Content added by the Ideology DLC](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/16px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Content added by the Ideology DLC") with much less effort. Slavery removes the conceited work restrictions, and gives an innate [mood](/wiki/Mood "Mood") buff that counters regular noble requirements. Meanwhile, being a wimp makes slave rebellions easy to quell. Slavery can be worth betraying the empire, if the noble has some very desirable [psycasts](/wiki/Psycast "Psycast"), like [berserk pulse](/wiki/Berserk_pulse "Berserk pulse"), [farskip](/wiki/Farskip "Farskip"), or [neuroquake](/wiki/Neuroquake "Neuroquake").

[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC") Somebody has deserted the [empire](/wiki/Empire "Empire"). Accepting them will mean that you'll become enemies with the empire. This quest appears after 26 days of the colony start. This quest won't appear if you are in an extreme biome. 

When accepting this quest:

## Desperate Refugees[[edit](/index.php?title=Quests&action=edit&section=18 "Edit section: Desperate Refugees")]

[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC") Desperate refugees are looking to stay at your colony to rest and regroup. 

When accepting this quest: 

  * Refugees join as quest lodgers, and will do work. These refugees will be affiliated with a temporary faction. The higher your current colonist population, the more refugees appear, but the less likely the quest is to generate. 
    * Refugees may offer to join the colony. This cannot happen if the refugee's [mood](/wiki/Mood "Mood") is below 50%. At 50% mood, the MTB to offer to join is 60 days, decreasing to 15 days at 100% mood. There is no penalty for not accepting this join offer.
    * Refugees may betray you. They will become enemies, pick up any nearby weapons, and attack colonists as if they were raiders. They will never randomly betray you if you get a Betrayal Offer yourself. They can still randomly betray you even if some of them have offered to join the colony. Overall, there is a 25% chance of receiving a Betrayal Offer and a 25% chance of refugees betraying you.
    * Refugees with [children](/wiki/Child "Child")[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC") will never randomly betray you if the [storyteller](/wiki/Storyteller "Storyteller") setting [Child enemies](/wiki/AI_Storytellers#Child_enemies "AI Storytellers") is turned off.
  * If a refugee is arrested, experiences a harmful surgery, or is killed, the other refugees will either leave your colony (failing the quest), receive a −12 [thought](/wiki/Thought "Thought"), or betray you immediately. Arresting a refugee who is already in an aggressive [mental state](/wiki/Mental_state "Mental state") does not trigger this reaction.



### Potential Rewards[[edit](/index.php?title=Quests&action=edit&section=19 "Edit section: Potential Rewards")]

  * Allowing the refugees to stay for the full duration and leave the map gives you a 50% chance to receive a random reward from 5 to 20 days later. The reward scales with the number of refugees you accepted and their duration of stay.
  * The leader of a non-hostile faction might send you a Betrayal Offer, in which case you get the quest reward if you kill all members of the refugee group. 
    * If a refugee has joined the colony _before_ the betrayal offer, then you don't need to kill them. If a refugee joins afterwards, or is [arrested](/wiki/Arrest "Arrest") and then recruited, then the quest fails.



**Notes:**

  * If you _really_ want a refugee to join your colony, then it's best to [arrest](/wiki/Arrest "Arrest") one of them immediately, to avoid the chance of betrayal and subsequent death. [Draft](/wiki/Draft "Draft") all the other refugees as far away as possible, for when they go hostile.
  * If you plan on capturing refugees, you can apply anesthetic to them. This allows you to capture every one of them for [whatever purposes you'll want](/wiki/Human_resources "Human resources").

[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC") A noble, leader, or machine persona has requested you to create a specific monument. 

When accepting this quest: 

  * You will immediately receive a monument marker through drop pod.

**Notes:**

  * If you want to preserve the monument, then watch out for [fire](/wiki/Fire "Fire") burning up [wooden](/wiki/Wood "Wood") structures. Shuttles from shuttle quests also start the monument destruction timer.
  * You can take the quest solely for the major threat that appears. You can break the monument to unleash a pack of [manhunting](/wiki/Manhunter "Manhunter") [grizzly bears](/wiki/Grizzly_bear "Grizzly bear") to disrupt an enemy [siege](/wiki/Siege "Siege"), or to call in a [sun blocker](/wiki/Sun_blocker "Sun blocker") with a fluid [ideoligion](/wiki/Ideoligion "Ideoligion"),[![Content added by the Ideology DLC](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/16px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Content added by the Ideology DLC") in order to take advantage of the [Combat in darkness](/wiki/Combat_in_darkness "Combat in darkness") precept.

## Ancient complex[[edit](/index.php?title=Quests&action=edit&section=32 "Edit section: Ancient complex")]

[![Content added by the Ideology DLC](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/16px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Content added by the Ideology DLC") This quest reveals an abandoned ancient complex on the world map, ready to be explored and looted by your colonists. There is also a variant of the quest whereby an [imperial shuttle](/wiki/Imperial_shuttle "Imperial shuttle") will be provided to take a specific number of colonists there. It takes off immediately once the correct number of colonists are inside, and usually lands at the edge of the map with the complex. 

You can reform the caravan once all opened rooms have been cleared of enemies. If your colonists arrived by shuttle, you can send them back, but only when there are no threats within the map. 

Ancient complexes are scaled to player wealth.[Determined when?]

The ancient complex is often littered with derelict structures and desiccated corpses with no information on how long they have been dead for and no decaying health. 

Some rooms are mostly empty, while others contain enemies: 

  * Room filled with [insectoids](/wiki/Insectoids "Insectoids").
  * Room filled with [mechanoids](/wiki/Mechanoids "Mechanoids").
  * Room containing [ancient unstable fuel node](/wiki/Ancient_unstable_fuel_node "Ancient unstable fuel node") that can explode.
  * Room filled with derelict ancient caskets. These will always contain hostile [ancients](/wiki/Ancients "Ancients") in suspended animation.
  * Seemingly empty rooms that trigger an insectoid [infestation](/wiki/Infestation "Infestation") incident upon entry.



The threats may be dormant, waking or exploding upon entry, when crates or other special objects in the room are tampered with, or when a fuel node explode in an adjacent room. They will not be triggered if the door of the room is opened but the cell of the door is not stepped on. As always, insectoids, mechanoids, and ancients are enemies and will try to kill each other on sight. 

You may run into [Hermetic crates](/wiki/Hermetic_crate "Hermetic crate") (contain any item, including [techprints](/wiki/Techprint "Techprint")[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC")), [Security crates](/wiki/Security_crate "Security crate") (contain equipment), [Ancient enemy terminals](/wiki/Ancient_enemy_terminal "Ancient enemy terminal") (call enemy faction) and [Ancient comms consoles](/wiki/Ancient_comms_console "Ancient comms console") (call a drop beacon of loot from random places, with the chance of triggering a raid at the same time) in any of the rooms. All except the comms console have a [Flammability](/wiki/Flammability "Flammability") of 0%[Verify], making them immune to the fires often started by the explosive fuel nodes.

This quest gives no extrinsic reward, and betraying the beggars has no penalty. However, it interacts with the [Charity](/wiki/Charity "Charity") precept. 

**Notes:**

  * Beggars can be arrested, and have _very_ low base resistance (4-8 with low population). If you find a beggar with desirable characteristics, don't be hesitant to capture one.
  * After arresting one beggar, the rest will run and become unable to be arrested, but will fight back when attacked, unlike both [prison breaks](/wiki/Prison_break "Prison break") and fleeing raiders

A selected hostile (usually permanently hostile) faction will arrive 10 hours after the crypto drone lands, and every 8 hours after that in an attempt to destroy the drone, with half of them aiming after any nearby colonist, whereas the other half attacks the drone. 

Upon successfully hacking the cryptodrone, the quest concludes, but the drone will initiate a self-destruct sequence within 30 seconds after its hacked, after which it will explode.

If the opposing faction's goodwill was raised to neutral before accepting the quest, then no raid will happen at all, despite the game's upcoming raid timer.

[![Content added by the Odyssey DLC](/images/thumb/7/7b/OdysseyIcon.png/16px-OdysseyIcon.png)](/wiki/Odyssey_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Odyssey DLC") You are now in the possession of a [grav engine].

While the engine is on the map, you'll passively get new sub-quests to receive new [gravcores](/wiki/Gravcore "Gravcore") and [gravlite panels](/wiki/Gravlite_panel "Gravlite panel") to expand and upgrade your gravship. 

These quests can spawn very far away from where your colony was when you receive them. Most quests can be as far as 160 tiles away from your colony. Some quests may require you to stay for many days, often in harsh conditions like the [Scarlands](/wiki/Scarlands "Scarlands") or even in [orbit](/wiki/Orbit "Orbit"). As such, traveling with a gravship is very recommended. Make sure your gravship has enough resources, food, medicine, as well as fully pressurized living areas if you're going to space. 

Once any 7 of these sub-quests are completed, you'll receive the location for the [mechanoid hive](/wiki/Mechanoid_hive "Mechanoid hive"). 

On non-Grav Ship scenario starts, the Initial Grav Ship quest fires on Day 6. The first sub-quest will fire roughly 1 day after investigating the Grav Ship Engine. 

On the Grav Ship Scenario, the first sub-quest will fire between days 5 and 8. 

Later sub-quests will fire every 15 to 30 days, weighted to have an average time between of 20 days. Grav Ship sub-quests fire only if the player has an installed Grav Engine and the player has less than 3 sub-quests available. 

Sub-quests will fire indefinitely until the player completes the Mech Hive quest. Once the Mech Hive is completed, Grav Cores can still be obtained through random quests.

Often times, your hacking will be detected and you won't be able to continue hacking it until a few hours go by.   * Destroying a stabilizer is much quicker, however doing this will immediately spawn reinforcements mechanoids - often more than the numbers of initial guarding mechanoids. Should you choose this option, make sure you're prepared to defend yourself and your gravship.

## Ship to the Stars[[edit](/index.php?title=Quests&action=edit&section=69 "Edit section: Ship to the Stars")]

Main article: [Endings#Ship to the Stars](/wiki/Endings#Ship_to_the_Stars "Endings")

The A.I Charlon Whitestone has contacted you. If you can reach the journey offer and activate the ship, you can escape this hellish planet. The Ship to the Stars quest is functionally the same as constructing your own [ship](/wiki/Ship "Ship"). This journey offer just gives you a way to leave the planet, without having to [research](/wiki/Research "Research") and gather resources for that ship. 

This quest appears roughly 20 days from game start. 

**Requirements:**

  * Travel to the journey offer. The journey offer will try to spawn as far away from you as possible. This means that this quest is much easier with 30% [map coverage](/wiki/World_generation#Create_world "World generation") than with 100% coverage. Even in a 30% world, it can take months or years to reach the event destination.
  * Prepare defenses and activate the [ship reactor](/wiki/Ship_reactor "Ship reactor"), which takes 15 days. During this time, you will be assaulted by countless [raids](/wiki/Raid "Raid") \- anywhere from 15 to 25 in total. This is completely independent from, and in addition to, your [storyteller](/wiki/Storyteller "Storyteller"), who will send their own raids on their normal schedule. This means that you should expect a _minimum_ of 1 raid per day, often multiple.



Each colonist or animal requires their own [ship cryptosleep casket](/wiki/Ship_cryptosleep_casket "Ship cryptosleep casket") to leave the planet, which may be desired on a personal level. The journey offer ship has space for 18 colonists. 

**Failure conditions:**

  * Destruction of any vital ship part. If you have the ability to create ship parts, then you can potentially repair the ship. However, if the [ship reactor](/wiki/Ship_reactor "Ship reactor") is destroyed, then you will need to start the 15 day activation process again.
  * Doing The Archonexus chain of quests. Accepting the first part of the archonexus quest will invalidate the journey offer ("the ship will fly away as you're settling the new colony")



For tips on traveling to the journey offer and defending Whitestone's ship, see [Endings](/wiki/Endings#Ship_to_the_stars "Endings"). 

After the ship reactor has activated, all you have to do is load your colonists and escape the planet. 

## Royal Ascent[[edit](/index.php?title=Quests&action=edit&section=70 "Edit section: Royal Ascent")]

Main article: [Endings#Royal Ascent](/wiki/Endings#Royal_Ascent "Endings")

[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC") The High Stellarch of the [empire](/wiki/Empire "Empire") is willing to perform a customary visit to any [noble](/wiki/Noble "Noble") of the Count rank or higher. If all goes well, then your colonists can leave to space via [imperial shuttle](/wiki/Imperial_shuttle "Imperial shuttle"). 

This quest will appear roughly 35 days from colony start. 

**Requirements:**

  * Have a colonist of [Count](/wiki/Titles#Count.2FCountess "Titles") rank and not be hostile to the [empire](/wiki/Empire "Empire") faction.
  * Have a spare bedroom suitable for a stellarch at the time of acceptance (see [Titles#Table of Requirements](/wiki/Titles#Table_of_Requirements "Titles")). Your count must also have a suitable bedroom.
  * House the High Stellarch for 12 days, a controllable guest. You can't just use a [cryptosleep casket](/wiki/Cryptosleep_casket "Cryptosleep casket") to preserve the stellarch until the end.
  * Like the ship launch, you will be assaulted by a massive quantity of raiders during the 12 days.
  * The High Stellarch must be kept above 25% average mood for the duration of his stay.



You will also receive 4 cataphract-level soldiers ([Stellic Wardens or Defenders](/wiki/Raiders#Empire "Raiders")). These soldiers can die without consequence. Like any other soldier guest, they will have the +12 _On Duty_ [moodlet](/wiki/Mood "Mood"). However, some of them will be conceited nobles. This includes the ability to use [psycasts](/wiki/Psycast "Psycast"). 

**Failure conditions:**

  * High Stellarch dies.
  * High Stellarch mood is too low for too long.
  * The [empire](/wiki/Empire "Empire") faction becomes hostile to you.



Your count/countess does _not_ have to stay alive in order succeed the quest. 

The Royal Ascent quest will be offered again periodically if it is failed. It takes roughly 22 days from failure to get this quest again. This quest technically fails if you abandon the colony it asks for [Gravship launch?], but it will refire again. 

If you've completed this quest, then an [imperial shuttle](/wiki/Imperial_shuttle "Imperial shuttle") will come pick up the stellarch and your colonists into the imperial ranks. Note that while the shuttle UI has a limit of 2,000 kg, you can fit as many colonists as you want by selecting the colonist and right clicking "Get into shuttle". 

## The Archonexus[[edit](/index.php?title=Quests&action=edit&section=71 "Edit section: The Archonexus")]

Main article: [Endings#The Archonexus](/wiki/Endings#The_Archonexus "Endings")

[![Content added by the Ideology DLC](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/16px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Content added by the Ideology DLC") The [archonexus core](/wiki/Archonexus_core "Archonexus core") is waiting to be found. Gather 3 map fragments - each fragment given in exchange for your entire colony - and you can go activate it. 

This quest first appears once you reach $150,000 colony [wealth](/wiki/Wealth "Wealth"). 

**Part 1** : 
    
    
    _A group of wild people/_**< FACTION NAME>**_are/is looking to expand their territory, and are interested in taking over_**< COLONY NAME>**_with all its wealth. If you can increase your total wealth to $350000, they will take the settlement. In trade, they offer you part of a map showing the location of an ancient archotech nexus._ - Part 1 Description 
    

In order to accept this quest, you must have a colony [wealth](/wiki/Wealth "Wealth") of $350,000 (in a single colony). If there is a valid alliable faction on the map, you must be allied with one in order to proceed. 

When accepting this part of the quest: 

  * All progress will be reset. Research will be reset to industrial levels. Any colonies you had will be procured by the faction in question.
  * You will start a new colony, anywhere on the map. 
    * You can bring 5 colonists, 5 [animals](/wiki/Animal "Animal"), 1 [relic](/wiki/Relic "Relic"), and 7 stacks of items. You can bring different amounts of different items; the list can be seen at [List of items](/wiki/Endings#List_of_items "Endings"). In addition, you will start with supplies from your [scenario](/wiki/Scenario "Scenario"). Food, equipment, but not building materials from scenario are given.
  * Whitestone's ship for the Ship to the Stars quest will fly away, failing the actual quest. You can still build your own [ship](/wiki/Ship "Ship"), if so desired.



No time passes during the travel to the new location. Pregnant animals will have the same stage of pregnancy on arrival. Growing season at the destination might affect the player's timing for accepting the quest. 

Once your colonists have landed in their new colony, the [major archotech structure](/wiki/Major_archotech_structure "Major archotech structure") be present in the center of the map. It must be studied in order to continue. 

**Part 2** : 
    
    
    _The map to the archonexus has three parts, and you hold one of them._**< FACTION A NAME>**_and_**< FACTION B NAME>**_also each hold parts of the map. They'll trade their parts to you for a wealthy new colony and all of its recorded research. The next map part is encoded in a very strange way. In order to interpret it, you must first learn more about archotechnology by studying the structure here._ - Part 2 Description 
    

Like with the first part of this quest, you must have another colony at $350,000 wealth, be allied with a specific faction (if present). You must also study the major archotech structure. 

When accepting this part of the quest: 

  * All progress will be reset. Research will be reset to industrial levels. Any colonies you had will be procured by the faction in question.
  * You will start a new colony, anywhere on the map. Same items as last time.



On the third colony (2nd restart), you will have a [grand archotech structure](/wiki/Grand_archotech_structure "Grand archotech structure") instead of a major archotech structure. This is functionally the same building, but you'll need to spend more time studying it. 

**Part 3** : 
    
    
    _You have two parts of the archonexus map._**< FACTION B NAME>**_have the last piece of the map, passed down from the time their ancestors survived the cataclysm. They'll give it to you if you give them a wealthy new colony in exchange. As before, the map part is encoded. In order to decode it, you must first study the archotech structure._ - Part 3 Description
    

Like with the other parts of this quest, you must have another colony at $350,000 wealth, be allied with a specific faction (if present). You must also study the grand archotech structure. 

**Unlike the other parts of this quest, you don't need to restart the colony for a 3rd time. In order to activate the archonexus core, you will need a _total_ of 3 colonies at $350,000 wealth and need to reset 2 times.**

When accepting this part of the quest: 

  * Progress will _not_ be reset. You must travel to the archonexus core, as indicated on the world map.
  * Once you have reached the [archonexus core](/wiki/Archonexus_core "Archonexus core"), all you have to do is activate it. There will be hostile [mechanoids](/wiki/Mechanoid "Mechanoid"), but these can be ignored.



Once activating the core, the game will end, permanently. You are unable to continue with colonists left behind, unlike the other endings. (However you can reload a previous save, if desired).
