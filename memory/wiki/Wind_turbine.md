## Acquisition[[edit](/index.php?title=Wind_turbine&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Acquisition")]

Wind turbines can be constructed once the [Electricity](/wiki/Research#Electricity "Research") research project has been completed. Each requires [![Steel](/images/thumb/9/90/Steel_c.png/16px-Steel_c.png)](/wiki/Steel "Steel") 100 [Steel](/wiki/Steel "Steel"), [![Component](/images/thumb/4/40/Component.png/16px-Component.png)](/wiki/Component "Component") 2 [Components](/wiki/Component "Component"), 3,300 [ticks](/wiki/Time "Time") (55 secs) of work modified by the [construction speed](/wiki/Construction_speed "Construction speed") of the builder, and a [construction](/wiki/Construction "Construction") skill of 4. 

Once constructed, they cannot be re-installed; they can only be deconstructed.  
Wind turbines can not be bought from traders. 

## Summary[[edit](/index.php?title=Wind_turbine&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Summary")]

[![StumpChoppedA \(cropped\).png](/images/thumb/6/6a/StumpChoppedA_%28cropped%29.png/40px-StumpChoppedA_%28cropped%29.png)](/wiki/Category:Stubs "Category:Stubs") | **This section is a[stub](/wiki/Category:Stubs "Category:Stubs").** You can help RimWorld Wiki by **[expanding it](https://rimworldwiki.com/index.php?title=Wind_turbine&action=edit). Wind turbines generate a variable amount of power based on the current wind speed, which in turn is based on ranges set by the current [Weather](/wiki/Weather "Weather"). The wattage is directly proportional to the windspeed. They output 2300W at 100% wind speed, and 3450W at 150% wind speed, the maximum. Note that the "max power output" listed in the infobox claims that 2300W is the maximum under "ideal conditions" - this is incorrect. Wind direction is not a factor, so orientation of the turbines doesn't matter; they will spin equally well in any direction. 

Like all generators, each tile of a wind turbine counts as a [power conduit](/wiki/Power_conduit "Power conduit"). Turbines directly adjacent to each other will connect (see Fig. 1), and appliances within 6 tiles can connect to any tile of a turbine 

Generators are temporarily disabled by sources of [EMP](/wiki/EMP "EMP"), with downtime being equal to half the amount of EMP damage dealt. During this time, it will not generate any power. Unlike mechanoids, generators do not adapt to EMP and thus can be continuously restunned. 

### Exclusion zone[[edit](/index.php?title=Wind_turbine&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Exclusion zone")]

The wind turbine's exclusion zone (7×18 white rectangle) is visible when the turbine is selected, or when placing the wind turbine blueprint. Any trees, mountains, roofs, and most buildings will reduce the turbine's output by 20% - five obstructed tiles reduce power to 0. The exclusion zone of several turbines may overlap, so long as the turbine building itself isn't in any of the zones. 

Low-lying objects like [solar generators](/wiki/Solar_generator "Solar generator") and [shelves](/wiki/Shelf "Shelf") can be placed without hindering the turbine's operation, and are an efficient use of space. [Growing zones](/wiki/Growing_zone "Growing zone") (if not set to trees) or artificial [flooring](/wiki/Floors "Floors") can also be used to prevent wild trees from growing. 

Additionally, wind turbines do not function in [orbit](/wiki/Orbit "Orbit")[![Content added by the Odyssey DLC](/images/thumb/7/7b/OdysseyIcon.png/16px-OdysseyIcon.png)](/wiki/Odyssey_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Odyssey DLC").

## Analysis[[edit](/index.php?title=Wind_turbine&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Analysis")]

Wind turbines are a renewable (i.e, fuel-free) source of energy, and have the advantage of requiring no [research](/wiki/Research "Research") past Electricity. Both [wood-fired generators](/wiki/Wood-fired_generator "Wood-fired generator") and [chemfuel generators](/wiki/Chemfuel_generator "Chemfuel generator") will constantly require a source of fuel. 

Wind itself is fairly inconsistent in terms of power, but this problem can be mitigated by installing [batteries](/wiki/Batteries "Batteries") (which require some research). With a battery, wind becomes a somewhat stable energy source, working [day and night](/wiki/Light#Daylight_timing_and_latitude "Light"). An [eclipse](/wiki/Eclipse "Eclipse") will completely shut down a [solar generator](/wiki/Solar_generator "Solar generator"), but no event can entirely stop wind. Only a small subset of other dangers, like fog or rain [weather controllers](/wiki/Weather_controller "Weather controller"),[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC") can reduce it. Note that, even with batteries, the exact power average can be unstable. 

Their other main drawback is the space requirement - a need for a large, open area clear of [trees](/wiki/Trees "Trees"), mountains, [buildings](/wiki/Buildings "Buildings"), [roofs](/wiki/Roof "Roof") and other tall constructions. This makes turbines harder to protect. You can still build a wall just outside of its zone, but supporting multiple turbines quickly becomes a time and space consuming project. The empty space can also be used to house short objects that won't block the wind. One option is solar generators, although this creates a risk of having a large amount of power generated in one space that needs to be properly protected. Solar generators also don't fit fully in the space of turbines, which makes land for trees to sprout and block the wind. Another empty space item is crops. Regardless of height, no crop will block the wind, and pawns will automatically cut trees in growing zones. Crops can however catch fire if a turbine is destroyed, creating the risk of food loss, and they may need to be planted in [bad soil](/wiki/Plants#Fertility "Plants") to fit with turbine placement. 

[Watermill generators](/wiki/Watermill_generator "Watermill generator") and [geothermal generators](/wiki/Geothermal_generator "Geothermal generator") offer completely consistent power, but require dedicated research, and can only be built in certain locations. 

### Tips[[edit](/index.php?title=Wind_turbine&action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: Tips")]

  1. Certain [weather](/wiki/Weather "Weather") conditions offer a positive modifier to wind, like Thunderstorm or Snow. If a [weather controller](/wiki/Weather_controller "Weather controller") of those types is deployed, then it may be in your interest to preserve them.
