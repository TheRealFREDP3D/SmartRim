## Acquisition[[edit](/index.php?title=Human_meat&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Acquisition")]

Human meat is obtained when a [cook](/wiki/Work#Cook "Work") butchers [humans](/wiki/Human "Human"). The base [Meat Yield](/wiki/Meat_Amount "Meat Amount") are described on the table below but the actual number depends on the [Butchery Efficiency](/wiki/Butchery_Efficiency "Butchery Efficiency") of the butcher, damage of the corpse, and a number of other factors. 

* Animal | Meat Yield   
---|---  
[Human](/wiki/Human "Human") | 140   
[Creepjoiner](/wiki/Creepjoiner "Creepjoiner")[![Content added by the Anomaly DLC](/images/thumb/3/32/AnomalyIcon.png/16px-AnomalyIcon.png)](/wiki/Anomaly_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Anomaly DLC") | 140   
  
Without the [Ideology DLC](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Ideology DLC"), butchering a human gives −6 [mood](/wiki/Mood "Mood") for all colonists in the colony, and an additional −6 to the butcher. The former moodlet does not stack, meaning butchering 100 corpses gives the same colony-wide mood as one (the butcher's penalty _does_ stack). [Psychopaths](/wiki/Psychopath "Psychopath") and pawns with [bloodlust](/wiki/Bloodlust "Bloodlust") ignore either butchering penalty, as do any [cannibals](/wiki/Cannibal "Cannibal"). 

The mood effects of butchering humans with an [Ideoligion](/wiki/Ideoligion "Ideoligion")[![Content added by the Ideology DLC](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/16px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Content added by the Ideology DLC") are based on the precept of the ideoligion the pawn follows.

## Summary[[edit](/index.php?title=Human_meat&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Summary")]

As a [raw food](/wiki/Raw_food "Raw food"), human meat can be be eaten raw, with a flat 2% chance of giving [food poisoning](/wiki/Food_poisoning "Food poisoning"), or cooked into a [meal](/wiki/Meal "Meal") with a food poisoning chance dependent on the skill of the cook and the [cleanliness](/wiki/Cleanliness "Cleanliness") of the kitchen. [Animals](/wiki/Animals "Animals") are immune to food poisoning from raw food but not from meals. When used in food recipes that require specific types of [nutrition](/wiki/Nutrition "Nutrition"), such as [fine meals](/wiki/Fine_meal "Fine meal"), human meat are classified as meat. 

Human meat is like any other [raw food](/wiki/Raw_food "Raw food"). It can be used to create [meals](/wiki/Meal "Meal"), or combined with other ingredients to produce fine, lavish meals, pemmican and kibble. 

Most humans despise eating human meat, which is cannibalism. Raw human meat gives −20 _Raw cannibalism_ [thought](/wiki/Thought "Thought") for 1 [day](/wiki/Time "Time"), and cooking human meat into [meals](/wiki/Meal "Meal") (including in [nutrient paste](/wiki/Nutrient_paste_meal "Nutrient paste meal")) gives −15 _Cooked cannibalism_ [thought](/wiki/Thought "Thought") for 1 [day](/wiki/Time "Time"). Pawns with the [Cannibal](/wiki/Cannibal_\(trait\) "Cannibal \(trait\)") trait instead gain +20 _Raw cannibalism_ [thought](/wiki/Thought "Thought") for 1 [day](/wiki/Time "Time") for raw human meat and +15 _Cooked cannibalism_ [thought](/wiki/Thought "Thought") for 1 [day](/wiki/Time "Time") for cooked human meat. The moodlets for raw and cooked cannibalism stack with each other, as do any moodlets from meal types. 

Depending on the [canibalism precept](/wiki/Cannibalism_\(precept\) "Cannibalism \(precept\)") [![Content added by the Ideology DLC](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/16px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Content added by the Ideology DLC") colonists gain instead: 

  * Cannibalism abhorrent: −20 _Ate human meat_ [thought](/wiki/Thought "Thought") for 1 [day](/wiki/Time "Time")
  * Cannibalism horrible: −12 _Ate human meat_ [thought](/wiki/Thought "Thought") for 1 [day](/wiki/Time "Time")
  * Cannibalism dissapproved: −5 _Ate human meat_ [thought](/wiki/Thought "Thought") for 1 [day](/wiki/Time "Time")
  * Cannibalism acceptable: _none_
  * Cannibalism preferred: +2 _Ate human meat_ [thought](/wiki/Thought "Thought") for 1 [day](/wiki/Time "Time")
  * Cannibalism required (strong): +4 _Ate human meat_ [thought](/wiki/Thought "Thought") for 1 [day](/wiki/Time "Time")
  * Cannibalism required (ravenous): +6 _Ate human meat_ [thought](/wiki/Thought "Thought") for 1 [day](/wiki/Time "Time")



Processing human meat, once it has been butchered, incurs no penalties. Human meat can be made into meals, used for animal feed, processed in [biofuel refinery](/wiki/Biofuel_refinery "Biofuel refinery") for [chemfuel](/wiki/Chemfuel "Chemfuel"), or put in a [biosculpter pod](/wiki/Biosculpter_pod "Biosculpter pod")[![Content added by the Ideology DLC](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/16px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Content added by the Ideology DLC") or [growth vat](/wiki/Growth_vat "Growth vat"),[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC") and nobody will feel bad about it unless it is eaten directly. 

When consumed by [Hemogenic](/wiki/Hemogenic "Hemogenic")[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC") pawns, human meat satisfies the [Hemogen](/wiki/Hemogen "Hemogen")[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC") need by +3.75 per 1 [nutrition](/wiki/Nutrition "Nutrition"), or ![Human meat](/images/thumb/f/fe/Meat_human_c.png/16px-Meat_human_c.png) 20, consumed.[Rounding?]. Note that only the raw meat provides this hemogen, eating [corpses](/wiki/Corpse "Corpse") or the meat cooked into [meals](/wiki/Meals "Meals") does not. 

Its market value is only [![Silver](/images/thumb/5/57/Silver_c.png/16px-Silver_c.png)](/wiki/Silver "Silver") 0.8 [silver](/wiki/Silver "Silver"), or 40% of regular meat. However, processing it into meals or [chemfuel](/wiki/Chemfuel "Chemfuel") will ignore its origin when considering market value. 

Human meat is also used as a building ingredient for the [cannibal platter](/wiki/Cannibal_platter "Cannibal platter"),[![Content added by the Ideology DLC](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/16px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Content added by the Ideology DLC") which requires ![Human meat](/images/thumb/f/fe/Meat_human_c.png/16px-Meat_human_c.png) 120\. 

## Analysis[[edit](/index.php?title=Human_meat&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Analysis")]

Butchering a human is already upsetting for a good majority of colonists. So in a colony not filled with cannibals, eating it should only be for absolute emergencies, in extreme [biomes](/wiki/Biome "Biome") like sea ice, or to please a cannibal on verge of [mental break](/wiki/Mental_break "Mental break"). For a cannibal, both the cooked cannibalism and raw cannibalism mood buffs stack, for a total of +35 mood. However, most cannibals still get −7 _Ate raw food_ [thought](/wiki/Thought "Thought") for 1 [day](/wiki/Time "Time"). You can further boost mood with [fine meals](/wiki/Fine_meal "Fine meal"), [lavish meals](/wiki/Lavish_meal "Lavish meal"), or their [carnivore](/wiki/Carnivore_fine_meal "Carnivore fine meal") [variants](/wiki/Carnivore_lavish_meal "Carnivore lavish meal"). Alternatively, the massive mood debuff might make human meat situationally useful. It may force a [tortured artist](/wiki/Tortured_artist "Tortured artist") into a mental break, or cause a [Crisis of belief](/wiki/Crisis_of_belief "Crisis of belief") in the [Ideology DLC](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Ideology DLC"). As for [Caravans](/wiki/Caravan "Caravan") and Raiding, Human Meat is one of the most abundant food sources that can be acquired while on-site by butchering several of the corpses found from the pawns killed and in [Gibbet cages](/wiki/Gibbet_cage "Gibbet cage"),[![Content added by the Ideology DLC](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/16px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Content added by the Ideology DLC") cooking them into Meals for slower spoilage. 

Using human meat for carnivorous animals (whenever raw or [kibble](/wiki/Kibble "Kibble")) isn't cannibalism, so the animals won't mind. It may be wiser to leave corpses unbutchered, as the butchering itself has a fairly large penalty to both the butcher and the colony. Note that colonists in base game or with the [Corpses: Ugly](/wiki/Corpses_\(precept\) "Corpses \(precept\)") precept[![Content added by the Ideology DLC](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/16px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Content added by the Ideology DLC") will get −4 mood from observing them. 

In the [Ideology DLC](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Ideology DLC"), a colony's [ideoligion](/wiki/Ideoligion "Ideoligion") can make this a different story. With the Cannibalism meme and/or precept, [raiders](/wiki/Raider "Raider") become a completely viable source of both food and positive moodlets. Setting the precept to Required (Ravenous) gives the greatest mood buffs, +6 per meal, but also gives a strong debuff when human meat isn't available. This is also possible in the base game, with a colony entirely composed of the cannibal trait. By adjusting the [scenario](/wiki/Scenario "Scenario") ("Forced trait"), you can set all starting colonists, or every pawn in the game, to be cannibals.
