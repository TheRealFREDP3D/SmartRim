**Food** is the basic source of nutrition to both [humans](/wiki/Human "Human") and [animals](/wiki/Animals "Animals"). Consuming food provides [saturation](/wiki/Saturation "Saturation"). Deprivation of food leads to [malnutrition](/wiki/Malnutrition "Malnutrition") and, if prolonged, [death](/wiki/Death "Death"). 

## Summary[[edit](/index.php?title=Food&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Summary")]

Food is measured in units of [nutrition](/wiki/Nutrition "Nutrition"). Most raw foods, as well as certain cooked foods like [pemmican](/wiki/Pemmican "Pemmican"), give 0.05 nutrition per unit. Prepared [meals](/wiki/Meal "Meal"), such as [simple meals](/wiki/Simple_meal "Simple meal") or [lavish meals](/wiki/Lavish_meal "Lavish meal"), generally give 0.9 or 1.0 nutrition. 

Baseline adult [humans](/wiki/Human "Human") require 1.6 [nutrition](/wiki/Nutrition "Nutrition") per day to avoid being hungry. They can "store" 1.0 nutrition at any one time. As they start to eat while they are "hungry" (0.25 [saturation](/wiki/Saturation "Saturation")), certain types of meals are subject to [overeating](/wiki/Overeating "Overeating"). A rough baseline of 2 meals per day, or 32 units of pemmican/raw food, is required to feed a person. 

By default, colonists will eat the tastiest valid food; from Lavish to Raw. Certain [traits](/wiki/Trait "Trait") or [precepts](/wiki/Precept "Precept")[![Content added by the Ideology DLC](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/16px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Content added by the Ideology DLC") may change their priorities. You can control what foods colonists are able to eat in the [Assign](/wiki/Assign "Assign") tab. You can also select a colonist's or prisoner's food policy in the Health tab. 

### Raw food[[edit](/index.php?title=Food&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Raw food")]

For a list of raw food items, see [Raw food](/wiki/Raw_food "Raw food").

Food found in its natural stage is raw. Most raw foods will give the −7 _Ate Raw Food_ [moodlet](/wiki/Mood "Mood") to an ordinary colonist. All raw food comes with an innate chance of [food poisoning](/wiki/Food_poisoning "Food poisoning"). [Milk](/wiki/Milk "Milk"), [berries](/wiki/Berries "Berries"), and [insect jelly](/wiki/Insect_jelly "Insect jelly") can be eaten without mood penalty, but can still give food poisoning. 

You can cook food into [meals](/wiki/Meal "Meal") at an [electric stove](/wiki/Electric_stove "Electric stove"), [fueled stove](/wiki/Fueled_stove "Fueled stove") or [campfire](/wiki/Campfire "Campfire"), or process it at a [nutrient paste dispenser](/wiki/Nutrient_paste_dispenser "Nutrient paste dispenser"). In addition to tasting better, cooked food allows you to get more nutrition off the same amount of raw food. For example, a [simple meal](/wiki/Simple_meal "Simple meal") costs 0.5 nutrition to make, but gives 0.9 nutrition when eaten - this equates to 180% more food. Food poisoning of a prepared meal is based off of Cooking skill and cleanliness of the room. [Nutrient paste](/wiki/Nutrient_paste_meal "Nutrient paste meal") can never cause food poisoning. 

Raw food can be obtained from a variety of sources: 

  * [Wild bushes](/wiki/Plants "Plants") can be [harvested](/wiki/Harvest "Harvest") to give [agave](/wiki/Agave "Agave") and [berries](/wiki/Berry_bush "Berry bush").
  * [Growing zones](/wiki/Growing_zone "Growing zone") can be used to plant [rice](/wiki/Rice_plant "Rice plant"), [potatoes](/wiki/Potato_plant "Potato plant"), [corn](/wiki/Corn_plant "Corn plant"), [strawberries](/wiki/Strawberry_plant "Strawberry plant") and [haygrass](/wiki/Haygrass "Haygrass") (for domesticated animals). 
    * [Hydroponics basins](/wiki/Hydroponics_basin "Hydroponics basin") can also be used to plant rice, potatoes or strawberries, and grows them at a considerably increased rate.
  * Meat is obtained from fresh non-mechanoid corpses, whether hunted or if they died via other means. [Corpses](/wiki/Human_resources "Human resources") may be eaten raw or butchered at a [butcher table](/wiki/Butcher_table "Butcher table").
  * Occasional drops from [cargo pods](/wiki/Cargo_pods "Cargo pods").



## Degradation[[edit](/index.php?title=Food&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Degradation")]

[![](/images/thumb/0/03/Food_has_rotted_away_in_storage.png/300px-Food_has_rotted_away_in_storage.png)](/wiki/File:Food_has_rotted_away_in_storage.png)

[](/wiki/File:Food_has_rotted_away_in_storage.png "Enlarge")

As of version [0.15.1279](/wiki/Version/0.15.1279 "Version/0.15.1279") (29 August 2016) there is a message telling you when food has spoiled.

Food will be destroyed if left unattended by two entirely separate factors: exposure and temperature. 

  * Items dropped in an area without a [roof](/wiki/Roof "Roof") will [deteriorate](/wiki/Deteriorate "Deteriorate") over time, and lose item HP.


  * Storing food in temperatures above 0 °C (32 °F) will spoil over time. Temperatures below 10 °C (50 °F) will slow down spoiling by a factor of `1/temp` in Celsius. See [Temperature](/wiki/Temperature#Temperature_Thresholds "Temperature") for details. 
    * In the early game, food spoilage can pose a threat if located on warm [biomes](/wiki/Biomes "Biomes"). A freezer room can be created by placing enough [coolers](/wiki/Cooler "Cooler") in proportion to its size - the bigger the storage, the more coolers required.



For example: a [raider](/wiki/Raider "Raider") is killed and drops a fine meal outdoors, while temperature is below freezing. The meal will not spoil, but it will still _deteriorate_ , and lose HP. Conversely, a meal stored indoors at room temperature will spoil rather than deteriorating. 

Once spoiled, food will vanish and can never be recovered. However, food can be eaten at any stage before spoiling with no negative effect. Food poisoning comes from other sources, such as a messy kitchen, an unskilled cook, or eating raw food.

All foods belong to one or more food types, which determine which animals they can be eaten by. 

## Analysis[[edit](/index.php?title=Food&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Analysis")]

As a rough rule of thumb - 25 tiles of [rice](/wiki/Rice_plant "Rice plant") / [potatoes](/wiki/Potato_plant "Potato plant") / [corn](/wiki/Corn_plant "Corn plant"), in ordinary soil, is enough to feed a single colonist in [Losing is Fun](/wiki/Difficulty "Difficulty") indefinitely, when: 

  1. They are cooked into and eaten as [simple meals](/wiki/Simple_meal "Simple meal").
  2. A grower's Plants skill is competent; 6 Plants is enough.
  3. Crops are harvested reasonably quickly. 25 tiles gives a little extra food in case of emergency, but assumes that Growing is priority #1 in the [Work](/wiki/Work "Work") tab.
  4. Your colony has a year-round growing season.



20 tiles of rice/corn in _rich soil_ (not potatoes) is also enough to feed a colonist, in the same conditions, with some food in reserve. Since a higher [difficulty](/wiki/Difficulty "Difficulty") reduces plant yield, on difficulties lower than Losing is Fun less food is required. If your biome doesn't have a winter, then just plant your tiles of rice, and you don't really have to worry about managing food unless [toxic fallout](/wiki/Toxic_fallout "Toxic fallout") happens. 

### Food crop comparison[[edit](/index.php?title=Food&action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: Food crop comparison")]

Of the three "main" food crops: 

  * [![Rice](/images/thumb/d/da/Rice.png/24px-Rice.png)](/wiki/Rice "Rice") [Rice](/wiki/Rice "Rice") grows quickly, but gives the least per harvest. Rice is stable; since each individual rice harvest is small, a [blight](/wiki/Blight "Blight") will not impact a rice harvest as much as it does with other crops. However, because it needs to be harvested more often, you will need more work for the same amount of food. Due to its grow speed, it is a great food to plant at the beginning of the game, or as a "last harvest" when winter is fast approaching.
  * [![Corn](/images/thumb/f/f8/Corn.png/24px-Corn.png)](/wiki/Corn "Corn") [Corn](/wiki/Corn "Corn") is the opposite of rice. It grows slowly, but gives the most per harvest. It needs to be harvested much less frequently, meaning corn takes much less work than rice. However, your corn harvest will be impacted more by disasters like blight and fire. Corn cannot be grown in [hydroponics](/wiki/Hydroponics "Hydroponics").
  * [![Potatoes](/images/thumb/4/42/Potatoes.png/24px-Potatoes.png)](/wiki/Potatoes "Potatoes") [Potatoes](/wiki/Potatoes "Potatoes") are in the middle in terms of speed, harvest size, and work. Potatoes are notable for their low Fertility Sensitivity, meaning the quality of soil impacts them much less. They are great if you are forced to plant in stony soil or gravel, but bad to grow in rich soil or hydroponics.



Rice, corn, and potatoes give roughly the same amount of food per day per plant, assuming regular soil is used. Rice is ever so slightly higher than the other 2 crops. 

Of the other available crops: 

  * [![Berries](/images/thumb/2/24/Berries.png/24px-Berries.png)](/wiki/Berries "Berries") [Strawberry plants](/wiki/Strawberry_plant "Strawberry plant") give less food per day than any of the three food crops. It grows faster than potatoes, but slower than rice. Its niche is that strawberries can be eaten raw without a mood penalty, but this is rarely useful given that cooking food into [meals](/wiki/Meal "Meal") will net you more effective food.
  * [![Hay](/images/thumb/d/d1/Hay_c.png/24px-Hay_c.png)](/wiki/Hay "Hay") [Hay](/wiki/Hay "Hay") is inedible to humans, but gives the most food per day of all plants. Slightly less work efficient per than corn. Hay can also be used for [straw matting](/wiki/Straw_matting "Straw matting").
  * [![Raw fungus](/images/thumb/e/ea/Raw_fungus.png/24px-Raw_fungus.png)](/wiki/Raw_fungus "Raw fungus") [Nutrifungus](/wiki/Nutrifungus "Nutrifungus")[![Content added by the Ideology DLC](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/16px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Content added by the Ideology DLC") is almost identical to potatoes; it grows slightly slower in regular soil, but has even less Fertility Sensitivity (meaning slightly better growth in rough soil). Nutrifungus' benefit is that it _has_ to be grown in complete darkness. This means that it can be grown indoors, allowing it to be grown in heated rooms in the winter, without having to use a power hungry [sun lamp](/wiki/Sun_lamp "Sun lamp"). As another benefit, nutrifungus is completely immune to [blight](/wiki/Blight "Blight"). However, most [ideoligions](/wiki/Ideoligion "Ideoligion") give −3 for _Ate Cooked Fungus_. [Tunnelers](/wiki/Tunneler_\(meme\) "Tunneler \(meme\)") enjoy eating fungus and despise any other plant. Tunnelers can construct [fungal gravel](/wiki/Fungal_gravel "Fungal gravel") to create fertile soil under their mountains.
  * [![Toxipotato](/images/thumb/f/f2/Toxipotatoes.png/24px-Toxipotatoes.png)](/wiki/Toxipotato "Toxipotato") [Toxipotatoes](/wiki/Toxipotato "Toxipotato")[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC") have no fertility sensitivity at all, and are the only food source that can be planted in polluted terrain. They provide strictly inferior food per work and per day to the standard potato, but do reach full growth between one and three days quicker depending on the terrain.[Early harvest?] They have double the chance to cause food poisoning when eaten raw.



### Food preparation[[edit](/index.php?title=Food&action=edit&section=7 "Edit section: Food preparation")]

  * [![Nutrient paste meal](/images/thumb/c/c7/Nutrient_paste_meal_a.png/16px-Nutrient_paste_meal_a.png)](/wiki/Nutrient_paste_meal "Nutrient paste meal") [Nutrient paste](/wiki/Nutrient_paste "Nutrient paste") is the most efficient way to prepare food, with 300% efficiency (0.3 nutrition of raw food required for a 0.9 nutrition meal) and zero chance of [food poisoning](/wiki/Food_poisoning "Food poisoning"), but inflicts a −4 [mood](/wiki/Mood "Mood") penalty for eating it. Great when work is in short supply or if there is no skilled cook, assuming the colony has enough research to build a [nutrient paste dispenser](/wiki/Nutrient_paste_dispenser "Nutrient paste dispenser").
  * [![Simple meal](/images/thumb/8/84/Simple_meal_a.png/16px-Simple_meal_a.png)](/wiki/Simple_meal "Simple meal") [Simple meals](/wiki/Simple_meal "Simple meal") are the simplest and most nutrionally efficient of the standard meals. Good for starting out if a cook is available or if nutrient paste isn't available.
  * [![Fine meal](/images/thumb/f/fe/Fine_meal_a.png/16px-Fine_meal_a.png)](/wiki/Fine_meal "Fine meal") [Fine meals](/wiki/Fine_meal "Fine meal") take more work and minimum skill to create for a +5 mood boost. There are three variants. Regular fine meals are as nutirtionally efficient as simple meals, but require vegetables and meat/animal products. [Vegetarian fine meals](/wiki/Vegetarian_fine_meal "Vegetarian fine meal") and [carnivore fine meals](/wiki/Carnivore_fine_meal "Carnivore fine meal") can be made using only one type of ingredient but require 50% more food to make. They are good for mid-game colonies that can afford to source meat/animal products (for regular fine meals) or to grow extra food (for vegearian fine meals).
  * [![Pemmican](/images/thumb/d/d0/Pemmican_c.png/16px-Pemmican_c.png)](/wiki/Pemmican "Pemmican") [Pemmican](/wiki/Pemmican "Pemmican") and [![Packaged survival meal](/images/thumb/e/ef/Packaged_survival_meal_a.png/16px-Packaged_survival_meal_a.png)](/wiki/Packaged_survival_meal "Packaged survival meal") [packaged survival meals](/wiki/Packaged_survival_meal "Packaged survival meal") can survive a long time or indefinitely without spoiling, respectively. They require vegetables and meat/animal products and are less efficient than regular fine meals.
  * [![Lavish meal](/images/thumb/f/f4/Lavish_meal_a.png/16px-Lavish_meal_a.png)](/wiki/Lavish_meal "Lavish meal") [Lavish meals](/wiki/Lavish_meal "Lavish meal") provide a +12 mood boost at the cost of requring more food, work, and minimum skill than fine meals; note that the mood boosts from fine meals and lavish meals stack.
