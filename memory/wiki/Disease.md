This article is about treatable illnesses. For physical damage, see [Injury](/wiki/Injury "Injury"). For chronic health conditions, see [Ailments](/wiki/Ailments "Ailments").

**Diseases** are temporary and treatable ailments, that pass away after a certain amount of time or treatment. 

## Contract[[edit](/index.php?title=Disease&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Contract")]

Most diseases are contracted as part of an [event](/wiki/Event "Event"). Your colony's [biome](/wiki/Biome "Biome") determines how common diseases are, as well as how common each disease is. However, some diseases happen situationally, regardless of biome: 

  * [Infection](/wiki/Infection "Infection") may occur after a bleeding [injury](/wiki/Injury "Injury"), burn, or frostbite is inflicted. Tending the injury in a clean room reduces the chance of infection.
  * [Lung rot](/wiki/Lung_rot "Lung rot") occurs randomly when inhaling enough [rot stink](/wiki/Rot_stink "Rot stink").
  * [Blood rot](/wiki/Blood_rot "Blood rot")[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC") and [paralytic abasia](/wiki/Paralytic_abasia "Paralytic abasia")[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC") are exclusively part of [quests](/wiki/Quest "Quest"), while [infant illness](/wiki/Infant_illness "Infant illness")[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC") can only happen during [birth](/wiki/Reproduction#Birth "Reproduction").



Note that with the exception of [Scaria](/wiki/Scaria "Scaria"), diseases are **not contagious** in RimWorld. Disease does not ever spread between colonists, though disease events often simulate "outbreaks" by inflicting multiple colonists at the same time. 

The drug [penoxycyline](/wiki/Penoxycyline "Penoxycyline") protects against [Malaria](/wiki/Malaria "Malaria"), [Plague](/wiki/Plague "Plague"), and [Sleeping sickness](/wiki/Sleeping_sickness "Sleeping sickness"). The disease event still happens, but colonists will not be affected. 

## Summary[[edit](/index.php?title=Disease&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Summary")]

Humans and animals can get diseases. Most diseases uses the _severity_ mechanic. As a disease progresses, its symptoms progressively get worse. If a fatal disease's severity reaches 1.0 (100%), then [death](/wiki/Death "Death") occurs. Most diseases go from minor -> major -> extreme. How fast a disease progresses depends on the disease itself.

## Treatment[[edit](/index.php?title=Disease&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Treatment")]

Most diseases can be tended to, just like an [injury](/wiki/Injury "Injury") or [ailment](/wiki/Ailment "Ailment"), by a [Doctor](/wiki/Doctoring "Doctoring") pawn and when the afflicted pawn is assigned to [Patient](/wiki/Work "Work") themselves. Tend Quality is determined by a variety of factors, including the doctor's [skill](/wiki/Skill "Skill") and the quality of [Medicine](/wiki/Medicine "Medicine") used. Generally, higher quality tends reduce the total time until recovery, though mechanics vary. How often a disease can be tended depends on the disease itself. 

There are 3 types of diseases to tend to: 

  * The disease will progress over time, and treatment slows down progression. Immunity is gained over time, dependent on [Immunity Gain Speed](/wiki/Immunity_Gain_Speed "Immunity Gain Speed") and independent of tend quality. Once immune, the disease will slowly fade and the colonist will stop being a patient. This applies to most fatal diseases, including the [flu](/wiki/Flu "Flu"), [infection](/wiki/Infection "Infection"), and [plague](/wiki/Plague "Plague").
  * The disease does not progress. Treatment must reach a _total tend quality_ (300%) before the disease is cured. For example, 4 tends at 80% quality would equal 320% total tend quality. Applies to [gut worms](/wiki/Gut_worms "Gut worms") and [muscle parasites](/wiki/Muscle_parasites "Muscle parasites").
  * The disease progresses over time, and treatment slows and even prevents progression. The disease fades only after a certain time. Applies to [lung rot](/wiki/Lung_rot "Lung rot"), [fibrous mechanites](/wiki/Fibrous_mechanites "Fibrous mechanites"), [sensory mechanites](/wiki/Sensory_mechanites "Sensory mechanites"), and [blood rot](/wiki/Blood_rot "Blood rot").[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC")



Certain diseases have specialized cures. For example, [infection](/wiki/Infection "Infection") in a non-vital part can be cured by amputating or [replacing](/wiki/Artificial_body_part "Artificial body part") a limb, [blood rot](/wiki/Blood_rot "Blood rot") can be instantly cured with [glitterworld medicine](/wiki/Glitterworld_medicine "Glitterworld medicine"), and [gut worms](/wiki/Gut_worms "Gut worms") can be treated by replacing the stomach. In addition, a [healer mech serum](/wiki/Healer_mech_serum "Healer mech serum") immediately cures any disease. 

### Immunity gain[[edit](/index.php?title=Disease&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Immunity gain")]

Main article: [Immunity Gain Speed](/wiki/Immunity_Gain_Speed "Immunity Gain Speed")

Some diseases are a race between the increasing severity of the disease and the increasing immunity to it. This immunity gain is controlled by several factors: 

  * Base immunity gain speed for the particular disease.
  * InfectionLuck, an uncontrollable generated value that adds randomness to the diseases progression. Each disease has a variance in Immunity gain speed of 20% up or down.
  * The [Immunity Gain Speed](/wiki/Immunity_Gain_Speed "Immunity Gain Speed") stat of the patient



Immunity Gain Speed is affected by the following: 

  * **[Food](/wiki/Food "Food")** : Pawns above 12.5% [Saturation](/wiki/Saturation "Saturation") do not have any penalties to immunity.
  * **[Rest](/wiki/Rest "Rest")** : Actively resting gives a bonus to speed. Resting in any [bedroll](/wiki/Bedroll "Bedroll") or [bed](/wiki/Bed "Bed") increases the stat, a [hospital bed](/wiki/Hospital_bed "Hospital bed") increases it further, and a hospital bed with an attached [vitals monitor](/wiki/Vitals_monitor "Vitals monitor") increases it the most.
  * **Age** : As a pawn gets closer to and past their species' life expectancy, immunity gain speed decreases. 
    * For baseline humans, immunity gain speed is gradually lost after age 54. [Genes](/wiki/Genes "Genes")[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC") like [cell instability](/wiki/Mild_cell_instability "Mild cell instability") can affect the pawns lifespan and, in turn, affect this progression.[Verify]
  * **[Blood Filtration](/wiki/Blood_Filtration "Blood Filtration")** : Mostly increased via [luciferium](/wiki/Luciferium "Luciferium"). Reduced with [kidney](/wiki/Kidney "Kidney") or [liver](/wiki/Liver "Liver") damage, slightly improved with a [detoxifier kidney](/wiki/Detoxifier_kidney "Detoxifier kidney")[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC").
  * **Direct boosts** : The [Super-immune](/wiki/Super-immune "Super-immune") trait, [Immunoenhancer](/wiki/Immunoenhancer "Immunoenhancer")[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC"), and [genes](/wiki/Gene "Gene")[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC") can increase immunity speed.



Note that these only apply to diseases with immunity mechanics. For example, you don't need to give a pawn with [gut worms](/wiki/Gut_worms "Gut worms") bedrest.
