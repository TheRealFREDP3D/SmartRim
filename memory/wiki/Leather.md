**Leathers** are a category of [material](/wiki/Material "Material") obtained when a [cook](/wiki/Skills#Cooking "Skills") butchers an [animal](/wiki/Animal "Animal") at a [butcher table](/wiki/Butcher_table "Butcher table") or [spot](/wiki/Butcher_spot "Butcher spot"). The type of leather is determined by the species butchered, with some leather types being produced by several species. See the individual pages of each leather for further details. The amount obtained depends on the [Leather Amount](/wiki/Leather_Amount "Leather Amount") of the butchered animal and the [Butchery Efficiency](/wiki/Butchery_Efficiency "Butchery Efficiency") of the butcher. Some animals, including [dryads](/wiki/Dryads "Dryads"), [insectoids](/wiki/Insectoids "Insectoids"), [chickens](/wiki/Chicken "Chicken"), and [ducks](/wiki/Duck "Duck"), do not yield leather when butchered. 

Note that [Patchleather](/wiki/Patchleather "Patchleather") is unique in that while it is classed as a leather for [stuff](/wiki/Stuff "Stuff") selection, it is obtained by combining other leathers rather than directly from an animal. 

## Comparison table[[edit](/index.php?title=Leathers&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Comparison table")]

Below is a list of material effects for leathers. When a [stuffable](/wiki/Stuff "Stuff") item is created from a leather, each of its base stats is multiplied by a factor for that stat belonging to the material used. Take "Armor - Sharp" for example. For this stat, [Plainleather](/wiki/Plainleather "Plainleather") has a _factor_ of 0.81 and a [duster](/wiki/Duster "Duster") has base stat of 0.3. Thus, the final "Armor - Sharp" of a Plainleather Duster before [quality](/wiki/Quality "Quality") multipliers is: 0.81 * 0.3 or 24.3% 

This process is then repeated for each stat. The material's HP factor is applied to the items base HP, flammability factor to flammability etc. Insulation is not expressed as a percentage and is thus slightly different. For an example of how to calculate the insulation for a given piece of apparel, see [Apparel](/wiki/Apparel#Insulation "Apparel")

Market value is the notable exception to this direct multiplicative relationship, but the value of a material does have a direct effect on that of the final product. See [Market Value](/wiki/Market_Value "Market Value") for details.

_Tanned, dried, scraped mink skin. Soft, sleek, and subtly luxurious, mink is used in premium winter clothing and high-end furnishings, meaning it fetches a high price._

[![Pantherafur b.png](/images/b/bb/Pantherafur_b.png)](/wiki/Panthera_fur "Panthera fur")

**[Panthera fur](/wiki/Panthera_fur "Panthera fur")**

_A soft furry pelt harvested from a big cat. It is difficult to pierce and insulates well against heat._

**Panthera fur** is a type of [leather](/wiki/Leather "Leather") produced when a [cook](/wiki/Skills#Cooking "Skills") butchers a big cat such as a [cougar](/wiki/Cougar "Cougar"), [lynx](/wiki/Lynx "Lynx"), or [panther](/wiki/Panther "Panther") at a [butcher table](/wiki/Butcher_table "Butcher table").

[![Patchleather b.png](/images/e/e7/Patchleather_b.png)](/wiki/Patchleather "Patchleather")

**[Patchleather](/wiki/Patchleather "Patchleather")**

_A weak textile created by cutting up and sewing together various types of leathers. Regardless of what kind of leather is used to create it, patchleather's irregular seams make it less tough and less insulating than any intact leather._

**Patchleather** is a type of [leather](/wiki/Leather "Leather") produced when a [tailor](/wiki/Skills#Crafting "Skills") combines different types of leather at a [tailor bench](/wiki/Electric_tailor_bench "Electric tailor bench"). Useful for combining unusably small amounts of different leathers into a single type to get a large enough quantity for use.

[![Pigskin b.png](/images/9/9f/Pigskin_b.png)](/wiki/Pigskin "Pigskin")

**[Pigskin](/wiki/Pigskin "Pigskin")**

_Tanned, dried, scraped pig skin. While a decent leather, it neither protects nor insulates particularly well._

**Pigskin** is a type of [leather](/wiki/Leather "Leather") produced when a [cook](/wiki/Skills#Cooking "Skills") butchers a [pig](/wiki/Pig "Pig") or a [wild boar](/wiki/Wild_boar "Wild boar") at a [butcher table](/wiki/Butcher_table "Butcher table").

[![Plainleather b.png](/images/6/67/Plainleather_b.png)](/wiki/Plainleather "Plainleather")

**[Plainleather](/wiki/Plainleather "Plainleather")**

_Tanned, dried, scraped skin. A good traditional material for making clothes and bags._

**Plainleather** is a type of [leather](/wiki/Leather "Leather") produced when a [cook](/wiki/Skills#Cooking "Skills") butchers one of a variety of medium-sized [animals](/wiki/Animals "Animals") at a [butcher table](/wiki/Butcher_table "Butcher table").

[![Rhinocerosleather b.png](/images/7/7a/Rhinocerosleather_b.png)](/wiki/Rhinoceros_leather "Rhinoceros leather")

**[Rhinoceros leather](/wiki/Rhinoceros_leather "Rhinoceros leather")**

_Tanned, dried, scraped rhinoceros skin. It is exceptionally durable in combat, but provides little protection from weather._

**Rhinoceros leather** is a type of [leather](/wiki/Leather "Leather") produced when a [cook](/wiki/Skills#Cooking "Skills") butchers a [rhinoceros](/wiki/Rhinoceros "Rhinoceros") at a [butcher table](/wiki/Butcher_table "Butcher table").

[![Sealskin b.png](/images/3/35/Sealskin_b.png)](/wiki/Sealskin "Sealskin")

**[Sealskin](/wiki/Sealskin "Sealskin")[![Content added by the Odyssey DLC](/images/thumb/7/7b/OdysseyIcon.png/16px-OdysseyIcon.png)](/wiki/Odyssey_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Odyssey DLC")**

_The tanned skin of various pinnipeds, such as seals, sea lions, and walruses. It's not particularly tough but it is extremely well insulated. Sealskin is luxuriously soft to the touch, fetching a high price._

**Sealskin** is a type of [leather](/wiki/Leather "Leather") produced when a [cook](/wiki/Skills#Cooking "Skills") butchers a pinniped at a [butcher table](/wiki/Butcher_table "Butcher table").

[![Thrumbofur b.png](/images/b/bc/Thrumbofur_b.png)](/wiki/Thrumbofur "Thrumbofur")

**[Thrumbofur](/wiki/Thrumbofur "Thrumbofur")**

_Furry pelt of a thrumbo. Luxurious and extraordinarily durable. Used for the highest-quality crafts for its insulation and strength._

**Thrumbofur** is a type of [leather](/wiki/Leather "Leather") produced when a [cook](/wiki/Skills#Cooking "Skills") butchers a [thrumbo](/wiki/Thrumbo "Thrumbo"). It is one of the best clothing materials in the game; thrumbofur apparel surpasses all other leathers in terms of protection, and it is one of the best insulators. High quality items can even match flak vests and helmets in terms of armor value.

[![Thrumbomane b.png](/images/0/00/Thrumbomane_b.png)](/wiki/Thrumbomane "Thrumbomane")

**[Thrumbomane](/wiki/Thrumbomane "Thrumbomane")[![Content added by the Odyssey DLC](/images/thumb/7/7b/OdysseyIcon.png/16px-OdysseyIcon.png)](/wiki/Odyssey_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Odyssey DLC")**

_The furry pelt of an alpha thrumbo. It is extraordinarily durable and luxurious - one can almost feel a psychic echo of the animal's ancient story through the gossamer fur. It is used for the highest-quality crafts for its insulation and strength._

[![Wolfskin b.png](/images/f/fe/Wolfskin_b.png)](/wiki/Wolfskin "Wolfskin")

**[Wolfskin](/wiki/Wolfskin "Wolfskin")**

_A soft furry pelt harvested from a wolf-like creature. It is difficult to pierce and insulates well against cold._

**Wolfskin** is a type of [leather](/wiki/Leather "Leather") produced when a [cook](/wiki/Skills#Cooking "Skills") butchers a [timber wolf](/wiki/Timber_wolf "Timber wolf"), [arctic wolf](/wiki/Arctic_wolf "Arctic wolf"), or [warg](/wiki/Warg "Warg") at a [butcher table](/wiki/Butcher_table "Butcher table").
