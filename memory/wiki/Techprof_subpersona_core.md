 Techprof subpersona core

A small AI core housing a low-grade subpersona specialized in teaching technology. When used, the AI will teach you the technology you're currently researching, instantly and for free. Can only be used once.

Acquisition[edit]

Techprof subpersona cores cannot be crafted nor bought from traders. Instead they can be acquired as a reward for performing quests. Note that quest rewards are selected with a weighted random generator, so there is no way to guarantee access to a core in a given play-through.

They can also rarely be found in ancient shrines.

Summary[edit]

Techprof subpersona cores can be used to instantly complete the research project that is currently selected for research by selecting a colonist, right-clicking the device, and selecting 'Use techprof subpersona core'.

Note that this only works on research projects that could otherwise research. Research without either the necessary prerequisites or techprints cannot be completed instantly using the core.

If no valid research project is selected, the core cannot be used. 

It has no effect on Anomaly research projects and attempting to use one will only complete the active regular research project, or if none is selected, prevent use of the core entirely.

Analysis[edit]

Techprof subpersona cores are rare, even compared to other non-craftables. A given colony is fairly likely to simply never see one. As such they should not be relied upon when planning progression. Additionally, short of doing many quests and taking them when offered, there is little in the way of strategy for acquiring them.

They are best used to quickly complete either a long research such as the spaceship research or an urgently needed one, such as flak armor.

How valuable they are in comparison to other reward will vary on a lot of factors that make it difficult to generalize for all colonies. A neanderthal New Tribe on a high difficulty with Research: Slow precept will value a core much more than genie New Arrivals on a low difficulty with Research: Very fast precept and a dozen Research specialists. Consideration should be given to what the research will give you and how long it will take you to unlock it without the core.

To get best use out of it, make sure that you don't use it on a research you've already spent research in. Consider swapping away from your current project just for the use of the core, and then swap back to complete traditional research.
