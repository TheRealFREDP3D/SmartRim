 The icon used for bionic-tier artificial body parts.

For all types of body part replacement, see artificial body parts.
Bionics may refer to one of several different body part replacements unlocked by the Bionic replacement research project. These are:

- Bionic arm

- Bionic eye

- Bionic ear

- Bionic heart

- Bionic leg

- Bionic spine

- Bionic stomach

- Bionic jaw

- Bionic tongue

They are equal or superior to normal body parts, and provide a bridge between the inferior-to-natural prosthetics and the superior but uncraftable archotech parts. Not all body parts are represented in every tier.

Bionics is also used informally to refer to artificial body parts generally, but is not used as such in-game.
