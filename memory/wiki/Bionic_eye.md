An advanced artificial eye. With its biogel nerve-link, internal signal pre-processor, and wide-spectrum sensors, it is better than a biological eye in almost every way. A lattice-dust healing system allows it to recover from damage.

## Acquisition[[edit](/index.php?title=Bionic_eye&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Acquisition")]

Bionic eyes can be crafted at a [fabrication bench](/wiki/Fabrication_bench "Fabrication bench") once the [Bionic replacements](/wiki/Research#Bionic_replacements "Research") research project has been completed. Each requires [![Plasteel](/images/thumb/8/8c/Plasteel_c.png/16px-Plasteel_c.png)](/wiki/Plasteel "Plasteel") 15 [Plasteel](/wiki/Plasteel "Plasteel"), [![Advanced component](/images/thumb/d/d6/Advanced_component.png/16px-Advanced_component.png)](/wiki/Advanced_component "Advanced component") 4 [Advanced components](/wiki/Advanced_component "Advanced component"), 26,000 [ticks](/wiki/Time "Time") (7.22 mins) of work modified by the [general labor speed](/wiki/General_Labor_Speed "General Labor Speed") of the crafter, and a [crafting](/wiki/Crafting "Crafting") skill of 8.   
Bionic eyes can be bought from the following traders: [Orbital exotic goods trader](/wiki/Trader/Orbital_Exotic_Goods_Trader "Trader/Orbital Exotic Goods Trader"), [Outlander base trader](/wiki/Trader/Base_Outlander_Standard "Trader/Base Outlander Standard"), [Outlander exotic goods trader](/wiki/Trader/Caravan_Outlander_Exotic "Trader/Caravan Outlander Exotic"), [Slave ship](/wiki/Trader/Orbital_Pirate_Merchant "Trader/Orbital Pirate Merchant"). 

They can also be found in [ancient shrines](/wiki/Ancient_shrine "Ancient shrine"), offered as a [quest](/wiki/Quest "Quest") reward, or [bought](/wiki/Trade "Trade") from exotic traders, [Empire](/wiki/Empire "Empire") traders, and at [Outlander](/wiki/Outlander "Outlander") [faction bases](/wiki/Faction_base "Faction base"). They may also be found occasionally inside of [flesh sacks](/wiki/Flesh_sack "Flesh sack") ,[![Content added by the Anomaly DLC](/images/thumb/3/32/AnomalyIcon.png/16px-AnomalyIcon.png)](/wiki/Anomaly_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Anomaly DLC") with a ~ 1.95% chance to drop from them. 

  


## Summary[[edit](/index.php?title=Bionic_eye&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Summary")]

Bionic eyes replace natural eyes. Bionic eyes have a 125% efficiency, which results in a +25% improvement to [Sight](/wiki/Sight "Sight") if two are installed. 

However, the game places 75% weighting on the more capable eye for the purposes of sight. Installing the first bionic eye on a healthy pawn would increase sight by +18.75%, while the second would provide a +6.25% increase. 

### Installation[[edit](/index.php?title=Bionic_eye&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Installation")]

Installing the part requires 2,500 [ticks](/wiki/Time "Time") (41.67 secs) of work, 2x medicine of [herbal](/wiki/Herbal_medicine "Herbal medicine") quality or better, and a [Medical skill](/wiki/Medical "Medical") of 5. 

Removing the part requires 2,000 [ticks](/wiki/Time "Time") (33.33 secs) of work, 1x medicine of [herbal](/wiki/Herbal_medicine "Herbal medicine") quality or better, and has no [Medical skill](/wiki/Medical "Medical") requirements. 

If the operation fails, the part will be destroyed. 

## Analysis[[edit](/index.php?title=Bionic_eye&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Analysis")]

Bionic eyes can repair damaged sight, and augment normal sight for healthy pawns. There are no prosthetic eyes - bionic eyes are the first replacement for a damaged natural eye, and eyes are easily scarred. Before Bionics are researched, getting an eye from a [trader](/wiki/Trader "Trader") is your best bet, unless you chance upon a [healer mech serum](/wiki/Healer_mech_serum "Healer mech serum") or rush for a [biosculpter pod](/wiki/Biosculpter_pod "Biosculpter pod")[![Content added by the Ideology DLC](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/16px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Content added by the Ideology DLC"). 

_Damaged_ sight reduces [general labor speed](/wiki/General_labor_speed "General labor speed") and a wide variety of other stats, slowing virtually any task a pawn can do. Improving Sight past 100% boosts: [Shooting Accuracy](/wiki/Shooting_Accuracy "Shooting Accuracy") & [Melee Hit Chance](/wiki/Melee_Hit_Chance "Melee Hit Chance"), [Melee Dodge Chance](/wiki/Melee_Dodge_Chance "Melee Dodge Chance"), [Medical Tend Quality](/wiki/Medical_Tend_Quality "Medical Tend Quality"), and [Cooking Speed](/wiki/Cooking_Speed "Cooking Speed"). Sight up to 110% also provides a slight boost to [Research Speed](/wiki/Research_Speed "Research Speed") and [Hacking Speed](/wiki/Hacking_Speed "Hacking Speed"). A bionic eye is roughly equal to +2 levels of Shooting or Melee, with a bonus for melee dodge chance. 

Prioritize replacing damaged eyes first, then getting a single bionic eye on healthy colonists, then potentially getting a second eye per colonist. [Archotech eyes](/wiki/Archotech_eye "Archotech eye") are a trader/quest -only upgrade, and should be installed in the same order. 

Compared to other bionic parts, the penalty for lost sight is so widespread that bionic eyes should be prioritized on anyone (reasonably important) with damaged/missing eyes. On a healthy pawn, eyes are better for combat, while arms and legs are better for work speed. Legs may also be better in combat if you rely on [kiting](/wiki/Kiting "Kiting") tactics, allowing you to reliably outrun many types of enemies. Which bionic should be installed first depends on playstyle, personal preference, and [difficulty](/wiki/Difficulty "Difficulty"). 

With the [blindsight](/wiki/Blindsight "Blindsight") meme, bionic eyes can be used to restore a pawn's sight. This allows the pawn to repeat the blinding ritual. Each blinding ritual has a chance to give them a psylink level. Only one bionic eye is required to enable a blinding ritual. At 100% ritual quality, there is a 50% chance to gain a psylink level. This means on average 2 bionic eyes will be spent per psylink level.

As with all bionics, it will give a mood buff to anyone with the [body modder](/wiki/Body_modder "Body modder") trait and a heavy penalty to any colonist who is a [body purist](/wiki/Body_purist "Body purist"). They are a strong option for body modders - not conflicting with any other. Also, having implants of any kind installed will annoy fellow colonists who have the Body Purist trait, stressing their relationship.
