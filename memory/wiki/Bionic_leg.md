A **bionic leg** is an [artificial body part](/wiki/Artificial_body_part "Artificial body part") that replaces a human's leg. They not only replace a damaged or destroyed leg, but improve over the natural limb. They are superior to all other legs save the [archotech leg](/wiki/Archotech_leg "Archotech leg").

## Acquisition[[edit](/index.php?title=Bionic_leg&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Acquisition")]

Bionic legs can be crafted at a [fabrication bench](/wiki/Fabrication_bench "Fabrication bench") once the [Bionic replacements](/wiki/Research#Bionic_replacements "Research") research project has been completed. Each requires [![Plasteel](/images/thumb/8/8c/Plasteel_c.png/16px-Plasteel_c.png)](/wiki/Plasteel "Plasteel") 15 [Plasteel](/wiki/Plasteel "Plasteel"), [![Advanced component](/images/thumb/d/d6/Advanced_component.png/16px-Advanced_component.png)](/wiki/Advanced_component "Advanced component") 4 [Advanced components](/wiki/Advanced_component "Advanced component"), 26,000 [ticks](/wiki/Time "Time") (7.22 mins) of work modified by the [general labor speed](/wiki/General_Labor_Speed "General Labor Speed") of the crafter, and a [crafting](/wiki/Crafting "Crafting") skill of 8.   
Bionic legs can be bought from the following traders: [Orbital exotic goods trader](/wiki/Trader/Orbital_Exotic_Goods_Trader "Trader/Orbital Exotic Goods Trader"), [Outlander base trader](/wiki/Trader/Base_Outlander_Standard "Trader/Base Outlander Standard"), [Outlander exotic goods trader](/wiki/Trader/Caravan_Outlander_Exotic "Trader/Caravan Outlander Exotic"), [Slave ship](/wiki/Trader/Orbital_Pirate_Merchant "Trader/Orbital Pirate Merchant"). 

They can also be found in [ancient shrines](/wiki/Ancient_shrine "Ancient shrine"), offered as a [quest](/wiki/Quest "Quest") reward, or [bought](/wiki/Trade "Trade") from exotic traders, [Empire](/wiki/Empire "Empire") traders, and at [Outlander](/wiki/Outlander "Outlander") [faction bases](/wiki/Faction_base "Faction base"). They may also be found occasionally inside of [flesh sacks](/wiki/Flesh_sack "Flesh sack") ,[![Content added by the Anomaly DLC](/images/thumb/3/32/AnomalyIcon.png/16px-AnomalyIcon.png)](/wiki/Anomaly_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Anomaly DLC") with a ~ 1.95% chance to drop from them. 

## Summary[[edit](/index.php?title=Bionic_leg&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Summary")]

Bionic legs replace the user's [entire organic leg](/wiki/Table_of_Human_Body_Parts "Table of Human Body Parts"). They are universal and can be installed on either the left or right side. The bionic leg has a part efficiency of 125%. This not only fully replaces the functionality of a normal leg - when coupled with the leg body part having a [Moving](/wiki/Moving "Moving") importance of 50%, it results in an increase in moving of 12.5% over natural for each bionic leg installed. 

Moving, in turn, affects the following [stats](/wiki/Stats "Stats"): [Hunting Stealth](/wiki/Hunting_Stealth "Hunting Stealth"), [Melee Dodge Chance](/wiki/Melee_Dodge_Chance "Melee Dodge Chance"), [Move Speed](/wiki/Move_Speed "Move Speed"). This results in a directly proportional change to a pawn's [move speed](/wiki/Move_speed "Move speed"), and an improvement in [melee dodge chance](/wiki/Melee_dodge_chance "Melee dodge chance") equivalent to an additional +2.25 levels of [Melee](/wiki/Melee "Melee") skill per leg replaced. 

As an artificial part, bionic legs will give a mood buff to anyone with the [Body modder](/wiki/Body_modder "Body modder") trait, and a mood penalty to anyone with the [Body purist](/wiki/Body_purist "Body purist") trait. 

### Installation[[edit](/index.php?title=Bionic_leg&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Installation")]

Installing the part requires 2,500 [ticks](/wiki/Time "Time") (41.67 secs) of work, 2x medicine of [herbal](/wiki/Herbal_medicine "Herbal medicine") quality or better, and a [Medical skill](/wiki/Skills#Medical "Skills") of 5. 

Removing the part requires 2,000 [ticks](/wiki/Time "Time") (33.33 secs) of work, 1x medicine of [herbal](/wiki/Herbal_medicine "Herbal medicine") quality or better, and has no [Medical skill](/wiki/Skills#Medical "Skills") requirements. The removal surgery yields one bionic leg. 

If the operation fails, the part will be destroyed. 

## Analysis[[edit](/index.php?title=Bionic_leg&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Analysis")]

[![StumpChoppedA \(cropped\).png](/images/thumb/6/6a/StumpChoppedA_%28cropped%29.png/40px-StumpChoppedA_%28cropped%29.png)](/wiki/Category:Stubs "Category:Stubs") | **This section is a[stub](/wiki/Category:Stubs "Category:Stubs").** You can help RimWorld Wiki by **[expanding it](https://rimworldwiki.com/index.php?title=Bionic_leg&action=edit). For all pawns, but especially pawns with jobs that require a lot of travel, e.g. prioritize growers and haulers who often travel across the map, over researchers who sit in one place. For people who dislike using killboxes, they are useful for [kiting](/wiki/Kiting "Kiting"). Additionally, the increase in movement leads to an increase in [Melee dodge chance](/wiki/Melee_dodge_chance "Melee dodge chance"), making it a useful part for melee pawns. 

Unlike organic parts, bionic legs don't contain subparts - all damage is taken by the main, 30 HP part, instead of spread throughout bones and toes. This means that a bionic leg is easier to destroy. However, the bionic part never feels [pain](/wiki/Pain "Pain"), never [bleeds](/wiki/Blood_loss "Blood loss"), and can never lose toes in the first place. Additionally, the possibility of part destruction may be mitigated by heavy armor.
