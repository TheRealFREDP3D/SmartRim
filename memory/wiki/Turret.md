# Turret

From RimWorld Wiki

Jump to navigation Jump to search

**Turrets** are automatic defenses that fire at enemies within range. They require [power](/wiki/Power "Power") to operate, and after firing require reloading or barrel changes that consume resources; this cost is dependent on difficulty. 

There are six constructable varieties: 

  * [Mini-turret](/wiki/Mini-turret "Mini-turret") \- weak and short ranged but small and cheap. Uses the [mini-turret gun](/wiki/Mini-turret_gun "Mini-turret gun"). Also found defending [faction bases](/wiki/Faction_base "Faction base").
  * [Autocannon turret](/wiki/Autocannon_turret "Autocannon turret") \- powerful and fast firing with a medium range and cost. Uses the [autocannon](/wiki/Autocannon "Autocannon").
  * [Uranium slug turret](/wiki/Uranium_slug_turret "Uranium slug turret") \- slow firing and costly but with very high damage, range and accuracy. Uses the [uranium slug cannon](/wiki/Uranium_slug_cannon "Uranium slug cannon").
  * [Foam turret](/wiki/Foam_turret "Foam turret") \- an unmanned but pawn-activated turret that automatically targets and fires [firefoam](/wiki/Firefoam "Firefoam") at any fires in range.
  * [Rocketswarm launcher](/wiki/Rocketswarm_launcher "Rocketswarm launcher") \- an unmanned but pawn-activated turret that fires a barrage of 12 rockets at a player designated point in range.
  * [Mortar](/wiki/Mortar "Mortar") \- a manned turret-like structure that requires no power and fires explosive [mortar shells](/wiki/Mortar_shell "Mortar shell") that must be crafted, bought, or scavenged. Powerful with a map wide range that ignores [line of sight](/wiki/Line_of_sight "Line of sight"), but inaccurate and slow.
  * [Turret pack](/wiki/Turret_pack "Turret pack")[![Content added by the Anomaly DLC](/images/thumb/3/32/AnomalyIcon.png/16px-AnomalyIcon.png)](/wiki/Anomaly_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Anomaly DLC") \- a [utility](/wiki/Utility "Utility") slot item that be deployed into a small [tactical turret](/wiki/Tactical_turret "Tactical turret") similar to a mini-turret, but with a limited, self-contained power supply.



Additionally, there are turrets unique to other factions, including: 

  * [Auto inferno turret](/wiki/Auto_inferno_turret "Auto inferno turret") [![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC") \- defends [Mechanoid clusters](/wiki/Mechanoid_cluster "Mechanoid cluster"). Always hostile, never needs rearming. Uses a turret specific variant of the [inferno cannon](/wiki/Inferno_cannon_\(Turret\) "Inferno cannon \(Turret\)").
  * [Auto charge turret ](/wiki/Auto_charge_turret "Auto charge turret") [![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC") \- defends [Mechanoid clusters](/wiki/Mechanoid_cluster "Mechanoid cluster"). Always hostile, never needs rearming. Uses the [light charge blaster](/wiki/Light_charge_blaster "Light charge blaster").
  * [Mini-slugger turret](/wiki/Mini-slugger_turret "Mini-slugger turret") [![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC") \- defends [Mechanoid clusters](/wiki/Mechanoid_cluster "Mechanoid cluster"). Always hostile, never needs rearming. Uses the [mini-slugger](/wiki/Mini-slugger "Mini-slugger").
  * [Auto mortar](/wiki/Auto_mortar "Auto mortar") [![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC") \- defends [Mechanoid clusters](/wiki/Mechanoid_cluster "Mechanoid cluster"). Always hostile, never needs rearming. Fires a projectile like the mortar but is otherwise more similar to the other mechanoid turrets.
  * [Fleshmass spitter](/wiki/Fleshmass_spitter "Fleshmass spitter")[![Content added by the Anomaly DLC](/images/thumb/3/32/AnomalyIcon.png/16px-AnomalyIcon.png)](/wiki/Anomaly_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Anomaly DLC") \- defends [fleshmass hearts](/wiki/Fleshmass_heart "Fleshmass heart").
  * [Ancient defender turret](/wiki/Ancient_defender_turret "Ancient defender turret")[![Content added by the Odyssey DLC](/images/thumb/7/7b/OdysseyIcon.png/16px-OdysseyIcon.png)](/wiki/Odyssey_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Odyssey DLC") \- defends various [landmarks](/wiki/Landmarks "Landmarks").
