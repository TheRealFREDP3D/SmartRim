# Malaria

From RimWorld Wiki

Jump to navigation Jump to search

Malaria is a potentially [fatal](/wiki/Death "Death") [disease](/wiki/Disease "Disease") that can be contracted by colonists in any biome, but is frequent in the [tropical rainforests](/wiki/Tropical_rainforest "Tropical rainforest") and [swamps](/wiki/Tropical_swamp "Tropical swamp"). 

## Overview[[edit](/index.php?title=Malaria&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Overview")]

General symptoms include impaired consciousness, blood filtration - and eventually pain, vomiting, and impaired manipulation. Advanced symptoms include frequent vomiting along with a loss of consciousness, and death. As Malaria affects blood filtration, the pawn's immune system becomes less effective as it progresses. 

If left untreated, Malaria can kill the affected person in 2.701 days from discovery. It takes around 3.106 days to develop immunity to Malaria; assuming the affected colonist is rested in an ordinary bed for the whole time, has nothing else affecting blood filtration, is well-fed, and under the age of 40. These figures are calculated assuming 91.35% blood filtration (the 'average' blood filtration throughout all stages of the disease); due to Malaria's nature, there's no reliable way to calculate the time to develop immunity. 

### Prevention[[edit](/index.php?title=Malaria&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Prevention")]

[Penoxycyline](/wiki/Penoxycyline "Penoxycyline") prevents colonists from falling sick to the Malaria, but does nothing to stop an infection already in progress. 

The [perfect immunity](/wiki/Perfect_immunity "Perfect immunity") gene[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC") will prevent the acquisition of this disease but will not cure pre-existing conditions present at the time of implantation. 

## Stages[[edit](/index.php?title=Malaria&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Stages")]

#### Minor[[edit](/index.php?title=Malaria&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Minor")]

  * −10% [Blood Filtration](/wiki/Blood_Filtration "Blood Filtration")
  * −5% [Consciousness](/wiki/Consciousness "Consciousness")



#### Major[[edit](/index.php?title=Malaria&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Major")]

  * Vomiting every 1.5 days (36 hours) on average.
  * +30% [Pain](/wiki/Pain "Pain")
  * −20% [Blood Filtration](/wiki/Blood_Filtration "Blood Filtration")
  * −12% [Consciousness](/wiki/Consciousness "Consciousness")
  * −8% [Manipulation](/wiki/Manipulation "Manipulation")



#### Extreme[[edit](/index.php?title=Malaria&action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: Extreme")]

  * Vomiting every 0.75 days (18 hours) on average.
  * Loss of [Consciousness](/wiki/Consciousness "Consciousness")[Actual effect?]
  * +30% [Pain](/wiki/Pain "Pain")
  * −22% [Blood Filtration](/wiki/Blood_Filtration "Blood Filtration")
  * −10% [Manipulation](/wiki/Manipulation "Manipulation")
  * [Death](/wiki/Death "Death") at 1 severity.



## Progression[[edit](/index.php?title=Malaria&action=edit&section=7 "Edit section: Progression")]

### Stages[[edit](/index.php?title=Malaria&action=edit&section=8 "Edit section: Stages")]

This disease is detected immediately. Severity goes up over time, and is slowed by treatment until immunity is reached. 

  * Minor - Severity: 0.0 - 0.77
  * Major - Severity: 0.78 - 0.9
  * Extreme - Severity: 0.91 - 0.99
  * Death - Severity: 1



### Humans[[edit](/index.php?title=Malaria&action=edit&section=9 "Edit section: Humans")]

  * When not immune, severity increases by 0.3702 per day.
  * When immune, severity decreases by 0.7297 per day.
  * Immunity increases by a base rate of 0.3145 per day when sick.
  * Treatment slows progression by a maximum of 0.232 per day.



## Treatment[[edit](/index.php?title=Malaria&action=edit&section=10 "Edit section: Treatment")]

See also: [Immunity Gain Speed](/wiki/Immunity_Gain_Speed "Immunity Gain Speed")

It's especially important that affected colonists are rested while they fight off Malaria due to its effect on Blood Filtration, and that you have plenty of medicine and a skilled doctor in hand. As with any other disease, you'll also want to make sure that the patient is well fed, rests as much as possible, and that they're promptly treated. 

Treatment is administered once every 12 hours. On a pawn that constantly rests, with 100% pre-Malaria [Immunity Gain Speed](/wiki/Immunity_Gain_Speed "Immunity Gain Speed"), you'll need at least 24% average treatment quality in order to sufficiently slow down Malaria's progress to the point where it doesn't kill the patient. With a [hospital bed](/wiki/Hospital_bed "Hospital bed") and [vitals monitor](/wiki/Vitals_monitor "Vitals monitor"), colonists can survive without treatment. However, this is far slower than treatment, even without medicine. 

### Vulnerable[[edit](/index.php?title=Malaria&action=edit&section=11 "Edit section: Vulnerable")]

For those with a base immunity gain rate of 37% or less, it will be very difficult to survive malaria. This includes people well past their [Life Expectancy](/wiki/Immunity_Gain_Speed#Age "Immunity Gain Speed"), and/or people with severe kidney and liver damage. Even with 100% [treatment quality](/wiki/Doctoring#Tend_quality "Doctoring") for all treatments and rest in a [hospital bed](/wiki/Hospital_bed "Hospital bed") with a [vitals monitor](/wiki/Vitals_monitor "Vitals monitor") connected, they will die without extra care. To save the colonist, you can do one or more of the following: 

  * Tend with [glitterworld medicine](/wiki/Glitterworld_medicine "Glitterworld medicine")
  * Administer [healer mech serum](/wiki/Healer_mech_serum "Healer mech serum") \- instantly treats the disease
  * Administer [luciferium](/wiki/Luciferium "Luciferium")
  * Perform an [organ transplant](/wiki/Health#Transplants "Health") for pawns with damage to their [liver](/wiki/Liver "Liver") or [kidneys](/wiki/Kidney "Kidney").
  * Install [detoxifier kidneys](/wiki/Detoxifier_kidney "Detoxifier kidney"). these improve immunity gain speed by 2.5% each. Prioritize installing detoxifier kidneys on already damaged kidneys.
  * Install up to two [immunoenhancers](/wiki/Immunoenhancer "Immunoenhancer") [![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC") for 8% per enhancer installed. Note that these require a kidney to be present for them to be installed into, so a transplant might be required if they are missing. Damaged kidneys are sufficient however.
  * Use the medic cycle of a [Biosculpter pod](/wiki/Biosculpter_pod "Biosculpter pod")[![Content added by the Ideology DLC](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/16px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Content added by the Ideology DLC").
  * Use the [preach health](/wiki/Preach_health "Preach health")[![Content added by the Ideology DLC](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/16px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Content added by the Ideology DLC") ability on the affected person
  * Use the [medical specialist](/wiki/Medical_specialist "Medical specialist")'s[![Content added by the Ideology DLC](/images/thumb/9/94/IdeologyIcon.png/16px-IdeologyIcon.png)](/wiki/Ideology_DLC "Content added by the Ideology DLC") immunity drive
  * Place the patient in a [cryptosleep casket](/wiki/Cryptosleep_casket "Cryptosleep casket") until you have one of the above.



## Version history[[edit](/index.php?title=Malaria&action=edit&section=12 "Edit section: Version history")]

In an earlier version (Alpha 17?) sufficiently good treatment could cause malaria to regress. This combined with the fact that malaria had a hidden stage back then meant that the disease could be "cured" and subsequently reappear several times, until the pawn finally gains immunity.
