An advanced artificial heart. It has synthetic muscle fibers for a realistic heartbeat, plus a high-flow pump for rapid circulation during high stress. It is better than a biological heart in almost every way.

The **bionic heart** is an [artificial body part](/wiki/Artificial_body_part "Artificial body part") that replaces the [heart](/wiki/Heart "Heart"). At 125% part efficiency it improves [blood pumping](/wiki/Blood_pumping "Blood pumping"), granting a small boost to a wide range of stats and tasks.

## Acquisition[[edit](/index.php?title=Bionic_heart&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Acquisition")]

Bionic hearts can be crafted at a [fabrication bench](/wiki/Fabrication_bench "Fabrication bench") once the [Bionic replacements](/wiki/Research#Bionic_replacements "Research") research project has been completed. Each requires [![Plasteel](/images/thumb/8/8c/Plasteel_c.png/16px-Plasteel_c.png)](/wiki/Plasteel "Plasteel") 15 [Plasteel](/wiki/Plasteel "Plasteel"), [![Advanced component](/images/thumb/d/d6/Advanced_component.png/16px-Advanced_component.png)](/wiki/Advanced_component "Advanced component") 4 [Advanced components](/wiki/Advanced_component "Advanced component"), 26,000 [ticks](/wiki/Time "Time") (7.22 mins) of work modified by the [general labor speed](/wiki/General_Labor_Speed "General Labor Speed") of the crafter, and a [crafting](/wiki/Crafting "Crafting") skill of 8.   
Bionic hearts can be bought from the following traders: [Orbital exotic goods trader](/wiki/Trader/Orbital_Exotic_Goods_Trader "Trader/Orbital Exotic Goods Trader"), [Outlander base trader](/wiki/Trader/Base_Outlander_Standard "Trader/Base Outlander Standard"), [Outlander exotic goods trader](/wiki/Trader/Caravan_Outlander_Exotic "Trader/Caravan Outlander Exotic"), [Slave ship](/wiki/Trader/Orbital_Pirate_Merchant "Trader/Orbital Pirate Merchant"). 

They can also be found in [ancient shrines](/wiki/Ancient_shrine "Ancient shrine"), offered as a [quest](/wiki/Quest "Quest") reward, or [bought](/wiki/Trade "Trade") from exotic traders, [Empire](/wiki/Empire "Empire") traders, and at [Outlander](/wiki/Outlander "Outlander") [faction bases](/wiki/Faction_base "Faction base"). They may also be found rarely inside of [flesh sacks](/wiki/Flesh_sack "Flesh sack"),[![Content added by the Anomaly DLC](/images/thumb/3/32/AnomalyIcon.png/16px-AnomalyIcon.png)](/wiki/Anomaly_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Anomaly DLC") with a ~ 1.95% of dropping. 

## Summary[[edit](/index.php?title=Bionic_heart&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Summary")]

The bionic heart replaces an organic heart and has a part efficiency of 125%, which improves [blood pumping](/wiki/Blood_pumping "Blood pumping") by +25%. This increases the [Rest Rate Multiplier](/wiki/Rest_Rate_Multiplier "Rest Rate Multiplier") by about +8%, and [Moving](/wiki/Moving "Moving") by ×105%. The Moving boost is a multiplier, rather than added on, so it further improves other movement increases. For example, 125% Moving, reached with 2 [bionic legs](/wiki/Bionic_leg "Bionic leg"), will be increased to 131% (125% * 1.05) rather than 130% (125% + 5%). Moving, in turn, affects the following [stats](/wiki/Stats "Stats"): [Hunting Stealth](/wiki/Hunting_Stealth "Hunting Stealth"), [Melee Dodge Chance](/wiki/Melee_Dodge_Chance "Melee Dodge Chance"), [Move Speed](/wiki/Move_Speed "Move Speed"). For further details, see those pages. 

Artificial hearts are also immune to [heart attacks](/wiki/Heart_attack "Heart attack") and [artery blockages](/wiki/Artery_blockage "Artery blockage"), preventing future cases and curing existing ones. 

### Installation[[edit](/index.php?title=Bionic_heart&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Installation")]

Installing the part requires 2,500 [ticks](/wiki/Time "Time") (41.67 secs) of work, 2x medicine of [herbal](/wiki/Herbal_medicine "Herbal medicine") quality or better, and a [Medical skill](/wiki/Skills#Medical "Skills") of 5. 

Removing the part requires 2,000 [ticks](/wiki/Time "Time") (33.33 secs) of work, 1x medicine of [herbal](/wiki/Herbal_medicine "Herbal medicine") quality or better, and has no [Medical skill](/wiki/Skills#Medical "Skills") requirements. 

The replaced heart will be harvested without the normal [organ harvest](/wiki/Thoughts#Misc "Thoughts") penalties if it was perfectly healthy during the operation. This organic heart can then be sold or stored for later use. 

If the operation [fails](/wiki/Doctoring#Failure "Doctoring"), the part will be destroyed. Unlike other heart transplants, there is no risk of immediate death from failure. 

## Analysis[[edit](/index.php?title=Bionic_heart&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Analysis")]

Like [bionic stomachs](/wiki/Bionic_stomach "Bionic stomach"), bionic hearts are of less obvious importance than [bionic legs](/wiki/Bionic_leg "Bionic leg"), [arms](/wiki/Bionic_arm "Bionic arm") or [eyes](/wiki/Bionic_eye "Bionic eye") due to its less impactful stat boosts. Even though the bonuses are small, faster sleep and movement help improve the productivity of a colonist. The movement speed in particular can make a difference when fleeing animals or mechanoids, and can be installed after legs. The bionic heart also stops and prevents [heart attacks](/wiki/Heart_attack "Heart attack"), which makes it very useful for [wake-up](/wiki/Wake-up "Wake-up") users or old people, especially if they already have an artery blockage. 

Unlike the [prosthetic heart](/wiki/Prosthetic_heart "Prosthetic heart") or a natural, biological heart, there is not a 25% chance of instant death in the event of failed installation. This makes it significantly safer to install. It also only requires a medical skill of 5 compared to the organic heart's 8. The prosthetic is lower still, requiring only 4. 

Installing a bionic heart on a healthy patient grants a [natural heart](/wiki/Heart "Heart"), worth [![Silver](/images/thumb/5/57/Silver_c.png/16px-Silver_c.png)](/wiki/Silver "Silver") 1200\. This is more than the bionic heart, worth [![Silver](/images/thumb/5/57/Silver_c.png/16px-Silver_c.png)](/wiki/Silver "Silver") 1030\. This means the surgery is theoretically profitable. Along with [Detoxifier lungs](/wiki/Detoxifier_lung "Detoxifier lung"),[![Content added by the Biotech DLC](/images/thumb/7/73/BiotechIcon.png/16px-BiotechIcon.png)](/wiki/Biotech_\(DLC\) "Content added by the Biotech DLC") Bionic hearts are the only artificial part which has a lower market value than their natural counterpart while also being strictly superior to their natural counterpart.
