Venom talon

[![Venom talon](/images/7/7a/Health_item_prosthetic.png)](/wiki/File:Health_item_prosthetic.png "Venom talon")

A retractable finger talon connected to a venom pump in the lower arm. The user can scratch an enemy while squirting venom into the wound. The talon is designed to be installed in the middle finger. It's fast, but poor at penetrating armor.

## Acquisition[[edit](/index.php?title=Venom_talon&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Acquisition")]

Venom talons can be crafted at a [machining table](/wiki/Machining_table "Machining table") once the [Poison synthesis](/wiki/Research#Poison_synthesis "Research") research project has been completed. Note that this research requires a [techprint](/wiki/Techprint "Techprint"). Each requires [![Steel](/images/thumb/9/90/Steel_c.png/16px-Steel_c.png)](/wiki/Steel "Steel") 40 [Steel](/wiki/Steel "Steel"), [![Component](/images/thumb/4/40/Component.png/16px-Component.png)](/wiki/Component "Component") 7 [Components](/wiki/Component "Component"), 15,000 [ticks](/wiki/Time "Time") (4.17 mins) of work modified by the [general labor speed](/wiki/General_Labor_Speed "General Labor Speed") of the crafter, and a [crafting](/wiki/Crafting "Crafting") skill of 5.   
Venom talons can be bought from the following traders: [Empire base trader](/wiki/Trader/Base_Empire_Standard "Trader/Base Empire Standard")[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC"), [Imperial trader caravan](/wiki/Trader/Empire_Caravan_Trader_General "Trader/Empire Caravan Trader General")[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC"), [Orbital imperial trader](/wiki/Trader/Orbital_Empire "Trader/Orbital Empire")[![Content added by the Royalty DLC](/images/thumb/4/41/RoyaltyIcon.png/16px-RoyaltyIcon.png)](/wiki/Royalty_DLC "Content added by the Royalty DLC"). 

They can also be traded from exotic [traders](/wiki/Traders "Traders"), and can be earned as a [quest](/wiki/Quests "Quests") reward.

### Installation[[edit](/index.php?title=Venom_talon&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Installation")]

Installing the part requires 2,500 [ticks](/wiki/Time "Time") (41.67 secs) of work, 2x medicine of [herbal](/wiki/Herbal_medicine "Herbal medicine") quality or better, and a [Medical skill](/wiki/Medical "Medical") of 5. 

Removing the part requires 2,500 [ticks](/wiki/Time "Time") (41.67 secs) of work, 1x medicine of [herbal](/wiki/Herbal_medicine "Herbal medicine") quality or better, and a [Medical skill](/wiki/Medical "Medical") of 5. 

If the operation fails, the part will be destroyed. 

## Analysis[[edit](/index.php?title=Venom_talon&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Analysis")]

The venom talon is a modestly powerful melee implant that can inflict the [toxic buildup](/wiki/Toxic_buildup "Toxic buildup") debuff when used. It is comparable to a [normal quality](/wiki/Quality "Quality") [steel](/wiki/Steel "Steel") [longsword](/wiki/Longsword "Longsword") against unarmored enemies, the [toxic buildup](/wiki/Toxic_buildup "Toxic buildup") it inflicts making up for the lower DPS, however it is significantly weaker against [armored](/wiki/Armor "Armor") foes due to its poor armor penetration. As a dedicated melee weapon it is outclassed slightly by other melee implants like the [hand talon](/wiki/Hand_talon "Hand talon"), and is massively outclassed by a [power claw](/wiki/Power_claw "Power claw"), [plasteel](/wiki/Plasteel "Plasteel") [longsword](/wiki/Longsword "Longsword") or [uranium](/wiki/Uranium "Uranium") [mace](/wiki/Mace "Mace"). It should not be considered as a pawn's primary offensive option. 

It is generally outclassed by similar body part weapons like the [hand talon](/wiki/Hand_talon "Hand talon") in terms of melee capabilities, and it cannot be used alongside two [bionic](/wiki/Bionic_arm "Bionic arm") or [archotech arms](/wiki/Archotech_arm "Archotech arm"). Note that toxic buildup will trigger the enemy [death on downed](/wiki/Death_on_downed "Death on downed") chance as of 1.5, so it cannot be used to capture raiders much better than normal. 

### Drawbacks[[edit](/index.php?title=Venom_talon&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Drawbacks")]

The main drawback of the venom talon is that it can not be simultaneously used with two [bionic](/wiki/Bionic_arm "Bionic arm") or [archotech arms](/wiki/Archotech_arm "Archotech arm"). [Manipulation](/wiki/Manipulation "Manipulation") above 100% does not affect [Shooting Accuracy](/wiki/Shooting_Accuracy "Shooting Accuracy"), and so for a dedicated gunfighter this may not be a significant drawback. However, [manipulation](/wiki/Manipulation "Manipulation") increases [general labor speed](/wiki/General_labor_speed "General labor speed"), meaning that if they have a role within your colony beyond fighting affected by [manipulation](/wiki/Manipulation "Manipulation"), such as crafting or constructing, they will be less effective at these roles than they otherwise could be. A pawn can still be equipped with one [bionic](/wiki/Bionic_arm "Bionic arm") or [archotech arm](/wiki/Archotech_arm "Archotech arm"), and one venom talon without it negatively affecting their melee capabilities after [1.1.2610](/wiki/Version/1.1.2610 "Version/1.1.2610") changed melee verb selection. 

If you choose two [bionic](/wiki/Bionic_arm "Bionic arm") or [archotech arms](/wiki/Archotech_arm "Archotech arm") over one arm and a venom talon but still want venom weaponry you also have the option of the [venom fangs](/wiki/Venom_fangs "Venom fangs"). The [venom fangs](/wiki/Venom_fangs "Venom fangs") are weaker than the venom talon, but have no conflicts with any implant and so can be used without compromise.
