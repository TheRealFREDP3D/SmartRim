# Research Tree

## Early Game Priority
1. **Smithing** — gateway to weapons and armor
2. **Electricity** — power for coolers, lights, turrets
3. **Batteries** — store excess wind power
4. **Air Conditioning** — freezer for food preservation
5. **Machining** — advanced weapons and components

## Mid Game
- **Microelectronics** — advanced power, comms
- **Fabrication** — craft advanced items
- **Multi-analyzer** — faster research

## Research Setup
- Dedicated researcher (Intellectual skill)
- Lit research bench
- Don't remove the only cook during shortage
- Research the next concrete need: preservation → power → defense → production

## Pre-Researched (Community Builder Mode)
These are already unlocked at game start in Community Builder difficulty:
- Electricity
- Stonecutting
- Passive Cooler
- Complex Furniture
- Complex Clothing
- Air Conditioning
- Nutrient Paste

## Research Name Notes
- "Medicine" research is actually called "Drug Production" in this game
- Drug lab crafting: healroot + cloth + neutroamine → herbal medicine
