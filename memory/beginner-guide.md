# Beginner Guide

## Recommended Setup

- **Scenario**: Crashlanded (3 colonists, balanced start)
- **Storyteller**: Cassandra Classic (predictable escalation)
- **Difficulty**: Medium / Adventure Story
- **Biome**: Temperate Forest (long growing season, wood, fertile soil)

## Day 0 (Pause Immediately!)

1. Unforbid all starting items (red X)
2. Draft colonists, equip weapons (rifle > pistol > knife)
3. Survey map: geysers, rich soil, mountains, ruins
4. Build shelter: 7x7 room with walls and door
5. Stockpile zone inside shelter (priority: Important)
6. Build 3 beds (1 per colonist)
7. Place table + chairs (avoids "ate without table" -3 mood)
8. Build horseshoe pin (recreation)

## Day 1

1. Build fueled stove + cook simple meal bill (Do until you have 10)
2. Build research bench
3. Plant rice (40-60 tiles) on rich soil if available
4. Harvest wild healroot (for herbal medicine)
5. Chop wood for materials
6. Set work priorities (Manual):
   - Everyone: Firefighting 1, Patient 1, Bed Rest 1, Basic 1
   - Cook: Cooking 1
   - Builder: Construction 1
   - Grower: Growing 1
   - Researcher: Research 1

## Days 2-3

1. Build butcher table + butcher forever bill
2. Build cooler room (7x7) with cooler at -5°C
3. Hunt easy animals (rabbits, deer — NOT predators)
4. Build sandbag cover near base entrance
5. Research Smithing (gateway to weapons)
6. Build medical beds (2-3) — toggle beds to medical

## Days 4-7

1. Move food to freezer
2. Build walls around base perimeter
3. Craft weapons after Smithing research
4. Plant healroot growing zone (needs Growing skill 8+)
5. Build drug lab for medicine crafting
6. Build wind turbine or solar panel for power

## Early raid window (UNVERIFIED day range)
The wiki does not give a "Days 8-15" first-raid window. Storyteller and raid points set threat size (wiki: AI_Storytellers, Raid_points). Session 5 (Phoebe, Community builder) had its first raid on Day 5. Plan defenses by Day 5 to be safe.

1. All colonists armed with weapons
2. Sandbag line with cover positions
3. Draft colonists, move to cover
4. Focus-fire ONE enemy at a time (critical!)
5. Retreat non-combatants to safe zone
6. Treat wounded immediately after fight
7. Bury/haul ALL corpses (wiki: "Colonist left unburied" thought after 4 game days in the open; per-corpse -10 is unverified)

## Days 16-30

1. Individual bedrooms (3x3 each, 1 bed, 1 light)
2. Separate kitchen (floored, clean — food poisoning!)
3. Recreation room with variety (horseshoes, chess, TV)
4. Power: solar panels + batteries
5. Trade caravans for medicine components

## Medicine Strategy (CRITICAL!)

- **Day 1**: Harvest wild healroot (any colonist with Plant Cut)
- **Day 2**: Plant healroot growing zone (needs Growing skill 8+)
- **Day 3**: Build drug lab
- **Day 5+**: Craft herbal medicine from healroot (wiki: herbal medicine is extracted from healroot; cloth + neutroamine + herbal are for industrial medicine, which needs Medicine production research)
- **Always**: Buy standard medicine from traders
- **Rule**: Restrict colonists to herbal medicine for minor injuries

## Defense Strategy

- Build killbox: single entrance corridor
- Sandbags inside corridor for cover
- Shooters behind sandbags
- Focus-fire one enemy at a time
- Melee pawn blocks doorway if enemies breach
- Retreat to medical area after fight
- Loot weapons from fallen raiders

## Research Priority Order

1. **Smithing** (Day 2-3) — weapons
2. **Batteries** (Day 4-5) — power storage
3. **Solar Panels** (Day 5-7) — reliable power
4. **Microelectronics** (Day 10+) — advanced research
5. **Machining** (Day 15+) — turrets, better weapons

## Critical Rules

- Pause often — spacebar is free
- Freeze food — cooler at -5°C to -10°C
- Bury/butcher bodies — unburied colonist corpses cause a "left unburied" thought after 4 days (wiki)
- Sandbags before first raid (wiki: 55% cover, built from Fabric or Leathery material, 5 units each; raid day is unverified)
- Don't hoard — wealth scales raid difficulty
- No pyromaniacs — they burn bases down
- Rice first — fastest food crop
- Cook meals — raw food = mood penalty
- **Harvest healroot Day 1** — medicine saves lives
- **Build medical beds** — downed colonists need treatment
- **Beware animals** — predators hunt colonists when hungry and keep attacking downed prey (wiki: Animals). Guinea pigs are herbivores; the Session 1 cause of death is not confirmed as a predator attack
