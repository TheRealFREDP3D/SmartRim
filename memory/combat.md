# Combat

## First Raid (Days 8-15)
- Most runs: first raid arrives in this window
- Have weapons + sandbags ready before it hits
- If behind: pause and re-prioritize immediately

## Raid Response Protocol
1. Spacebar → pause
2. Shelter non-combatants
3. Close exposed doors
4. Draft all combat-capable colonists
5. Move behind sandbag line
6. Focus-fire one enemy at a time
7. Tend wounds after fight
8. Haul/bury bodies
9. Strip raider weapons

## Cover Mechanics
- Sandbags: best early cover
- Rock chunks: decent cover
- Standing in open = suicide
- Enemies get hit penalties from cover

## Focus Fire
- Select everyone, right-click ONE enemy
- Do not let pawns pick own targets
- Split fire = how you lose
- Kill one at a time to reduce incoming damage

## Weapons (Early Game)
- Starting weapons: rifle, knife, pistol
- Raider guns often better than starting gear
- Sniper Rifle: first major upgrade (long range; exact tile range unverified)
- Careful Shooter trait adds huge value

## Tiers
- Tier 1: Starting weapons (pistol, knife, rifle)
- Tier 2: Crafted weapons (club, short bow)
- Tier 3: Researched weapons (gun, sniper rifle)
- Tier 4: Advanced (charge rifle, minigun)

## After Raid
- Bandage wounds first
- Haul or butcher bodies (corpse mood drain)
- Strip weapons from raiders
- Repair damaged buildings
- Check colonist mood (combat stress)
