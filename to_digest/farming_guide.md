# Rimworld - Farming

## Usable knowledge from the RimWorld Farming Guide transcript

This extracts the practical, actionable information on agriculture mechanics, growing methods, individual plants (stats, pros/cons, use cases), special/unique trees, wild plants, and related tips. Values are as stated in the transcript (growth times in days, yields as base/minimum harvest amounts, etc.). Skill requirements, light, temperature, and other rules are core mechanics.

### Core Agriculture Mechanics

- **Planting skill**: No hard minimum for most plants, but 5–6+ is recommended early to avoid wasting time training on wild crops/trees. Higher skill improves:
  - Harvest yield (chance of zero yield at low levels).
  - Planting/harvesting speed.
  - Foraging amount on caravans.
  - Pruning speed on Gauranlen trees.
- **Light requirement**: Most plants need ≥51% light (natural sunlight or sun lamps). Exceptions exist (e.g., fungus needs darkness; some tolerate lower light).
- **Temperature**: Average growth range ~42.8–107.6 °F (varies by plant). Outside the range growth slows, then stops, then the plant dies. Pawns refuse to plant if growth is currently impossible.
- **Seasons**: Without greenhouses, outdoor growth stops in winter in most biomes. Tropical/temperate regions have longer viable outdoor seasons.
- **Blight & fire**: Blight spreads up to 4 tiles; fire up to 2 tiles. Use road/empty space gaps (ideally 4 tiles between fields for blight safety, 2 for firebreaks). Separate fields help contain both.
- **Wildlife & raids**: Animals eat crops; raiders burn them. Walls or hunters help protect fields.
- **Hydroponics note**: Not all plants can grow in hydroponics. Trees generally cannot grow under full roofs.

### Growing Setups

1. **Open fields**  
   Simple: designate grow zones.  
   Pros: Easy, no power needed.  
   Cons: Weather/temperature exposure, animal raids, fire/blight risk. Prioritize fertile soil where possible.

2. **Full greenhouse**  
   Walls + roof + sun lamp(s) + optional hydroponics.  
   Power cost example (efficient setup): sun lamp ~2,900 W daytime; total with hydroponics ~4,580 W day / 1,680 W night.  
   Pros: Temperature control, year-round growth, protection.  
   Cons: High power, limited plant selection (no trees under full roof; some crops ground-only).

3. **Hybrid / open-roofed greenhouse**  
   Keep ≥¾ of the roof intact so the game still treats the area as “indoors” for temperature control, while leaving holes for trees/sunlight.  
   Pros: Trees possible, natural light for some plants, easier early-game setup that can later convert to full greenhouse.  
   Cons: Drafts make temperature harder to regulate; still needs power for heaters/coolers in extreme seasons.

**General tip**: Self-sufficient colonies benefit strongly from at least one greenhouse. Early hybrid setups transition well into full controlled environments.

### Food Crops

| **Rice**      | 3            | 6         | Fastest early food. High labor demand (constant plant/harvest). Switch after 1–2 harvests unless space-limited (gravship/hydroponics) or short growing season (tundra). Good for chemfuel later. 40-day shelf life. |

**Shelf-life ranking (approx.)**: Corn (60) > Rice (40) > Potato (30).

### Clothing / Material Crops

- **Cotton**: 8 days, yield 10 cloth (no processing needed). Early clothing/furniture/flooring; later still useful for replacements and trade (dusters, etc.). Always worth a small plot.
- **Devilstrand**: 22.5 days, yield 6. Mid/late-game clothing upgrade (dusters ≈ flak, pants/shirts ≈ Kevlar-level protection). High trade value. Ground-only (no hydroponics). **Cannot get blighted**. Research and plant early; needs large fields or greenhouse. One of the highest-value crops overall.

### Other Utility Crops

- **Haygrass**: 7 days, yield 18. Animal feed / straw flooring (burns extremely fast). Process into kibble preferred. Useful for animal-focused colonies or winter feed. Rancher ideologies may prefer it.
- **Fiber corn**: 6 days, yield 2 wood. Poor wood source, but: grows at only 30% light (normal lamps suffice for underground), no skill/resource barrier, useful for tree-hating ideologies (wood without chopping trees), early training crop.
- **Bonsai tree** (Ideology): Planted only in bonsai pots. ~13.5-day lifespan, provides wood on manual harvest + 15 beauty (plus pot material bonus). Grows in lamp light. Small mood buff for tree-loving ideologies; does not anger tree-haters. Treat as beauty plant with wood side-benefit (pawns do not auto-chop).
- **Tinctoria**: 2 days, yield 1 dye. Only purpose is dyeing apparel. Matching a pawn’s favorite color and/or ideology color gives +1 or +2 mood. Low effort, useful late-game flavor/mood.

### Medicine & Drug Crops

- **Healroot**: 7 days. Min skill 8. Critical for herbal medicine. Attracts wildlife → wall fields or keep hunters ready. Prefers hydroponics or fertile soil. Stops growing (but does not die) in winter. Always prioritize.
- **Hops**: 5 days, yield 8. Min skill 3. 25 hops → 5 wort → 5 beer. Easy early/mid money or recreation.
- **Smokeleaf**: 7.5 days, yield 9. Min skill 4. 4 leaves = 1 joint. Decent early tribal trade, but consciousness risk (can kill if overused) and better cash crops exist later.
- **Psychoid**: 9 days, yield 8. Min skill 6. Grows on polluted soil. Early: psychoid tea (mood/health). Later: flake/yayo (high-value drugs). Strong at every stage if drugs are allowed.

### Decorative Plants

- **Dandelions**: Ground-only, long beauty duration (~20 days). Can be planted in pens as minor animal food (haygrass is better).
- **Roses**: ~7.5-day life, 14 beauty. Good balance of beauty vs. maintenance. Strong early-game mood tool.
- **Daylilies**: 18 beauty, short life (~4.5 days) → higher replacement labor.
- Recommendation: Roses for most of the game; switch to sculptures late-game for higher beauty with zero maintenance.

### Trees (Standard)

Require Tree Sowing research + min skill 6. Need spacing (common efficient layout: 3×3 pockets with trees in corners, walkways between). Biome-native options:

- **Temperate**: Oak (slower/higher yield), Poplar (faster).
- **Tropical**: Teak (slow), Cecropia, Palm, Bamboo (fastest).
- **Arid/extreme desert**: Cactus (surprisingly high wood-per-day), Drago.
- **Cold (tundra etc.)**: Birch, Pine. (None on ice sheets/sea ice.)
- **Swamps/bogs**: Cypress (unique: grows on mud/marsh), Maple, Willow (broader).
- **Pollution trees**: Grey pine, pebble cactus, rat palm, etc. — low yield, ugly; only if nothing else grows or for role-play.
- **Glow Forest special**: Timber shroom (plantable there; does not count as tree-cutting for ideologies).

**Tip**: Cactus has the highest wood yield per day among standard trees in relevant data.

### Special / Unique Trees

- **Anima Tree** (Royalty): Natural psycaster path via meditation (tribal backstories preferred). Fire/cold/toxic resistant, no sunlight needed, thrumbo-safe. Destroyed → psychic scream + mood debuff (worse with higher sensitivity). Regenerates ~30 days. Alpha beavers will eat it.
- **Gauranlen Tree** (Ideology): Grown from pods (every ~60 days, or ~30 with Tree Connection). Keep clear of artificial structures and other trees. Link a sensitive/planting pawn for pruning + dryads (wood, medicine, defense). Moss adds beauty. Watch for beavers.
- **Pollux Tree** (Biotech): Spawns with pollution thresholds (checked every 15 days). Clears pollution in ~7-tile radius (1 tile every 10 hours, no waste packs). Seeds available from traders/crates. Valuable for mechanoid/late industrial play. Beavers still a threat.
- **Harbinger Tree** (Anomaly): Appears after monolith activation. Feeds on corpses, raw meat, skulls, bones. Grows fully → harvest twisted meat + biofuel. Relatively benign (does not attack pawns).
- **Arcan Tree** (space/ Odyssey-related): Seeds from crates/safes (especially near existing Arcan trees on asteroids). Provides air in enclosed spaces + converts nearby ground to fertile soil (0.5 in-game hours per tile in ~13×13 circular radius). Works underground, desert, space. Extremely valuable for indoor greenhouses of ground-only crops (devilstrand, corn, etc.) and vacuum environments.

### Wild / Harvestable Plants (Non-Plantable Directly)

Designate grow zones around them and disable sowing → pawns harvest when mature.

- **Wild healroot & berry bushes**: Excellent early emergency medicine/food.
- **Wild agave** (arid/desert): Berry-bush equivalent; cannot be eaten raw without mood penalty. Good for caravan foraging.
- **Ambrosia**: Event plant (temperate/swamp/rainforest/boreal/arid). High-value recreation or trade. Auto-harvest zones work well.
- **Agrilux** (caves/Glow Forest): Fungus + light source (up to 41% light).
- **Psilocaps** (mainly Glow Forest; also insect hives): Safe-ish high (inspiration or catatonic risk). Decent sell value.
- **Boomshrooms** (Glow Forest / insect lairs): Chemfuel source + light (50%). Explosive at ≥65% growth — handle carefully.

**Caravan note**: Planting skill improves foraging of berries/agave while traveling.

### High-Level Strategy Takeaways

- Early game: Rice (or potatoes on poor soil) + healroot + cotton + whatever wilds are available. Secure a small greenhouse or hybrid ASAP.
- Mid game: Transition to corn for bulk food, devilstrand for clothing/armor, psychoid for cash/drugs, haygrass if animal-heavy.
- Late game / specialized: Hydroponics for rice/potatoes/berries/fungus; ground plots or Arcan-fertilized soil for corn/devilstrand; decorative plants or sculptures for beauty; special trees for unique benefits (pollution cleanup, dryads, air + fertile soil, etc.).
- Always protect high-value long-grow crops (devilstrand, trees) from fire, blight, animals, and raids.
- Ideology & scenario matter heavily: fungus tolerance, tree-cutting memes, rancher focus, pollution levels, space bases, etc., change optimal choices.
